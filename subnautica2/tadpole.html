1 Why the Tadpole Changes Everything

🐸

Tadpole

★★★★☆ — Your First Submarine
Early-Mid Game

The Tadpole is your first and most transformative vehicle in Subnautica 2. This compact submarine gives you unlimited oxygen, 200m depth capability, and a safe mobile base to explore the alien ocean. Without it, you're stuck swimming. With it, the entire planet opens up.

★ What This Means for You

The Tadpole isn't just transportation — it's survival. Swimming below 100m drains your oxygen fast. The Tadpole lets you explore deep wrecks, scan fragments safely, and carry resources back to base. Every other vehicle in the game builds on this foundation.

4 Optimal Collection Route

Follow this order to minimize backtracking and maximize efficiency.

① Scan All 3 Tadpole Fragments

Fragment 1: Northeast Canyon wreckage (~280m depth) — follow the northeast beacon signal
Fragment 2: North Old Habitat ruins (~350m) — deep wreck with Leviathan nearby, approach cautiously
Fragment 3: Southwest Cliff ledge (~160m) — easiest to reach first, has great views
Pro tip: Fragment 3 is easiest — go there first to unlock the blueprint, then use your knowledge of the Tadpole's layout to find the other two faster.

② Gather Basic Materials

Farm Limestone Outcrops in Safe Shallows for Titanium (need 20 total for 2 Ingots)
Harvest Creepvine Seed Clusters in Kelp Forest for Silicone Rubber
Mine Quartz nodes in caves for Glass
Find Gold in Sandstone Outcrops (Kelp Forest caves have the best spawn rate)

③ Craft Components

Craft 2 Titanium Ingots (10 Titanium each)
Craft Glass (2 Quartz)
Craft Copper Wire (2 Copper) → then System Chip
Craft 2 Batteries (2 Acid Mushroom + 1 Copper each) → then Power Cell

④ Deploy and Build

Deploy Mobile Vehicle Bay near your base (needs 1 Titanium Ingot + 1 Lubricant)
Interact with Vehicle Bay → Select Tadpole → Build!
TADPOLE ACQUIRED

5 ⚠ Gotcha Tips — Don't Waste Your Time

Read this BEFORE you start.

⚠ Fragment 2 is in Leviathan territory

The North Old Habitat fragment is near a Leviathan patrol route. Listen for roars, stay close to the seafloor, and bring a Seaglide to escape quickly. Scan fast and leave.

⚠ You need a Seaglide first

Two of the three fragments are below 200m. Without a Seaglide (or High Capacity Air Tank), you won't have enough oxygen to reach them. Build a Seaglide before attempting fragment collection.

⚠ Titanium Ingots need 10 Titanium EACH

You need 20 Titanium total for the 2 Ingots. That's about 40-50 Limestone Outcrops. Farm in Safe Shallows where they're abundant and safe.

⚠ Gold is harder to find than it looks

Gold only comes from Sandstone/Shale Outcrops at ~25% rate. Kelp Forest caves have the best density. Bring a spare Oxygen Tank for longer cave dives.

⚠ Don't forget the Mobile Vehicle Bay

You can't build the Tadpole from your handheld Fabricator. You MUST craft and deploy a Mobile Vehicle Bay first (1 Titanium Ingot + 1 Lubricant).

6 🎁 Free Benefits — Loot Along the Way

Don't leave empty-handed.

🎁 Fragment hunting = free Titanium and Lithium

While traveling to fragment locations: You'll pass through Grassy Plateaus (Titanium, Copper) and Kelp Forest caves (Lithium, Gold). Scan everything — you'll need Lithium for Base Builder later.

🎁 Old Habitat exploration

The North wreck contains data logs (story progression), supply crates (free food/water), and sometimes unlocked storage lockers with useful early-game items.

🎁 Cave diving for Gold

While searching Sandstone Outcrops for Gold: You'll find Silver Ore (for Wiring Kit), Lead (for radiation suit/base), and cave sulfur deposits (for Crash Powder).

🎁 Kelp Forest resource run

While getting Creepvine Seeds: Collect Creepvine Samples (for Fiber Mesh → First Aid Kits), Metal Salvage (4x Titanium when recycled), and scan Stalker teeth (for Enamelled Glass later).
