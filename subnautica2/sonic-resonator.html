1 Why the Sonic Resonator Finds What Others Miss

Sonic Resonator

★★★☆☆ — Detects Hidden Resources
Early-Mid Game

The Sonic Resonator emits a pulse that highlights hidden resources, fragments, and points of interest within a 100m radius. Those outcrops you swam past? The fragment hidden behind a rock wall? The resource buried in sand? The Resonator reveals them all.

★ What This Means for You

Subnautica 2's ocean is designed to hide things. The Resonator is your answer to 'I know there's something here but I can't find it.' It turns blind searching into targeted gathering, cutting exploration time by half.

4 Optimal Collection Route

Follow this order to minimize backtracking and maximize efficiency.

① Scan Fragments

Both in accessible wrecks — Kelp Forest (~70m) and Grassy Plateaus (~100m). Use Seaglide if you don't have Tadpole yet.

② Get Magnetite

The bottleneck. Best early source: Lava Geysers at Safe Shallows/Kelp edges (~150m). Use Seaglide + extra O2 tanks. Park Tadpole at safe distance from heat.

③ Craft

All materials at Fabricator → Equip in tool slot

5 ⚠ Gotcha Tips — Don't Waste Your Time

Read this BEFORE you start.

⚠ Magnetite is the gatekeeper

Everything cool in Subnautica 2 needs Magnetite, and early-game Magnetite sources are limited. Lava Geysers are your best bet before Depth Module MK1.

⚠ 100m range — get close

The Resonator only pings within 100m. Don't expect it to reveal things across the biome. Use it methodically in areas where you suspect hidden items.

6 🎁 Free Benefits — Loot Along the Way

Don't leave empty-handed.

🎁 Lava Geyser exploration

While getting Magnetite: Thermal Vents (for future reactor plans), unique flora scans, and sometimes small wreckage with supplies.

🎁 Kelp Forest wreck

While getting fragments: Data logs, supply crates, and often a Seaglide Fragment if you haven't found one yet.
