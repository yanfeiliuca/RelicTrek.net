1 Why Solar Power Changes Exploration

☀

Photovoltaic Charger

★★☆☆☆ — Solar Recharge
Early Game

The Photovoltaic Charger slowly recharges your Tadpole's Power Cell whenever you're near the surface during daytime. It's free, unlimited power — park your Tadpole at 50m during the day and watch the battery fill up without spending a single resource.

★ What This Means for You

This module effectively removes the range anxiety of early-game exploration. Combined with Engine Efficiency, you can explore indefinitely during the day. Park near the surface, grab a snack IRL, come back to a fully charged sub.

4 Optimal Collection Route

Follow this order to minimize backtracking and maximize efficiency.

① Scan Fragments + Gather

Both fragments in shallow wrecks (40-60m). While there, grab Quartz from nearby caves and Copper from Limestone Outcrops. This is the easiest vehicle upgrade.

② Install

Craft at Vehicle Mod Station → Install

5 ⚠ Gotcha Tips — Don't Waste Your Time

Read this BEFORE you start.

⚠ Only works near surface during DAY

Must be above ~80m depth and it must be daytime. Doesn't work at night or deep. Plan your recharging stops accordingly.
