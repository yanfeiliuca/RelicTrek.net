1 Why Habitat Builder Is Your Second Home

🏗

Habitat Builder

★★★★☆ — Build Underwater Bases
Early-Mid Game

The Habitat Builder is the tool that turns the alien ocean into your domain. It lets you construct underwater bases — rooms, corridors, observatories, moonpools, fabricators, and more. A well-placed base is your safe harbor, storage hub, crafting center, and vehicle dock all in one.

★ What This Means for You

Swimming back to your Lifepod every time you need to craft or sleep wastes enormous amounts of time. A strategically placed base near key biomes eliminates travel time, provides unlimited oxygen, and gives you a true sense of progression. This is the tool that transforms you from a survivor into a settler.

4 Optimal Collection Route

Follow this order to minimize backtracking and maximize efficiency.

① Scan 3 Fragments

Fragment 1: Safe Shallows wreck (~30m) — easiest, often one of the first things you find
Fragment 2: Kelp Forest wreck (~50m) — while getting Creepvine Seeds
Fragment 3: Grassy Plateaus (~100m) — deepest, use Seaglide or Tadpole

② Gather Complex Materials

Lubricant — Creepvine Seed Cluster from Kelp Forest
Silver Ore — Sandstone Outcrops in Kelp Forest caves (~30% rate)
Table Coral Sample — Harvest flat table corals in Safe Shallows (easy to miss if you don't know what they look like)
Gold — Sandstone Outcrops (~25% rate)

③ Craft Components

Craft Wiring Kit (2 Silver Ore)
Craft Computer Chip (2 Gold + 1 Copper Wire + 1 Table Coral)
Gather 4 Titanium from Limestone Outcrops

④ Build

All components at Fabricator → Equip in tool slot

5 ⚠ Gotcha Tips — Don't Waste Your Time

Read this BEFORE you start.

⚠ Table Coral is easy to miss

Table Coral looks like flat, round purple/green plates on reef walls. It's NOT the tall tube coral or brain coral. Look for flat table-like structures on Safe Shallows reefs.

⚠ Silver Ore is harder than Titanium

Silver only comes from Sandstone Outcrops at ~30% rate. You need 2 Silver Ore for the Wiring Kit. Kelp Forest caves have the best Sandstone density — bring extra O2.

⚠ Base location matters enormously

Don't build your first base in Safe Shallows — too shallow, limited resources. Grassy Plateaus (~100m) is ideal: central location, access to multiple biomes, thermal vents nearby for power.

6 🎁 Free Benefits — Loot Along the Way

Don't leave empty-handed.

🎁 Safe Shallows wreck

While getting Fragment 1: Starting supplies, data logs, often unlocked storage with free batteries/food. Plus easy Table Coral samples on nearby reefs.

🎁 Kelp Forest resource run

While getting Fragment 2 + Lubricant: Collect Metal Salvage (4x Titanium when recycled), Stalker Teeth (for Enamelled Glass later), and scan unique flora/fauna.

🎁 Grassy Plateaus

While getting Fragment 3: Ideal base location — central, safe, great views. Also has Shale Outcrops (Gold, Lithium) and access to Jellyshroom Caves entrance.

🎁 Cave diving for Silver

While hunting Silver Ore: Find Gold (same outcrops), Lead (for Radiation Suit), and unique cave creatures to scan for PDA.
