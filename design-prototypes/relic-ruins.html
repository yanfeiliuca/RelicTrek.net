Ruins Greatsword

Colossal Weapon of the Ancient
♦

Overview

The Ruins Greatsword is one of the most visually stunning colossal weapons in Elden Ring. Wielded by the Misbegotten Warrior at Redmane Castle, this weapon deals devastating physical damage and boasts the unique skill Wave of Destruction — a gravity-wave shockwave that sends enemies flying and shatters their guard.

Damage Type Weapon Skill Weight Requirements Scaling
Physical / Magic Wave of Destruction 23.0 STR 50 / INT 16 STR B / INT D
⚒

Acquisition Path

The Ruins Greatsword is dropped by the Misbegotten Warrior boss at Redmane Castle in southeastern Caelid. Unlike many other colossal weapons, it cannot be crafted — it must be earned through combat. Follow the steps below to claim this ancient relic.

Reach Caelid
Journey east from Limgrave through the Smoldering Church
Redmane Castle
Navigate the fortress outskirts and breach the main gate
Defeat Warrior
Challenge the Misbegotten Warrior in the castle plaza
Claim Greatsword
Loot the Ruins Greatsword from the fallen warrior

Optimal Route

Fastest path to Redmane Castle from Limgrave. This route minimizes exposure to scarlet rot while maximizing Site of Grace activations for safe checkpoints.

Limgrave Starting Point
Begin at the First Step Site of Grace. Stock up on healing items.
~0 min
Fort Haight → Bridge of Sacrifice
Ride Torrent east across the bridge. Activate Bridge of Sacrifice grace.
~5 min
Smoldering Church — Caelid Entry
First Site of Grace in Caelid. Rest and prepare for rot resistance.
~10 min
Caelid Highway South
Follow the southern highway to avoid the swamp. Watch for giant crows.
~18 min
Redmane Castle Main Gate
Activate the grace outside the castle. The Misbegotten Warrior awaits inside.
~25 min

Gotcha Tips

⚠

Festival Event Warning: Redmane Castle has a festival event that changes boss availability — if Starscourge Radahn's festival is active, the castle layout will be different and the Misbegotten Warrior may not spawn! Check the courtyard for festival banners. If present, you must defeat Radahn first or reload the area after the festival ends.

  • The Misbegotten Warrior is highly aggressive — roll into his attacks, not away
  • His leap attack has a long wind-up — use this window to cast buffs or heal
  • Summoning Spirit Ashes at the start will split his aggro, making the fight manageable
  • If the festival is active, speak to Jerren in the castle to begin the Radahn fight
  • After obtaining the sword, visit a Smithing Table to unlock its Ash of War slot
