Rivers of Blood

Legendary Arcane Katana

♦ Overview

Rivers of Blood is one of the most powerful katanas in Elden Ring, infamous for its Corpse Piler weapon skill that unleashes a flurry of blood-coated strikes. A must-have for Arcane bleed builds.

Damage Type Weapon Skill Weight Requirements
Slash / Pierce (Bleed) Corpse Piler 6.5 STR 12 / DEX 18 / ARC 20
⚒ Crafting Recipe

Rivers of Blood is obtained by defeating Bloody Finger Okina at the Church of Repose in Mountaintops of the Giants. No crafting required — but getting there is the real challenge.

⚠ Gotcha Tips
⚠ Invasion Warning
Okina invades instantly upon approaching the church — be prepared for a PvP-style fight before you can summon help.
  • 💡 Okina is vulnerable to frost. Freezing Pot can interrupt his moves.
  • 🌎 If you burn the Erdtree before killing Okina, he will NOT spawn. Do this first!
  • 🛡 Bring high bleed resistance gear — Okina's own Rivers of Blood will proc bleed fast.
  • 👣 Light the Church of Repose site of grace BEFORE triggering the invasion.
