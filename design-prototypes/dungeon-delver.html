Zenith

The Ultimate Sword — A journey through all of Terraria

⚔
♦

Overview

Zenith is the most powerful sword in Terraria, forged by combining 13 different swords from across the game's progression. It's the ultimate flex and a true journey through the entire game. When swung, it summons a cascading arc of every sword in its recipe, tearing through enemies with devastating force.

Optimal Route

The most efficient order to collect all 13 swords — minimize backtracking and prepare ahead:

Start: Craft Copper Shortsword
First thing after spawning. Keep it safe in a chest.
Early Exploration: Starfury + Enchanted Sword
Check Floating Islands and Underground shrines while exploring. Build sky bridges early.
Jungle Phase: Blade of Grass + Bee Keeper
Farm Jungle Spores and Stingers. Build an arena and defeat Queen Bee.
Underworld: Fiery Greatsword
Mine Hellstone at the bottom of the world. Need 60 Hellstone Bars total.
Dungeon: Muramasa + Craft Night's Edge
Beat Skeletron, loot Dungeon chests. Combine all 4 at Demon/Crimson Altar.
Hardmode: Excalibur + Breaker Blade
Defeat all 3 Mechanical Bosses for Hallowed Bars. Breaker Blade from WoF.
Endgame: Seedler + Star Wrath + Meowmere
Defeat Plantera and Moon Lord. These are the rarest drops — may need multiple attempts.
Final: Forge Zenith at Mythril Anvil
Combine all 13 swords. The ultimate weapon is yours!
⚠

Gotcha Tips

⚠ Critical Warning
Enchanted Sword is the rarest pre-Hardmode component. It only spawns in Underground shrines, and not every world generates a shrine. You may need to search multiple worlds or fish for Titanium Crates in Hardmode as an alternative!
  • Don't throw away your Copper Shortsword — you'll need it for the final craft!
  • Build an item storage system early. You'll collect many swords along the journey.
  • Enchanted Sword shrines are easier to find using a Starfury or Light Pets for visibility.
  • In Journey Mode, you can duplicate swords after researching them to help friends.
  • Meowmere and Star Wrath only drop from Moon Lord — expect 3-5 attempts on average.
  • Create a dedicated chest near your anvil labeled "Zenith Components" to stay organized.
  • Some swords (like Night's Edge) are crafting ingredients — craft them as soon as possible.
