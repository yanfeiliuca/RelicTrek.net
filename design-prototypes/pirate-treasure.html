Master Sword

The Blade That Seals the Darkness

One-Handed Weapon

The legendary blade wielded by the hero across ages. Sealed within the Light Dragon, it awaits one with sufficient strength of heart to draw it forth once more.

♦

Overview

The Master Sword is Link's iconic weapon in Tears of the Kingdom. Unlike BOTW, you must traverse the Depths, build zonai vehicles, and recover your hearts to pull it from the Light Dragon's head. The journey is perilous but the reward is Hyrule's most powerful blade.

Attribute Value Notes
Damage (Base) 30 Doubles near malice/sealed enemies
Fused Potential +25 (Silver Lynel Horn) Highest base damage with fuse
Durability 40 / Unbreakable* Recharges in 10 min if broken
Location Light Dragon's Head Flies at ~2200m altitude
Required Hearts 2 Full Rows (16+) Temporary hearts do NOT count

Optimal Route

The fastest way to get the Master Sword. This route minimizes travel time by chaining sky towers and using the Depths for fast travel.

Complete "Camera Work in the Depths"

Talk to Josha in Lookout Landing to unlock camera and geoglyph quest chain.

Collect All 11 Dragon's Tears

Visit each geoglyph, take a picture, and watch the memory. Track them in Adventure Log.

Complete "Find the Fifth Sage"

Progress the main quest until Mineru's spirit temple is complete. This is required for the sword questline.

Light Dragon Appears

After all tears, the dragon transforms and begins its route across all sky regions.

Launch from Typhlo Ruins Tower

Best launch point — the dragon passes directly overhead at ~5:00 AM game time.

Land & Pull the Sword

Use your flying device to reach the dragon's head. Press A to pull with 2 full heart rows.

⚠

Gotcha Tips

The Light Dragon flies HIGH

It cruises at approximately 2,200 meters altitude — bring plenty of Zonai charge! A single balloon won't get you there; bring multiple fans and a backup battery.

  • ➤ Temporary hearts do NOT count — only permanent heart containers matter for pulling the sword.
  • ➤ The dragon cannot be stopped or slowed. Time your approach carefully.
  • ➤ If you fall, use your paraglider — the dragon's updraft will catch you and restore stamina.
  • ➤ Save before attempting! If you don't have enough hearts, the pull will fail and you'll need to chase again.
  • ➤ Best time: Dawn (5:00 AM) at Typhlo Ruins — the dragon passes closest to the tower.
  • ➤ Equip climbing gear or Zora armor — the dragon's back is slippery and angled.
