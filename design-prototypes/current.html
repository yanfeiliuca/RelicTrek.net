Rivers of Blood

Legendary Arcane Katana — bleed build signature weapon

⚔
Type Katana (Melee / Arcane)
Weight 6.5
Scaling STR E / DEX D / ARC D
01 Overview

Rivers of Blood is one of the most sought-after katanas in Elden Ring. It features a unique Weapon Art "Corpse Piler" that unleashes a rapid flurry of blood-infused slashes, building bleed status extremely fast. This weapon is a must-have for Arcane-focused bleed builds.

05 Gotcha Tips
⚠ Missable: If you defeat the Fire Giant and then the final boss of the area before killing Okina, he will not spawn. Get this weapon before progressing too far.
⚠ Scaling Bug: Prior to Patch 1.03, Arcane scaling was broken. Ensure your game is updated to the latest version for full damage output.
ℹ Build Synergy: Pair with the White Mask helmet and Lord of Blood's Exultation talisman for a massive damage boost when bleed procs.
ℹ FP Cost: Corpse Piler costs 17 FP per use. Consider investing in Mind or equipping a Cerulean Flask for extended fights.
