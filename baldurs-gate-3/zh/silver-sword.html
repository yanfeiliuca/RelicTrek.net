星界银剑

难度：★★★★★
传奇 巨剑 第3幕 莱埃泽尔任务

1 概览

星界银剑是博德之门3中叙事上最重大、机制上最强大的武器之一。这把传奇巨剑造成1d10+3挥砍伤害，并在对抗智力吞噬者和其他异怪的攻击检定上获得优势——这一加成在第三章的几场关键遭遇战中被证明是极其有效的。

星界银剑与吉斯洋基传说紧密相连。这些武器由吉斯洋基铁匠在星界位面使用数个世纪以来对抗灵吸怪压迫者的战争中传承下来的技术锻造。银剑不仅仅是一把武器——它是吉斯洋基身份的象征，是一件神圣的神器。

在机制上，星界银剑是一把双手巨剑，具有多功能属性。其1d10+3挥砍伤害使其成为游戏中基础伤害最高的武器之一。对抗智力吞噬者的优势出奇地强大——智力吞噬者出现在第三章的几场主要遭遇战中。

星界银剑还拥有一个独特的武器动作星界之刃，每次短休可以激活一次。这个能力使刀刃被星界能量包围持续3轮，每次攻击额外造成1d6力场伤害，并使武器无视挥砍伤害抗性。

2 获取方式

星界银剑是通过莱埃泽尔的同伴任务链获得的，该任务链跨越游戏的所有三幕，并在第三幕的一系列关键决定中达到高潮。这个任务名为吉斯洋基战士。

The Companion Quest Chain

通往银剑的道路需要完成莱埃泽尔个人任务的每个阶段。这不是一个简单的收集任务——它是一个深度叙事驱动的体验，对莱埃泽尔的角色发展和游戏结局有重大影响。

Critical Choice: Voss's Proposal

在第三幕中，到达博德之门的下城区后，你会遇到由基斯拉克·沃斯领导的吉斯洋基小队。沃斯揭示了关键信息：传说中已死的吉斯洋基领袖俄耳甫斯王子实际上被主要反派囚禁。

The Path to the Silver Sword

要获得星界银剑，你必须遵循沃斯的提议并致力于释放俄耳甫斯王子。这涉及一系列特定选择：首先同意沃斯的联盟，其次说服莱埃泽尔信任沃斯，第三推进主线故事到达星界监狱。

Orpheus's Reward

在从星界监狱释放俄耳甫斯王子并完成与他的对话序列后，星界银剑作为吉斯洋基荣誉恢复的象征被赐予莱埃泽尔。俄耳甫斯亲自将刀刃交给莱埃泽尔。

3 前置需求

⚔️ Prerequisite: Lae'zel in Your Party (Active)

Lae'zel must be in your active party — not just recruited, but actually traveling with you — at multiple critical points throughout the quest chain. If Lae'zel is dismissed to camp during key conversations, quest triggers may fail to activate. Keep her in your party from Act 1 through Act 3 to ensure all dialogue flags fire correctly.

⚔️ Prerequisite: Progress Through All 3 Acts

The Silver Sword quest chain begins in Act 1 when you first recruit Lae'zel at the Ravaged Beach, continues through Act 2 with encounters at Moonrise Towers involving githyanki forces, and culminates in Act 3. You cannot rush to the Silver Sword — it requires completing the entire narrative arc.

⚔️ Prerequisite: Free Orpheus from the Astral Prison

You must complete the Iron Throne prison sequence and then navigate the Mind Flayer Colony to reach the Astral Prison where Orpheus is held. This requires progressing the main Act 3 story past the point where you rescue the Gondian prisoners from the Iron Throne. The Astral Prison is accessed through a portal in the deepest section of the Mind Flayer Colony.

⚔️ Required: Agree to Voss's Alliance

When Kith'rak Voss approaches you at the Sewer Entrance (X:-190, Y:65) in Act 3, you must agree to his alliance proposal. Declining or attacking Voss locks you out of the Orpheus questline and therefore the Silver Sword. The correct dialogue choices are: "I'm listening" → "I'll help free Orpheus". Any hostile response fails the quest.

⚔️ Required: Persuade Lae'zel OR High Approval

After agreeing to Voss's alliance, Lae'zel will react — she may want to attack Voss or resist the plan. You must either: (1) pass a Persuasion check (DC 18) to calm her down, OR (2) have Lae'zel's approval above 40, in which case she trusts your judgment automatically. If you fail both, Lae'zel attacks Voss, the alliance breaks, and the Silver Sword is lost.

⚔️ Required: Do NOT Side with the Emperor

In the Mind Flayer Colony, the Emperor (the illithid entity who has been guiding you) will attempt to convince you to let Orpheus remain imprisoned. You must reject the Emperor's proposal and insist on freeing Orpheus. If you side with the Emperor, Orpheus stays imprisoned, Voss abandons you, and the Silver Sword quest fails permanently. The correct dialogue choice is: "Orpheus deserves his freedom".

⚔️ Recommended: High Persuasion Character

Multiple key dialogue checks in this quest chain require Persuasion, Intimidation, or Insight. A Bard, Warlock, or Paladin with +10 or higher Persuasion will have a much easier time navigating the political minefield of githyanki-illithid relations. Consider bringing a high-Charisma character to all conversations with Voss and Orpheus.

⚔️ Recommended: Complete the Iron Throne Beforehand

The Astral Prison is most easily accessed after completing the Iron Throne prison rescue (the Gondian sequence). This quest triggers automatically as part of the main story in Act 3. Ensure you have strong underwater combat capabilities and gear that grants cold resistance before attempting the Iron Throne, as it features submerged sections with water-based enemies.

4 详细路线

这是从第一幕到第三幕通往星界银剑的完整任务链。必须在关键时刻做出正确选择。

ACT 1 — Foundation:
→ Recruit Lae'zel at the Ravaged Beach (she is trapped in a mind flayer pod)
→ Keep her in your active party for all major story events
→ At the Mountain Pass, encounter Kith'rak Voss (githyanki patrol)
→ CRITICAL: Do NOT attack Voss — use diplomacy or deception
→ Complete Act 1 and travel to the Shadow-Cursed Lands

ACT 2 — Development:
→ At Moonrise Towers, encounter more githyanki forces
→ Speak with Lae'zel after each major event — build her approval above 40
→ Choices that increase Lae'zel's approval: supporting strength, combat prowess, githyanki culture
→ Choices that decrease Lae'zel's approval: showing weakness, allying with illithids, being merciful to enemies
→ Complete Act 2 and arrive in Baldur's Gate (Act 3)

ACT 3 — The Critical Chain:
→ Enter the Lower City of Baldur's Gate
→ Progress the main story until the Steel Watch quest triggers
→ Navigate to the Sewer Entrance (X:-190, Y:65)
→ CRITICAL ENCOUNTER: Kith'rak Voss appears

Voss Dialogue Sequence:
→ "I'm listening" (not hostile)
→ Hear his proposal about Orpheus
→ "I'll help free Orpheus" — accept the alliance
→ Lae'zel reacts — Persuade her (DC 18) or have 40+ approval
→ Voss gives you the Astral Prism Key

The Iron Throne & Mind Flayer Colony:
→ Progress the main story to the Iron Throne prison sequence
→ Rescue the Gondian prisoners (combat-heavy underwater sequence)
→ Continue to the Mind Flayer Colony (accessed through the Iron Throne)
→ Navigate the colony — defeat mind flayers and intellect devourers
→ Reach the deepest section and locate the Astral Prison portal

The Emperor's Test:
→ The Emperor confronts you before the portal
→ He argues that Orpheus should stay imprisoned
→ CRITICAL: Reject the Emperor's proposal
→ Select: "Orpheus deserves his freedom"
→ If you have high approval with the Emperor, you may need to pass an Intimidation check (DC 20)

Freeing Orpheus:
→ Enter the Astral Prison through the portal
→ Speak with Prince Orpheus — complete the full dialogue tree
→ Choose to release him from his astral chains
→ Orpheus is freed — cutscene triggers
→ RECEIVE: Silver Sword of the Astral Plane (Legendary Greatsword)
→ Orpheus joins as a temporary ally for the final battle

Alternative: Lae'zel's Kith'rak Path

如果莱埃泽尔接受她的吉斯洋基遗产并选择成为基斯拉克军阀，银剑会以关于领导和荣誉的额外对话授予。武器的属性在两条路径中完全相同——差异纯粹是叙事性的。

5 Gotcha Tips

⚠️ LAe'ZEL MUST BE IN YOUR PARTY — NOT AT CAMP

莱埃泽尔需要在你的 active party 中——不仅仅是招募了，而是实际与你同行——在沃斯遭遇战、主脑对抗和俄耳甫斯对话的多个关键节点。如果她在这些时刻在营地，任务标记可能无法触发。

⚠️ SIDING WITH THE EMPEROR LOCKS THE SWORD

如果你选择站在主脑一边并让俄耳甫斯保持被囚禁，银剑任务将永久失败。在该周目中没有办法从这一决定中恢复。主脑会提供一个替代奖励，但对于近战构筑来说劣于银剑。

⚠️ KILLING VOSS IN ACT 1 LOCKS THE QUEST

如果你在第一章的山口遭遇战中攻击并杀死了基斯拉克·沃斯，他不会在第三幕出现提供联盟。没有沃斯，俄耳甫斯任务无法启动，银剑也无法获得。

⚠️ LAe'ZEL'S APPROVAL IS HARD TO RAISE

莱埃泽尔有一些最苛刻的好感度要求。她重视力量、战斗能力、吉斯洋基文化和直率。她鄙视软弱、对敌人的仁慈和对权威的顺从。要达到40+好感度，始终选择攻击性对话选项。

⚠️ THE SILVER SWORD IS BOUND TO LAe'ZEL

虽然任何拥有巨剑熟练的角色都可以装备星界银剑，但它在主题上与莱埃泽尔绑定，她 wield it 时有独特的配音对话。如果你把剑给另一个角色，你会错过这些独特的互动。

⚠️ ORPHEUS CAN DIE DURING THE ESCAPE

当你从星界监狱释放俄耳甫斯时，一场对抗灵吸怪守卫的战斗遭遇战会立即触发。如果俄耳甫斯在这场战斗中死亡，任务可能失败，银剑将不会被授予。在逃脱序列中不惜一切代价保护俄耳甫斯。

⚠️ ASTRAL EDGE DOES NOT STACK WITH HEX

星界之刃武器动作的加值力场伤害不与 hex 的加值黯蚀伤害叠加——你获得较高的那个，不是两者。然而，星界之刃确实与巨武器大师、神圣重击和优势骰叠加。

⚠️ THE SWORD CAN BE LOST IF YOU BETRAY ORPHEUS

在获得银剑后，之后背叛俄耳甫斯并重新囚禁他是可能的。这样做不会从库存中移除银剑，但会永久禁用星界之刃武器动作。如果你背叛俄耳甫斯，剑会恢复为标准+3巨剑。

Narrative Benefits

俄耳甫斯任务线是博德之门3中叙事最丰富的序列之一。释放俄耳甫斯从根本上改变了最终战斗的动态——他作为14级战士加入，拥有独特的吉斯洋基能力。

此外，选择释放俄耳甫斯而不是遵从主脑的反对是主线故事中的一个重大转折点。它表明你的队伍重视自由和正义而非便利和力量。
