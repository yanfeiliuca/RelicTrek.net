尼鲁纳

Legendary Trident | Act 3 | 难度： ★★★★★ | Circus of the Last Days

📋 Overview

尼鲁纳是一把号令雷电原始狂怒的传奇三叉戟，隐藏在博德之门3中最 whimsical yet treacherous encounters 之一后面。与大多数躺在黑暗地下城尽头或强大Boss手中的传奇武器不同，尼鲁纳被藏在一个宝箱中， at the end of a psychedelic journey through a tropical jungle。

作为传奇三叉戟，尼鲁纳在成功命中时造成1d8 + 2穿刺伤害， backed by a +2 weapon enchantment。然而，这把武器的真正身份在于其雷鸣特殊效果：每次攻击都会触发雷鸣爆炸，对附近敌人造成额外范围伤害。

雷鸣爆炸效果使尼鲁纳独特地适合经常面对多个对手的近战 focused 角色。当你用三叉戟攻击敌人时， resulting thunder burst radiates outward in a 5-foot radius。

武器的美学与其力量相匹配：尼鲁纳的三叉尖端 crackle with contained lightning，柄部包裹着似乎吸收光线而非反射它的 dark storm-leather。

Beyond its combat applications, the Nyrulna carries an air of mystery regarding its origin。这把武器没有制造者印记，也没有与被遗忘国度中任何神祇或派系的联系。

⚔ Acquisition Method

尼鲁纳的获取在传奇武器中是独特的，因为它不涉及战斗或地下城探索。Instead, it is a puzzle-and-exploration reward obtained through social interaction and clever thinking。

Phase 1: Find the Circus of the Last Days

最后之日马戏团是一个旅行嘉年华， set up in the Rivington district of Baldur's Gate。它在你在第三章取得初步进度后变得可访问。马戏团很难错过—— colorful tents, banners, and noise。

To reach the circus from the Act 3 starting point:

  • Travel to the Rivington waypoint (unlocked automatically upon Act 3 arrival)
  • Head north through the refugee camp, following the sounds of music and cheering
  • Look for the large striped tent and colorful decorations at the northern edge of the district
  • Speak with Lucretious at the entrance if the gate is barred — she will grant entry

    • 阿喀比是一个灯神——一个强大的元素生物—— who has disguised himself as a carnival barker and set up a Wheel of Fortune game。他欢快的举止隐藏了 trickster nature。

    Phase 2: Meet Akabi the Djinn

    接近阿喀比并发起对话。他会热情地解释他的转盘游戏并鼓励你旋转。这里的对话选项对于触发尼鲁纳获取路径至关重要：

    如果你通过了感知检定，你的角色意识到阿喀比的转盘在机械上被动了手脚，永远不会落在大奖上。阿喀比变得防御性并试图将其当作笑话。

  • "What can I win?" → Akabi describes various prizes (all relatively mundane)
  • "I'll spin the wheel!" → Begins the minigame, but you will NOT win the Nyrulna this way
  • "Something seems off about this wheel..." → Perception DC 18 to notice the wheel is rigged; this opens the Nyrulna path
  • [Rogue] "I can see the mechanism. It's weighted." → Automatic success with Sleight of Hand proficiency

    • 在发现被动手脚的转盘后，你有几个选项来继续：直接对抗阿喀比、自己修复转盘、敲诈阿喀比，或攻击阿喀比。

    Phase 3: Expose Akabi's Trick

    After discovering the rigged wheel, you have several options for how to proceed:

  • Confront Akabi: Accuse him outright and demand he play fair. This leads to a Persuasion or Intimidation check (DC 16) to force him to remove the weight from the wheel.
  • Fix the Wheel Yourself: Use Sleight of Hand (DC 20) to surreptitiously remove the weighting mechanism while Akabi is distracted. This is the stealthy approach that avoids direct confrontation.
  • Blackmail Akabi: Threaten to expose him to Lucretious and the circus management. Deception DC 18, but may result in additional rewards beyond the wheel spin.
  • Attack Akabi: Initiate combat. NOT RECOMMENDED — Akabi is a CR 12 djinn with devastating spellcasting abilities and high-level elemental magic. Defeating him is possible but extremely difficult and unnecessary.

    • 推荐方法：使用巧手选项自己修复转盘，或通过说服检定迫使阿喀比公平游戏。无论哪种方式，结果都是一样的：下次有人旋转转盘时， rigging is removed。

    Phase 4: Spin the "Fixed" Wheel (The Trick)

    作为传奇三叉戟，尼鲁纳在成功命中时造成1d8 + 2穿刺伤害， backed by a +2 weapon enchantment。然而，这把武器的真正身份在于其雷鸣特殊效果：每次攻击都会触发雷鸣爆炸，对附近敌人造成额外范围伤害。

  • If the rigging was NOT successfully removed: You win one of the minor prizes (potions, minor gold, etc.) and can try again.
  • If the rigging WAS successfully removed: The wheel lands on the grand prize — and Akabi, enraged that you have beaten his game, teleports you to a remote jungle as "punishment" for being a clever cheater.

    • 被传送到丛林正是你想要的。This jungle is a hidden area not accessible through any other means, and it contains the chest with the Nyrulna。

    Phase 5: Survive the Jungle and Find the Chest

    丛林是一个小而危险的地图，充满了 aggressive wildlife and environmental hazards。Upon arrival, your entire party is deposited in a clearing surrounded by dense tropical vegetation。

    The jungle contains:

  • Tropical Spiders (CR 4) — Venomous spiders that use Web and poison attacks
  • Jungle Panthers (CR 5) — Fast predators with Pounce and multiattack
  • Carnivorous Plants (CR 3) — Stationary enemies that use grapple and acid attacks
  • Poisonous Spore Clouds — Environmental hazards that deal ongoing poison damage
  • Quicksand Pits — Difficult terrain that can restrain characters (Athletics DC 16 to escape)

    • 丛林包含：热带蜘蛛、丛林黑豹、食肉植物、有毒孢子云和流沙坑。The chest containing the Nyrulna is located behind a waterfall in a small cave。

    1. From the arrival clearing, head east through the dense foliage
    2. Defeat or bypass the spider nest in the large tree (Perception DC 16 to spot an alternate path around)
    3. Cross the rope bridge over the ravine (Athletics DC 14, or use Misty Step/Fly)
    4. Follow the river downstream to the waterfall
    5. Enter the cave behind the waterfall — the chest is visible on a stone pedestal

    The chest is locked and trapped。Inside, you find the Nyrulna, 500 gold, and a Potion of Storm Giant Strength。

    Phase 6: Return to the Circus

    After looting the chest, look for the return portal — a shimmering distortion in the air near the arrival clearing。返回马戏团后，你可以用新获得的传奇武器与阿喀比对质。

  • Ranged weapons for spider nests (avoid triggering Web traps in melee)

  • Healing potions for poison damage over time

    After exposing the rigged wheel, choose your method of dealing with Akabi:

If failed, he catches you and teleports you to the jungle immediately (still progresses the quest, but you miss the satisfaction of outsmarting him).
  • Option B — Persuasion: "Remove the weight. Play fair." → Persuasion DC 16. Akabi grudgingly agrees but warns you not to win "too much."
  • Option C — Intimidation: "Fix the wheel, or I fix your face." → Intimidation DC 18. Same outcome as Persuasion but with different dialogue.

    Recommended Jungle Path:

    1. From the clearing, head southeast toward the large tree with visible webs (spider nest)
    2. Either fight the spiders (moderate difficulty) or use the hidden path around the north side (Perception DC 16 to spot)
    3. Continue east to the ravine edge; the rope bridge is visible spanning the gap
    4. Cross the bridge — Athletics DC 14 for each character, or use Misty Step/Dimension Door/Fly to bypass
    5. Follow the river south from the bridge; the sound of falling water grows louder
    6. Reach the waterfall at the southern edge of the map
    7. Enter the cave behind the waterfall — the chest is immediately visible on a stone pedestal

    Step 8: Open the Chest

    如果你通过了感知检定，你的角色意识到阿喀比的转盘在机械上被动了手脚，永远不会落在大奖上。阿喀比变得防御性并试图将其当作笑话。

    Opening Procedure:

  • Send your highest Perception character to examine the chest — they will detect the trap automatically with DC 14
  • Disarm the trap: Sleight of Hand DC 18 (use Guidance and a proficient character)
  • Pick the lock: Sleight of Hand DC 20 (Thieves' Tools required; 2-3 attempts expected)
  • Open the chest and claim your rewards

    • Step 9: Loot and Return

    The chest contains:

  • Nyrulna (Legendary Trident) — The primary reward
  • 500 Gold — Akabi's lost winnings, now yours
  • Potion of Storm Giant Strength — Sets Strength to 29 for 1 hour
  • Thunderstone (x3) — Throwable items that deal thunder damage in an area

    在这里，获取路径出现了一个令人愉悦的转折。一旦你揭露了阿喀比的作弊行为，你就可以要求在新公平的转盘上免费旋转一次。当你旋转它时，会出现两种结果之一。如果作弊装置没有被成功移除，你会赢得一个小奖品。如果作弊装置被成功移除了，转盘会落在大奖上——阿喀比会愤怒地将你传送到偏远的丛林作为惩罚。

  • Wyll — Views the jungle as a test of character; approval increase for keeping cool
  • Gale — Fascinated by the planar magic used in the teleport; asks questions about djinn magic
