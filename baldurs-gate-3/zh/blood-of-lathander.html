📋 Overview

The Blood of Lathander stands as one of the most powerful and sought-after weapons in all of Baldur's Gate 3, a LEGENDARY mace hidden deep within the crumbling halls of Rosymorn Monastery in the Mountain Pass region of Act 1. Named after Lathander, the Morninglord — the god of dawn, spring, and renewal — this sacred weapon embodies the divine light that pushes back against the shadows threatening the Sword Coast.

This legendary mace deals 1d8 + 2 bludgeoning damage on a successful hit, but its true power lies in its extraordinary magical properties. The Blood of Lathander grants its wielder access to the Sunbeam spell, a devastating 6th-level evocation spell that projects a brilliant beam of light dealing 6d8 radiant damage to all creatures in its path. Even more remarkably, the mace possesses a once-per-long-rest resurrection ability that can bring a fallen companion back from death with 50% of their hit points — a game-changing feature that can turn the tide of the most desperate battles.

Beyond its combat applications, the Blood of Lathander carries immense narrative weight. The weapon represents the eternal struggle between light and darkness that defines the Baldur's Gate 3 storyline. Its acquisition requires players to solve one of the most intricate environmental puzzles in the game, navigate the politically complex Crèche Y'llek occupied by githyanki warriors, and ultimately prove themselves worthy of wielding a god's blessing.

What makes this weapon truly exceptional for Act 1 is how dramatically it shifts your party's power curve. Acquiring the Blood of Lathander before venturing into the Shadow-Cursed Lands of Act 2 gives your party a massive advantage. The Sunbeam spell alone can decimate groups of shadow creatures vulnerable to radiant damage, while the resurrection ability provides invaluable insurance during difficult encounters. Many veteran players consider this acquisition to be the single most impactful power spike available in the first act of the game.

The weapon's lore connects deeply to the githyanki storyline involving Lae'zel and the search for the Mysterious Artefact (the Astral Prism). The githyanki have occupied Rosymorn Monastery and established Crèche Y'llek in its depths, creating a tense military atmosphere where every conversation can turn hostile. Navigating this environment requires careful diplomacy, strategic thinking, and thorough exploration to uncover the hidden chamber where the Blood of Lathander has rested for centuries.

⚔ Acquisition Method

Obtaining the Blood of Lathander requires completing a multi-step quest chain that combines environmental puzzle-solving, stealth or combat encounters, and precise timing. The acquisition can be broadly divided into three major phases: Reaching Rosymorn Monastery, Solving the Stained-Glass Window Puzzle, and Navigating the Ceremonial Chamber Traps. Each phase presents unique challenges that test different aspects of your party's capabilities.

Phase 1: Reaching Rosymorn Monastery

The journey begins at the Mountain Pass, accessible after leaving the Emerald Grove or Blighted Village areas. From the Mountain Pass waypoint, head northeast along the mountain trail. You will encounter a bridge guarded by Eldra Luthrinn and a group of Flaming Fist mercenaries who are investigating strange githyanki activity in the region. You can bypass them through dialogue (Deception or Persuasion checks), or simply sneak past if you prefer to avoid conversation.

Continue across the bridge and follow the path upward. The monastery becomes visible as a massive stone structure perched on the mountainside, its ancient walls weathered by centuries of wind and snow. Upon entering the monastery grounds, you will encounter several hostile Eagles nesting in the courtyard — these are no ordinary birds but surprisingly tough opponents. Clear them or sneak past to gain access to the main building.

Phase 2: The Stained-Glass Window Puzzle

Inside the monastery, make your way to the main chamber with the massive stained-glass window depicting Lathander's symbol — a golden sunrise over mountains. This room contains the first major puzzle. You will notice four pedestals arranged around the chamber, each designed to hold a ceremonial weapon. The puzzle requires placing the correct weapons on the correct pedestals based on clues hidden in the environment.

The solution comes from examining the stained-glass window itself and the mural paintings on the walls nearby. Each section of the window depicts a different Dawn Master — ancient monks who once tended this sacred site — along with their associated weapon. The correct placement is:

  • Dawn Master Vaseid — Place the Mace (found on a pedestal in the same room)
  • Dawn Master Seed — Place the War Pick (found in a chest on the upper floor, accessed via the broken staircase)
  • Dawn Master Stockhold — Place the Morningstar (found in the room to the east, guarded by traps)
  • Dawn Master Welkinglory — Place the Battleaxe (found in a locked chest in the courtyard area)

    • When all four weapons are correctly placed, a hidden mechanism activates, granting you the Dawnmaster's Crest — a key item that will prove essential for safely claiming the Blood of Lathander later. If you attempt to take the mace without this crest, you will face a catastrophic trap that can easily wipe your entire party.

    Phase 3: Crèche Y'llek and the Ceremonial Chamber

    With the Dawnmaster's Crest in hand, proceed to the lower levels of the monastery where Crèche Y'llek is located. The githyanki have established a military outpost here, and their leader Kith'rak Therezzyn maintains strict order. You can either fight your way through the crèche or use diplomacy to gain access — if Lae'zel is in your party, special dialogue options become available that can significantly ease navigation.

    The Blood of Lathander is housed in a secret chamber accessed through the Inquisitor's Chamber. You must speak with Inquisitor Viconia or find the hidden entrance to the ceremonial chamber. The room itself is protected by multiple layers of magical defenses:

  • A barrier of radiant energy that damages anyone who approaches
  • A failsafe trap that, if triggered, begins charging a devastating explosion
  • The Blood of Lathander itself, suspended in a beam of concentrated light

    • Here is where the Dawnmaster's Crest becomes crucial. Interact with the weapon's housing mechanism and insert the crest to safely deactivate the barriers. Without it, you have approximately 4 rounds to escape the chamber before the failsafe detonates, destroying the weapon and likely killing your entire party. With the crest, the weapon descends safely into your hands, and you can leave without incident.

    Alternative: The Theft Route

    It is technically possible to acquire the Blood of Lathander without solving the stained-glass puzzle, but this route is extremely dangerous and generally not recommended for first-time players. If you have a character with exceptional thievery skills (Sleight of Hand proficiency, high Dexterity, and bonuses from items like the Gloves of Thievery), you can attempt to steal the mace directly from its housing. This triggers the failsafe trap immediately, giving you only 4 rounds to escape the blast radius.

    To survive this approach, you must:

  • Have Gale or another wizard prepared with Misty Step or Dimension Door
  • Position your entire party near the exit before attempting the theft
  • Successfully pass a high DC Sleight of Hand check (DC 25)
  • Use Dash actions or teleportation to clear the blast radius within 4 rounds

    • ⚠ Critical Warning

    The theft route is HIGHLY RISKY. If any party member is caught in the blast, they will take approximately 100 fire damage — instant death for most Act 1 characters. Always save before attempting this method. The puzzle route using the Dawnmaster's Crest is the intended and significantly safer acquisition method.

    🗺 Step-by-Step Route

    Follow this comprehensive step-by-step guide to acquire the Blood of Lathander from start to finish. Each step includes precise location details, map coordinates, NPC interactions, and the exact dialogue choices required for the optimal path.

    Step 1: Travel to the Mountain Pass (Act 1)

    From your camp or any waypoint, fast travel to the Mountain Pass waypoint at coordinates X: -154, Y: 30. This area becomes accessible after completing or bypassing the Emerald Grove conflict. As you approach the bridge leading to Rosymorn Monastery, you will encounter Eldra Luthrinn (Flaming Fist Scout) and her patrol.

    Dialogue Options with Eldra:

  • "What are you doing up here?" → Eldra explains the githyanki presence (no check required)
  • "I'm looking for the githyanki crèche." → Persuasion DC 14 to gain information about the monastery layout
  • "Stand aside." → Intimidation DC 16 or initiate combat with the Flaming Fist patrol
  • [Rogue] "You didn't see me." → Deception DC 18 to bypass conversation entirely

    • Pro Tip: Passing the Persuasion check with Eldra marks the monastery location on your map and gives you a hint about the githyanki leader, Kith'rak Therezzyn. This information is valuable for the later diplomatic route through the crèche.

    Step 2: Cross the Bridge and Ascend

    Proceed across the stone bridge at X: -138, Y: 42. Be prepared for a potential encounter with githyanki patrols in this area — if you have not yet encountered githyanki in your playthrough, a squad of warriors may ambush you here. Defeat them or use stealth to bypass.

    Follow the winding mountain path upward. You will pass several stone markers and weathered statues of Lathander. At X: -120, Y: 58, the path opens into the Rosymorn Monastery Courtyard. Save your game here — the eagle encounter is upcoming.

    Step 3: Clear the Courtyard Eagles

    The monastery courtyard is occupied by a nest of Giant Eagles (CR 3 beasts, approximately 45 HP each). There are typically 3-4 eagles in the initial encounter, with possible reinforcements arriving if the fight extends too long.

    Eagle Encounter Strategy:

  • The eagles have high mobility and will use Flyby Attack to hit and retreat without provoking opportunity attacks
  • Position ranged characters on the broken walls for elevation advantage (+2 to attack rolls)
  • Use spells like Hold Animal or Web to ground flying enemies
  • Focus fire one eagle at a time — their individual damage is moderate but combined attacks can overwhelm a low-level party
  • Lightning-based spells deal bonus damage due to the eagles' wet feathers from mountain mist

    • After defeating the eagles, thoroughly search the courtyard. A locked chest hidden behind a crumbling wall at X: -112, Y: 65 contains the Battleaxe of the Dawn Master — one of the four puzzle weapons. Use Thieves' Tools (DC 15) to open it.

    Step 4: Enter the Main Monastery Hall

    The main entrance to the monastery is at X: -118, Y: 62. Push open the heavy oak doors (or pick the lock DC 18 if you want to maintain stealth). Inside, you enter the Great Hall, a massive chamber with vaulted ceilings and the iconic stained-glass window at the far end.

    Immediately upon entering, have your highest Perception character scout the area. There are several important interactable objects:

  • Ceremonial Mace — On the pedestal directly ahead, glowing with faint golden light
  • Ancient Scripture — On a lectern to the left, contains lore about the Dawn Masters
  • Hidden Lever — Behind the collapsed pillar on the right (Perception DC 18), opens a shortcut to the upper floor
  • Trapped Floor Tiles — Leading to the eastern wing (Perception DC 16 to spot, DC 18 Sleight of Hand to disarm)

    • Step 5: Solve the Stained-Glass Window Puzzle

    Examine the stained-glass window at the northern end of the Great Hall. The window depicts four panels, each showing a Dawn Master with their weapon. Reading the Ancient Scripture on the lectern provides additional context. The puzzle requires placing the correct ceremonial weapon on each of the four pedestals arranged in a semicircle before the window.

    Weapon Locations:

    Dawn MasterWeaponLocationHow to Obtain
    VaseidMaceMain pedestal in Great HallAlready in place, interact to take
    SeedWar PickUpper floor, broken roomClimb via broken staircase or use Jump/Misty Step
    StockholdMorningstarEastern wing storage roomDisarm traps (DC 16), pick lock on weapon rack (DC 18)
    WelkingloryBattleaxeCourtyard chestThieves' Tools DC 15, obtained in Step 3

    Puzzle Solution Order: The pedestals are labeled with engravings matching each Dawn Master. Place the weapons in this exact order:

    1. Take the Ceremonial Mace from the center pedestal — this temporarily deactivates the magical barrier protecting the eastern wing
    2. Travel to the eastern wing (watch for trap tiles) and retrieve the Morningstar from the trapped weapon rack
    3. Use the hidden lever behind the collapsed pillar (or Jump/Misty Step) to reach the upper floor and collect the War Pick
    4. If you have not already, retrieve the Battleaxe from the courtyard chest
    5. Return to the Great Hall and place each weapon on its matching pedestal

    When all four weapons are correctly placed, a resonant chime echoes through the hall. The Dawnmaster's Crest materializes in a hidden compartment at the base of the central altar. This golden medallion is the key to safely claiming the Blood of Lathander.

    Step 6: Navigate to Crèche Y'llek

    With the Dawnmaster's Crest secured, proceed to the lower levels of the monastery. The entrance to Crèche Y'llek is through a heavy iron door in the western wing of the monastery, at X: -125, Y: 55.

    If Lae'zel is in your party, she will initiate a conversation when you approach the door. Her presence opens unique dialogue options with the githyanki guards:

  • [Lae'zel] "We are githyanki. Let us pass." → Automatic passage if Lae'zel has been cooperative with githyanki culture
  • [Lae'zel] "I am a kith'rak in service to Vlaakith." → Deception DC 18 (requires knowing about githyanki hierarchy from earlier conversations)
  • "We come in peace." → Persuasion DC 16 to enter without combat
  • Attack the guards → Immediate combat encounter with 4 githyanki warriors

    • Diplomatic Route: If you successfully pass the dialogue checks, the githyanki allow you to enter the crèche as "honored guests." You can freely explore most areas, though certain sections remain restricted. You can speak with Kith'rak Therezzyn in the command chamber for additional lore and potential side quests.

    Combat Route: If you choose to fight, prepare for a challenging encounter. The githyanki warriors are highly mobile fighters with multiattack capabilities and resistance to psychic damage. Use crowd control spells and focus fire on the Githyanki Knight first, as their leadership aura buffs nearby allies.

    Step 7: Enter the Inquisitor's Chamber

    Make your way through the crèche to the Inquisitor's Chamber at the lowest level. If you are on the diplomatic route, you may need to pick a lock (DC 20) or find an alternative entrance through a ventilation shaft (Perception DC 18 to spot the entrance at X: -140, Y: 48).

    Inside the Inquisitor's Chamber, you will find Inquisitor Viconia (or her subordinate if she has been dealt with elsewhere in the story). The chamber contains the entrance to the Ceremonial Room where the Blood of Lathander is sealed. Look for a glowing archway at the northern end of the room — this is the sealed entrance.

    Step 8: Claim the Blood of Lathander

    Enter the Ceremonial Chamber through the glowing archway. The Blood of Lathander floats in a column of brilliant golden light at the center of the room, suspended above an ornate pedestal. As you approach, a magical barrier crackles with radiant energy — approaching without the Dawnmaster's Crest triggers the failsafe sequence.

    SAFE METHOD (With Dawnmaster's Crest):

  • Approach the pedestal and interact with the glowing mechanism
  • Select the dialogue option: "Insert the Dawnmaster's Crest into the slot"
  • The magical barriers dissolve harmlessly, and the weapon slowly descends
  • Interact with the Blood of Lathander to add it to your inventory
  • A warm golden light washes over your party — Lathander's blessing acknowledges the worthy

    • DANGER METHOD (Without Crest — NOT RECOMMENDED):

  • Attempt to grab the weapon directly or use Sleight of Hand to steal it
  • The failsafe activates — a loud klaxon sounds and the chamber begins filling with radiant energy
  • You have exactly 4 rounds (24 seconds) to escape before detonation
  • Each round, the chamber fills with more damaging radiant light (2d6 radiant damage per round to all inside)
  • On round 4, the explosion deals 10d10 fire + 10d10 radiant damage — instant death for almost any Act 1 character

    • Step 9: Escape and Celebrate

    With the Blood of Lathander safely in your inventory, make your way back out of the crèche. If you took the diplomatic route, the githyanki may become hostile upon realizing their sacred chamber has been breached — be prepared for a running fight through the crèche corridors. If you already cleared the crèche, simply walk out.

    Return to your camp and equip the Blood of Lathander on your chosen character. The weapon is most effective on:

  • Shadowheart — If she remains a cleric of Selûne or Shar, the mace complements her spellcasting and gives her a powerful melee option
  • Your main character — If you are playing a cleric, paladin, or any class that benefits from Wisdom-based spellcasting
  • Gale — Surprisingly effective; Gale can use the Sunbeam spell at its highest potency with his high spellcasting modifier

    • ⚠ Gotcha Tips

    After guiding hundreds of players through the Blood of Lathander acquisition, we have identified the most common mistakes, hidden dangers, and easily missed details. Read this section carefully to avoid frustrating setbacks.

    Gotcha #1: The Failsafe Timer Is REAL

    Many players assume they can tank the failsafe explosion or out-heal the damage. You cannot. The final detonation deals an average of 110 damage of mixed fire and radiant types. At Act 1 levels (5-7), even your tankiest character with maximum HP and temporary hit points will be obliterated. Always get the Dawnmaster's Crest. Do not try to be clever about stealing the weapon without it.

    Gotcha #2: You Can Permanently Lose the Weapon

    If the failsafe detonates while the Blood of Lathander is still in its housing, the weapon is DESTROYED. It does not respawn, it does not drop as loot, and there is no way to recover it in that playthrough. This is one of the very few truly missable legendary items in Baldur's Gate 3. Always save before entering the Ceremonial Chamber.

    Gotcha #3: The Eastern Wing Traps Reset

    The trap tiles in the eastern wing leading to the Morningstar weapon rack reset after each long rest. If you leave the monastery to rest and return, you must disarm them again. Additionally, the trapped weapon rack itself has a secondary trap that triggers if you fail the Sleight of Hand check by 5 or more — this deals 3d6 piercing damage and alerts nearby enemies.

    Gotcha #4: Githyanki Diplomacy Has Consequences

    If you take the diplomatic route through Crèche Y'llek and later betray the githyanki by taking the Blood of Lathander, this can affect Lae'zel's approval and potentially lock you out of certain githyanki-related story paths. Conversely, if you massacre the crèche before claiming the weapon, you lose access to unique dialogue and potential allies later in the game. Consider your roleplay approach carefully.

    Gotcha #5: The Puzzle Weapons Can Be Missed

    Each of the four ceremonial weapons required for the puzzle can be permanently missed if you are not thorough:

  • The War Pick requires accessing the upper floor — easy to miss if you do not explore vertically
  • The Morningstar is behind trapped tiles that many players walk around and never investigate
  • The Battleaxe is in an optional courtyard chest that blends into the environment
  • If you accidentally sell or lose any puzzle weapon, there is no replacement — you must reload a save

    • Gotcha #6: Shadowheart's Reaction Matters

    If Shadowheart is in your party when you claim the Blood of Lathander, she has unique dialogue about the Morninglord. Depending on whether she has embraced or rejected Shar at this point, her reaction varies significantly. If she is on her dark Shar path, she may voice disapproval about wielding a weapon of light — though this does not prevent equipping it. On her redemption path, she speaks reverently about Lathander and gains a small amount of inspiration.

    Gotcha #7: The Crest Only Works Once

    The Dawnmaster's Crest is consumed when used to deactivate the barriers protecting the Blood of Lathander. If you somehow lose it before reaching the Ceremonial Chamber (for example, by having it stolen by a githyanki pickpocket, which can happen in the crèche), you cannot craft a replacement. Keep the crest on your main character in a protected inventory slot.

    ⚠ Spoiler Warning: Act 3 Consequences

    Carrying the Blood of Lathander into certain late-game encounters can trigger unique dialogue options, particularly when dealing with followers of Shar or undead enemies. The weapon's radiant nature makes it especially effective in the Shadow-Cursed Lands and against certain Act 3 bosses. However, there are no negative consequences for wielding it at any point — Lathander's blessing is unconditional.

    • Strategic Campaign Advantages

    The Blood of Lathander provides benefits that extend far beyond its immediate statistics:

  • Shadow-Cursed Lands Dominance: The Sunbeam spell and radiant light aura make Act 2's shadow areas significantly easier. Most shadow creatures are vulnerable to radiant damage, and the built-in light source frees up a cantrip slot normally used for Light
  • Party Composition Flexibility: The built-in resurrection ability means you can safely leave a cleric's Revivify spell unprepared, opening up more versatile spell preparation
  • Economic Savings: The resurrection ability saves approximately 200 gold per use compared to buying Scrolls of Revivify from merchants
  • Roleplay Prestige: NPCs throughout the game recognize the Blood of Lathander when equipped, triggering unique dialogue with priests, paladins, and followers of Shar
  • Achievement Progress: Acquiring any legendary item contributes toward collection-based achievements

    • Synergy Builds

    The Blood of Lathander excels in specific character builds:

  • Light Domain Cleric: Doubles down on radiant damage, with the mace providing a powerful melee option when spells are depleted
  • Oath of Devotion Paladin: Flavor-perfect combination; the mace's radiant damage synergizes with the paladin's Divine Smite
  • Life Domain Cleric: The resurrection ability complements the life cleric's healing focus, creating an unmatched support character
  • Any Frontline Caster: Characters who mix melee combat with spellcasting benefit from the versatile weapon-plus-spell combination
