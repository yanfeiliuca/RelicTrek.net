洛山达之血

传奇钉锤 | 第1幕 | 难度：★★★★★ | 罗斯莫恩修道院

📋 Overview

洛山达之血是博德之门3中最强大、最令人梦寐以求的武器之一。这是一把传奇钉锤，深藏在第一章山口区域罗斯莫恩修道院破败的厅堂深处。这把神圣武器以晨曦之主洛山达——黎明、春天与复苏之神——命名， embody the divine light that pushes back against the shadows threatening the Sword Coast。

这把传奇钉锤在命中时造成1d8 + 2钝击伤害，但它真正的力量在于其非凡的魔法特性。洛山达之血赋予持有者施放阳炎射线法术的能力，这是一道毁灭性的6环塑能法术，对路径上的所有生物造成6d8光耀伤害。更令人惊叹的是，这把钉锤拥有每长休一次的复活能力，可以将一位倒下的同伴以50%生命值的状态从死亡中拉回。

除了战斗用途之外，洛山达之血还承载着巨大的叙事分量。这把武器代表了博德之门3剧情中光明与黑暗之间永恒斗争的本质。获得它需要玩家解开游戏中最复杂的环境谜题之一， navigate the politically complex 育婴所Y'llek occupied by githyanki warriors, and ultimately prove themselves worthy of wielding a god's blessing。

这把武器在第一章就如此卓越的原因在于它能如此戏剧性地改变队伍的实力曲线。在踏入第二章的受Shadow-Cursed Lands之前获得洛山达之血，能为队伍带来巨大的优势。单是阳炎射线法术就足以消灭大量对光耀伤害脆弱的暗影生物，而复活能力在困难遭遇战中提供了宝贵的保障。许多资深玩家认为这是第一章中能获得的最具影响力的实力飞跃。

⚔ Acquisition Method

获得洛山达之血需要完成一个多步骤任务链，结合环境解谜、潜行或战斗遭遇，以及精确的时机把握。获取过程大致分为三个主要阶段：抵达罗斯莫恩修道院、解开彩色玻璃窗谜题，以及 navigate the Ceremonial Chamber Traps。每个阶段都呈现出独特的挑战，考验队伍的不同能力。

Phase 1: Reaching Rosymorn Monastery

旅程始于山口区域，在离开翠绿林地或疫病村落区域后即可到达。从山口传送点出发，沿着山道向东北方向前进。你会遇到一座由埃尔达·卢瑟林和一群火焰之拳雇佣兵把守的桥梁，他们正在调查该地区奇怪的吉斯洋基活动。你可以通过对话绕过他们，或者直接潜行过去。

继续过桥并沿着小路向上走。修道院逐渐显现为一座坐落在山腰上的巨大石质建筑，其古老的墙壁被数个世纪的风雪侵蚀。进入修道院庭院后，你会遇到几只在这个区域筑巢的鹰。这些可不是普通的鸟类，而是异常强悍的对手。清除它们或潜行通过以进入主建筑。

Phase 2: The Stained-Glass Window Puzzle

这把传奇钉锤在命中时造成1d8 + 2钝击伤害，但它真正的力量在于其非凡的魔法特性。洛山达之血赋予持有者施放阳炎射线法术的能力，这是一道毁灭性的6环塑能法术，对路径上的所有生物造成6d8光耀伤害。更令人惊叹的是，这把钉锤拥有每长休一次的复活能力，可以将一位倒下的同伴以50%生命值的状态从死亡中拉回。

答案来自检查彩色玻璃窗本身和附近墙壁上的壁画。窗户的每个部分描绘了不同的黎明大师以及他们的 associated weapon。正确的放置方式是：

  • Dawn Master Vaseid — Place the Mace (found on a pedestal in the same room)
  • Dawn Master Seed — Place the War Pick (found in a chest on the upper floor, accessed via the broken staircase)
  • Dawn Master Stockhold — Place the Morningstar (found in the room to the east, guarded by traps)
  • Dawn Master Welkinglory — Place the Battleaxe (found in a locked chest in the courtyard area)

    Phase 3: Crèche Y'llek and the Ceremonial Chamber

    这把武器在第一章就如此卓越的原因在于它能如此戏剧性地改变队伍的实力曲线。在踏入第二章的受Shadow-Cursed Lands之前获得洛山达之血，能为队伍带来巨大的优势。单是阳炎射线法术就足以消灭大量对光耀伤害脆弱的暗影生物，而复活能力在困难遭遇战中提供了宝贵的保障。许多资深玩家认为这是第一章中能获得的最具影响力的实力飞跃。

    这把武器的传说与莱埃泽尔的吉斯洋基剧情线以及搜寻神秘遗物紧密相连。吉斯洋基人占领了罗斯莫恩修道院并在其深处建立了育婴所Y'llek，营造出一种紧张的军事氛围，每一次对话都可能变为敌对。要揭开洛山达之血已沉睡数个世纪的隐藏密室，需要谨慎的外交、战略性的思维和彻底的探索。

  • A barrier of radiant energy that damages anyone who approaches
  • A failsafe trap that, if triggered, begins charging a devastating explosion
  • The Blood of Lathander itself, suspended in a beam of concentrated light

  • Have Gale or another wizard prepared with Misty Step or Dimension Door
  • Position your entire party near the exit before attempting the theft
  • Successfully pass a high DC Sleight of Hand check (DC 25)
  • Use Dash actions or teleportation to clear the blast radius within 4 rounds

    • ⚠ Critical Warning

    盗窃路线极具风险。如果任何队伍成员被爆炸波及，他们将承受约100点火焰伤害——对大多数第一章角色而言是即死。Always save before attempting this method。使用黎明大师纹章的谜题路线是预期且 significantly safer acquisition method。

    🗺 Step-by-Step Route

    继续过桥并沿着小路向上走。修道院逐渐显现为一座坐落在山腰上的巨大石质建筑，其古老的墙壁被数个世纪的风雪侵蚀。进入修道院庭院后，你会遇到几只在这个区域筑巢的鹰。这些可不是普通的鸟类，而是异常强悍的对手。清除它们或潜行通过以进入主建筑。

    Step 1: Travel to the Mountain Pass (Act 1)

    进入修道院后，前往拥有描绘洛山达象征的彩色玻璃窗的主厅。这个房间包含第一个主要谜题。你会注意到房间周围排列着四个基座，每个基座都设计用于放置一件仪式武器。这个谜题要求根据隐藏在环境中的线索，将正确的武器放在正确的基座上。

    Dialogue Options with Eldra:

  • "What are you doing up here?" → Eldra explains the githyanki presence (no check required)
  • "I'm looking for the githyanki crèche." → Persuasion DC 14 to gain information about the monastery layout
  • "Stand aside." → Intimidation DC 16 or initiate combat with the Flaming Fist patrol
  • [Rogue] "You didn't see me." → Deception DC 18 to bypass conversation entirely

    • 通过说服检定与埃尔达对话会在你的地图上标记修道院位置，并给你关于吉斯洋基领袖基斯拉克·特雷津的提示。This information is valuable for the later diplomatic route through the crèche。

    Step 2: Cross the Bridge and Ascend

    答案来自检查彩色玻璃窗本身和附近墙壁上的壁画。窗户的每个部分描绘了不同的黎明大师以及他们的 associated weapon。正确的放置方式是：

    沿着蜿蜒的山路向上走。你会经过几座石制标记和洛山达的风化雕像。At X: -120, Y: 58, the path opens into the 罗斯莫恩修道院庭院。在这里存档——老鹰遭遇战即将来临。

    Step 3: Clear the Courtyard Eagles

    修道院庭院被一群巨型鹰占据。这些野兽约有45点生命值。初次遭遇通常有3-4只鹰，如果战斗持续时间太长，可能会有增援。

    Eagle Encounter Strategy:

  • The eagles have high mobility and will use Flyby Attack to hit and retreat without provoking opportunity attacks
  • Position ranged characters on the broken walls for elevation advantage (+2 to attack rolls)
  • Use spells like Hold Animal or Web to ground flying enemies
  • Focus fire one eagle at a time — their individual damage is moderate but combined attacks can overwhelm a low-level party
  • Lightning-based spells deal bonus damage due to the eagles' wet feathers from mountain mist

    • 击败老鹰后，彻底搜索庭院。A locked chest hidden behind a crumbling wall at X: -112, Y: 65 contains the 黎明大师战斧——四件谜题武器之一。Use 盗贼工具 (DC 15) to open it。

    Step 4: Enter the Main Monastery Hall

    当四件武器全部正确放置后，一个隐藏的机关被激活，授予你黎明大师纹章——这把钥匙物品对于安全地获取洛山达之血至关重要。如果你试图在没有这个纹章的情况下拿走钉锤，你将面临一个灾难性的陷阱，可以轻易消灭你的整个队伍。

    Immediately upon entering, have your highest Perception character scout the area. There are several important interactable objects:

  • Ceremonial Mace — On the pedestal directly ahead, glowing with faint golden light
  • Ancient Scripture — On a lectern to the left, contains lore about the Dawn Masters
  • Hidden Lever — Behind the collapsed pillar on the right (Perception DC 18), opens a shortcut to the upper floor
  • Trapped Floor Tiles — Leading to the eastern wing (Perception DC 16 to spot, DC 18 Sleight of Hand to disarm)

    • Step 5: Solve the Stained-Glass Window Puzzle

    手持黎明大师纹章，前往修道院的下层，那里是育婴所Y'llek的所在地。吉斯洋基人在这里建立了军事前哨，他们的领袖基斯拉克·特雷津维持着严格的秩序。你可以一路战斗穿过育婴所，或者使用外交手段获得进入许可。如果莱埃泽尔在你的队伍中，会出现特殊对话选项，显著简化导航。

    Weapon Locations:

    Dawn MasterWeaponLocationHow to Obtain

    SeedWar PickUpper floor, broken roomClimb via broken staircase or use Jump/Misty Step

    Puzzle Solution Order: The pedestals are labeled with engravings matching each Dawn Master. Place the weapons in this exact order:

    1. Take the Ceremonial Mace from the center pedestal — this temporarily deactivates the magical barrier protecting the eastern wing
    2. Travel to the eastern wing (watch for trap tiles) and retrieve the Morningstar from the trapped weapon rack
    3. Use the hidden lever behind the collapsed pillar (or Jump/Misty Step) to reach the upper floor and collect the War Pick
    4. If you have not already, retrieve the Battleaxe from the courtyard chest
    5. Return to the Great Hall and place each weapon on its matching pedestal

    当四件武器全部正确放置后，一声洪亮的钟声在大厅中回荡。黎明大师纹章在中央祭坛底部的隐藏隔间中显形。This golden medallion is the key to safely claiming the Blood of Lathander。

    Step 6: Navigate to Crèche Y'llek

    手持黎明大师纹章，前往修道院的下层。育婴所Y'llek的入口位于修道院西翼的一扇沉重的铁门后，坐标 X: -125, Y: 55。

    如果莱埃泽尔在你的队伍中，当你接近大门时她会发起对话。她的存在开启了与吉斯洋基守卫的独特对话选项：

  • [Lae'zel] "We are githyanki. Let us pass." → Automatic passage if Lae'zel has been cooperative with githyanki culture
  • [Lae'zel] "I am a kith'rak in service to Vlaakith." → Deception DC 18 (requires knowing about githyanki hierarchy from earlier conversations)
  • "We come in peace." → Persuasion DC 16 to enter without combat
  • Attack the guards → Immediate combat encounter with 4 githyanki warriors

    • 如果你成功通过了对话检定，吉斯洋基人允许你作为贵宾进入育婴所。你可以自由探索大部分区域，尽管某些部分仍然受限。你可以在指挥室与基斯拉克·特雷津交谈以获取额外的 lore 和支线任务。

    洛山达之血被安置在通过审判官室进入的秘密密室中。你必须与审判官维康妮亚交谈，或者找到通往仪式密室的隐藏入口。房间本身受到多层魔法防御的保护：

    Step 7: Enter the Inquisitor's Chamber

    穿过育婴所前往最低层的审判官室。如果你走外交路线，你可能需要撬锁 (DC 20) 或找到通风管道的替代入口。

    黎明大师纹章在这里变得至关重要。与武器的存放机关互动并插入纹章以安全地解除屏障。没有它，你大约有4轮时间逃离密室，然后保险装置引爆，摧毁武器并可能杀死你的整个队伍。有了纹章，武器会安全地降入你的手中，你可以无事后离开。

    Step 8: Claim the Blood of Lathander

    通过发光拱门进入仪式密室。洛山达之血漂浮在房间中央一束灿烂的金色光柱中，悬挂在华丽的基座上方。As you approach, a magical barrier crackles with radiant energy。

    SAFE METHOD (With Dawnmaster's Crest):

  • Approach the pedestal and interact with the glowing mechanism
  • Select the dialogue option: "Insert the Dawnmaster's Crest into the slot"
  • The magical barriers dissolve harmlessly, and the weapon slowly descends
  • Interact with the Blood of Lathander to add it to your inventory
  • A warm golden light washes over your party — Lathander's blessing acknowledges the worthy

    • DANGER METHOD (Without Crest — NOT RECOMMENDED):

  • Attempt to grab the weapon directly or use Sleight of Hand to steal it
  • The failsafe activates — a loud klaxon sounds and the chamber begins filling with radiant energy
  • You have exactly 4 rounds (24 seconds) to escape before detonation
  • Each round, the chamber fills with more damaging radiant light (2d6 radiant damage per round to all inside)
  • On round 4, the explosion deals 10d10 fire + 10d10 radiant damage — instant death for almost any Act 1 character

    • Step 9: Escape and Celebrate

    将洛山达之血安全收入 inventory 后，从育婴所原路返回。如果你走了外交路线，吉斯洋基人在意识到他们的神圣密室被闯入后可能会 hostile——准备好穿过育婴所走廊的逃亡战。If you already cleared the crèche, simply walk out。

    不解决彩色玻璃窗谜题就获取洛山达之血在理论上是可能的，但这条路线极其危险，通常不推荐新手玩家尝试。如果你有一个拥有卓越盗窃技能的角色，你可以尝试直接从存放处偷走钉锤。这会立即触发保险装置陷阱，只给你4轮时间逃离爆炸半径。

  • Shadowheart — If she remains a cleric of Selûne or Shar, the mace complements her spellcasting and gives her a powerful melee option
  • Your main character — If you are playing a cleric, paladin, or any class that benefits from Wisdom-based spellcasting
  • Gale — Surprisingly effective; Gale can use the Sunbeam spell at its highest potency with his high spellcasting modifier

    • ⚠ Gotcha Tips

    返回营地并将洛山达之血装备到你选择的角色身上。The weapon is most effective on影心、主角以及盖尔。

    Gotcha #1: The Failsafe Timer Is REAL

    在引导数百名玩家获取洛山达之血后，我们总结了最常见的错误、隐藏的危险以及容易被忽略的细节。Read this section carefully to avoid frustrating setbacks。

    Gotcha #2: You Can Permanently Lose the Weapon

    许多玩家假设他们可以承受 failsafe explosion 或通过治疗抵消伤害。你不能。最终爆炸平均造成110点混合火焰和光耀伤害。在第一章等级下，即使是你最肉的角色也会被消灭。Always get the Dawnmaster's Crest。不要试图在没有它的情况下偷取武器。

    Gotcha #3: The Eastern Wing Traps Reset

    如果 failsafe 在洛山达之血仍在存放处时引爆，武器将被摧毁。它不会重生，不会作为战利品掉落，在该周目中也无法恢复。这是博德之门3中为数不多的真正会错过的传奇物品之一。Always save before entering the Ceremonial Chamber。

    Gotcha #4: Githyanki Diplomacy Has Consequences

    东翼通往晨星武器架的陷阱瓷砖每次长休后重置。如果你离开修道院休息后返回，你必须再次解除它们。此外， trapped weapon rack 本身有一个 secondary trap，如果你巧手检定失败5点或更多就会触发——造成3d6穿刺伤害并惊动附近敌人。

    Gotcha #5: The Puzzle Weapons Can Be Missed

    在开始洛山达之血任务之前，确保你的队伍满足以下先决条件。未能充分准备可能导致错失机会、同伴死亡或武器本身的永久损失。

  • The War Pick requires accessing the upper floor — easy to miss if you do not explore vertically
  • The Morningstar is behind trapped tiles that many players walk around and never investigate
  • The Battleaxe is in an optional courtyard chest that blends into the environment
  • If you accidentally sell or lose any puzzle weapon, there is no replacement — you must reload a save

    • Gotcha #6: Shadowheart's Reaction Matters

    如果你通过育婴所Y'llek走外交路线，之后背叛吉斯洋基人拿走洛山达之血，这可能会影响莱埃泽尔的好感度，并可能锁定某些吉斯洋基相关的剧情路线。相反，如果你在获取武器前屠杀了育婴所，你会失去后期游戏中独特对话和潜在盟友的 access。Consider your roleplay approach carefully。

    • Strategic Campaign Advantages

    The Blood of Lathander provides benefits that extend far beyond its immediate statistics:

  • Shadow-Cursed Lands Dominance: The Sunbeam spell and radiant light aura make Act 2's shadow areas significantly easier. Most shadow creatures are vulnerable to radiant damage, and the built-in light source frees up a cantrip slot normally used for Light
  • Party Composition Flexibility: The built-in resurrection ability means you can safely leave a cleric's Revivify spell unprepared, opening up more versatile spell preparation
  • Economic Savings: The resurrection ability saves approximately 200 gold per use compared to buying Scrolls of Revivify from merchants
  • Roleplay Prestige: NPCs throughout the game recognize the Blood of Lathander when equipped, triggering unique dialogue with priests, paladins, and followers of Shar
  • Achievement Progress: Acquiring any legendary item contributes toward collection-based achievements

    • Synergy Builds

    The Blood of Lathander excels in specific character builds:

  • Light Domain Cleric: Doubles down on radiant damage, with the mace providing a powerful melee option when spells are depleted
  • Oath of Devotion Paladin: Flavor-perfect combination; the mace's radiant damage synergizes with the paladin's Divine Smite
  • Life Domain Cleric: The resurrection ability complements the life cleric's healing focus, creating an unmatched support character
  • Any Frontline Caster: Characters who mix melee combat with spellcasting benefit from the versatile weapon-plus-spell combination
