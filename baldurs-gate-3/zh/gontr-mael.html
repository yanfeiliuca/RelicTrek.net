冈特·梅尔

Legendary Longbow | Acts 2-3 | 难度： ★★★★★ | Gauntlet of Shar → House of Hope

📋 Overview

冈特·梅尔是一把拥有毁灭性力量的传奇长弓，能够将 even a modestly-built ranged character 转变为神圣毁灭的引擎。它的名字源于古老的吉斯洋基战斗方言，大致翻译为死亡使者。

作为传奇长弓，冈特·梅尔造成1d8 + 2穿刺伤害，附带+2武器附魔。然而，这把弓的标志性特性是其能够通过每次攻击施放引导箭的能力。

冈特·梅尔的获取跨越多个章节和主要任务线，使其成为游戏中最长的物品搜寻之一。The journey begins in the treacherous Gauntlet of Shar in Act 2, but the bow itself is not found there。

⚔ Acquisition Method

冈特·梅尔的获取是一个跨越两个章节的旅程，weaves through some of the most significant story content in the game。

Phase 1: Complete the Gauntlet of Shar (Act 2)

获得冈特·梅尔的第一 prerequisites 是完成莎尔试炼场，一个位于第二章受Shadow-Cursed Lands下方的巨大地下城。

作为传奇长弓，冈特·梅尔造成1d8 + 2穿刺伤害，附带+2武器附魔。然而，这把弓的标志性特性是其能够通过每次攻击施放引导箭的能力。

  • Multiple trials testing stealth, combat prowess, and puzzle-solving ability
  • The Umbral Gem collection challenge — you must find four Umbral Gems to progress
  • Encounters with Balthazar's necromantic creations
  • The climactic confrontation with Aylin the Nightsong

    Phase 3: Gain Access to the House of Hope (Act 3)

    冈特·梅尔的获取是一个跨越两个章节的旅程，weaves through some of the most significant story content in the game。

    To unlock the House of Hope:

  • Progress the main quest until you encounter Hope in your dreams and visions
  • Speak with Mizora if she is in your camp — she has information about the House of Hope's location
  • Complete the Save Hope questline that becomes available in the Lower City
  • Obtain the Helldusk Key or equivalent entry method to breach the planar barrier

    • 获得冈特·梅尔的第一 prerequisites 是完成莎尔试炼场，一个位于第二章受Shadow-Cursed Lands下方的巨大地下城。

    Phase 4: Defeat the Enemies of Hope

    要到达莎尔试炼场，前往受Shadow-Cursed Lands的索姆陵墓并通过隐藏入口下降。试炼场本身就是一个 sprawling complex， featuring multiple trials testing stealth, combat prowess, and puzzle-solving ability。

  • Hope's Tormentors — Devilish creatures tasked with breaking the spirits of imprisoned souls
  • Pleasure Demons — Succubi and incubi who maintain the illusion of paradise
  • Mephistopheles' Guard — Elite devil soldiers who protect the House's inner sanctum
  • The Archivist — A powerful devil who catalogues the souls and memories of the damned

    Phase 5: Claim the Gontr Mael

    使冈特·梅尔从仅仅强大转变为真正卓越的是其与远程角色构筑的协同效应。使用这把弓的游侠可以将标准猎手印记伤害与引导箭的光耀爆发结合，而游荡者可以从极远距离触发偷袭，并获得下次攻击的优势。

  • All bodies of defeated named enemies (use ALT to highlight lootable corpses)
  • Locked and trapped chests in the area (DC 20-25 locks, DC 18-22 traps)
  • Hidden compartments behind paintings and tapestries (Perception DC 18-22)
  • The Archivist's study, which contains multiple valuable items

    Character Build Requirements

    While any character can technically equip the Gontr Mael, certain proficiencies and abilities maximize its effectiveness:

  • Longbow Proficiency: Required to avoid disadvantage on attack rolls. Classes with natural proficiency: Ranger, Fighter, Rogue, Bard (college-dependent), Paladin (Oath of the Ancients)
  • Dexterity 14+: Recommended minimum for consistent hit chance; Dexterity 16+ optimal
  • Archery Fighting Style: If available (Fighter, Ranger), provides +2 to attack rolls with ranged weapons
  • Sharpshooter Feat: Negates long-range and cover penalties — HIGHLY recommended for maximum effectiveness
  • Spellcasting Ability: The Guiding Bolt effect uses the wielder's spellcasting modifier for its DC; higher is better

    • Combat Preparation for House of Hope

    The House of Hope enemies are some of the most dangerous in Act 3. Recommended preparations:

  • Party level 10-12 (minimum 10 for acceptable challenge)
  • Radiant damage spells prepared (devils are vulnerable or non-resistant to radiant)
  • Healing Word or Mass Healing Word for action-economy healing
  • Remove Curse or Greater Restoration for devilish debuffs
  • Potions of Speed for burst damage rounds
  • Arrows of various types (lightning, acid, radiant) for elemental coverage

    🗺 Step-by-Step Route

    冈特·梅尔拥有博德之门3中任何传奇武器中最广泛的前置条件链。This section provides a comprehensive checklist to ensure you have met every requirement。

    Act 2, Part 1: Entering the Gauntlet of Shar

    随着艾琳被解救且阴影诅咒被削弱，继续完成第二章的剩余部分。在月出之塔击败凯瑟里克·索姆以完成该章节，并过渡到第三章的博德之门。

    到达博德之门后，你将可以进入下城区，并最终进入上城区。在你取得足够的第三章主线剧情进度后，希望之屋将变得可进入。

    希望之屋是第三章中独特的地点——一个作为天堂扭曲仿制品的口袋维度，由大魔鬼墨菲斯托创造，用于折磨被困其中的灵魂。

    要解锁希望之屋：推进主线任务直到你在梦境和幻象中遇到希望，如果你的营地中有米佐拉则与她交谈，完成拯救希望任务线。

    希望之屋通常通过传送门进入，该传送门在完成先决任务后出现在魔鬼之费区。或者，如果你追随卡菈克的剧情线，她的地狱引擎任务可能会通过不同的路线引导你到达希望之屋。

    一旦进入希望之屋，你必须穿越其看似美丽的厅堂，并对抗为其黑暗目的服务的生物。希望之屋中栖息着几种类型的敌人。

    Act 2, Part 2: The Nightsong Chamber

    穿越希望之屋的战斗具有挑战性——这里的敌人是第三章中最强悍的之一，拥有高护甲值、可观的生命值池和魔鬼抗性。光耀伤害在这里特别有效。

    弓本身是在希望之屋中受守卫的宝库或储藏室中找到的，通常在击败命名首领敌人或完成与释放被囚禁灵魂相关的特定挑战后。

  • Choose dialogue options that challenge Shar's teachings
  • Pass Persuasion DC 21 to convince Shadowheart to spare Aylin (made easier with high approval)
  • Alternatively, remove Shadowheart from the party before this encounter and free Aylin yourself
  • If Shadowheart has already rejected Shar earlier in her quest, she will support freeing Aylin automatically

    Act 2, Part 3: Defeating Ketheric Thorm

    Story Requirements: Reach Act 2, Find the Gauntlet of Shar, Complete Gauntlet Trials, Free 夜之歌艾琳, Defeat 凯瑟里克·索姆, Reach Act 3, Unlock House of Hope。

    冈特·梅尔存放在华丽宝箱中，或由希望之屋的精英守卫之一携带。在击败相关区域的敌人后，彻底搜查所有尸体、上锁的宝箱和隐藏隔间。

    Act 3, Part 1: Arriving in Baldur's Gate

    当你找到冈特·梅尔时，它呈现为一把工艺非凡的长弓，其木质部分注入了像心跳般脉动的金色光线纹理。拾取时，你的角色会感到一股光耀的温暖涌动。

    剧情要求：到达第二章、找到莎尔试炼场、完成试炼、解救夜之歌艾琳、击败凯瑟里克·索姆、到达第三章、解锁希望之屋。

    虽然从技术上讲任何角色都可以装备冈特·梅尔，但某些熟练和能力可以最大化其效果：长弓熟练、敏捷14+、箭术战斗风格、神射手专长。

    Act 3, Part 2: The House of Hope

    Step 12: Defeat 凯瑟里克·索姆 in the three-phase battle at the tower's summit。His death triggers the Act 2 ending cinematic。

    希望之屋的敌人是第三章中最危险的之一。推荐准备：队伍等级10-12、光耀伤害法术、治疗真言、移除诅咒、速度药水。

    这份详细攻略涵盖了从第二章莎尔试炼场到第三章希望之屋的每一步，确保你不会错过冈特·梅尔。请仔细遵循每个阶段。

    第一步：快速旅行到阴影诅咒之地索姆陵墓传送点，坐标X:-185, Y:85。如果你尚未发现该地点，从终光旅店或大陵墓传送点旅行并向东北方向前进。

    第二步：进入陵墓并找到主墓后面的隐藏通道。入口机关涉及按正确顺序与三幅壁画互动。

    ⚠ Gotcha Tips

    Step 17: Defeat the pleasure demons in the Garden of Delights。These succubi and incubi use charm effects and psychic damage。

    Gotcha #1: Killing Aylin Locks the Bow

    Step 6: Navigate the Silent Library to find the fourth Umbral Gem, defeating or bypassing 巴尔萨扎's undead guardians along the way。

    Gotcha #2: The Bow Can Be Stolen from You

    第三步：下降到莎尔试炼场。初始区域，柔步试炼，考验你的潜行。派出游荡者或拥有无踪步的角色来收集第一颗幽影宝石。

    Gotcha #3: Guiding Bolt Uses Spell Slots

    第四步：进行同我试炼，在那里你与自己队伍的暗影复制体战斗。这些复制体拥有你的能力和装备，使其成为游戏中最有趣的遭遇战之一。

    Gotcha #4: House of Hope Enemies Scale with Level

    第五步：完成信仰飞跃试炼，这是一个平台跳跃挑战，你必须在黑暗之坑上不可见的平台之间跳跃。看破隐形或妖火可以显现平台。

    Gotcha #5: The Hidden Vault Resets on Long Rest

    Step 10: Exit the Gauntlet through the now-open western passage。You emerge near 月出之塔 with 艾琳's aid unlocked for the 凯瑟里克·索姆 battle。

    Gotcha #6: Multiple Gontr Maels? No.

    第七步：将四颗幽影宝石放入试炼场内殿的容器中。夜之歌监狱的门打开， revealing 艾琳被困在水晶监狱中。

    Incidental Gauntlet of Shar Loot

    Completing the Gauntlet yields substantial additional rewards:

  • Spear of Night — Rare spear, excellent for Shadowheart if she rejected Shar
  • Umbral Gems — Valuable crafting components worth 500+ gold each
  • Shar's Ritual Dagger — Uncommon dagger with necrotic damage rider
  • Scrolls of Darkness and Invisibility — Utility scrolls for stealth scenarios
  • Ancient Shar Texts — Lore items that grant inspiration to certain backgrounds

    • Incidental House of Hope Loot

    The House of Hope is treasure-rich beyond the Gontr Mael:

  • Helldusk Armor — Legendary heavy armor found in the same general area (separate guide available)
  • Infernal Weapons — Multiple rare and very rare arms from devil enemies
  • Soul Coins — 5-10 soul coins for Karlach's engine or trading
  • Platinum and Gold — Approximately 3,000-5,000 gold in coins and valuables
  • Devilfoil Masks — Collection items with unique properties (see our Devilfoil Mask guide)
  • Hope's Freedom — Story outcome that affects the game's ending

    • Strategic Campaign Advantages

  • Advantage Generation: The Guiding Bolt effect means your party generates advantage on demand, dramatically increasing crit chance and Sneak Attack availability
  • Radiant Damage Access: Classes without native radiant damage (rogues, non-Light clerics) gain a powerful tool against undead and devil enemies that dominate late-game encounters
  • Action Economy: A ranger with Gontr Mael can Hunter's Mark → Attack → Guiding Bolt in a single turn, dealing damage comparable to a 6th-level spell using only a 1st-level slot
  • Legendary Status: Carrying a legendary weapon unlocks unique dialogue throughout Act 3, particularly with celestial beings and devil NPCs

    • Experience Breakdown

    Completing the full Gontr Mael quest chain awards approximately 35,000-45,000 XP across both acts:

  • Gauntlet of Shar completion: ~12,000 XP
  • Ketheric Thorm battle: ~8,000 XP
  • Act 3 city exploration (relevant portions): ~5,000 XP
  • House of Hope encounters: ~15,000-20,000 XP
  • Gontr Mael acquisition: ~2,000 XP
