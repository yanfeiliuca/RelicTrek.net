Baldur's Gate 3

50+ million players. 200+ hours of content. Legendary weapons and armor hidden behind choices, quests, and secrets. Every location, every dialogue option, every consequence — nothing missed.

Blood of Lathander

★★★★★ Legendary

The ultimate mace — 1d8+2 bludgeoning, Sunbeam spell once per long rest, and resurrection once per long rest. Hidden in the Rosymorn Monastery.

Gontr Mael

★★★★★ Legendary

Legendary longbow that shoots Guiding Bolt. Complete the Gauntlet of Shar and defeat Raphael's enemies in the House of Hope.

Nyrulna

★★★★★ Legendary

Legendary trident with 1d8 piercing, creates a thunder explosion on impact. Find in the chest after completing Akabi's wheel in the Circus.

Shar's Spear of Evening

★★★★★ Legendary

1d8 piercing + 1d8 necrotic. Only obtainable if Shadowheart embraces Shar in Act 2. The dark counterpart to Selune's weapon.

Duellist's Prismatic Gloves

★★★★☆ Very Rare

+2 to spell attack rolls, add proficiency to spell damage. Loot from the House of Hope vault or trade with specific merchants.

Helldusk Armor

★★★★★ Legendary

21 AC, fire resistance, Fly spell. Stolen from the House of Hope — requires completing a multi-stage heist or killing Raphael.

Moonlantern

★★★★☆ Very Rare

Reveals invisible enemies and protects from Shadow-Cursed Lands. Obtained from the Thorm family or crafted from pixie dust.

Silver Sword of the Astral Plane

★★★★★ Legendary

1d10+3 slashing, advantage against intellect devourers. Lae'zel's companion quest reward — requires specific choices in Act 3.

Devilfoil Mask

★★★★☆ Very Rare

+2 Charisma, advantage on Persuasion/Deception. Found in the House of Hope or from devil traders in Baldur's Gate.

Shadowheart's Spear of Night

★★★★☆ Very Rare

Selune's weapon for Shadowheart's redemption path. 1d8+2 piercing + radiant damage. Counterpart to Shar's Spear.
