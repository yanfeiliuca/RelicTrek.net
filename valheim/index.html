Valheim

12 million Vikings strong. 9 biomes, 5 bosses, and a crafting system that rewards exploration. 10 essential weapons and armor sets that define your journey from Meadows to Ashlands.

Porcupine

★★★★★ Endgame

Needle + Fine Wood + Iron. The ultimate mace — 95 blunt + 47 pierce. Defeat Yagluth and venture into the Plains for needles.

Frostner

★★★★★ Endgame

Silver + Ymir Flesh + Freeze Gland + Ancient Bark. 93 frost + 40 spirit damage. The best elemental weapon in Valheim.

Blackmetal Sword

★★★★☆ Late Game

Black Metal + Fine Wood + Linen Thread. 95 slash damage. Defeat Bonemass, build a portal to Plains, and find Fuling villages.

Iron Sledge

★★★★☆ Mid-Late Game

Ancient Bark + Iron + Ymir Flesh + Draugr Elite Trophy. AoE sledgehammer with 75 blunt damage. Perfect for mining and crowds.

Draugr Fang

★★★★☆ Late Game

Ancient Bark + Silver + Deer Hide + Guck. Best bow in the game — 78 pierce + 24 poison. Venture into the Swamp for Guck.

Carapace Armor Set

★★★★★ Endgame

32 armor per piece. Carapace from Seekers in the Mistlands + Black Metal. The strongest armor before the Ashlands update.

Wolf Armor Set

★★★★☆ Late Game

20 armor per piece, frost resistance. Silver + Wolf Pelt + Wolf Trophy. Essential for Mountain biome exploration.

Padded Armor Set

★★★★☆ Late Game

26 armor per piece. Iron + Linen Thread. The pre-Mistlands endgame armor — still useful for Plains farming.

Stagbreaker

★★☆☆☆ Early Game

Core Wood + Deer Trophy + Leather. 38 blunt AoE. Your first real weapon — craft it immediately after Eikthyr.

Bronze Mace

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

Wood + Bronze + Leather. 53 blunt + 2x damage to skeletons. The weapon that carries you through the Swamp and Bonemass.
