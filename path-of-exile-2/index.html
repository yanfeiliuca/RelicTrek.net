Path of Exile 2

The deepest crafting system in ARPG history. Currency orbs, item bases, corruption, and six-linking — 10 essential items and mechanics every Exile must master.

Tabula Rasa

★★★★☆ Farming

6-linked white robe — no stats, 6 white sockets. Best leveling item. Farm from Humility cards in The Blood Aqueduct.

Headhunter

★★★★★ Ultra Rare

Gain rare monster mods on kill for 20 seconds. The most sought-after belt. Farm from The Doctor cards or Uber content.

Mageblood

★★★★★ Ultra Rare

4 permanent magic utility flask effects. The ultimate quality-of-life belt. Drop from Uber Pinnacle bosses or farm The Apothecary cards.

Mirror of Kalandra

★★★★★ Rarest Item

Create a perfect duplicate of any non-unique item. Rarer than Exalted Orbs by 1000:1. Only use on mirror-tier items.

Exalted Orb Crafting

★★★★☆ Advanced

Add a random modifier to a rare item. Master the meta-crafting prefixes/suffixes block strategy for controlled item creation.

Six-Link Armor

★★★★☆ Mid-Late Game

6-linked body armor for your main skill. Farm with Orb of Fusing or target-divination cards. 1500+ fusing on average.

Uncorrupted 6-Mod Vessel

★★★★★ Endgame

The Uncorrupted Vessels in PoE 2 are rare endgame jewels with up to 6 modifiers. Farm them from Tier 17 maps.

Regal Orb Farming

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

Upgrade a magic item to rare (adds 1 random mod). Essential for crafting. Farm from vendors, drops, and recipes.

21/23 Quality Skill Gem

★★★★☆ Endgame

Level 21 gem with 23% quality. Best DPS upgrade. Vendor recipe with 20% gem + Gemcutter's Prism, or farm level 21 from Vaal Temple.

Divine Orb Farming

★★★★☆ Endgame

Reroll numeric values on modifiers. The endgame currency. Farm from 6-mod rare items selling to vendors, or boss drops.
