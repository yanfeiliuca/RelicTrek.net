21/23 Quality Skill Gem

★★★★☆ Endgame
Skill Gem Level 21 23% Quality

A 21/23 Skill Gem is the maximum attainable power level for any active or support gem in Path of Exile 2. The "21" refers to gem level 21 (one level above the normal cap of 20), and "23" refers to 23% quality (3% above the normal cap of 20%). While these numbers sound small, the damage impact is enormous. A level 21 main skill gem typically deals 10–15% more damage than its level 20 counterpart due to the base damage scaling curve, while 23% quality adds another 3–8% depending on the quality bonus type. Combined with five support gems in a six-link armor, upgrading every gem from 20/20 to 21/23 represents a 20–35% total DPS increase for most builds. In a game where endgame Pinnacle bosses have hundreds of millions of HP, a 30% damage boost can be the difference between a 5-minute deathless kill and a 10-minute attrition fight where you eventually run out of flasks. The 21/23 gem system is PoE 2's final character optimization layer, accessed only after your gear, passive tree, and flasks are perfected. It is the definition of endgame min-maxing.

★ What Makes It Special

In PoE 2, skill gems level by being socketed in your gear while you gain experience. They naturally cap at level 20. To exceed this cap, you need specific methods: the vendor recipe for quality, Vaal corruption for level, and drop-only level 21 gems from specific content. Unlike gear that can be replaced when you find an upgrade, your 21/23 gems stay with you permanently (unless corrupted into something else). They are the one part of your character that represents pure, permanent investment. A full set of 21/23 gems for a six-link plus supports takes weeks to acquire but pays off for the rest of the league.

2 21/23 Acquisition Methods

Method 1: Gemcutter's Prism Vendor Recipe (Quality)

The most reliable method for obtaining 23% quality gems. This vendor recipe converts your level 20, 20% quality gem into a level 1, 23% quality gem, which you must then re-level to 21.

Input (Sell to Vendor)OutputNotes
1 × Level 20 Skill Gem (any quality)1 × Level 1, 20% Quality Gem of same typeReset to level 1 with 20% qual
1 × Level 20 Gem + 1 × Gemcutter's Prism1 × Level 1, 20% Quality GemSame as above; GCP not consumed if gem already 20% qual
1 × Level 20, 20% Qual Gem + 1 × Gemcutter's Prism1 × Level 1, 23% Quality GemThe target recipe for 23% quality
The 23% Quality Pipeline:
Step 1: Level a gem to 20 naturally (equip, gain XP)
Step 2: Quality it to 20% using Gemcutter's Prisms (1% per GCP)
Step 3: Vendor recipe: Lv20/20% gem + 1 GCP = Lv1/23% gem
Step 4: Re-equip the level 1 gem and level it back to 20/21
Step 5: During leveling, Vaal Orb corruption for chance at lv21
↬ → Level 21, 23% quality gem (or level 20/23 if corruption fails)
⚠ The Vendor Recipe Resets Level to 1

The 23% quality vendor recipe DEMOTES your gem from level 20 back to level 1. This means you must re-level the gem from scratch, which takes approximately 100–150 million experience points — roughly 8–15 hours of mapping depending on clear speed. You cannot use the gem during this time if it needs to be equipped for leveling. The standard strategy is to keep a "work in progress" gem in your alternate weapon swap set (gems in the secondary weapon slot still gain XP even if the weapon is not active). This way your main skill stays at 20 while the 23% version levels in the background.

Method 2: Vaal Orb Corruption (Level 21)

Vaal Orbs can corrupt gems in your inventory, with several possible outcomes. This is the primary method for obtaining level 21 gems:

Corruption OutcomeChanceResult
No change~25%Gem becomes corrupted, level and quality unchanged

-1 gem level~25%Level 20 → Level 19 (bricked for most purposes)

⚠ Corruption Is Permanent

Once a gem is corrupted, it cannot be modified by any currency item. You cannot quality it further, you cannot level it with Lens items, and you cannot change its color. The corruption outcome is permanent and irreversible. This means you should ONLY corrupt a backup copy of your gem, never your primary equipped gem. Keep your level 20/20 gem equipped for safety, level a second copy to 20/20, and corrupt the copy. If the corruption hits level 21, great. If it bricks to level 19, your main gem is unaffected and you try again with another copy.

Method 4: Empower Support Gem (Effective Level 22+)

While not a 21/23 gem itself, the Empower Support gem increases the level of supported active gems by +1 at level 2, +2 at level 3, and +3 at level 4 (corrupted). This is a guaranteed, non-RNG way to exceed level 20:

A level 3 Empower is expensive (~1–2 Divine Orbs) but provides a guaranteed +2 gem levels without any corruption RNG. A level 4 Empower (+3 levels) is one of the most expensive gems in the game (5–15 Divine Orbs) but is the single largest support gem DPS increase available. Most endgame builds run Empower as one of their six support gems specifically for this reason.

3 Materials & Drop Sources

Every currency item and material needed for the 21/23 gem journey.

● Gemcutter's Prism (GCP) × 20+ per gem

Function: Increases gem quality by 1% per GCP. Need 20 per gem for base qualitying, plus 1 extra for the 23% vendor recipe.
Vendor recipe: Sell a gem with 20% quality = 1 Gemcutter's Prism. This is the primary source.
Drop source: All monsters level 12+, currency chests, strongboxes. Moderately rare.
Drop rate: ~0.3% of currency drops. About 1 GCP per 300–500 monsters.
Trade value: 1–2 Chaos Orbs per GCP early league, 0.5–1 chaos late league.
Pro tip: Pick up ALL quality gems that drop (they show a quality percentage when dropped). Any gem with 1–19% quality can be combined with others in a vendor recipe: sell gems whose quality totals 40% = 1 GCP. For example, a 12% qual gem + 15% qual gem + 13% qual gem = 40% total = 1 GCP. This "qual stacking" recipe is how most players farm GCPs passively.

● Vaal Orb × 4–8 per gem attempt

Function: Corrupts gems to potentially grant +1 level or +1–3% quality. Also used to corrupt maps for Vaal Temple access.
Vendor recipe: 7 Vaal skill gems (the skill gems, not Vaal Orbs) + 1 Sacrifice Vaal fragment = 1 Vaal Orb.
Drop source: Vaal side areas (guaranteed 1–2 per area), Atziri/uber content, corrupted map bosses, Delve vaal cities.
Drop rate: ~1 Vaal Orb per 2–3 Vaal side areas. About 0.1% of general currency drops.
Trade value: 1–2 Chaos Orbs each.
Pro tip: Vaal Orbs are also needed to corrupt maps into Vaal Temple (sell 1 Vaal Orb + 1 Temple Map = Vaal Temple). Budget 10–15 Vaal Orbs for your full gem corruption project (5–8 for gem corruptions, 3–5 for map corruptions to access Vaal Temple).

● Gem Experience & Leveling Strategy

Gem level 1 to 20: ~119 million experience points. At efficient mapping (tier 14+), this takes 8–15 hours of playtime depending on clear speed and XP modifiers.
Gem level 20 to 21: Gems do NOT level past 20 naturally. Level 21 can ONLY be obtained through Vaal corruption (+1 outcome) or direct drop from Vaal Temple/corrupted zones.
Weapon swap leveling: Gems socketed in your alternate weapon set (press X to swap) still gain experience from monster kills, even though the weapon is not active. This is the key technique for leveling your 23% quality backup gem while keeping your main gem at 20 for combat.
Experience boost sources: "% increased experience gain for socketed gems" gear (Fated Uniques, specific crafting mods), party play (more monsters), Delve (high density), Breach/Legion encounters (many monsters in one spot).
Pro tip: Level ALL your support gems to 20 simultaneously by filling every unused socket with a gem. Even gems you don't use can be leveled to 20 and sold (level 20 gems have trade value) or used in the quality vendor recipe.

● Vaal Temple Map Access

What it is: A special corrupted map area containing only Vaal-themed monsters and guaranteed corrupted item drops, including level 21 gems.
How to access: Corrupt a regular Temple Map with a Vaal Orb (25% chance to become Vaal Temple). OR purchase Vaal Temple directly from trade (2–5 chaos).
Difficulty: High. Equivalent to Tier 15+ map with additional Vaal monster modifiers. Requires 75%+ resistances and 3,000+ life.
Drop table: 3–8 corrupted gems per clear, with ~3–8% chance for any specific gem to be level 21. About 1 level 21 gem per 3–5 Vaal Temple runs on average.
Pro tip: Run Vaal Temple with a "Gems" loot filter to highlight drops. The gem drops are corrupted, so they cannot be further modified, but a level 21/20 corrupted gem is still better than a level 20/20 uncorrupted gem for most builds.

5 Gotcha Tips

The painful lessons and hidden mechanics of gem optimization.

⚠ Corrupting Your Only Copy Is Catastrophic

Never, EVER corrupt your only copy of a gem you are actively using. Corruption is irreversible and has a 50% chance of making the gem worse (25% -1 level, 25% no change but corrupted tag prevents further modification). Always level a backup gem to 20/20 before attempting corruption. Your main gem stays equipped and functional while the backup takes the 50/50 gamble. If you corrupt your only Lightning Arrow gem and it drops to level 19, you just lost 10+ hours of leveling and your build's primary damage source.

⚠ Vaal Gems Replace the Original Skill

When a Vaal Orb transforms a gem into its Vaal variant (e.g., Lightning Arrow → Vaal Lightning Arrow), the original skill is replaced entirely. The Vaal version is a completely different skill with different mechanics, soul gain requirements, and usage patterns. This outcome is rare (~0–1% for gems that have Vaal versions), but if it happens to your main skill gem, you may need to restructure your entire build. Only corrupt gems that have acceptable Vaal variants, or accept the risk.

⚠ Quality Caps at 23%

The maximum gem quality is 23%. This can only be achieved through the vendor recipe (level 20/20% gem + 1 GCP = level 1/23% gem). Direct GCP application stops at 20%. If you quality a gem to 20% with GCPs and then use the vendor recipe, you get 23%. If you try to apply more GCPs to a 20% gem directly, nothing happens. Many new players waste GCPs trying to push past 20% without knowing the recipe exists.

⚠ Level 21 Gems From Drops Are Corrupted

Any level 21 gem that drops from Vaal Temple or corrupted zones is automatically corrupted. This means it cannot be further modified — no quality changes, no level changes, nothing. A dropped level 21/20 gem cannot be improved to 21/23. Conversely, a gem you leveled to 20/23 and then corrupted to 21/23 is ALSO corrupted but started with higher quality. The theoretical "perfect" 21/23 gem must be created by: leveling to 20 → quality to 20% → vendor recipe to 23% → re-level to 20 → corrupt for +1 level (25% chance). This is why true 21/23 gems are so rare and valuable.

⚠ Gems Stop Leveling at Your Level Cap

A gem cannot exceed your character level. If you are level 85, your gems cannot level past 85 even if they have enough XP. Since gem level 20 requires ~119M XP (roughly equivalent to character level 70–75 worth of XP), this is rarely an issue for the level 20 threshold. But if you're leveling multiple gems simultaneously and one falls behind, it may be because you leveled up slowly during that period. Gems gain XP based on monster kills, not a flat rate.

⚠ Some Gems Don't Benefit From Levels

Not all gems scale significantly with gem level. Utility gems like "Cast When Damage Taken," "Trigger Gems," and most aura gems have flat effects that improve minimally or not at all with levels. For these gems, quality is the only important factor, and even that is marginal. Do not waste Vaal Orbs trying to get level 21 on a Cast When Damage Taken gem — the trigger threshold barely changes. Focus your 21/23 efforts on damage gems and primary supports only.

⚠ Alternate Quality Gems (PoE 2 Addition)

PoE 2 introduces Alternate Quality gems from specific league content (Heist, primarily). These gems have DIFFERENT quality effects than standard quality. For example, a standard "Increased Critical Strikes" support gives +% crit chance per quality, while the alternate quality version might give +% crit multi instead. An alternate quality 20% gem may outperform a standard 23% gem for certain builds. Check your build guide or Path of Building to determine if alternate quality is worth pursuing before committing to the 23% standard quality grind.

⚠ Enlighten & Enhance Are Gem Types Too

"Enlighten" (reduces mana reservation of supported gems) and "Enhance" (increases quality of supported active gems) are special support gems that ONLY scale with gem level. A level 3 Enlighten reduces reservation by 4% vs 3% at level 2 — a build-defining difference for aura-stackers. These gems cannot be bought from vendors and must be obtained through drops or corruption. They are extremely expensive (level 3 Enlighten = 2–5 Divine Orbs). If your build needs one, prioritize buying it over crafting weapon or armor upgrades.

6 Free Benefits

Everything else you gain while working toward 21/23 gems.

💰 Gemcutter's Prism Stockpile

Farming GCPs through the quality gem vendor recipe naturally accumulates a stockpile that far exceeds what you need for your own gems. A typical player ends up with 50–100 excess GCPs after completing their 21/23 set. These sell for 1–2 chaos each, representing 50–200 chaos in side income. Alternatively, save them for future characters or to quality gems you intend to sell (a 20% quality level 20 gem sells for 2–5x the price of a 0% quality one on the trade site).

🔍 Vaal Orb Accumulation

Running Vaal side areas and Vaal Temple for gem drops also generates a steady supply of Vaal Orbs. These are needed for map corruptions, item corruptions for implicits, and Vaal Temple map creation. Most players who pursue 21/23 gems end the league with 50–100 excess Vaal Orbs, worth 1–2 chaos each. The gem farm funds your entire endgame corruption pipeline.

⚡ Alternate Weapon Swap Awareness

The technique of leveling gems in your alternate weapon swap is one of the most powerful quality-of-life tricks in PoE. Once you develop the habit, you will always have a full set of gems passively leveling in the background. This means every character you make benefits from instant level 20 gems (level them while playing your main). It also means you can stockpile leveled gems to sell or use for future builds without any dedicated farming time.

📚 Gem Synergy Knowledge

The process of optimizing every gem in your six-link forces you to understand how gems interact. You learn which support gems have the highest damage multipliers, which have hidden penalties (like "Concentrated Effect" reducing area), and how quality affects each differently. This knowledge is invaluable for build creation, gear evaluation, and helping other players. A PoE player who understands gem mechanics at this level can create viable builds from scratch rather than copying guides.

🎯 Level 20 Gem Sales

While leveling your main gems to 21/23, you will inevitably level many other gems to 20 in your spare sockets. These level 20 gems sell for 5–20 chaos each on the trade site (depending on gem popularity). A popular support like "Increased Area of Effect" at level 20 sells for 15–25 chaos. This "gem farming" side hustle can fund your entire build progression. Just equip popular gems in every empty socket and vendor the level 20 copies.

Divine Orb Farming

Farm Divine Orbs from the 6-mod rare vendor recipe, Pinnacle boss drops, and Heist currency chests. The final step in min-maxing every gear slot after your gems are 21/23.
