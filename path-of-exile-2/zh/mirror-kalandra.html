卡兰德的魔镜 当前

通货物品 | 传奇 | 难度：★★★★★

一、概览

卡兰德的魔镜是整个流放之路中最稀有、最有价值、最具神话色彩的物品。这种标志性的通货物品以其独特的镜子图标和空灵的银色光泽而闻名，在游戏的经济和文化中占据着独特的地位，这是其他任何物品——无论是猎首腰带、法师之血，还是最完美的打造的稀有武器——都无法比拟的。当玩家对任何非传奇物品使用卡兰德的魔镜时，它会创建该物品的完美、完全相同的复制品，包括所有词缀、插槽颜色、链接、品质和物品等级。原始物品保持完好，而全新的副本进入玩家的背包，与原始物品在每个方面都无法区分，只是带有"已镜像"标签，防止它再次被镜像。

这种一次性消耗品的含义是惊人的。在一个游戏中，打造一件完美的六连胸甲，带有T1生命、三重抗性和伤害词缀，可能需要消耗数百颗崇高石、神圣石和无数小时的交易和工作台合成，卡兰德的魔镜代表了合成精英与普通富豪之间的终极经济桥梁。那些打造镜阶物品的玩家——通常通过数千次崇高石打击、精华合成、庄园重铸和觉醒者石组合——生产的物品如此珍贵，只有魔镜才能负担得起复制它们。这些镜像副本随后在公开市场上以足以使休闲玩家破产的价格交易，建立了一个围绕镜阶合成服务的整个经济生态系统。

卡兰德的魔镜与其他通货物品之间的兑换率作为流放之路经济的实际金标准。当其他通货因赛季机制、合成主流和机器人活动而波动时，魔镜相对于赛季总财富上限保持着非常稳定的估值。在流放之路2的早期体验阶段，魔镜与神圣石的比率已稳定在800:1到1200:1之间，意味着一颗卡兰德的魔镜大约价值一千颗神圣石。为了正确看待这一点，一个高效刷图的 dedicated 玩家可能在整个赛季积累50-100颗神圣石——使得自然魔镜掉落对绝大多数玩家来说是一生一次的事件。

除了其原始经济价值之外，卡兰德的魔镜在流放之路社区中拥有巨大的文化意义。魔镜掉落以与赢得彩票头奖同样的热情来庆祝。当玩家在全局聊天中链接魔镜时，会引发祝贺、羡慕和不可置信的 outpouring。在直播中掉落魔镜的主播会看到观众人数飙升，因为消息传遍社区。使用魔镜"镜像"物品的行为通常伴随着仪式感，玩家邀请朋友见证这一刻并截图留念。这种文化重量将魔镜从单纯的通货提升到了传奇的领域——即使是最疲惫的老兵也会以真正的兴奋来回应的物品。

重要的是，卡兰德的魔镜对传奇物品不起作用。试图在猎首腰带、法师之血或纯白之袍上使用魔镜将失败并显示错误消息。此限制旨在保持最强大传奇物品的稀有性和独占性——如果魔镜可以复制传奇，整个传奇经济将在第一个魔镜掉落后的几天内崩溃。魔镜的力量保留给稀有物品，这是真正的终局合成画布，玩家的技能、知识和经济投资在这里汇聚，创造出统计上的艺术品。

在流放之路2中，卡兰德的魔镜保留其核心功能，并可能对获取率和掉落来源进行调整，以适应续作的重新设计终局。基本事实保持不变：找到卡兰德的魔镜是可能发生在流放之路玩家身上的单一最具影响力经济事件。它可以资助整个赛季的BD构建，从合成服务处购买多个镜像物品，或作为终极财富和运气的象征存放在仓库中。了解魔镜如何进入经济、如何使用以及如果你有幸找到一颗该如何应对，是每个严肃的流放之路2玩家必备的知识。

二、获取方式

卡兰德的魔镜无法通过任何商人配方制作，无法通过任何商人配方获得，也无法像其他追逐物品那样通过命运卡刷取进行精确的目标定位。它的获取是流放之路2中纯随机机会的最纯粹表达，这种有意的设计选择是其神秘感和经济角色的核心。理解有限的获取途径对于设定现实的期望和识别运气是否降临到你身上至关重要。

2.1 自然掉落——彩票中奖

卡兰德的魔镜可以从游戏中的任何怪物、任何宝箱、任何可破坏物体和任何产生通货物品的奖励来源中掉落。这种普遍掉落资格与游戏中任何物品中最低的掉落权重相匹配。估计的基础掉率因内容等级而异：

这些数字来自社区数据聚合的估计，应理解为粗略指南而非精确数字。Grinding Gear Games从未公布过卡兰德的魔镜的官方掉率，实际掉率可能因隐藏因素、特定赛季调整和账户级别掉落保护系统而有所不同。

2.2 魔镜碎片命运卡

在流放之路2中，引入了新的魔镜获取命运卡系统，提供了一条理论上确定性的（尽管实际上几乎不可能）通过刷取获得魔镜的途径。魔镜碎片是可以20张套装兑换一颗完整卡兰德的魔镜的命运卡。单个碎片从游戏中最具挑战性的内容中掉落：

  • Uber Pinnacle Bosses: ~0.1-0.3% chance to drop a Mirror Shard per kill
  • Depth 500+ Delve: Extremely rare drops from Delve bosses at maximum depth
  • Tier 17-18 Awakened Maps: Elevated drop rate in the highest tier map content
  • League Endgame Bosses: Some league-specific pinnacle encounters have Shard on drop table

以估计需要的20张碎片和每次Boss击杀0.1-0.3%的掉率，通过碎片完成魔镜所需的预期Uber Boss击杀数约为6,000到20,000次。作为参考，即使是最 dedicated 的Boss刷取者通常每赛季也只完成100-300次Uber Boss遭遇。这使得碎片途径在技术上具有确定性，但对所有极端玩家来说都是实际上不可能的。

2.3 交易区经济学

对于99.99%永远不会看到自然魔镜掉落的玩家来说，交易仍然是唯一现实的获取途径。交易区中的魔镜定价：

  • Week 1-2: Mirrors typically unavailable (no one has found one yet)
  • Week 3-4: 1000-1500 Divine Orbs (first Mirrors enter market)
  • Month 2: 800-1200 Divine Orbs (supply normalizes)
  • Mid-Late League: 600-1000 Divine Orbs (market saturation)

购买魔镜的决定应该与它能实现什么进行权衡。对大多数玩家来说，通过合成和交易购买直接将1000颗神圣石投入角色装备，比单颗魔镜提供更多的实际力量。魔镜主要被打算运营镜阶合成服务的玩家购买——作为商业投资来复制物品以获取利润。

三、材料与掉落来源

3.1 最高概率掉落来源

While the Mirror of Kalandra can theoretically drop anywhere, certain content types offer measurably higher chances:

Uber Pinnacle Bosses — Highest single-source drop rate (~0.01-0.05%)
Delirium Mirror Encounters — Rare drop from the Delirium reward structure at high tiers
Deep Delve Bosses (500+) — Aul, the Crystal King and other depth bosses
Grand Heist Reward Rooms — "Thieves' Trinkets" and "Unusual Gems" rooms at high tier
Simulacrum Wave 30 — The final wave of Delirium Simulacrum has elevated Mirror weight

3.2 最大化魔镜掉落机会

虽然你无法以任何可靠性直接刷取魔镜，但你可以构建你的游戏方式，以最大化游戏为你进行的"掷骰"次数：

  • Run fully juiced Tier 16 maps: Maximum item quantity means more total drops, which means more opportunities for the Mirror drop table to roll. Every percentage of increased quantity matters when you are making millions of rolls.
  • Stack monster density: Beyond, Delirium, Breach, and Legion mechanics add hundreds of additional monsters per map. More monsters killed = more drop rolls.
  • Farm Uber bosses regularly: Uber bosses have the highest per-encounter Mirror drop rate. If you enjoy bossing, the chance at a Mirror is a welcome bonus.
  • Push deep Delve: Depth 400+ Delve encounters have progressively increasing rare drop weights. Delve bosses at depth 500+ represent some of the best Mirror opportunities.
  • Run Simulacrum: Wave 30 Simulacrum completions have a meaningful Mirror drop chance, and the splinter farming process generates currency along the way.
  • Play consistently: The most reliable factor in Mirror acquisition is total hours played. Players who put in 500+ hours per league have orders of magnitude more drop rolls than casual players.

四、"刷取路线"——最大化你的机会

卡兰德的魔镜在传统意义上没有真正的"刷取路线"。没有区域、地图或策略可以以有意义的可靠性针对魔镜掉落。相反，本节概述了如何构建你的通用终局游戏玩法，以最大化统计掷骰次数，在一个赛季中给予你最高（仍然很小）的看到魔镜的机会。

Step 1 — Build a Fast, Durable Mapper: Your primary goal is to clear as many monsters per hour as possible. Choose a build with excellent area coverage, strong movement speed, and sufficient defense to avoid deaths that slow your pace. Popular options include Tornado Shot Deadeye, Lightning Arrow Raider, Poisonous Concoction Pathfinder, and various autobomber setups.
Step 2 — Juice Every Map Maximally: Roll Tier 16 maps with Orb of Alchemy + Orb of Scouring until you hit 25%+ pack size and 80%+ item quantity. Apply Delirium Orbs (particularly Skittering, Armaments, or Divination for bonus drops). Use Winged Scarabs for maximum monster density. Add Beyond through the map device crafting option.
Step 3 — Integrate League Mechanics: Every mechanic that adds monsters increases your Mirror drop rolls. Prioritize Breach (opens monster portals), Legion (frozen army spawns hundreds of mobs), Harbinger (adds extra packs), and Delirium (scales all drops). Run these mechanics in every map without exception.
Step 4 — Weekly Uber Boss Rotation: Set a goal to kill 5-10 Uber pinnacle bosses per week. These have the highest per-encounter Mirror drop rate and also provide valuable secondary drops (boss-specific uniques, Voidstones, awakened gems). Even without a Mirror, this content is profitable.
Step 5 — Push Delve Depth: When you accumulate Sulphite from mapping, push Delve as deep as your build allows. Target depth 400+ for increased rare drop weights and depth 500+ for boss encounters with meaningful Mirror chances. The deeper you go, the better the rewards.
Step 6 — Run Simulacrum: Save Delirium Splinters until you can form a Simulacrum (requires 300 splinters). Run Simulacrum to wave 30 for a concentrated drop session with elevated Mirror weight on the final waves. Even without a Mirror, Simulacrum is profitable from cluster jewel and unique drops.
Step 7 — Play the League: The most important factor is total playtime. A player who runs 10,000 maps in a league has ten times the Mirror drop opportunities of a player who runs 1,000 maps. Consistent, efficient play over weeks and months is the only meaningful strategy.
Step 8 — If a Mirror Drops: STOP. Do not immediately use it. Assess your options: (A) Use it to duplicate a mirror-tier item you or a friend has crafted, (B) Sell it for 800-1200 Divine Orbs and fund multiple characters, or (C) Offer "mirror service" to other players — charging a fee (typically 50-100 Exalted/ Divine Orbs) to duplicate their items using your Mirror.

4.1 "魔镜服务"经济

The most sophisticated economic activity surrounding Mirrors is the mirror service business model:

  1. Wealthy players craft "mirror-tier" items — rare items with perfect or near-perfect modifiers
  2. These items are displayed in a dedicated forum thread or trade listing with "Mirror Service Available"
  3. Players who own a Mirror of Kalandra pay a fee (plus providing the base item to be mirrored) to use their Mirror on the crafted item
  4. The Mirror owner keeps the fee, the customer receives a perfect mirrored copy
  5. The original crafter's item remains untouched and can be mirrored infinitely

这创造了一种共生的经济关系：合成师有动力追求完美物品，魔镜拥有者有收入来源，非魔镜玩家可以以远低于合成成本的价格获得镜阶装备。流放之路历史上最著名的镜阶物品已被镜像数百次，为它们的所有者产生了数千颗崇高石的服务费。

4.2 镜阶合成

To understand what makes an item worthy of a Mirror, consider what goes into crafting one:

  • Base Selection: An ilvl 86+ influenced base with the optimal implicit modifier
  • Essence Spamming: 100-500 Deafening Essences to hit a tier-one prefix
  • Exalted Orb Slams: 20-50 Exalted Orbs to fill remaining suffixes with desirable mods
  • Crafting Bench: Bench-craft the most controllable mod (typically life or a resistance)
  • Divine Orb Perfection: 50-200 Divine Orbs to roll perfect values on all modifiers
  • Harvest Reforging: Using Sacred Blossoms and high-tier Harvest crafts to improve odds
  • Awakener's Orb: Combining two influenced items for dual influence modifiers

一件真正的镜阶物品的总投资可能超过500-1000颗崇高石。这种投资只有在物品可以被镜像以获利时才是合理的——没有魔镜经济，追求完美合成在经济上是不理性的。

五、注意事项与关键警告

⚠ 陷阱#1：无法复制传奇物品

卡兰德的魔镜不能用于传奇物品。这包括猎首腰带、法师之血、纯白之袍和游戏中的每个其他传奇物品。试图这样做将失败并返还你的魔镜。魔镜只适用于稀有（黄色）物品和某些特殊基底类型。在尝试镜像之前，始终验证你的目标物品是否为稀有物品。

⚠ 陷阱#2：复制的物品无法修改

卡兰德的魔镜创建的任何物品都会获得"已镜像"标签。已镜像物品不能以任何方式修改——没有工作台、没有崇高石、没有神圣石、没有品质调整、没有插槽变化、没有链接、没有腐化。物品永久固定在其当前状态。这意味着原始物品在镜像之前必须是完美的——原始物品中的任何缺陷都会永久复制到副本中。

⚠ 陷阱#3：不要在普通物品上使用魔镜

找到第一颗魔镜的新手玩家有时会恐慌地将它用在拥有的最好的物品上——这通常是一件不错但远非完美的稀有物品。这被广泛认为是流放之路中最大的浪费。一颗用在"还不错"的胸甲上而非镜阶物品上的魔镜，代表了数千颗神圣石的损失价值。如果你找到了魔镜但没有镜阶物品，卖掉它。不要将它用在任何非完美的物品上。

⚠ 陷阱#4：魔镜诈骗警惕

The high value of Mirrors makes them a target for scammers. Common scams include:

  • Trade window swaps: Scammer puts Mirror in trade, then quickly swaps for an Exalted Orb or Chaos Orb before you confirm. Always triple-check the item icon and name.
  • Fake mirror services: Players offer to mirror your item "for a discount" but have no Mirror, taking your fee and disappearing. Only use established, vetted mirror service providers with forum reputation.
  • Price manipulation: Scammers artificially inflate or deflate Mirror prices in trade listings to trick inexperienced traders. Check multiple sources before pricing your Mirror.

⚠ 陷阱#5：独狼区——魔镜困境

在独狼区，找到魔镜创造了一个独特的困境。没有来自其他玩家的镜阶物品访问权限，也没有可以出售的交易市场，你的选择是有限的：用在你最好的自制品上（与交易区的镜阶物品相比可能次优）、无限期保存等待合成值得的东西（魔镜闲置的机会成本），或用于复制有价值的合成基底以在组队独狼区与朋友交易（如果玩组队独狼区）。

  • Use it on the best item you have crafted (likely suboptimal compared to trade league mirror-tier items)
  • Save it indefinitely waiting to craft something worthy (opportunity cost of the Mirror sitting unused)
  • Use it to duplicate a valuable crafting base for SSF trading with friends (if playing group SSF)

大多数在独狼区找到魔镜的玩家 pragmatic 地将其用在最好的自制品上，接受理论上的最大价值无法在缺乏交易基础设施的情况下实现。

⚠ 陷阱#6：赛季区 vs 永久区经济

在赛季结束时，所有角色和物品转移到永久标准区。标准区的魔镜与挑战区的魔镜价值截然不同：

  • Challenge League Mirrors: 800-1200 Divine Orbs (high demand, limited supply, fresh economy)
  • Standard League Mirrors: 200-400 Divine Orbs equivalent (oversupply from league migrations, deflated economy)

如果你在赛季晚期找到魔镜，考虑在挑战区使用或出售它再进行迁移，那里的需求和价格更高。

六、免费福利与额外知识

6.1 如果你找到魔镜该怎么办

The most important question in Path of Exile is: "I just found a Mirror — what now?" Follow this checklist:

  1. Take a screenshot. You will want to remember this moment. Post it on Reddit for karma.
  2. Do not tell random players in global chat immediately. You will be spammed with trade requests and begging messages.
  3. Decide: use or sell? If you have access to a mirror-tier item (friend's crafting, established mirror service), using the Mirror may provide more personal value. If not, selling is almost always the better economic choice.
  4. If selling: Price check current Mirror rates on the official trade site. List for a competitive price — Mirrors are liquid and sell quickly if priced correctly.
  5. If using: Find a mirror-tier item through the official forums, Discord communities, or trusted in-game connections. Negotiate a mirror fee (typically 50-100 Divine Orbs worth of currency or items).
  6. Invest the proceeds wisely. 1000 Divine Orbs can fund 3-5 fully geared characters, a Headhunter, a Mageblood, and still leave hundreds for crafting. Do not blow it all on gambling.

6.2 魔镜掉落的历史

Some of the most famous Mirror moments in Path of Exile history include:

  • First documented Mirror drop in Open Beta, 2013 — caused a server-wide announcement
  • Streamer "Kripparrian" dropping a live on stream in 2014, viewed by 50,000+ people
  • A player finding two Mirrors in the same map in 2016 — statistically one of the rarest events in gaming history
  • SSF player finding a Mirror after 8,000 hours of play — proving persistence can be rewarded
  • Speedrunner finding a Mirror during a level 1-100 race, causing a 10-minute pause in the broadcast

6.3 传说中的镜阶物品

The most famous mirror-tier items that have been duplicated hundreds of times include:

  • "The Eternity Shroud" legacy ES body armors: Perfect tier-1 ES, life, triple resistance, explode mod
  • +3 bow weapons: +3 to Level of Socketed Bow Gems, tier-1 attack speed, double damage, crit chance
  • Aura stacking shields: Recover 5% of Mana when you Block, +1 to Level of All Spell Skill Gems, tier-1 life
  • Ring crafts: Triple tier-1 elemental damage with attacks, life, two resistances, -mana cost
  • Amulet crafts: +1 to Level of All Skill Gems, crit multi, multi-elemental damage, life, tier-1 attributes

  • 法师之血指南——永久药剂效果，终极体验优化
  • 崇高石合成——创造镜阶物品的艺术
  • 神圣石刷取——大规模通货生成

6.5 最终视角

卡兰德的魔镜在游戏史上占据着独特的地位。它不仅仅是一件稀有物品——它是一种文化现象，催生了表情包、歌曲、数万点赞的Reddit帖子和整个围绕它的经济子系统。与神圣石的1000:1兑换率、彩票般的掉率、使用它的仪式以及魔镜服务经济，所有这些结合在一起创造了一件超越其游戏功能的物品。无论你 inventory 中是否曾经拥有过一颗，仅仅知道魔镜存在——知道在瓦尔克拉斯某个幸运的流亡者刚刚永远改变了他们的游戏人生——就是流放之路2终局如此引人注目的部分原因。继续刷图。继续杀戮。也许，只是也许，有一天魔镜会找到你。
