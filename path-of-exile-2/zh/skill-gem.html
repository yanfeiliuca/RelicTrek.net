21/23品质技能石

★★★★☆ 终局内容
技能石 21级 23%品质

21/23技能石是流放之路2中任何主动或辅助宝石可达到的最高力量等级。"21"指宝石等级21（比正常上限20高一级），"23"指23%品质（比正常上限20%高3%）。虽然这些数字听起来很小，但伤害影响是巨大的。21级主技能石通常比20级版本多造成10-15%的伤害，因为基础伤害缩放曲线，而23%品质根据品质奖励类型再增加3-8%。结合六连护甲中的五个辅助宝石，将每个宝石从20/20升级到21/23代表大多数BD的20-35%的总DPS提升。在一个终局巅峰Boss拥有数亿HP的游戏中，30%的伤害提升可能是5分钟无死亡击杀和10分钟消耗战之间的差别，你最终会用尽药水。21/23宝石系统是PoE 2的最终角色优化层，只有在你的装备、被动树和药水都完善后才能触及。它是终局极限优化的定义。

★ 为什么它特殊

在PoE 2中，技能石通过在装备中插槽并获取经验来升级。它们自然上限为20级。要超过这个上限，你需要特定的方法：品质的商人配方、瓦尔腐化获取等级，以及特定内容直接掉落的21级宝石。与找到升级装备时可以替换的装备不同，你的21/23宝石永久伴随着你（除非被腐化成其他东西）。它们是角色中代表纯粹、永久投资的唯一部分。六连加辅助的完整21/23宝石套装需要数周时间来获取，但会在整个赛季中持续回报。

2 21/23获取方法

方法一：宝石匠棱镜商店配方（品质）

获得23%品质宝石最可靠的方法。此商人配方将你的20级20%品质宝石转换为1级23%品质宝石，然后你必须重新将其练到21级。

The 23% Quality Pipeline:
Step 1: Level a gem to 20 naturally (equip, gain XP)
Step 2: Quality it to 20% using Gemcutter's Prisms (1% per GCP)
Step 3: Vendor recipe: Lv20/20% gem + 1 GCP = Lv1/23% gem
Step 4: Re-equip the level 1 gem and level it back to 20/21
Step 5: During leveling, Vaal Orb corruption for chance at lv21
↬ → Level 21, 23% quality gem (or level 20/23 if corruption fails)
⚠ 商店配方将等级重置为1

23%品质的商人配方将宝石从20级降至1级。这意味着你必须从头重新练级，这需要大约1-1.5亿经验值——根据清图速度大约8-15小时刷图。在此期间，如果你需要装备该宝石来升级，则无法使用它。标准策略是将"进行中"的宝石放在备用武器切换套中（宝石在副手武器插槽中即使武器未激活也能获得经验）。这样你的主技能保持20级，而23%版本在后台升级。

方法二：瓦尔宝珠腐化（21级）

瓦尔石可以腐化你背包中的宝石，有几种可能的结果。这是获得21级宝石的主要方法：

Corruption OutcomeChanceResult
No change~25%Gem becomes corrupted, level and quality unchanged

-1 gem level~25%Level 20 → Level 19 (bricked for most purposes)
+1–3% quality~12.5%20% → 21–23% quality (only if not already 23%)
+3% quality (guaranteed max)~12.5%If already 23%, this becomes "no change"
Transforms to Vaal Gem~0%Only for gems with Vaal variants; changes skill entirely
⚠ 腐化是永久的

一旦宝石被腐化，它就不能被任何通货物品修改。你不能进一步提升它的品质，不能用透镜物品练级，也不能改变它的颜色。腐化结果是永久且不可逆的。这意味着你应该只腐化宝石的备份副本，绝不能是你主要装备的宝石。保留你的20/20宝石安全装备，将第二个副本练到20/20，然后腐化副本。如果腐化命中21级，很好。如果毁成19级，你的主宝石不受影响，你可以再用另一个副本重试。

Phase 1 (Mapping, Days 1–5):
↳ Equip main skill gem, level naturally to 20
↳ Buy a SECOND copy of the same gem (level 1 from vendor, 1 chance orb)
↳ Place second gem in alternate weapon swap set for passive leveling

↳ Obtain Vaal Orbs (farm Vaal side areas, Atziri fragments)

Phase 4 (Completion, Days 12–15):
↳ 23% quality gem reaches level 20 in weapon swap
↳ If corruption hit level 21 on a 23% qual gem: DONE (21/23)
↳ If corruption hit level 21 on 20% qual: quality to 23% via recipe, re-level
→ Full 21/23 gem set for six-link + all supports

3 材料与获取位置

Every currency item and material needed for the 21/23 gem journey.

● Gemcutter's Prism (GCP) × 20+ per gem

Function: Increases gem quality by 1% per GCP. Need 20 per gem for base qualitying, plus 1 extra for the 23% vendor recipe.
Vendor recipe: Sell a gem with 20% quality = 1 Gemcutter's Prism. This is the primary source.
Drop source: All monsters level 12+, currency chests, strongboxes. Moderately rare.
Drop rate: ~0.3% of currency drops. About 1 GCP per 300–500 monsters.
Trade value: 1–2 Chaos Orbs per GCP early league, 0.5–1 chaos late league.
Pro tip: Pick up ALL quality gems that drop (they show a quality percentage when dropped). Any gem with 1–19% quality can be combined with others in a vendor recipe: sell gems whose quality totals 40% = 1 GCP. For example, a 12% qual gem + 15% qual gem + 13% qual gem = 40% total = 1 GCP. This "qual stacking" recipe is how most players farm GCPs passively.

● Vaal Orb × 4–8 per gem attempt

Function: Corrupts gems to potentially grant +1 level or +1–3% quality. Also used to corrupt maps for Vaal Temple access.
Vendor recipe: 7 Vaal skill gems (the skill gems, not Vaal Orbs) + 1 Sacrifice Vaal fragment = 1 Vaal Orb.
Drop source: Vaal side areas (guaranteed 1–2 per area), Atziri/uber content, corrupted map bosses, Delve vaal cities.
Drop rate: ~1 Vaal Orb per 2–3 Vaal side areas. About 0.1% of general currency drops.
Trade value: 1–2 Chaos Orbs each.
Pro tip: Vaal Orbs are also needed to corrupt maps into Vaal Temple (sell 1 Vaal Orb + 1 Temple Map = Vaal Temple). Budget 10–15 Vaal Orbs for your full gem corruption project (5–8 for gem corruptions, 3–5 for map corruptions to access Vaal Temple).

● Gem Experience & Leveling Strategy

Gem level 1 to 20: ~119 million experience points. At efficient mapping (tier 14+), this takes 8–15 hours of playtime depending on clear speed and XP modifiers.
Gem level 20 to 21: Gems do NOT level past 20 naturally. Level 21 can ONLY be obtained through Vaal corruption (+1 outcome) or direct drop from Vaal Temple/corrupted zones.
Weapon swap leveling: Gems socketed in your alternate weapon set (press X to swap) still gain experience from monster kills, even though the weapon is not active. This is the key technique for leveling your 23% quality backup gem while keeping your main gem at 20 for combat.
Experience boost sources: "% increased experience gain for socketed gems" gear (Fated Uniques, specific crafting mods), party play (more monsters), Delve (high density), Breach/Legion encounters (many monsters in one spot).
Pro tip: Level ALL your support gems to 20 simultaneously by filling every unused socket with a gem. Even gems you don't use can be leveled to 20 and sold (level 20 gems have trade value) or used in the quality vendor recipe.

● Vaal Temple Map Access

What it is: A special corrupted map area containing only Vaal-themed monsters and guaranteed corrupted item drops, including level 21 gems.
How to access: Corrupt a regular Temple Map with a Vaal Orb (25% chance to become Vaal Temple). OR purchase Vaal Temple directly from trade (2–5 chaos).
Difficulty: High. Equivalent to Tier 15+ map with additional Vaal monster modifiers. Requires 75%+ resistances and 3,000+ life.
Drop table: 3–8 corrupted gems per clear, with ~3–8% chance for any specific gem to be level 21. About 1 level 21 gem per 3–5 Vaal Temple runs on average.
Pro tip: Run Vaal Temple with a "Gems" loot filter to highlight drops. The gem drops are corrupted, so they cannot be further modified, but a level 21/20 corrupted gem is still better than a level 20/20 uncorrupted gem for most builds.

5 注意事项

The painful lessons and hidden mechanics of gem optimization.

⚠ 腐化唯一副本是灾难性的

绝不要腐化你正在使用的宝石的唯一副本。腐化是不可逆的，有50%几率使宝石更糟（25% -1等级，25%无变化但腐化标签阻止进一步修改）。始终将备份宝石练到20/20后再尝试腐化。你的主宝石保持装备和功能正常，而备份承担50/50的赌博。如果你腐化了唯一的闪电箭宝石并且它降到19级，你刚刚损失了10+小时的练级时间和你BD的主要伤害来源。

⚠ 瓦尔技能石替换原始技能

当瓦尔石将宝石转换为其瓦尔变体（例如闪电箭→瓦尔闪电箭）时，原始技能被完全替换。瓦尔版本是一个完全不同的技能，有不同的机制、灵魂获取要求和使用模式。这种结果很少见（对于有瓦尔版本的宝石约0-1%），但如果发生在你的主技能上，你可能需要重组整个BD。只腐化有可接受的瓦尔变体的宝石，或接受风险。

⚠ 品质上限为23%

最大宝石品质为23%。这只能通过商人配方实现（20级20%品质宝石 + 1 GCP = 1级23%品质宝石）。直接用GCP提升品质上限为20%。如果你用GCP将宝石品质提升到20%，然后使用商人配方，你会得到23%。如果你尝试直接在20%宝石上使用更多GCP，什么也不会发生。许多新玩家浪费GCP试图超过20%却不知道该配方存在。

⚠ 掉落的21级技能石是腐化的

从瓦尔神庙或腐化区域掉落的任何21级宝石都会自动腐化。这意味着它不能被进一步修改——没有品质变化、没有等级变化、什么都没有。掉落的21/20宝石不能被改善到21/23。相反，你练到20/23然后腐化到21/23的宝石也是腐化的，但起始品质更高。理论上"完美"的21/23宝石必须通过以下方式创建：练到20级→品质到20%→商人配方到23%→重新练到20级→腐化+1等级（25%几率）。这就是为什么真正的21/23宝石如此稀有和珍贵。

⚠ 技能石在你等级上限停止升级

宝石不能超过你的角色等级。如果你是85级，你的宝石即使有足够经验也不能超过85级。由于20级宝石需要约119M XP（大致相当于角色70-75级的XP），这很少是20级阈值的问题。但如果你同时练多个宝石，其中一个落后了，可能是因为你在那段时间升级缓慢。宝石基于怪物击杀获得XP，不是固定速率。

⚠ 部分技能石不受等级影响

并非所有宝石都随宝石等级显著缩放。实用宝石如"受伤时施放"、"触发宝石"和大多数光环宝石具有随等级几乎不改善或完全不改善的固定效果。对于这些宝石，品质是唯一重要的因素，即使这也是微不足道的。不要浪费瓦尔石试图让"受伤时施放"达到21级——触发阈值几乎不变化。将你的21/23努力集中在伤害宝石和主要辅助上。

⚠ 替代品质技能石（PoE 2新增）

PoE 2从特定赛季内容（主要是夺宝）引入了替代品质宝石。这些宝石具有与标准品质不同的品质效果。例如，标准的"增加暴击率"辅助每1%品质提供+%暴击率，而替代品质版本可能提供+%暴击倍率。对于某些BD，替代品质20%宝石可能优于标准23%宝石。在投入23%标准品质磨练之前，检查你的BD指南或Path of Building以确定替代品质是否值得追求。

⚠ 启蒙与增幅也是技能石类型

"启蒙"（减少被辅助宝石的法力保留）和"增幅"（增加被辅助主动宝石的品质）是只随宝石等级缩放的特殊辅助宝石。3级启蒙比2级多减少1%保留——对于光环堆叠者来说这是BD决定性的差异。这些宝石不能从商人处购买，必须通过掉落或腐化获得。它们极其昂贵（3级启蒙=2-5颗神圣石）。如果你的BD需要其中一个，优先购买它胜过合成武器或护甲升级。

6 额外收获

Everything else you gain while working toward 21/23 gems.

💰 Gemcutter's Prism Stockpile

Farming GCPs through the quality gem vendor recipe naturally accumulates a stockpile that far exceeds what you need for your own gems. A typical player ends up with 50–100 excess GCPs after completing their 21/23 set. These sell for 1–2 chaos each, representing 50–200 chaos in side income. Alternatively, save them for future characters or to quality gems you intend to sell (a 20% quality level 20 gem sells for 2–5x the price of a 0% quality one on the trade site).

🔍 Vaal Orb Accumulation

Running Vaal side areas and Vaal Temple for gem drops also generates a steady supply of Vaal Orbs. These are needed for map corruptions, item corruptions for implicits, and Vaal Temple map creation. Most players who pursue 21/23 gems end the league with 50–100 excess Vaal Orbs, worth 1–2 chaos each. The gem farm funds your entire endgame corruption pipeline.

⚡ Alternate Weapon Swap Awareness

The technique of leveling gems in your alternate weapon swap is one of the most powerful quality-of-life tricks in PoE. Once you develop the habit, you will always have a full set of gems passively leveling in the background. This means every character you make benefits from instant level 20 gems (level them while playing your main). It also means you can stockpile leveled gems to sell or use for future builds without any dedicated farming time.

📚 Gem Synergy Knowledge

The process of optimizing every gem in your six-link forces you to understand how gems interact. You learn which support gems have the highest damage multipliers, which have hidden penalties (like "Concentrated Effect" reducing area), and how quality affects each differently. This knowledge is invaluable for build creation, gear evaluation, and helping other players. A PoE player who understands gem mechanics at this level can create viable builds from scratch rather than copying guides.

🎯 Level 20 Gem Sales

While leveling your main gems to 21/23, you will inevitably level many other gems to 20 in your spare sockets. These level 20 gems sell for 5–20 chaos each on the trade site (depending on gem popularity). A popular support like "Increased Area of Effect" at level 20 sells for 15–25 chaos. This "gem farming" side hustle can fund your entire build progression. Just equip popular gems in every empty socket and vendor the level 20 copies.
