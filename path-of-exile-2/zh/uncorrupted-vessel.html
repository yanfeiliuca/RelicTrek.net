1 为什么需要这个物品

🔶

未腐化6词容器

★★★★★ 终局内容
珠宝 6条词缀 T17地图

The Uncorrupted 6-Mod Vessel represents the pinnacle of jewel-based character customization in Path of Exile 2. Vessels are a new jewel subtype introduced in PoE 2's endgame system, replacing traditional jewels with a more powerful and flexible modifier framework. Each Vessel can roll up to 6 explicit modifiers (compared to 4 on legacy jewels), providing enormous build-defining potential. The "Uncorrupted" designation means the item retains its ability to be further modified with currency — once corrupted, a Vessel becomes immutable and cannot have its modifiers changed, though it may gain a powerful corruption implicit. A perfect 6-mod Uncorrupted Vessel with synergistic affixes can provide more character power than an entire gear slot, effectively giving you a "seventh item" of raw stat density. These items only drop from Tier 17 (T17) maps, the highest non-Uber tier of endgame content, making them both extremely rare and extremely valuable. A well-rolled 6-mod Vessel for a popular build can trade for 10–50+ Divine Orbs depending on modifier synergy and roll quality.

★ 为什么它特殊

Vessels in PoE 2 are fundamentally different from PoE 1 jewels. They occupy the same passive tree socket slots but can roll up to 6 modifiers instead of 4, and their modifier pool includes build-defining stats not found on other gear: "X% increased effect of non-curse auras," "X% increased maximum life if you've dealt a critical strike recently," and "X% of physical damage converted to elemental damage." The 6-mod cap means a single Vessel can provide 50% or more of a specific build's required conversion or scaling, freeing up entire gear slots for defensive stats.

⚠ 容器上的前缀/后缀锁定

Like all rare items, Vessels have a maximum of 3 prefixes and 3 suffixes (total 6). When using Vessel Resonators to add mods, the new mod must fit in an available prefix or suffix slot. If you already have 3 prefixes, the next added mod MUST be a suffix, and vice versa. Use a PoE crafting tool to check your current prefix/suffix allocation before adding mods. A common mistake is filling all 3 prefix slots with mediocre mods, leaving no room for the high-value "% increased maximum life" or "% increased damage" prefixes that define a good vessel.

3 T17地图准备

Running Tier 17 maps efficiently requires specific character benchmarks and map preparation. Running them under-geared wastes currency and yields no vessels.

● Character Benchmarks for T17 Survival

Effective HP (EHP): Minimum 80,000 EHP for softcore, 150,000+ for hardcore. Calculate as: (Life + Energy Shield) × (1 + resistance overcap) × damage reduction layers.
Damage: 2–5 million DPS for comfortable clear, 10M+ for deathless bossing. Check with Path of Building.
Movement: 150%+ movement speed or reliable movement skill (Flame Dash, Leap Slam, Whirling Blades).
Recovery: Life leech cap at 20%+ or substantial life regeneration (500+/sec). Flasks must have "instant recovery on low life" or similar.
Immunities: Freeze immunity (flask or boot mod), Bleed immunity (flask or passive tree), Curse reduction (80%+ if running hex-aura maps).
Pro tip: Do not attempt T17 maps before you can comfortably farm T16 maps deathless. The damage spike from T16 to T17 is roughly 40% for monsters and bosses. If T16 feels risky, T17 will feel impossible.

● Map Investment Costs

Base T17 Map: 30–80 chaos depending on layout (open layouts like Tropical Island command premium; linear like Canyon are cheaper).
Alchemy Orb + Chaos spam: Roll for 80%+ quant, favorable mods (avoid "Monsters have X% increased area damage" and similar deadly combos). Budget 5–10 chaos per map for rolling.
Sextants (awakened): 3–8 chaos each, 4 used per map set = 12–32 chaos. Prioritize "additional monsters," "converted to X," or "contains guarded valuables."
Scarabs: 2–15 chaos each depending on type. Divination Scarabs, Breach Scarabs, and Ritual Scarabs are best for vessel farming.
Fragments: Sacrifice fragments or Maven/Sirus/Exarch/Eater invitations add 5–25% quantity each.
Total per-map investment: 60–150 chaos for a fully juiced T17 vessel farm.
Expected return: 1 vessel per 7–10 maps (12–15% drop rate), plus 50–100 chaos in other drops per map. ROI is positive after 5–8 maps with decent luck.

● Vessel Resonator Farming

Primary drop source: Tier 15+ map bosses (20% chance), Heist Thaumaturgic chests (15% chance per wing), Delve depth 250+ encounters (10% chance per node).
Drop rate per T17 map: 0.3–0.8 Resonators per full clear depending on content density and scarabs used.
Trade cost: 5–15 chaos each. If farming yourself, expect to invest 20–30 maps per 10 Resonators.
Usage efficiency: Each Resonator adds exactly 1 mod. To take a 3-mod vessel to 6 mods, you need 3 Resonators minimum (assuming you never brick by filling prefix/suffix slots poorly). Budget 4–5 Resonators per vessel due to suboptimal mod allocation.
Pro tip: Buy Resonators from the trade site in bulk (stacks of 20+) for 10–20% discount. Solo farming them is inefficient unless you are also running Heist or Delve for other objectives.

● Map Layouts That Maximize Vessel Drops

Best layouts (efficient clear, high monster count):
• Tropical Island — open, linear beach layout with natural pack density from jungle areas. 180–220 monsters base. Vessel drop rate feels highest here (community consensus).
• Canyon — narrow corridor, easy to full-clear with chain/projectile builds. 150–180 monsters. Low map cost makes it budget-friendly.
• Shore — compact open area with predictable boss location. 160–200 monsters. Good for speed farming.
• Promenade — indoor layout with many side rooms. 200–250 monsters. Boss has multiple phases (slower) but higher total drops.
Worst layouts: Labyrinthine (too spread out), Cells (backtracking), Phantasmagoria (dead ends). Avoid these even if the map mod rolls are good.
Pro tip: Sextant for "Map contains 3 additional Breaches" or "Map contains Ritual Altars" adds 50–100+ additional monsters per map, effectively increasing your vessel drop chance by 30–50%.

5 注意事项

The expensive mistakes, hidden mechanics, and veteran knowledge that separate lucky drops from consistent vessel farming.

⚠ 腐化不可逆

Using a Vaal Orb on a Vessel has a 25% chance to "brick" the item (turn it into a rare with random mods), a 50% chance to add a beneficial implicit while keeping existing mods, and a 25% chance to change nothing but add the "Corrupted" tag. Once corrupted, the item cannot be modified by any currency. Never corrupt a Vessel unless you are completely satisfied with its explicit modifiers and are willing to lose the item. The standard strategy is to get perfect 6-mod rolls with Divine Orbs FIRST, then corrupt as the final step.

⚠ 词缀等级比词缀数量更重要

A 6-mod Vessel with tier 5 rolls is often worse than a 4-mod Vessel with tier 1 rolls. Each modifier has tiers (tier 1 being the highest roll range). A "% increased maximum life" mod ranges from 4% (tier 7) to 12% (tier 1). A 6-mod vessel with six tier 5–7 mods provides less total power than a carefully crafted 4-mod vessel with four tier 1 mods. Always check the tier of each mod, not just the count. Use PoE's built-in item tier display (enable in UI options) or third-party tools.

⚠ 容器词缀可能冲突

Certain modifier combinations are mutually exclusive or counter-synergistic. For example, you cannot roll both "% increased physical damage" and "% increased elemental damage with attacks" as prefixes on the same vessel — they share a mod group. Similarly, "% reduced mana reservation" and "% increased aura effect" occupy different slots but a build typically only needs one. Plan your ideal modifier combination before crafting to avoid wasting Resonators on impossible rolls. Use craftofexile.com's Vessel section to check mod groups.

⚠ T17地图词缀组合可能毁掉刷图

Some T17 map modifier combinations are effectively unrunable for most builds. The deadly combos to avoid: "Players have -12% maximum resistances" + "Monsters deal 100% extra damage as fire" (insta-death from any hit), "Monsters have 100% increased area of effect" + "Monsters have 40% increased attack/cast speed" (unavoidable damage), "Players cannot leech life/mana" (kills leech-dependent builds entirely), "Monsters reflect X% of elemental damage" (kills most elemental builds unless they have reflect immunity). Always read the full mod list before running a T17. Vaaling a map can add deadly mods — only vaal maps you have already read and deemed safe.

⚠ 容器背包空间问题

Vessels are 1×1 items (like jewels) but they drop frequently enough to clog inventory during mapping. Set up a dedicated "Vessels" quad tab in your stash (premium tab recommended for color-coding). Without organization, you will end up with dozens of unID vessels scattered across multiple tabs, making it impossible to evaluate your collection. Sort by mod count (3-mod in one corner, 4-mod in another, etc.) and review your stockpile at the end of each session.

⚠ 交易区 vs 独狼区容器策略

In trade leagues, the most efficient path is often to farm generic currency in T16 maps and BUY 5–6 mod vessels from other players, then Divine them to perfect rolls. Self-farming T17 maps specifically for vessels costs 60–150 chaos per map and may not be profitable unless your build can run them in under 10 minutes deathless. In SSF, you have no choice but to self-farm, so invest heavily in Atlas passives for vessel drop chance and run the most efficient layouts. SSF players should also save every 3–4 mod vessel instead of vendoring them — even a mediocre vessel is better than an empty socket.

⚠ 容器固定词缀与随机词缀

Vessels come with an implicit modifier (like all jewels) that ranges in value. The implicit can be "% increased damage," "+X to maximum life," or other stats. This implicit is rolled when the item drops and can be rerolled with Blessed Orbs. A tier 1 implicit adds 2–4% more power than a tier 5 implicit. Always check your implicit tier and use Blessed Orbs (cheap, 1:1 with chromatics) to maximize it before investing expensive Resonators or Ancient Shards. The implicit cannot be changed by any other means except Blessed Orbs.

⚠ 赛季初期容器冲刺

In the first week of a new league, Vessel Resonators and 5-mod vessels command extreme premiums (2–3x their normal price). If you are among the first players reaching T17 maps, you can fund your entire endgame build by selling vessels and Resonators instead of using them. Conversely, if you are not in the first wave, wait until week 2–3 when prices normalize before purchasing vessels for your build. The early-league vessel market is one of the most profitable flip opportunities for dedicated racers.

6 额外收获

Everything else you gain while farming for 6-mod uncorrupted vessels in Tier 17 content.

💰 Atlas Completion & Maven Witnessing

Running T17 maps completes the highest tier of Atlas bonuses and witnesses map bosses for Maven's invitations. By the time you farm 20+ T17 maps, you will have enough witnessed bosses for multiple Maven's Crucible runs, which drop Maven's Orbs (2–5 Divine Orbs value each), Elevated Sextants, and exclusive unique items. The vessel farm directly funds your Atlas progression with zero extra effort.

🔍 Pinnacle Boss Access

Sustained T17 mapping generates Pinnacle boss sets (Sirus, Maven, Exarch, Eater of Worlds, Uber Elder). Each Pinnacle kill drops 1–3 Divine Orbs worth of loot plus exclusive uniques and fragments. Many T17 vessel farmers report that Pinnacle boss drops exceed vessel profits in raw currency, making vessels a bonus rather than the primary income. The discipline of running high-tier maps naturally progresses you toward the most profitable content in the game.

⚡ Build Knowledge & Mechanical Skill

Tier 17 maps demand precise play. You will learn to read dangerous modifier combinations, position for boss mechanics, manage flask timing under pressure, and optimize your damage rotation. These skills are non-negotiable for Uber bossing and HC survival. Players who farm T17 content regularly develop game sense that translates to every other activity in PoE 2, from Heist to Delve to Pinnacle bossing.

📚 League Mechanic Mastery

Juiced T17 maps incorporate multiple league mechanics simultaneously (Breach + Legion + Ritual + Delirium). Managing all of these at once while maintaining clear speed is a complex skill. Vessel farmers naturally become experts at every league mechanic because they encounter them constantly. You will learn optimal Ritual deferral strategies, Breach stone collection efficiency, Legion splinter farming patterns, and Delirium reward optimization — knowledge that pays dividends across your entire PoE career.

🎯 Stash Tab Wealth Accumulation

Full-juice T17 maps generate enormous volumes of loot: raw currency, divination cards, fragments, fossils, resonators, unique items, influenced bases, and scarabs. Even without a single vessel drop, a session of 5 T17 maps typically yields 300–600 chaos in sellable loot. Vessel farming is one of the highest raw-currency-per-hour activities in the game. Your vessel drops are essentially a bonus on top of already-profitable mapping.

Divine Orb Farming

★★★★☆ Endgame

Divine Orbs reroll the numeric values on all modifiers of an item. A 6-mod Vessel with tier 1 mods but low rolls can gain 30–50% more effectiveness from Divine Orb optimization. Farm Divine Orbs from the 6-mod rare vendor recipe or boss drops, then apply them to push your vessel from good to mirror-tier.
