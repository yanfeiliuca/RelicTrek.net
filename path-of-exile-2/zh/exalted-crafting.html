崇高石合成 当前

元合成系统 | 通货合成 | 难度：★★★★☆

一、概览

崇高石是流放之路2复杂且富有回报的合成系统的基石。这种闪耀的金色通货物品，在稀有物品上使用时，会从该物品类型的可用词缀池中添加一个新的随机词缀。与改造石重掷或精华确定性合成等更简单的合成方法不同，崇高石引入了真正的随机性——它落在物品上的词缀可能是改变游戏规则的一阶词缀，完成你的完美BD，也可能是一个几乎无用的低阶属性，实际上毁掉了这件装备。这种高风险高回报的机制是流放之路2终局物品进阶的核心，也是整个元合成系统建立的基础。

元合成——策略性地使用工作台配方，结合崇高石、剥离石、神圣石和其他通货物品——代表了流放之路2中玩家驱动物品创造的巅峰。通过谨慎应用"前缀不可变更"和"后缀不可变更"工作台合成，结合针对性的崇高石打击、剥离石赌博和庄园重铸机制，经验丰富的合成师可以系统地将物品推向统计完美。一件元合成物品可能经历20-50个单独的合成步骤，消耗数十颗崇高石、数百颗改造石和数千个低级通货，才能产生一个可以在交易市场上获得高价的结果。

崇高石合成的重要性远远超出了单个玩家装备升级的范围。崇高石是流放之路2交易生态系统中连接休闲玩家和硬核玩家的主要经济桥梁。通过自然刷图获得崇高石的休闲玩家可以将它们出售给消耗崇高石的硬核合成师。这种通货流动维持了整个合成经济，崇高石在交易市场上每小时易手数千次，因为玩家购买、出售和消费它们用于他们的合成项目。

在流放之路2中，合成系统经过精炼和扩展，增加了新的影响词缀、额外的工作台配方和重新设计的合成材料需求。虽然在PoE 1中建立的基本原则保持不变，但具体的词缀池、物品等级要求和最佳策略已经演变。了解崇高石合成的当前状态——包括哪些词缀在哪些物品等级可用、前缀和后缀锁定机制如何运作，以及何时崇高石打击与何时使用确定性替代方案——对于任何希望最大化角色装备潜力或通过合成市场创造财富的玩家来说都是必不可少的。

本指南涵盖了崇高石合成知识的全部范围，从每个新手都会问的"什么时候该用崇高石打击？"的基本问题，到用于创造价值数百颗神圣石的镜阶物品的高级元合成序列。无论你是合成你的第一件终局稀有物品还是规划复杂的多步骤项目，这里概述的原则都将为你节省通货、减少挫败感并改善你的合成结果。

二、合成方法——崇高石系统

2.1 崇高石——基础功能

当崇高石用于一件词缀少于6个的稀有物品时（最多3个前缀和3个后缀），它会从物品可用的词缀池中添加一个随机词缀。该词缀从所有符合条件的前缀或后缀中随机选择，按它们的阶概率和物品等级限制加权。添加的词缀不能是物品上已有的词缀（不重复），且必须来自未满的词缀池（如果所有3个前缀位都满了，只能添加后缀，反之亦然）。

2.2 元合成：前缀/后缀不可变更

The crafting bench in PoE 2 offers two critical meta-crafting recipes:

  • "Prefixes Cannot Be Changed" — Costs 2 Exalted Orbs (PoE 1 cost, subject to change in PoE 2). Protects all existing prefixes from modification during subsequent crafting actions.
  • "Suffixes Cannot Be Changed" — Costs 2 Exalted Orbs. Protects all existing suffixes from modification during subsequent crafting actions.

这两个配方，当与崇高石和剥离石结合使用时，构成了元合成的基础。一般原则是：

  1. Craft or roll your desired prefix(es) onto the item
  2. Apply "Prefixes Cannot Be Changed" from the bench
  3. Use Orb of Scouring (removes all mods except protected prefixes) or craft/annul suffixes
  4. Exalt slam for new suffixes, knowing prefixes are safe
  5. Repeat the process for prefixes if needed

2.3 增加工艺（庄园）

In Path of Exile 2, the Harvest crafting system has been redesigned but retains the powerful "Augment" craft:

  • Augment Physical: Adds a guaranteed physical modifier
  • Augment Fire/Cold/Lightning: Adds a guaranteed elemental modifier of the chosen type
  • Augment Life: Adds a guaranteed life modifier
  • Augment Critical: Adds a guaranteed critical modifier
  • Augment Speed: Adds a guaranteed attack/cast speed modifier

这些合成在功能上类似于崇高石，但保证特定的词缀类型。它们比崇高石贵得多，也更难获得，使崇高石成为一般合成的主力，而增幅合成则保留用于特定的最后润色。

2.4 多步元合成序列

A typical meta-crafting project for a high-end rare item follows this sequence:

  1. Base Selection: Acquire an item level 86+ influenced base with the correct implicit
  2. Essence Spam: Use Deafening Essences to force a tier-1 prefix (e.g., Deafening Essence of Greed for +flat life)
  3. Bench Lock: Apply "Prefixes Cannot Be Changed" (2 Exalted Orbs)
  4. Scour Suffixes: Use Orb of Scouring to wipe suffixes, preserving locked prefixes
  5. Exalt Slam #1: Add first suffix (e.g., resistance). If bad, Annul and retry
  6. Exalt Slam #2: Add second suffix (e.g., another resistance or stat)
  7. Bench Craft: Craft remaining suffix from bench (e.g., missing resistance)
  8. Divine Orb: Roll values on all modifiers to high tiers
  9. Lock and Re-craft: If Divine rolls poorly on a critical mod, lock and Divine again
  10. Quality: Apply Perfect Fossil or Hillock bench for 30% quality

这个序列的成本从"足够好"的10颗崇高石到真正的镜阶物品的200+崇高石不等。知道何时停止——认识到"足够好"通常比追求完美更好——是合成中最重要的技能之一。

三、材料与掉落来源

3.1 崇高石——主要合成通货

Exalted Orb — Currency | Stack Size: 20 (10 in Currency Tab) | Adds random modifier to rare item

崇高石可以从任何能掉落通货物品的怪物、宝箱或容器中掉落。掉率很低，但明显高于神圣石或魔镜。在物品数量良好的T16地图中，普通玩家大约每2-5小时刷图可以获得1颗崇高石。更高的清图速度、更多加成的地图和组队游戏可以大幅提高这个比率。

3.2 核心合成材料

Orb of Annulment — Removes one random modifier from a rare item. Essential for meta-crafting to remove bad mods.
Divine Orb — Rerolls the numerical values of all explicit modifiers on an item. Used after Exalt slams to perfect modifier tiers.
Orb of Scouring — Removes all modifiers from an item, downgrading it to normal. Used with bench locks to wipe undesired mods.
Regal Orb — Upgrades a magic item to rare, adding one random modifier. Used as the bridge between Alteration spam and Exalt slamming.
Orb of Alteration — Rerolls modifiers on a magic item. Used in bulk (100-1000) for initial mod rolling before Regaling and Exalting.
Orb of Transmutation — Upgrades a normal item to magic. The starting point for most crafting sequences.

3.4 崇高石掉落来源与掉率

Understanding where Exalted Orbs come from helps with both acquisition and pricing:

  • Monster Drops (Tier 16 maps): ~1 Exalted per 500-2000 map kills depending on quantity mods
  • Currency Chests (Heist): Currency-focused Heist contracts have elevated Exalted drop rates
  • Expedition Currency Explosions: Logbook remnants with "(20-40)% increased Currency item drops" can yield multiple Exalts
  • Divination Cards: "The Hoarder" (1 Exalted, 50 cards), "The Saint's Treasure" (2 Exalted, 10 cards), "The Thief" (1 Exalted, 20 cards)
  • Vendor Recipe: Sell a full set of shaped or elder items (ilvl 75+) for 2 Exalted Orbs
  • Map Boss Drops: Tier 16 map bosses have elevated currency drop rates
  • League Mechanics: Ritual, Delirium, and Harbinger encounters frequently reward Exalted Orbs

3.5 崇高碎片配方

一套完整的已鉴定稀有物品（每个装备槽位各一件：头盔、胸甲、手套、靴子、腰带、护身符、两个戒指、武器/盾牌），总物品稀有度75%+，可以出售给商人换取2个崇高碎片。20个崇高碎片合成为1颗崇高石。虽然速度慢，但这个确定性配方为专注的玩家提供了可靠（如果乏味）的崇高石来源。

四、合成路线——分步项目

本节为常见终局物品提供具体的合成路线。每条路线包括材料需求、预期成本和分步说明。

4.1 路线：合成+3召唤头盔

Step 1: Acquire an ilvl 86+ Bone Helmet (base minion damage implicit). Ensure it is uncorrupted and not already crafted.
Step 2: Apply Orb of Scouring if needed to make it a normal item. Use an Orb of Transmutation to make it magic.
Step 3: Spam Deafening Essence of Dread until you hit "+2 to Level of Socketed Minion Gems" (this is the Essence mod). This may take 20-50 Essences depending on luck.
Step 4: Use a Regal Orb to upgrade to rare and add one random modifier. If the Regal hits a bad mod, you can Annul it or start over with a new base.
Step 5: Apply "Suffixes Cannot Be Changed" from the crafting bench (costs 2 Exalted Orbs).
Step 6: Use Orb of Scouring to remove all prefixes. Your suffixes (including the +2 minion gems) are protected.
Step 7: Bench craft "Can have up to 3 Crafted Modifiers" (2 Exalted Orbs) + "+1 to Level of Socketed Support Gems" (1 Exalted Orb) + "Minions deal (20-30)% increased Damage" (1 Divine Orb equivalent).
Step 8: Alternatively, Exalt slam for natural prefixes instead of bench crafting, then finish with a single bench mod for the missing stat.
Step 9: Use Divine Orbs to roll high values on all modifiers. Target tier-1 rolls on life and resistance values.
Step 10: Hillock craft in Transportation Safehouse for 28-30% quality, then apply Armourer's Scraps to reach 30%.

4.2 路线：合成物理武器（日蚀长杖/精美之刃）

Step 1: Acquire an ilvl 83+ influenced base (Crusader, Warlord, or Hunter depending on desired influence mods). Ensure 30% quality via Perfect Fossil crafting.
Step 2: Use Aberrant + Jagged Fossils in a Resonator to roll flat physical damage and attack speed. Target: tier-1 "% increased Physical Damage" + tier-1 "Adds X to Y Physical Damage" + open suffix for "+% to Critical Strike Chance."
Step 3: If you hit 2 good prefixes and 1 bad suffix, apply "Suffixes Cannot Be Changed" and Scour the bad prefix.
Step 4: Exalt slam for a suffix. Desired outcomes: "+% to Critical Strike Multiplier," "+% to Global Critical Strike Chance," or "+X to Strength/Dexterity." If you hit a bad suffix, Annul and retry.
Step 5: Bench craft the missing modifier — typically "(16-20)% increased Attack Speed" if not already present as a natural roll.
Step 6: Divine Orb for perfect values: 170%+ increased physical damage, maximum flat phys, high crit chance/multi.
Step 7: Link to 6-sockets using Jeweller's Orbs, then 6-link using Orb of Fusing. Average cost: 150-350 Fusings.
Step 8: Apply "(20-30)% increased Quality" via Hillock bench if not already at 30%.

4.3 路线：合成施法者项链

Step 1: Acquire an ilvl 82+ Agate Amulet (all-attributes base) or Turquoise Amulet (dex/int base). Shaper or Hunter influence preferred.
Step 2: Alt-Regal method: Spam Orbs of Alteration until you hit tier-1 "+1 to Level of All Skill Gems" or "+1 to Level of All [Element] Skill Gems" as a prefix.
Step 3: Regal Orb to add a suffix. If the Regal hits a good suffix (crit multi, life, resistance), proceed. If bad, consider Annuling or restarting.
Step 4: Apply "Prefixes Cannot Be Changed" bench craft (2 Exalted Orbs).
Step 5: Scour off all suffixes, preserving your +1 gems prefix.
Step 6: Exalt slam for suffix #1 (crit multi preferred). If good, proceed. If bad, Annul and retry (protected prefixes are safe from Annul).
Step 7: Exalt slam for suffix #2 (life or resistance). Bench craft the remaining suffix slot.
Step 8: Divine Orb for perfect gem level rolls and high-tier suffix values.

五、注意事项与常见合成错误

⚠ 陷阱#1：剥离石赌博

剥离石从稀有物品上移除一个随机词缀。当你有3个好词缀和1个坏词缀时，剥离石有25%几率移除坏的——75%几率移除好的。许多合成师因为一连串糟糕的剥离运气而失去了整个项目。始终要考虑，用新基底重新开始是否比反复剥离和重新崇高石打击更便宜。沉没成本谬误毁掉的合成项目比坏RNG还多。

⚠ 陷阱#2：物品等级要求

每个词缀都有物品等级(ilvl)要求——基底物品获得该词缀的最低物品等级。例如，T1的"+99最大生命"需要ilvl 86。如果你在一件ilvl 82的胸甲上使用崇高石打击，T1生命词缀不可能掷出，无论你的运气多好。在开始昂贵的合成之前，始终验证基底物品等级。查看流放之路维基或Craft of Exile工具以获取目标词缀的确切ilvl阈值。

⚠ 陷阱#3：影响词缀冲突

不同的物品影响（塑界者、长老、圣战者、猎人、救赎者、军阀）有不同的词缀池。某些词缀是特定影响专属的。你不可能在猎人影响物品上掷出圣战者词缀。在规划合成时，验证所有目标词缀是否能在同一种影响类型上共存。一些镜阶物品需要觉醒者石组合来将两种不同的影响词缀池合并到一件物品上。

⚠ 陷阱#4：工作台合成覆盖

当你在工作台上合成一个词缀（例如"+30最大生命"）时，它会占用一个词缀位。如果你之后崇高石打击并自然命中了相同类型的词缀，它无法落在物品上，因为工作台合成版本已经占据了该槽位。但是，你可以移除工作台合成，该槽位就会重新开放。一些合成师故意工作台合成"占位"词缀来阻止坏的崇高石结果，然后移除占位并再次崇高石打击——尽管这是一种昂贵的技巧。

⚠ 陷阱#5："赌狗崇高"陷阱

新手玩家经常在穿着的任何稀有物品上使用他们的第一颗崇高石，希望获得幸运的升级。这几乎总是浪费。一颗崇高石用在 mediocre 的稀有物品上，有90%+的几率添加一个低阶不相关的词缀。将你的崇高石保存用于针对高质量基底的元合成，或者出售换取混沌石来直接购买更好的装备。唯一的例外是，如果你有一件真正的好物品，有一个开放词缀位，其中多个可能的崇高石结果都是可接受的。

⚠ 陷阱#6：不使用流放合成模拟器

在向真实的合成项目投入昂贵通货之前，始终要在Craft of Exile (craftofexile.com)上进行模拟。这个免费的网页工具模拟数千次合成尝试，向你展示平均成本、成功率和目标结果的概率分布。许多在纸上看起来"简单"的合成实际上只有5%的成功率，平均成本50+崇高石。模拟可以避免灾难性的合成决策。

⚠ 陷阱#7：合成前先打品质

物品品质会在词缀应用之前影响基底物品的防御或攻击值。如果你在所有词缀合成之后通过完美化石或Hillock工作台添加30%品质，品质只影响基础值——不影响合成词缀。为了达到最大效果，在掷词缀之前将物品品质提升到28-30%，特别是在护甲上，品质会影响词缀缩放的基础护甲/闪避/能量护盾值。

六、免费福利与进阶知识

6.1 合成作为通货生成方法

Beyond personal gear upgrades, crafting is one of the most powerful wealth-generation methods in Path of Exile 2:

  • Crafting for Profit: Buy bases cheaply, craft with Essences + bench, sell completed items at 2-5x material cost. The key is crafting items that many builds want (life + resist gear, +1 amulets, minion helmets).
  • Meta-Crafting Services: Offer your Exalted Orbs and crafting knowledge as a service. Players bring you their nearly-perfect items and pay a fee for the final Exalt slams and Divine Orb rolling.
  • Bulk Crafting: Buy 10-20 similar bases (e.g., ilvl 84 Stygian Vises), Essence craft them all, and sell the best outcomes. This averages out RNG across multiple attempts.
  • Harvest Farming: Dedicated Harvest farmers sell Augment and Reforge crafts to meta-crafters for 1-5 Divine Orbs per craft. This requires deep atlas investment in Harvest spawn rate.

6.2 流放合成工具——必备资源

Craft of Exile (craftofexile.com) is the single most valuable external tool for PoE 2 crafting. Its features include:

  • Crafting Simulator: Run 10,000 simulated crafting attempts to estimate average costs
  • Mod Database: Search all available modifiers by item type, influence, and item level
  • Emulator: Practice crafting sequences without spending real currency
  • Price Checker: Integration with trade sites to estimate the value of crafted items

Using this tool before any Exalted Orb investment of 5+ Exalts is non-negotiable for serious crafters.

6.3 流放之路2相比流放之路1的合成变化

Path of Exile 2 introduces several changes that affect Exalted Orb crafting:

  • New Modifiers: PoE 2 adds new modifier types for the redesigned gem system and new Ascendancy classes
  • Item Level Changes: Some tier thresholds have been adjusted for the new campaign structure
  • Bench Recipe Updates: Several crafting bench recipes have new costs and unlock conditions
  • Socket System: The new socket system (independent of item links) removes the need to craft links, but quality still affects base values
  • Influence System: Influenced items work differently in PoE 2 — some old influence combos are no longer possible
  • Currency Stack Sizes: Increased stack sizes for Exalted Orbs (20) and Divine Orbs (50) in currency tabs

6.4 崇高石打击的"三法则"

在决定是否对物品使用崇高石打击时，应用"三法则"：只有至少3种可能的结果是可接受的改进时才崇高石打击。如果你的物品有一个开放后缀位，40+个可能后缀中只有2个是理想的，不要崇高石——几率太糟糕了。如果10+个结果都是可接受的（例如，任何抗性都可以），崇高石就成为合理的赌博。这个简单的启发式方法可以避免绝大多数糟糕的崇高石决策。

6.5 何时停止合成

最重要的合成技能是知道何时停止。在开始任何合成之前设定预算，并严格遵守。经验丰富的合成师的常见规则：

  • Budget crafts: Set limit at 5 Exalted Orbs. If not done by then, sell the半成品 (half-finished item) and start over.
  • Mid-tier crafts: Set limit at 20 Exalted Orbs. Chasing perfection beyond this point usually costs more than buying the finished item.
  • Mirror-tier crafts: Set limit at 100 Exalted Orbs per crafting session. Walk away and return with fresh perspective if you hit the limit.

盈利合成师和通货耗尽者之间的区别通常是纪律，而不是运气。流放之路中最好的合成师是那些知道何时放弃并重新开始的人。

6.7 合成精通总结
