Minecraft

Over 300 million copies sold. 700+ craftable items. 10 game-changing builds every serious player needs. Every ore vein, every dungeon, every hidden mechanic — nothing missed.

Beacon

★★★★★ Endgame

Speed, Haste, Resistance, Jump Boost, Strength. Requires defeating the Wither and 164 mineral blocks. The ultimate flex.

Netherite Armor Set

★★★★☆ Late Game

Best armor in the game. Mine Ancient Debris at Y=-59 in the Nether, smelt with gold, upgrade diamond gear at Smithing Table.

Conduit

★★★★☆ Mid-Late Game

Underwater breathing, clear vision, and attacks hostile mobs. Requires a Heart of the Sea from buried treasure + 8 Nautilus Shells.

Enchanting Table + 15 Bookshelves

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

Level 30 enchantments — Fortune III, Efficiency V, Protection IV. Needs diamonds, obsidian, and a cow farm for 45 leather.

Ender Chest

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

Portable cross-dimensional storage. 8 obsidian + 1 Eye of Ender. Access your items from anywhere in any dimension.

Slow Falling Potion

★★★★☆ Late Game

Essential for Elytra flight safety. Requires Phantom Membrane — only drops if you DON'T sleep for 3+ days.

Anvil

★★☆☆☆ Early-Mid Game

31 iron ingots for repairing, renaming, and combining enchanted items. Surprisingly expensive early on.

Powered Rail System

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

Gold-powered booster rails for minecart transport. Each rail needs 6 gold + stick + redstone torch. Build a full transport network.

Eye of Ender

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

Required to find and activate the End Portal. Blaze Powder + Ender Pearl. The gateway to the Ender Dragon and Elytra.

Firework Rocket (Flight 3)

★★☆☆☆ Mid Game

Elytra fuel for sustained flight. Paper + 3 Gunpowder = 3-second flight. Build a creeper farm for unlimited gunpowder.
