Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

10 essential fused weapons, armors, and upgrades. Every dragon-tear location, every diamond, every hidden shortcut across Hyrule, Sky, and Depths.

Master Sword

★★★★★ Late Game

The legendary blade of evil's bane. Restore it to full power across sky, surface, and depths. 12 Dragon's Tears across all three layers.

Hylian Shield

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

90 defense, the strongest shield in the game. Hidden in Hyrule Castle Docks or purchased for a hefty price after a long quest chain.

Lightscale Trident

★★★★☆ Mid Game

Mipha's legacy weapon. Requires a Zora Spear, Diamond, and Flint — all the way to Zora's Domain. 22 base attack + water warrior bonus.

Scimitar of the Seven

★★★★☆ Mid Game

Urbosa's signature blade. Requires Gerudo Scimitar + Diamond + Flint in Gerudo Town. 32 attack with lightning flash charge.

Great Eagle Bow

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

Revali's weapon of choice. Requires Swallow Bow + Diamond + Wood bundle at Rito Village. Triple-shot with 28x3 damage.

Boulder Breaker

★★★★☆ Mid Game

Daruk's signature crusher. Requires Cobble Crusher + Diamond + Flint at Goron City. 36 attack, breaks ore in one hit.

Fierce Deity Set

★★★★★ Late Game

Attack Up x3, the strongest combat armor. Requires completing the entire Majora's Mask Easter egg quest line across multiple regions.

Barbarian Armor Set

★★★★☆ Mid-Late Game

Attack Up per piece, found in three Labyrinths scattered across Hyrule. Each is a massive maze with hidden underground entrances.

Champion's Leathers

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

5 defense + Master Sword beam boost. Requires finding the well-hidden tailor and collecting rare materials across multiple regions.

Radiant Armor Set

★★★☆☆ Early-Mid Game

Glow-in-the-dark bone armor with attack bonuses at night. Requires collecting Poes from the Depths and finding the mysterious stylist.
