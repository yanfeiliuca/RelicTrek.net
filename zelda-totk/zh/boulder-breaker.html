1 为什么这件物品重要

The 碎岩巨剑 is the undisputed king of raw two-handed power in 塞尔达传说：王国之泪. With a staggering 36 base attack, it outclasses every other 英杰 weapon in terms of sheer damage per swing. If the 大鹫弓 is the ultimate ranged weapon, the 碎岩巨剑 is its melee counterpart, delivering devastating blows that turn 莱尼尔s, 瘴气 Spawn, and dungeon bosses into paste.

What truly sets the 碎岩巨剑 apart from other two-handed weapons is its unique ability to destroy ore deposits and weak rocks in a single hit. No other weapon in the game — not even other two-handers of comparable attack power — offers this level of mining efficiency. Normally, cracking open ore deposits takes multiple hits and wears down your weapon's durability. With the 碎岩巨剑, one swing is all it takes. This translates to massive time savings when farming for 钻石s, Rubies, 蓝宝石s, and other gems, especially in the 地底 where ore deposits are plentiful but enemies are relentless. You won't need to carry a separate mining tool anymore — the 碎岩巨剑 is your weapon and your pickaxe rolled into one devastating package.

耐久度 is another major selling point. 双手 weapons typically have lower durability counts due to their high damage output, but the 碎岩巨剑 is engineered to last. At 60 durability points, it can withstand prolonged combat encounters and extensive mining sessions before needing a reforge. When you factor in the 余料建造 system — attaching a high-damage material like a 白银莱尼尔 Hoof (+55 attack) or a Mighty 烟雾蘑菇 — the 碎岩巨剑's effective damage climbs into the triple digits. A fused 碎岩巨剑 with endgame materials can reach 90+ attack per swing, making it one of the highest DPS weapons in the entire game.

Let's compare the 碎岩巨剑 to other heavy hitters. The Royal Claymore (base 36 attack) matches the 碎岩巨剑's damage but lacks the ore-breaking passive ability. The Mighty 莱尼尔 Crusher can technically reach higher attack numbers with 余料建造, but 莱尼尔 weapons are notoriously fragile and difficult to farm consistently. The 鬼神大剑 is faster but requires a full armor set bonus to truly shine. None of these alternatives offer the 碎岩巨剑's perfect storm of high base damage, durability, mining utility, and the ability to be reforged infinitely. Once you've experienced the convenience of one-shotting every ore deposit you see while wielding a weapon that also obliterates 白银莱尼尔s, going back to anything else feels like a downgrade.

From a progression standpoint, the 碎岩巨剑 arrives at the perfect time. By the time you've completed the 阿沅 questline and reached 鼓隆城, you're firmly in the mid-game and facing tougher enemies that require more powerful gear. The 碎岩巨剑 represents a significant power spike that carries you comfortably into the late game. It's the kind of weapon that becomes a permanent fixture in your inventory — a reliable, reforgeable powerhouse that you can always count on when the situation demands overwhelming force. Whether you're clearing 瘴气-infested chasms in the 地底, farming 岩石巨人es for gems, or simply enjoying the cathartic satisfaction of pancaking 波克布林s into the dirt, the 碎岩巨剑 delivers every single time.

5 注意事项

The journey to forge the 碎岩巨剑 is one of the most treacherous in the game. Death Mountain doesn't forgive unprepared adventurers. Here's every trap, pitfall, and gotcha you need to know about:

🔥 EXTREME HEAT — 耐火石铠 Is Essentially 需要
  • The heat on Death Mountain inflicts continuous, rapid damage that will kill an unprepared player in seconds. This isn't the mild warmth of lower 奥尔汀 — this is extreme heat that ignores regular heat-resistant food.
  • The Flamebreaker armor set (sold in 鼓隆城) provides complete immunity to extreme heat when wearing at least one piece. Two pieces provide additional defense bonuses. Each piece costs 600 rupees.
  • If you can't afford Flamebreaker armor, craft 防火 药剂 using 防火 Lizards or Smotherwing Butterflies. You'll need multiple elixirs for the full trip — one lasts about 6 minutes and 30 seconds.
  • Equipping a 蓝宝石-fused weapon or wearing the 蓝宝石 circlet provides heat resistance but NOT fireproofing. Do not confuse heat resistance with fire immunity — they are different mechanics, and fireproofing is what you need on Death Mountain.
🛤️ Broken Rail Sections — Bring 左纳乌 Devices
  • The mine cart rail system that leads up Death Mountain is severely damaged in 王国之泪. Multiple sections have gaps, broken supports, or missing carts entirely.
  • Bring Fan 左纳乌 Devices and Cart 左纳乌 Devices in your inventory. You'll need to 究极手assemble working carts on broken sections, attach Fans for propulsion, and sometimes build bridges using available materials.
  • Alternatively, skip the rails entirely and use a combination of climbing, 通天术, and 力巴尔's Gale (if unlocked) to scale the mountain manually. This is often faster and less frustrating than wrestling with the rail system.
  • Save your game before attempting difficult cart segments — falling into lava means instant death and a long walk back.
🦎 Fire Like-Lizards and Flamepees Are Deadly
  • Death Mountain is home to some of the most dangerous enemy variants in the game. Fire Like-Likes blend into the lava and rocky terrain, ambushing players who get too close. They deal massive fire damage and have high health pools.
  • Flamepees (fire-based 八爪怪s) launch explosive fireballs with homing capabilities. Their projectiles can knock you off cliffs or into lava.
  • Fire-Breath 蜥蜴战士 patrol the trails in groups and can overwhelm you with coordinated fire attacks. Ice-element weapons and arrows are highly effective against them — bring a supply of 冰冷果 or White 丘丘 Jelly.
  • Always have a backup plan for falling into lava —米法's Grace can save you once, but don't rely on it.
🔨 The 碎岩剑 Can Be Hard to Find
  • Unlike the 飞燕弓 which has a guaranteed spawn in 利特村, the 碎岩剑's guaranteed spawns are less reliable and may already be looted.
  • If the 鼓隆城 racks are empty, your best farming method is Black 莫力布林 along the Death Mountain Trail. These are tough enemies — don't underestimate them. Bring strong weapons and healing items.
  • The 碎岩剑 has 30 durability, which is low for a two-handed weapon. Store it safely and don't use it for combat or mining before 制作 the 碎岩巨剑.
🗿 阿沅 Quest Line Must Be Completed First
  • The 鼓隆铁匠 will NOT craft the 碎岩巨剑 until you've completed the 阿沅 of 鼓隆城 main quest and defeated 巨砾哥马 at the Fire Temple.
  • This questline involves investigating the Goron mining operations, dealing with the marbled rock plague affecting the Gorons, and ultimately ascending Death Mountain to enter the Fire Temple.
  • The Fire Temple dungeon is a multi-floor vertical climb with rail cart puzzles and a challenging boss fight at the top. Come prepared with healing items, strong weapons, and plenty of 左纳乌 charges for your devices.
  • Completing this quest also unlocks 阿沅 as a 贤者 companion, which is an excellent bonus (see Free 收益 below).
💎 钻石 Shortage — Plan Ahead
  • The 钻石 requirement is the biggest bottleneck for this recipe. With only a ~5% drop rate from rare ore deposits, you could spend an hour farming without success if RNG isn't on your side.
  • Always prioritize hunting 稀有岩石巨人 over smashing random deposits. The 稀有岩石巨人 at Lake Ferona (near 鼓隆城) respawns every 血月 and is a guaranteed 钻石 source.
  • If you have Sensor+, set it to track "钻石" before exploring the 地底. This will ping whenever you're near a 钻石-containing deposit, saving enormous amounts of time.
  • Consider purchasing 钻石s from Ramella as a last resort — 1,000 rupees is expensive but guarantees the material when you need it most.
