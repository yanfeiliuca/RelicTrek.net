1 为什么这件物品重要

The 七宝匕首 is more than a curved sword — it's a piece of 格鲁德 history and a direct link to 乌尔波扎, the fierce 英杰 who commanded lightning itself. In Breath of the Wild, 乌尔波扎 was the warm yet formidable leader of the 格鲁德, and her weapon carries that same duality: elegant in design, devastating in execution.

What Makes It Special

At 32 base attack, the 七宝匕首 is tied for the highest base damage of any craftable one-handed weapon in Tears of the Kingdom. Only a handful of unique weapons (like the 大师之剑 at full power) surpass it. What truly sets it apart, however, is the unique charge attack animation — a lightning-infused dash strike that channels the spirit of 乌尔波扎's Fury from Breath of the Wild.

When you perform a charged spin attack with the Scimitar, 林克 unleashes a crackling electric arc that extends beyond the blade's physical reach, dealing bonus damage and stunning enemies weak to electricity. This isn't just a visual flourish — the lightning effect has actual gameplay impact, making the weapon devastating against water-based enemies, groups of closely-packed foes, and anything standing in a puddle.

As a one-handed weapon, the Scimitar pairs naturally with a shield, giving you a balanced offense-defense loadout that spears and two-handed weapons can't match. The swing speed is fast, the combo potential is high, and the ability to quickly transition from attacking to blocking makes it ideal for aggressive playstyles that still value survivability.

The journey to 格鲁德小镇 is one of the most challenging overland treks in the game. You'll cross scorching deserts, navigate blinding sandstorms, and face enemies adapted to the harshest environment in 海拉鲁. But that journey is also one of 王国之泪's most rewarding — the 格鲁德 Desert is packed with secrets, from the mysterious sand-shrouded ruins to the underground grottos hiding some of the best equipment in the game.

From a practical standpoint, 32 base attack puts the Scimitar in elite company. 余料建造d with a high-tier material like a 白银莱尼尔 Saber Horn (+55), you're wielding an 87-damage one-handed sword — enough to defeat most standard enemies in 2-3 hits. The FOMO Angle

The 七宝匕首 is one of four 英杰 weapons, alongside the 光鳞之枪 (米法), 大鹫弓 (力巴尔), and 碎岩巨剑 (达尔克尔). Completing the set isn't just about power — it's about paying homage to the four 英杰s who gave their lives fighting Calamity Ganon. Each weapon tells a story, and 乌尔波扎's blade carries some of the most emotional weight of all.

Beyond the narrative, skipping 格鲁德小镇 means missing one of the game's most unique regions. The 格鲁德 culture, architecture, and questlines are completely distinct from the rest of 海拉鲁. 5 注意事项

⚠ HEAT RESISTANCE, NOT COLD RESISTANCE

格鲁德 Desert requires HEAT resistance, not cold resistance. This trips up every player at least once. The 格鲁德 Canyon and 格鲁德小镇 area are scorching hot. The Snowquill Tundra armor (cold resistance) will NOT help you here — in fact, wearing cold-resistant gear in the desert makes the heat damage worse. You need either: (1) the Desert Voe armor set (purchased in 格鲁德小镇 Secret Club or at Kara Kara Bazaar), (2) Heat-Resistant 药剂 cooked from 冰冷蜜瓜 + Monster Parts, or (3) foods cooked with 冰冷蘑菇s or 冰冷蜜瓜s. Level 1 heat resistance works for the canyon and town. Level 2 is required for the open desert between Kara Kara Bazaar and 格鲁德小镇.

⚠ MALE CHARACTERS NEED VOE ARMOR

If you're playing as 林克 (male), you need Voe armor to enter 格鲁德小镇. 格鲁德小镇 traditionally prohibits men ("Voe") from entering. In 王国之泪, you'll need the full 格鲁德 Voe armor set (Desert Voe Headband, Desert Voe Spaulder, Desert Voe Trousers) to pass the guards. You can purchase this set at the 格鲁德 Secret Club after obtaining the password, or find pieces in chests throughout the desert. Without Voe armor, you'll be thrown out of town regardless of your quest progress. Female characters (if using a character mod) do not face this restriction.

⚠ GERUDO SCIMITAR AVAILABILITY

The 格鲁德之刀 can be frustratingly elusive if NPCs haven't restocked. Unlike the 卓拉长枪, which has consistent 蜥蜴战士 drops, the 格鲁德之刀 relies heavily on weapon rack respawns after quest completion. If you're visiting 格鲁德小镇 before completing the questline, your only source is fighting 格鲁德 soldiers in the desert — and they're tough. Save before engaging soldier patrols. If no scimitar drops, reload. Once the town questline is done, the barracks weapon rack becomes your go-to source, respawning every 血月.

⚠ EXTREME HEAT DRAIN

The open desert between Kara Kara Bazaar and 格鲁德小镇 deals passive heat damage. Without Level 2 heat resistance, your health drains at approximately 0.25 hearts per second. In a 5-minute crossing, that's 15 hearts — enough to kill most mid-game players. Always check your heat resistance level before leaving Kara Kara Bazaar. Cook 冰冷蜜瓜 + Monster Part + Monster Part for a 10:00 Heat-Resistant Elixir. Bring extra healing food as a buffer.

⚠ SAND SHROUD / SANDSTORMS

Sand shrouds and sandstorms reduce visibility to near-zero. During a sandstorm, your map becomes unreadable and distant landmarks disappear. If a storm hits during your desert crossing, find shelter immediately — enemy camps with tents, rock overhangs, or dive into the nearest chasm entrance. Sandstorms typically last 3-5 minutes in-game time. Do NOT attempt to navigate during a storm unless you're following a road with visible path markers.

⚠ MOLDUGA TERRITORY

莫尔德拉吉克 patrol the 格鲁德 Desert sands. These massive subterranean beasts detect vibrations — walking doesn't trigger them, but sprinting, riding horses, and using sand seals at full speed does. A 莫尔德拉吉克 attack deals 8-10 hearts of damage and sends you flying. If you want to fight them, stand on a rock outcropping and shoot bomb arrows at the sand ripple. If you want to avoid them, walk slowly around their patrol zones (marked by dark sand circles on the surface).

⚠ DURABILITY & RE-CRAFTING

The 七宝匕首 has 60 durability and can break. Plan to re-craft it periodically. After your first craft, the re-制作 process is much faster since you only need to fast-travel to 格鲁德小镇 with the materials. Keep a spare 格鲁德之刀 and 钻石 in your inventory so you can immediately re-craft after breakage. The blacksmith is always available post-questline.

The total sell value is 1,500+ 卢比 if you sell the surplus. Bring a Stone Smasher or 碎岩巨剑 to clear deposits in one hit.

🎯 推荐下一步: 大鹫弓

With the 七宝匕首 forged and the 格鲁德 questline complete, your next 英杰 weapon target should be the 大鹫弓 at 利特村. You'll already have 钻石-farming experience and plenty of 打火石. Head northwest from 监视堡垒 toward the 海布拉 region — the cold mountains are a dramatic change from the scorching desert, and 力巴尔's triple-shot bow is arguably the most fun weapon to use in the entire game. Its 28x3 damage output (84 effective per shot) makes it the highest-DPS ranged weapon available.
