1 为什么这件物品重要

The 光鳞之枪 isn't just another spear — it's a direct connection to 米法, the Zora 英杰 from Breath of the Wild, and one of the most emotionally resonant weapons in Tears of the Kingdom. When you hold this trident, you're wielding a piece of 海拉鲁's history.

What Makes It Special

At 22 base attack, the 光鳞之枪 sits at the upper end of spear-class weapons. But raw numbers don't tell the full story. The trident's true value lies in its combination of exceptional reach, fast attack speed, and the ability to be re-crafted indefinitely as long as you have the materials. Unlike unique weapons that disappear forever when they break, the 光鳞之枪 is a permanent addition to your arsenal — a renewable source of high-tier piercing damage.

长枪s in 王国之泪 are the fastest weapon class, and the 光鳞之枪 pairs that speed with damage output that rivals many two-handed weapons. Its thrust attacks have longer range than standard swords, making it ideal for poking enemies from a safe distance, knocking swimmers out of water, and dealing with flying threats like 蝙蝠 and Aerocudas. The weapon also features a unique water-themed aesthetic that glows faintly when equipped near bodies of water — a purely cosmetic but undeniably cool detail.

Why It's Worth the Effort

The journey to obtain this weapon takes you through some of the most beautiful and mechanically interesting areas of the game. 卓拉领地 is no longer the serene waterfall city you remember from Breath of the Wild — it's been blanketed in mysterious Sludge, creating a quest chain that fundamentally changes the region. Completing this quest and earning the right to craft the 光鳞之枪 means you've experienced one of 王国之泪's best regional storylines.

From a practical standpoint, the trident serves as an excellent 余料建造 base. Attach a high-damage material like a 白银莱尼尔 Saber Horn (55 damage) and you're looking at a 77-damage spear with the fastest swing speed in the game. For players who prioritize DPS (damage per second) over raw per-hit numbers, this combination is arguably the best in the entire game. Even without 余料建造 enhancements, the 22 base attack is more than sufficient for the majority of mid-game content.

The FOMO Angle

If you're building a 英杰 weapon collection, the 光鳞之枪 is non-negotiable. It pairs with the 七宝匕首 (乌尔波扎), 大鹫弓 (力巴尔), and 碎岩巨剑 (达尔克尔) to form the complete set of four 英杰 armaments. Each tells a story. Each represents the legacy of a fallen hero who fought Calamity Ganon 100 years ago. Missing this one leaves a conspicuous hole in both your inventory and your narrative experience.

Additionally, the quest to unlock 丹拓's 制作 services introduces you to 希多's character arc — one of the most satisfying companion stories in the game. You'll miss significant character development and one of the most spectacular dungeon sequences if you skip this region entirely.

3 材料与位置

🔱 卓拉长枪

类型: Base weapon (长枪 class) — 7 base attack, used as the 制作 core
获取位置： The 卓拉长枪 has several reliable spawn locations in and around the 拉聂尔 region:

Primary Source — Blue 蜥蜴战士 Drops: Blue 蜥蜴战士 throughout the 拉聂尔 Wetlands and along the Zora River have approximately a 15% chance to drop a 卓拉长枪 on defeat. Focus your farming on the camps between Zodobon Highlands and Oren Bridge. There are typically 3-4 蜥蜴战士 per camp, so expect to clear 3-5 camps for a drop.

武器 Rack Spawns (Post-Sludge): After completing the "Restoring the 卓拉领地" regional questline, check the weapon racks inside 卓拉领地. A 卓拉长枪 respawns on the rack near Mogawak 神庙 (0254, 0945, 0005) every 血月 cycle. This is the most reliable source once unlocked.

拉聂尔 Promenade Ruins: Search the collapsed buildings and hollow logs in the promenade area (coordinates approximately 0570, 0800, 0010). 武器 occasionally spawn in these ruins.

Enemy Camp Near Ruto Lake: A small 蜥蜴战士 encampment on the eastern shore of Ruto Lake (0680, 1100, 0025) has a chance to contain a 卓拉长枪-wielding enemy. Clear the camp and check drops.

专业提示： The 卓拉长枪 is a common weapon. If you're struggling to find one, save your game before attacking a 蜥蜴战士 camp. If no spear drops, reload and try again. The save-scum method works because weapon drops are randomized on enemy spawn.

掉率： ~15% from Blue 蜥蜴战士 in 拉聂尔. Guaranteed 1 per 血月 from 卓拉领地 weapon rack (after quest completion).

专业提示： Always bring a Stone Smasher or 碎岩巨剑 when ore farming. These weapons break ore deposits in a single hit. 炸弹花s work too but are slower and consume resources.

⚡ 打火石 × 5

类型: Common mineral — used for 制作 and starting fires
获取位置： 打火石 is the easiest material on this list. You'll likely pick up 5+ just by playing the game normally, but if you need to farm specifically:

1. Standard Ore Deposits: The gray-brown rocks found on cliff faces, in caves, and along riverbeds. Every single one has a high chance to drop 1-2 打火石. Look along the path from 监视堡垒 to 卓拉领地 — there are dozens of ore deposits on the cliff sides of 拉聂尔 Road and Zora River.

Recommended Farming 路线: Start at Jiosin 神庙 (-0240, 0050, 0020) near 监视堡垒. Head east along the main road. Every 200 meters or so, climb the nearest cliff and break 2-3 ore deposits. By the time you reach 卓拉领地, you'll have 10-15 打火石 minimum.

掉率： ~80% per standard Ore Deposit (1-2 打火石 each). Guaranteed from Stone Pebblits. 3-5 per 岩石巨人 kill.

专业提示： 打火石 has weight. If your inventory is full, drop some apples or mushrooms. You need 5 for the trident, but grab 10-15 while you're out — you'll need more for the 碎岩巨剑 later.

步骤-by-步骤 Breakdown

步骤 1: 监视堡垒 to 拉聂尔 Wetlands (10-15 min)

Exit 监视堡垒 through the east gate. Follow the main road until you reach Rebonae Bridge. Along the way, keep an eye on the cliff faces to your left and right. Break every ore deposit you see — you'll need the 打火石, and you might get lucky with a 钻石 from a 稀有 Ore Deposit. The 拉聂尔 Wetlands begin immediately after the bridge. The terrain becomes marshy, so use 通天术 to bypass shallow water sections.

步骤 2: 蜥蜴战士 Camp Hunting in Wetlands (10-20 min)

Your priority is finding a 卓拉长枪. There are 4-5 蜥蜴战士 camps scattered throughout the wetlands. The most reliable locations are: (1) the camp on the small island at coordinates (0350, 0650, 0005), (2) the camp under the overpass near Tukarok 神庙 (0420, 0720, 0010), and (3) the camp on the eastern edge near Ruto Lake (0680, 0800, 0020). Clear all enemies in each camp. If no 卓拉长枪 drops after 3 camps, consider saving before engaging the next camp and reloading if the drop fails.

步骤 3: Zora River Path (10-15 min)

Follow the Zora River northward. The east bank has fewer enemies and more ore deposits. You'll pass beneath waterfalls — use 通天术 to climb through them quickly. At Oren Bridge, cross to the west side briefly to check a potential 蜥蜴战士 camp, then continue north. The terrain becomes more mountainous as you approach 卓拉领地.

步骤 4: Entering 卓拉领地 (5 min)

The entrance to 卓拉领地 is a massive stone staircase carved into the mountainside. If this is your first visit, you'll trigger a cutscene showing the Sludge covering the city. Proceed up the stairs and enter the domain. Fast-travel to Ihen-a 神庙 if you've already unlocked it.

步骤 5: Sludge Questline (30-60 min, first visit only)

If this is your first time in 卓拉领地 post-upheaval, you 必须 complete the regional questline to access 丹拓. Speak to Yona near the statue of 米法, then find 希多 at 米法 Court. This triggers the Water Temple dungeon — a spectacular sky-based dungeon featuring 希多 as your companion. After completing the temple and defeating the boss, return to 卓拉领地. The Sludge will be cleared.

步骤 6: 制作 at 丹拓's Workshop (2 min)

With the questline complete, 丹拓 is accessible at his workshop on the middle tier of 卓拉领地, near the large waterwheel. Speak to him, select "制作 光鳞之枪," and hand over your materials. Enjoy the cutscene and your new weapon.

5 注意事项

⚠ WEAPON SPECIFICITY

丹拓 ONLY accepts the specific "卓拉长枪." He will NOT accept the Silverscale 长枪, Fishing Harpoon, or any other spear-type weapon. The 卓拉长枪 has a distinct appearance with blue Zoran wrapping on the shaft. Double-check your inventory — if you have a Silverscale 长枪 instead, it won't work. The Silverscale 长枪 is silver/white and has a different model entirely.

⚠ SLUDGE QUEST PREREQUISITE

You must clear the Sludge Around 卓拉领地 questline to access 丹拓. On your first visit, the entire city is covered in Sludge. 丹拓's workshop is inaccessible, and he won't offer 制作 services until the Sludge is cleared. This requires completing the full regional quest culminating in the Water Temple. Plan for 30-60 minutes of questing before you can craft. The quest is excellent and worth doing, but don't expect to walk in and craft immediately.

⚠ DIAMOND RARITY

钻石s are genuinely rare. Don't expect to find one on your first ore deposit. Bring a Stone Smasher or 碎岩巨剑 (or at minimum, a strong two-handed weapon) when farming 稀有 Ore Deposits. 炸弹花s work in a pinch but are resource-inefficient. If you've been saving 钻石s from earlier in the game, use one now. If not, budget 15-20 minutes of dedicated 稀有 Ore Deposit farming in 海布拉 or the 地底 before heading to 卓拉领地.

⚠ DURABILITY WARNING

The 光鳞之枪 can break. Unlike the 大师之剑, this is not an unbreakable weapon. It has 70 durability points, which is above average for spears but still finite. The good news: you can re-craft it as many times as you want by bringing the same materials back to 丹拓. Keep a stockpile of 卓拉长枪s and 钻石s so you never have to scramble for materials after your trident breaks mid-dungeon.

⚠ LIZALFOS COMBAT TIPS

蜥蜴战士 in the 拉聂尔 region are often found near water, making them harder to hit with fire-based attacks. They also have a habit of swimming away and spitting projectiles from a distance. Use electric attacks to stun them (water conducts electricity), or snipe from range with arrows. If a 蜥蜴战士 dives underwater, wait on the shore — they'll resurface eventually.

⚠ FAST TRAVEL SHORTCUT

If you've already unlocked Ihen-a 神庙 inside 卓拉领地, you can skip the entire journey and fast-travel directly. This is incredibly useful for re-制作 the trident after it breaks. Always activate every shrine you pass — the fast-travel network is your most valuable resource in 王国之泪.
