传说 防具 (3 Pieces) ★★★★☆ 中后期

1 为什么这件物品重要

The 蛮族套装 is one of the most iconic and sought-after armor sets in 塞尔达传说：王国之泪. Comprising three pieces — the 蛮族头盔, 蛮族服, and 蛮族短裤 — each piece grants the 攻击力提升 bonus, stacking with every additional piece equipped. With all three pieces worn and fully upgraded, the 蛮族套装 provides a devastating offensive boost that rivals even the legendary 鬼神套装.

攻击力提升 ×3 = One of the highest damage outputs in the game. The 蛮族套装 is the go-to choice for players who want raw combat power without the elaborate hidden quest chain required for the 鬼神套装. While the 鬼神套装 ultimately edges ahead in pure attack potential when fully upgraded, the 蛮族套装 is obtainable earlier, requires less cryptic puzzle-solving, and offers higher base defense values. For many players, this is the practical choice for an endgame offensive build.

Each piece of the 蛮族套装 is found at the heart of one of 海拉鲁's three massive 洛美城堡遗迹 — enormous stone mazes that have appeared in the series since Breath of the Wild. These Labyrinths are not merely cosmetic; each one is a genuine navigational challenge with high walls, dead ends, enemies, and treasure chests scattered throughout. Completing all three Labyrinths is a rite of passage for any serious 王国之泪 player.

But the challenge doesn't end at the surface. In 王国之泪, every Labyrinth has been expanded with a 地底 section — an underground extension accessible only after solving the surface maze. Dropping into the dark, 瘴气-filled underbelly of each Labyrinth adds a second layer of puzzle-solving and combat to the acquisition process, making this one of the most substantial armor quests in the game.

The 蛮族套装 is particularly valuable for:

  • Combat-focused builds — pairing with Mighty meals and high-attack weapons for maximum DPS
  • 莱尼尔 hunting — the sustained damage output makes Golden 莱尼尔s far more manageable
  • 地底 exploration — offensive power helps you clear 瘴气-infected enemy camps efficiently
  • Boss rush challenges — the Coliseum and DLC combat trials become significantly easier
  • Speedrunning and challenge runs — many routing strategies incorporate this set for its reliable power

2 Acquisition: The Three 洛美城堡遗迹

The 蛮族套装 cannot be crafted. Instead, each piece is found inside a treasure chest located at the end of one of 海拉鲁's three 洛美城堡遗迹. Each Labyrinth requires you to:

  1. Navigate the surface maze and reach the central shrine structure
  2. Interact with the terminal to open the way to the 地底 section
  3. Drop into the 地底 and navigate the underground maze
  4. Reach the final terminal to unlock the treasure chest containing the armor piece

5 注意事项 — What Can Go Wrong

The 蛮族服 quest is straightforward compared to the Fierce Deity hunt, but each Labyrinth has its own set of pitfalls that can waste hours or get you killed. Here's every major gotcha and how to avoid it.

🔴 通天术 Works — But Not Everywhere
The classic strategy for Labyrinth navigation is using 通天术 to clip through the walls and bypass the maze entirely. This works well in the 南洛美城堡遗迹 and moderately well in the North Lomei version. However, the walls in the 洛美岛 are significantly thicker in many sections, and 通天术 will fail to find a valid exit point. Don't rely exclusively on 通天术 for the Island — be prepared to actually navigate the maze if needed. Bring stamina-recovery food in case you need to climb over sections instead.
🔴 The 地底 Are Pitch Black
Every Labyrinth's 地底 section is in a completely unlit area of the underground. Without 光亮花的种子s, 采矿套装, or glowing weapon 余料建造s, you will be navigating blind. The 瘴气 pools make this even more dangerous since you can't see them until you're standing in one. Minimum recommendation: 20 光亮花的种子s and the 采矿面罩 (purchased from a 地底 bargainer statue for 100 魄). The 采矿套装 provides a permanent glow effect that makes 地底 exploration infinitely more manageable.
🔴 瘴气 = Permanent Max Heart Drain (Until Cured)
步骤ping in 瘴气 in the 地底 sections temporarily reduces your maximum hearts. These broken hearts cannot be recovered with standard food — you must either return to the surface, stand in a light pool, or eat food made with 向阳草 to restore them. Always carry 5–10 向阳草 meals when entering any 地底 section. A single misstep into a large 瘴气 pool can drain 5+ hearts instantly, leaving you vulnerable to the enemies that patrol these areas.
🔴 North Lomei Requires Level 2 防寒防护
The 塔邦挞 Tundra surrounding the 北洛美城堡遗迹 is one of the coldest areas in the game. Level 1 防寒防护 is NOT sufficient — you will still take damage over time. You need either: (a) the full 羽饰套装 upgraded to at least ★2, (b) the 羽饰套装 + a Spicy meal providing Level 2, or (c) two pieces of cold-resistant armor plus an active cold-resistance buff. Underestimating the cold is the #1 reason players abandon the North Lomei run and have to return later.
🔴 South Lomei's Temperature Swings
The 格鲁德 Highlands plateau where South Lomei sits experiences extreme temperature swings between day and night. Daytime requires heat resistance (Level 2 recommended); nighttime requires cold resistance. If you're doing the surface maze and 地底 back-to-back, you may experience both extremes in a single visit. Bring both types of resistance meals, or plan your arrival for early morning / late afternoon when temperatures are moderate.
🔴 Enemies Respawn on 血月
The 地底 sections of each Labyrinth contain enemy camps that respawn every 血月. If you're returning to a Labyrinth to retrieve a missed treasure chest or to farm additional materials, be prepared to re-clear the enemies. The good news is that 血月 respawns also reset the treasure chests in some side rooms, giving you another chance at loot.
🔴 Don't Forget the Side Chests
Each Labyrinth surface maze contains 3–5 hidden treasure chests with valuable loot — typically high-level weapons, rupees, rare materials, and sometimes even Star Fragments or 大左纳乌能源s. These chests are easy to miss when focused on reaching the center. After claiming your Barbarian piece, do a second sweep of the Labyrinth or use an online interactive map to locate any chests you bypassed. The 南洛美城堡遗迹 in particular has a chest with a 骑士之剑 that's excellent for early-game 余料建造 experiments.
