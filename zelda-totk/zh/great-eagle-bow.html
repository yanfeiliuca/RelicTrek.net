1 为什么这件物品重要

The 大鹫弓 is arguably the most devastating ranged weapon in 塞尔达传说：王国之泪, and every serious adventurer should prioritize 制作 it as soon as they unlock the ability to repair 英杰 weapons. This isn't just a bow — it's 力巴尔's signature weapon, a masterpiece of 利特族工艺 that fires three arrows simultaneously for a staggering 28 base damage per arrow, totaling 84 damage per shot when all three connect.

What makes the 大鹫弓 truly exceptional is its raw DPS potential. While other bows may boast higher single-arrow damage numbers, none match the sheer volume of projectiles this weapon unleashes. When fused with high-damage materials — a Gibdo Bone, Mighty 烟雾蘑菇, or 白银莱尼尔 Hoof, for example — each of the three arrows carries the fused material's damage bonus. This multiplicative effect means the 大鹫弓 scales harder than any other bow in the game. A well-fused 大鹫弓 can output over 200 damage per shot, making it the go-to weapon for 莱尼尔 farming, 瘴气-infused boss encounters, and the 地底' toughest challenges.

The triple-shot mechanic isn't just about damage, either. Firing three arrows at once means three chances to trigger critical hits, three chances to apply elemental effects, and three times the projectile coverage for hitting airborne or fast-moving targets. If you struggle with precision aiming, the spread pattern actually helps — you're more likely to land at least one hit. For players who rely on 余料建造 to attach elemental items like 电流果 or 冰冷果, the 大鹫弓 effectively becomes an elemental shotgun, applying status effects across a wide area of effect.

There's a FOMO angle here too: the 大鹫弓 is not found in chests or dropped by enemies. It must be crafted by the 利特族工匠 in 利特村, and that artisan only becomes available after resolving the Blizzard crisis through the Wind Temple main quest. This means you can't just stumble upon it — you have to earn it. The journey to 利特村 is long, cold, and dangerous, but the payoff is one of the most satisfying weapons in the entire game. Every other bow feels like a toy once you've experienced the raw power of 力巴尔's creation.

Compare the 大鹫弓 to the Royal 弓 (38 damage, single shot) or the Mighty 莱尼尔 弓 (also 3-shot, but lower durability and harder to farm consistently). The 大鹫弓 offers the best balance of high base damage, reliable multi-shot, and the ability to reforge it infinitely — as long as you have the materials. Once you have the recipe memorized and a stockpile of 钻石s, this bow becomes a permanent fixture in your inventory, something you can always fall back on when the going gets tough.

2 余料建造 / 制作 配方

Take the following materials to the 利特族工匠 in 利特村, located in the workshop near the village center. The artisan will forge the 大鹫弓 for you free of charge — you only need to provide the materials.

The 利特族工匠's workshop is marked by a forge and anvil icon on your map once you've unlocked 利特村. Look for a bird-like craftsman near the center of the village, standing beside a workbench with feathers and woodworking tools. Speak to them with the materials in your inventory to trigger the 制作 dialogue. The artisan will also repair a broken 大鹫弓 if you've already crafted one and it shattered — same materials required.

Total material cost per craft: 1 飞燕弓 + 1 钻石 + 5 木柴. This is relatively cheap for a weapon of this caliber. The main bottleneck is the 钻石, which is a rare drop from ore deposits. However, compared to other 英杰 weapons, the 大鹫弓 has one of the most affordable material lists, making it practical to reforge multiple times throughout your playthrough.

Where to find: The 飞燕弓 can be found inside 利特村 itself — check the weapons rack in the village's upper levels near the armor shop. It also drops from 利特族敌人 patrolling the 塔邦挞 Frontier region, particularly around 塔邦挞 Great Bridge (coordinates approximately -3600, 0980, 0240) and the Flight Range. Drop rate: Guaranteed from the 利特村 rack (respawns). ~30% chance from 利特族弓箭手 in 塔邦挞.

The best farming locations include: (1) 稀有岩石巨人 enemies — these golden-glowing Talus variants are guaranteed to drop at least one 钻石 upon defeat. They appear in multiple locations across 海拉鲁, including near 格鲁德 Summit, 海布拉 West Summit, and the 地底. (2) Yiga Clan Hideout — Yiga Blademasters and Yiga Footsoldiers occasionally drop 钻石s. Drop rate: 100% from trees (1–3 per tree).
Tip: 木柴 stacks up to 999 and is used in many recipes, so don't hesitate to farm 50+ pieces while you're at it. You'll need it for cooking, building, and other 英杰 weapon repairs.

4 最优路线

This route assumes you're starting from 监视堡垒 in central 海拉鲁, which is the natural early-game hub. Total estimated time: 25–40 minutes depending on whether you fight enemies or run past them. Make sure you have 防寒防护 Level 2 before attempting — see 注意事项 below.

1
Start at 监视堡垒 鸟望台 — Launch into the sky and glide northwest toward 海拉鲁丘陵. Your goal is the Lindor's Brow 鸟望台 (coordinates approximately -1901, 1243, 0297), which provides a fantastic vantage point for gliding deep into the 塔邦挞 region. If this tower isn't unlocked yet, ride northwest on foot or by horse through 海拉鲁平原.
2
Unlock Lindor's Brow 鸟望台 — If needed, activate this tower. The launch will reveal a huge portion of the northwest map, including the 塔邦挞 Frontier and 海布拉 Mountains. From the sky, look for the distinct silhouette of 利特村 — it's a tall, spiral wooden structure built into the side of a canyon wall, impossible to miss from altitude.
3
Glide or travel to 塔邦挞 Great Bridge — The bridge (coordinates -3600, 0980, 0240) marks the entrance to 利特族领地. This area is often patrolled by 利特族敌人 carrying 飞燕弓s — if you don't already have one, this is your chance to grab it. Check enemy camps on both sides of the bridge. The 利特族弓箭手 here respawn, so you can farm if needed.
4
Follow the canyon road to 利特村 — From 塔邦挞 Great Bridge, follow the winding path upward through 塔邦挞 Frontier. The road is clearly marked and passes through several points of interest: 塔邦挞 Village Ruins (good for scavenging), 利特驿站 (rest and stock up on arrows), and Flight Range (a 利特族训练场 with weapon racks). Continue climbing — the temperature drops sharply as you ascend.
5
Enter 利特村 — The village entrance is at approximately -3619, 1813, 0218. You'll know you're close when you see the massive spiral pillar structure. Before entering, make sure your cold resistance is active — without it, the cold will sap your health rapidly. The village is beautiful but frigid, built into the canyon walls with multiple vertical levels connected by ramps and updrafts.
6
收集 the 飞燕弓 — Once inside 利特村, head to the upper levels near the armor shop. Look for a weapon rack — the 飞燕弓 is typically displayed here. If it's not there (you may have already taken it), check the Flight Range outside the village or farm 利特族弓箭手 in 塔邦挞. Grab it and stow it safely — don't break it before 制作!
7
Gather 5 木柴 — If you don't already have 木柴, exit the village and chop trees in the lower 塔邦挞 Hills where it's warmer. You need only 5 pieces — two or three trees will suffice. Alternatively, check nearby monster camps for pre-chopped 木柴 bundles.
8
Obtain 1 钻石 — If you don't have a 钻石 already, the most reliable method is to hunt a 稀有岩石巨人. The nearest one to 利特村 is located in the 海布拉 Mountains region — consult your map for the 稀有岩石巨人 marker, or use Sensor+ to track 钻石s in nearby caves. Alternatively, if you're short on time, purchase one from Ramella in 鼓隆城 (though this requires a detour). 地底 exploration near the 塔邦挞 area can also yield 钻石s from rare ore deposits.
9
Visit the 利特族工匠 — With 飞燕弓, 钻石, and 5 木柴 in your inventory, locate the 利特族工匠 in the village center workshop. The forge area is marked on your map with an anvil icon after the Wind Temple quest is complete. Speak to the artisan and select "Forge the 大鹫弓." Watch the 制作 animation and claim your new weapon.
10
Test your new bow! — Exit the village and find a target. The 大鹫弓 feels incredible the first time you fire it — three arrows streaking toward your target in a tight spread. Head to the Flight Range nearby to practice your aim and get used to the triple-shot arc. Welcome to the big leagues.

5 注意事项

The road to the 大鹫弓 is straightforward but packed with potential pitfalls. Here's everything that can go wrong — and how to avoid it.

🥶 The Cold Will Kill You — Bring 防寒防护 Level 2
  • 利特村 and the entire 海布拉 region inflict severe cold that rapidly drains health. Level 1 cold resistance is not enough for extended exploration.
  • The Snowquill armor set (purchased in 利特村 itself) provides Level 2 cold resistance when wearing 2+ pieces. If you can't afford it, cook Cold-Resist 药剂 using 暖暖草果s, 暖暖蘑菇s, or 暖暖香草.
  • Equipping a fire-element weapon (like a 红宝石-fused sword) provides minor warmth but is NOT a substitute for proper cold resistance.
🌪️ The Blizzard Quest Must Be Completed First
  • The 利特族工匠 will NOT be available until you complete the 丘栗 of 利特村 main quest and clear the Wind Temple.
  • This means fighting the Colgera boss, navigating the sky islands above 海布拉, and resolving the endless blizzard covering the region.
  • Attempting to visit the artisan before this quest results in the workshop being closed and the craftsman nowhere to be found.
  • Plan accordingly — you can't just rush to 利特村 and grab the bow in the first hour of the game. This is a mid-game weapon.
🏹 The 飞燕弓 Can Break Before 制作ing
  • It's surprisingly easy to accidentally break or drop the 飞燕弓 while fighting your way to 利特村. If this happens, you'll need to farm a replacement from 利特族敌人 or wait for the village weapon rack to respawn after a 血月.
  • Pro tip: Store the 飞燕弓 in your horse's saddlebags or at your house until you're ready to craft. Don't use it in combat — use a disposable weapon instead.
  • The 飞燕弓 has low durability (30 hits), so treat it like the precious 制作 material it is.
🎒 Inventory Space Issues
  • 木柴 stacks to 999 but takes up a precious inventory slot. If you're carrying multiple bow types already, make sure you have room for both the 飞燕弓 and the 大鹫弓 after 制作.
  • Consider expanding your bow inventory slots at 伯库林 before making this trip. You'll want the extra space for all the powerful bows you'll collect in 海布拉 anyway.
🗺️ Getting Lost in 海布拉
  • The 海布拉 region is massive and vertically complex. It's easy to wander into areas you're not prepared for, especially around 海布拉 Peak and the Rospro Pass.
  • Always have a stash of 向阳草s for 瘴气 exposure (if dipping into 地底 entrances), and bring plenty of stamina-restoring food for climbing.
  • Use 通天术 liberally in the canyon areas — there are many overhangs and hidden paths that shortcut the main road.
⏳ 钻石 Farming Takes Time
  • Don't expect to find a 钻石 in the first ore deposit you crack. With only a ~5% drop rate from rare deposits, you may need to smash 20+ deposits to get one.
  • The most reliable source is a 稀有岩石巨人 (100% drop), but these are mini-boss fights that require preparation. Bring a strong two-handed weapon fused with a Boulder or another blunt object for maximum damage against the Talus's ore weak point.

6 Free 收益 Along the 路线

The journey to 利特村 isn't just about the 大鹫弓 — it's one of the most rewarding expedition routes in the entire game. Here's what else you'll unlock and collect along the way:

🛒
利特村 Shops — Best Arrow Vendors in the Game
利特村 hosts the Brazen Beak armor shop (Snowquill set) and an excellent general store with a high-volume arrow restock. The general store sells bundles of arrows at competitive prices, and the inventory refreshes with every 血月. This is arguably the best place in 海拉鲁 to stock up on arrows before a major dungeon or boss fight. You'll also find Arrows x 10 bundles here that are hard to find elsewhere.
🌪️
Wind Temple Access
Completing the Wind Temple quest to unlock the artisan also grants you access to one of the most visually stunning dungeons in the game. Beyond the story progress, the Wind Temple contains multiple treasure chests, a Heart Container, and unlocks 丘栗 as a 贤者 companion. 丘栗's gust ability is invaluable for exploration, combat, and puzzle-solving across all of 海拉鲁.
⛩️
海布拉 区域 神庙s
The route to 利特村 passes by or near numerous shrines: Gatakis 神庙 (near 利特村 itself, a 劳鲁's Blessing shrine), Orochium 神庙, Osuan 神庙, and several others in the 塔邦挞 Frontier. Each shrine grants a Light of Blessing (collect 4 to upgrade hearts or stamina) and provides a fast-travel point for future trips. 海布拉 shrines often contain valuable cold-weather equipment and rupee caches.
🍄
稀有 Cold-区域 材料
海布拉 is home to some of the most valuable 制作 materials in the game. While traveling, collect 冰冷蘑菇s (used in cold-resist cooking and armor upgrades), Cold Darners (dragonflies that sell for good rupees or make excellent elixirs), 暖暖草果s, Sizzlefin Trout, and 冰冷果. These materials are essential for late-game armor upgrades and high-level cooking recipes that you can't easily obtain in warmer regions.
🦅
丘栗 — 贤者 of Wind Companion
Completing the 利特村 questline unlocks 丘栗 as a permanent 贤者 companion. His ability creates a powerful gust of wind that propels you forward while gliding, effectively extending your glide range by 50% or more. This ability transforms how you explore 海拉鲁, making previously unreachable sky islands accessible and turning long climbs into effortless glides. 丘栗 also fights alongside you, providing ranged support with his own bow attacks.
💰
Loot from the Journey
The 塔邦挞 Frontier is rich with monster camps, treasure chests, and hidden caves. You'll pass through areas with 莱尼尔 spawns (for high-end weapons and 莱尼尔 parts), Black and Silver 莫力布林 camps (strong fused weapons), and numerous caves containing ore deposits with gems. The Flight Range itself contains practice targets that reward arrows. Expect to return from this trip with your inventory overflowing, not just with the 大鹫弓 but with a full haul of rupees, gems, weapons, and cooking materials.
