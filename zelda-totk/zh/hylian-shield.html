5 注意事项

The 海利亚盾 acquisition has more hidden pitfalls than you might expect. Here is everything that can go wrong and how to prevent it.

⚠️ 瘴气 Damage Is Permanent Until Healed

Unlike normal damage, 瘴气 reduces your maximum heart count — broken hearts cannot be recovered with normal food. You must eat a meal containing 向阳草 or return to a 破魔之根 in the 地底 to restore broken hearts. Entering 海拉鲁城堡 without 向阳草 recovery food is a recipe for disaster.

Recommended meal: Cook 1 向阳草 + 4 Hylian Herbs for a simple recovery dish. For maximum effect: 3 向阳草 + 2 Hylian Rice for full 瘴气 recovery.

⚠️ Enemies Respawn on 血月

All enemies in 海拉鲁城堡, including the 瘴气 莫力布林 and 波克布林s in the docks, respawn every 血月. If you're planning multiple castle runs (for other treasures), you will need to clear them again. The good news: the shield chest does not respawn its contents — once claimed, it's empty on return visits.

⚠️ The 盾 CAN Technically Break

Despite its legendary reputation, the 海利亚盾 does have a durability value — it's just extremely high. Under normal use (blocking regular enemy attacks), it lasts 40+ hours. However:

  • Blocking 莱尼尔 charge attacks drains durability noticeably
  • 盾-surfing on rough terrain (rock, not snow/sand) drains durability fast
  • Repeatedly blocking explosive barrels will eventually break it

When it breaks, your only replacement sources are: (1) 格兰特's shop in 一始村 (3,000 rupees, one per 血月), or (2) rare amiibo drops. There is no in-castle respawn for the chest.

⚠️ Vendor Only Sells One Per 血月

格兰特son's stock of the 海利亚盾 is limited to one per 血月 cycle. If you break your shield and need a replacement, you must either wait for the next 血月 or use an alternate method. This makes the purchased shield more precious than the castle-found one in some ways — you can't just run back and grab another.

⚠️ Don't 余料建造 a Material You Can't Replace

余料建造 a material to the 海利亚盾 adds its properties but also means you lose that material if the shield breaks. Before fusing that rare 莱尼尔 Hoof, ask yourself: "Am I okay with losing this forever if my shield breaks?" For general exploration, fuse common materials (mine cart, spring, pot lid). Save rare fuses for boss encounters where the shield won't take incidental damage.

⚠️ Castle 瘴气 Requires Specific Preparation

General healing food does NOT restore 瘴气-broken hearts. Players entering 海拉鲁城堡 with only cooked apples and meat skewers will find themselves permanently reduced to minimum health. This is the #1 reason castle runs fail. Your inventory for any castle expedition should contain:

  • Minimum 3 向阳草 recovery meals
  • 15+ 光亮花的种子s
  • At least one weapon with high fuse damage (for the 莫力布林)
  • A backup shield in case your current one breaks mid-run

⚠️ The Docks 莫力布林 Can One-Shot Low-Health Players

The 瘴气 莫力布林 guarding the shield chest deals massive damage and inflicts 瘴气 on hit. If you have fewer than 8 hearts, a direct hit will likely kill you. Recommended strategies:

  • Stealth approach: Crouch-walk behind it, use a sneakstrike for 8x damage
  • Ranged approach: 炸弹花 arrows from an elevated position
  • 通天术 skip: Use 通天术 on the ceiling to bypass the encounter entirely

⚠️ Water in the Moat Is NOT Safe

The castle moat contains 瘴气-infused water in certain areas. Swimming through these patches applies 瘴气 damage even underwater. Stick to the stone walkways or use the 卓拉铠甲's swim dash to cross quickly. Building a raft is the safest option for low-stamina players.
