传说 武器

1 为什么这件物品重要

The 大师之剑 is not just the most iconic weapon in the 塞尔达 franchise — it is the single best one-handed sword in Tears of the Kingdom for general combat, 余料建造 potential, and narrative significance. Unlike Breath of the Wild where you simply needed 13 Heart Containers to pull it from 克洛格森林, 王国之泪 completely reimagines the acquisition method. This time, the sword is embedded in the head of the Light Dragon (Naydra's transformed state) and cannot be obtained until you complete the "英帕 and the 地画s" main quest and collect all 12 龙之泪s scattered across 海拉鲁's 地面, 天空, and 地底.

Once restored, the 大师之剑 has a base attack power of 30, which may sound underwhelming compared to some late-game fused weapons. But here's the key: the 大师之剑 possesses a unique property called "Sword of Legend" that makes it unbreakable during key moments. More importantly, it has exceptional synergy with the 余料建造 system — fusing a 白银莱尼尔 Horn (55 attack) to the 大师之剑 creates a weapon with 85 base attack that never runs out of durability in critical combat scenarios. No other one-handed weapon in the game offers this combination of reliability and raw power.

What Makes the 大师之剑 Special in 王国之泪

  • Unlimited 耐久度 in Critical Moments: The sword glows blue and consumes no durability when fighting 瘴气-afflicted enemies, bosses, and in the 地底. This effectively makes it an infinite-use weapon against the hardest content in the game.
  • Best-in-Class 余料建造 Compatibility: The 大师之剑 accepts 余料建造 materials better than any other one-handed weapon. A fused 白银莱尼尔 Horn creates an 85-attack weapon with the swing speed of a one-hander.
  • Beam 攻击力 at Full Hearts: Throwing the 大师之剑 at full health launches the iconic Sword Beam, dealing ranged damage for zero durability cost. This is devastating against flying enemies and 八爪怪s.
  • 需要 for True Ending: You cannot access the complete ending sequence and final boss encounter without the restored 大师之剑. If you want to see the full conclusion of 王国之泪's story, this weapon is mandatory.
  • Unique Model and Lore: The restored 大师之剑 has a distinct glowing appearance that sets it apart from every other weapon in the game. It is the narrative centerpiece of the entire 龙之泪s questline.

Bottom line: if you plan to tackle the 地底, defeat Ganon, or explore the game's hardest content, the 大师之剑 is not optional — it is the single most important item you can obtain. The questline to get it is long, but the reward permanently upgrades your combat capability across the entire game.

FOMO Warning: The 龙之泪s questline unlocks 12 full cutscenes that explain the backstory of the Imprisoning War, 塞尔达's time travel, and the origin of the 魔王. These memories are permanently missable if you rush past them. Even if you don't care about the sword itself, the lore revelations make this questline essential for understanding 王国之泪's plot.

5 注意事项

Here is everything that can and will go wrong during the 大师之剑 questline — and exactly how to avoid each pitfall.

⚠️ 必须 Complete Great 天空 Island First

You cannot start "英帕 and the 地画s" until you finish the entire Great 天空 Island tutorial, receive the 普尔亚平板, and speak with 普尔亚 at 监视堡垒. If you try to find 英帕 before this, she won't appear at New Serenne Stable. This is a hard quest lock — no workarounds.

⚠️ 精力 Check — The #1 Failure Point

The 大师之剑 pull requires two full 精力槽s minimum. This means 10 精力 Vessel upgrades from Goddess Statues (40 神庙 completions). If you have less than this, you will fail the pull and be thrown off the dragon. The game does not warn you about this beforehand.

Solutions: Cook Enduring Fried Wild Greens (1 毅力胡萝卜 + 海拉鲁 Herbs) for bonus yellow stamina. The full Glide armor set reduces stamina consumption while airborne. Stack both for a comfortable margin of error.

⚠️ Tears Must Be Viewed in Order for Lore Coherence

While you can physically collect the tears in any order after Tear #1, the memory cutscenes are designed to be watched sequentially. If you collect Tear #12 before Tear #2, you'll see the finale before the setup — major spoilers for what is arguably the best storytelling in the game. If you care about the narrative, follow the numbered order in this guide.

⚠️ Light Dragon Moves on a Fixed Path

The Light Dragon follows a fixed counterclockwise flight path around 海拉鲁 at high altitude. It does not stop, slow down, or respond to player actions. If you fall off, you cannot whistle for it. You must either:

  • Fast-travel to a point ahead of its path and wait (it takes roughly 20 real-time minutes for a full circuit)
  • Use a 左纳乌 flying device to intercept it mid-flight
  • Wait at a campfire to advance time and reposition

Best interception points: 天空 Islands directly above its path, or the Thyphlo Ruins 鸟望台 for easy elevation.

⚠️ 迷途森林 Fog Will Teleport You

Tear #9 is inside the 迷途森林, which has been corrupted by 瘴气. Walking into the fog from the surface will teleport you back to the entrance — there is no surface path through. You must use the 地底 entrance (Ninjis 神庙) and 通天术, or glide from a 天空 Island directly above the target coordinates.

⚠️ 地底 瘴气 Damage Stacks Up Fast

The final tear in the 地底 beneath 海拉鲁城堡 is surrounded by heavy 瘴气. Without 瘴气 resistance armor or 向阳草 recovery food, you will lose maximum hearts rapidly. Bring at least 5 cooked meals containing 向阳草 (向阳草 + Hylian Rice makes a solid recovery dish). The 地底 armor set (purchased from Bargainer Statues) reduces 瘴气 damage by 50%.

⚠️ The Sword Has a 10-Minute Cooldown

If your 大师之剑 runs out of energy (durability), it enters a 10-minute real-time cooldown before it recharges. During this time, it cannot be used. However, this cooldown only applies when the sword is NOT glowing blue. During 瘴气 encounters and boss fights, the sword has unlimited durability and never enters cooldown. Manage your non-瘴气 combat accordingly.

⚠️ Don't 余料建造 a Material You're Not Ready to Lose

When the 大师之剑 enters its 10-minute cooldown, any fused material is locked with it. If you fused a rare 白银莱尼尔 Horn, you won't be able to use it on another weapon until the sword recharges. Consider fusing common materials for exploration and saving rare fuses for known 瘴气 encounters.

The 12 龙之泪s collectively tell the story of the Imprisoning War — the ancient conflict between the first King 劳鲁, the Six 贤者s, and the 魔王 盖侬多夫. Without collecting these tears, you will not understand:

This is the emotional core of 王国之泪's narrative. Speedrunners skip it; completionists cherish it.
