夜光套装

Bone-themed glow-in-the-dark armor. Stal Skeleton disguise + attack boost at night. 制作ed by the mysterious stylist using 魄 from the 地底.

1. 为什么这件物品重要

The 夜光套装 is one of the most visually distinctive and mechanically unique armor collections in 塞尔达传说：王国之泪. Comprising three pieces — the 夜光面罩, 夜光紧身服, and 夜光紧身裤 — this bone-themed ensemble transforms 林克 into a literal walking skeleton, glowing with an eerie luminescence that cuts through the darkest corners of 海拉鲁. But this is far more than a cosmetic novelty. The 夜光套装 is a legitimate combat powerhouse for a very specific and surprisingly effective playstyle: the bone weapon build.

When all three pieces are equipped, the 夜光套装 grants the "Bone 攻击力提升" set bonus. This passive effect significantly increases the damage output of any weapon that has been 余料建造d with a bone-based material — 波克布林 Arms, 莫力布林 Arms, Stal heads, and other skeletal components. In practical terms, this means a simple stick fused with a 波克布林 arm can outperform many mid-tier standard weapons. The damage scaling is substantial enough that bone-fused weapons become competitive with fully upgraded metal weapons, and they are far easier and cheaper to acquire in large quantities.

Beyond the damage boost, the 夜光套装 provides a unique utility effect: 骷髅伪装. When wearing any piece of the set, Stal-type enemies — the dismembered skeletons that rise from the ground at night — will not attack 林克 on sight. They will regard you as one of their own, allowing you to walk through Stal encampments unmolested, set up ambushes, or simply ignore them while focusing on other threats. This is particularly valuable in regions dense with Stal enemies, such as the 地底 and certain nighttime areas of 海拉鲁平原.

The third major benefit is the nighttime glow. Every piece of the 夜光套装 emits a bright green luminescence that acts as a constant light source in dark environments. When exploring the 地底 (where darkness is pervasive and dangerous), the caves beneath 海拉鲁, or any unlit underground area, this built-in illumination saves inventory space on 光亮花的种子s and reduces the need for the 采矿套装 set. The glow is bright enough to reveal terrain details, enemy positions, and collectible items in a respectable radius around 林克.

The FOMO factor: The 夜光套装 can only be obtained from a single hidden NPC in the 地底 who accepts only 魄 as currency. There is no shop in the surface world that sells this armor, no shrine that rewards it, and no quest that gives it as a prize. If you never venture into the 地底 — or if you explore them without knowing where to look — you will never see this armor. For players who enjoy unique builds, nighttime combat, or 地底 exploration, missing the 夜光套装 means losing access to one of the most creative armor concepts in the entire game.

PRO TIP: The 夜光套装's bone damage boost stacks with weapon base damage and any additional 余料建造 effects. A high-durability base weapon (like a Gnarled Long Stick or Soldier's Claymore) fused with a high-tier Stal skull and wielded with the full 夜光套装 equipped can deal damage comparable to endgame weapons. This makes it one of the most cost-efficient damage builds in 王国之泪, since bone materials are infinitely renewable from Stal enemies that respawn every night.

3. 材料与位置

魄 — The 地底 Currency

👻

魄 Spirits Need: 500 total Found Only: 地底

魄 are the exclusive currency of the 地底 — ghostly, glowing spirits that drift through the perpetual darkness below 海拉鲁. They are not items you pick up in the traditional sense; instead, they are automatically absorbed when 林克 walks close enough to them. Your 魄 total is tracked separately from 卢比 and is displayed in your inventory screen. The only way to spend 魄 is at 魔人像s and special vendors like the 神秘造型师.

There are three tiers of 魄, each with a different point value:

  • Small 魄 (Blue): Worth 1 魄 point. The most common variety. These faint blue orbs are scattered throughout the 地底 in massive quantities. They appear individually or in small clusters near 破魔之根s, along travel paths, and around abandoned structures. Easy to spot — they emit a soft blue glow.
  • Large 魄 (Green): Worth 5 魄 points. Less common but noticeably more valuable. Large 魄 appear as larger, brighter green orbs and are typically found in slightly more dangerous or out-of-the-way areas. They often spawn near mini-boss encounters, in the center of wide-open 地底 plains, and near abandoned mines. A single Large 魄 is equivalent to five Small 魄, so prioritize these when routing.
  • Grand 魄 (Red): Worth 20 魄 points. The rarest and most valuable. Grand 魄 are brilliant red orbs that stand out dramatically against the dark 地底 environment. They are typically found in hard-to-reach locations: atop rock formations, inside half-buried ruins, near boss arenas, and in areas protected by 瘴气 pools or strong enemies. Finding just 25 Grand 魄 covers half your total requirement. Always go out of your way to collect these.

Best farming locations:

  • Near 破魔之根s: Every 破魔之根 in the 地底 has a cluster of Small 魄 (3-8) in its immediate vicinity. Since 破魔之根s are your primary fast travel network in the 地底, you will passively collect hundreds of 魄 just by activating them. Always circle the area after activating a new 破魔之根.
  • Flat open areas: The wide, flat plains of the central 地底 (beneath 海拉鲁平原 and the surrounding regions) are densely populated with 魄 of all sizes. These areas are relatively safe, have minimal 瘴气 coverage, and offer high visibility. Run in a sweeping pattern to collect dozens per pass.
  • Abandoned mines: The various abandoned mine structures scattered throughout the 地底 consistently spawn Large and Grand 魄. These locations often have 左纳乌能源 deposits as well, making them dual-purpose farming spots. Check inside buildings, on elevated platforms, and near broken mining equipment.
  • 瘴气 edges: The boundaries between 瘴气-covered and safe terrain often have higher 魄 concentrations, particularly Large 魄. Approach carefully — 瘴气 reduces your maximum hearts while you stand in it.
  • Beneath major surface settlements: The 地底 directly beneath 卡卡利科村, 哈特诺村, 格鲁德小镇, and 利特村 tend to have elevated 魄 spawn rates. The area beneath 海拉鲁城堡 is exceptionally dense with Grand 魄 but is also extremely dangerous.

Respawn mechanic: 魄 respawn after every 血月, just like surface enemies and materials. If you thoroughly clear a farming route, you can return after the next 血月 to collect the same 魄 again. This makes the 500 魄 target very achievable over multiple sessions — you do not need to marathon-farm them all in one trip.

Navigating the 地底 — 需要 Preparation

🕯️

The 地底 are pitch black. Without a light source, you cannot see terrain features, enemy positions, or even 魄 spirits beyond a few feet from 林克. Before attempting to farm 500 魄, ensure you have at least one of the following:

  • 光亮花的种子s (minimum 30-50): Throw these on the ground or fuse them to arrows to create permanent light sources in the 地底. They grow into glowing plants that illuminate a wide radius. Farm them from surface caves (almost every cave has 2-5 seeds) or purchase from Beedle at stables.
  • 采矿套装 (full set preferred): This armor set provides a constant glow effect when equipped, eliminating the need for 光亮花的种子s. The pieces are found in chests at various 地底 locations. Even one piece (the Mask is easiest to find) provides enough light for basic navigation.
  • 破魔之根s as anchors: Each activated 破魔之根 illuminates a massive circular area around it. Your farming route should hop between 破魔之根s, collecting 魄 in each illuminated zone before moving to the next.

瘴气防护

🖤

瘴气 Protection Highly Recommended

瘴气 is the pervasive dark substance that covers much of the 地底 floor. Standing in 瘴气 slowly reduces your maximum hearts — not just your current health, but your maximum health pool. This reduction persists until you return to the surface or stand in 破魔之根 illumination. To mitigate 瘴气:

  • 向阳草 cooking: Cook meals with 向阳草s (found on 天空 Islands) to create dishes that restore hearts lost to 瘴气. These are essential for extended 地底 exploration.
  • Deep Firefly elixirs: Cook Deep Fireflies (found in the 地底 near water) to create 瘴气防护 elixirs that temporarily prevent 瘴气 damage.
  • Stick to high ground: 瘴气 tends to pool in low-lying areas. Stay on elevated terrain, rock formations, and ruins to avoid it entirely.
  • 破魔之根 proximity: Standing near an active 破魔之根 nullifies 瘴气 in its radius. Plan your route to stay within 破魔之根 illumination as much as possible.

4. 最优路线

This route is designed to be completed in the early-to-mid game, as soon as you have access to the 地底 and at least one active 破魔之根. You do not need any story progression beyond the initial tutorial and the ability to reach a chasm entrance. Total time varies significantly based on how many 魄 you have already collected passively.

1

Preparation — 地面 收集ion (10-15 minutes)

Start point: 监视堡垒 or any stable.

Before descending, gather supplies: 30-50 光亮花的种子s (check caves near your starting point — caves almost always have Brightbloom plants inside), 5-10 向阳草s (cook into 瘴气-healing meals — farm from nearby 天空 Islands if needed), a bow with at least 20 arrows (for attaching 光亮花的种子s to illuminate distant areas), and any healing items you have stockpiled.

If you have already obtained any piece of the 采矿套装 from previous 地底 trips, equip it now. If not, 光亮花的种子s will carry you through this route.

Recommended entry: The chasm near 海拉鲁城堡 -0253, 1011, 0005 or the one near 监视堡垒 itself.

2

Enter the 地底 — First 破魔之根 (10 minutes)

Fast travel to your chosen chasm entrance and drop in. Use your paraglider to slow descent. As you fall, look for a faint blue-white glow below — this is a 破魔之根. Aim for it and land nearby.

Activate the 破魔之根 immediately. This does three things: illuminates the surrounding area (making navigation possible), adds a fast travel point, and nullifies 瘴气 in its radius. Circle the 破魔之根 and collect all nearby Small 魄 — there are usually 3-8 per 破魔之根.

Throw a 光亮花的种子 in each cardinal direction from the 破魔之根 to extend your visible range. Look for additional 魄 in the illuminated areas and collect them. Mark any Grand 魄 or Large 魄 you spot but cannot safely reach — you will return later if needed.

3

魄 Farming Loop — Central 地底 (30-60 minutes)

Using your activated 破魔之根 as a hub, expand outward in a spiral pattern. The goal is to reach and activate additional 破魔之根s while collecting every 魄 along the way. Follow this priority system:

Priority 1 — Grand 魄 (red, 20 points): These are unmistakable — bright red orbs visible from great distances. If you spot one, alter your route to collect it immediately. They are rare enough that missing even one is a significant setback. Common locations: atop rock spires, inside ruined structures, near abandoned mine equipment, at the edge of 瘴气 pools.

Priority 2 — Large 魄 (green, 5 points): Bright green orbs, visible at medium range. These are your workhorse currency — collecting 40 Large 魄 covers the entire 500 point requirement with no Small 魄 needed. Check near abandoned mines, mini-boss spawn points, and open plain areas.

Priority 3 — Small 魄 (blue, 1 point): Abundant but individually weak. 收集 them as you pass but do not go far out of your way for individual Small 魄. Clusters of 5+ are worth a detour; single 魄 are not.

路线 pattern: Move from 破魔之根 to 破魔之根 in a chain. At each 破魔之根, activate it, circle for nearby 魄, throw 光亮花的种子s to scout the perimeter, then move to the next visible 破魔之根 on the horizon. The central 地底 (beneath 海拉鲁平原) has enough 破魔之根s spaced closely enough that you can hop between them without spending more than a minute in true darkness.

PRO TIP: Bring 左纳乌 charges and a Fan/Wing device. In wide-open 地底 areas, you can attach a Fan to a wing and glide long distances at high speed, covering massive areas quickly and spotting 魄 from the air. This is the fastest way to farm large regions. Build a simple aircraft at any 左纳乌 Device Dispenser in the 地底.
4

Locate the 神秘造型师 (10-15 minutes)

Once you have accumulated 500 魄, it is time to find the vendor. The 神秘造型师 is located near the 平原魔人像 in the central 地底. From any central 地底 破魔之根, navigate toward the coordinates 0000, 0000, -0800 (approximate — look for the 魔人像 icon on your 地底 map if you have revealed the area).

魔人像s appear as large stone figures with outstretched hands, visible from a distance even in the dark. They are always illuminated by a soft light. The 神秘造型师 stands directly beside one of these statues. If you have not revealed the map section yet, follow 破魔之根s northward from your starting position until the statue comes into view.

Approach the stylist and interact. The purchase menu will show all three Radiant 防具 pieces. Buy them in any order, though we recommend Mask → Tights → Shirt to spread the cost evenly. Each purchase is confirmed individually and the armor piece is added to your inventory immediately.

5

Return to 地面 and Test (5 minutes)

Fast travel back to the surface via any activated 破魔之根. Find a safe spot, open your inventory, and equip all three Radiant 防具 pieces. Observe 林克's appearance — he should now look like a glowing skeleton. If you have any bone materials in your inventory (波克布林 Arm, 莫力布林 Arm, Stal skull), fuse one to a weapon and test the damage output against a nearby enemy. The damage numbers should be noticeably higher than without the set.

Wait until nightfall and approach a Stal enemy spawn point. The Stals should ignore you unless you attack first. The disguise is working. Congratulations — you now have one of the most unique and fun armor sets in Tears of the Kingdom.

Total estimated route time: 60-100 minutes for the full 500 魄 farm and purchase, assuming you start from zero 魄. If you have already collected some 魄 from casual 地底 exploration, adjust downward accordingly. The route is designed to be done in segments — farm 魄 across multiple sessions, returning to the surface to restock 光亮花的种子s as needed. There is no time pressure.

5. 注意事项

The 地底 are unforgiving, and the 夜光套装 acquisition has several pitfalls that can waste hours of progress or get you killed. Study this section before your first descent.

⚠️ 地底 Survival — The Hard Truths

  • The 地底 are DARK: This cannot be overstated. Without 光亮花的种子s or the 采矿套装, you are essentially blind. You will walk into 瘴气 pools, fall into chasms, stumble into enemy camps, and miss 魄 that are inches from your face. Do not enter the 地底 without at least 20 光亮花的种子s. Ever.
  • 瘴气 reduces MAXIMUM hearts: Not current health — maximum health. If you have 10 hearts and stand in 瘴气 long enough, your maximum might drop to 4 hearts. This effect persists until you either return to the surface, stand in 破魔之根 light, or eat 瘴气-healing food. In the worst case, a single hit from an enemy can kill you because your maximum health has been reduced to almost nothing. Check your heart container count constantly while in the 地底.
  • No natural light: Unlike the surface world, there is no day/night cycle in the 地底. It is permanently dark. Your only light sources are 光亮花的种子s, 破魔之根s, the 采矿套装, and weapon effects. Plan accordingly.
  • Falling is fatal: The 地底 have bottomless pits just like the surface. Falling into one means death and a respawn at your last surface save point, losing all unsaved progress. Use your paraglider liberally when navigating uncertain terrain in the dark.

⚠️ 魄 Farming Efficiency — Common Mistakes

  • Farming without a route: Wandering randomly in the 地底 is inefficient and dangerous. Always move from 破魔之根 to 破魔之根 in a planned pattern. This ensures you have fast travel points, 瘴气 protection, and illuminated areas for safe collection.
  • Ignoring Grand 魄: Many players focus on collecting every Small 魄 they see and skip risky-looking Grand 魄 locations. This is backwards. A single Grand 魄 is worth 20 Small 魄. Spending two minutes climbing a rock spire for one Grand 魄 is vastly more efficient than spending two minutes collecting 20 individual Small 魄. Always prioritize Grand 魄, even if they look out of the way.
  • Not using 左纳乌 vehicles: The central 地底 are wide-open plains perfect for 左纳乌 aircraft. A simple Fan+Wing glider lets you cover 10x the ground area in the same time, spotting 魄 from the air that would be invisible on foot. If you are not using vehicles, you are farming inefficiently.
  • Forgetting 血月 respawns: If you clear a farming area and do not have enough 魄, remember that ALL 魄 respawn on the next 血月. There is no need to search for "fresh" areas — just re-run your previous route after the 血月 cutscene plays.

⚠️ Stylist Location & Purchase Issues

  • The stylist is NOT marked on your map: There is no quest marker, no map icon, and no NPC indicator for the 神秘造型师 until you are practically standing next to him. You must navigate to the 平原魔人像 manually. The statue itself is visible on the 地底 map once you have activated nearby 破魔之根s — look for the stone hand icon.
  • 魔人像s look similar: There are multiple 魔人像s throughout the 地底. The 神秘造型师 is specifically at the Plains statue in the central region. Other statues have different vendors or no stylist at all. Do not waste 魄 at the wrong statue.
  • 魄 are NOT 卢比: You cannot use 卢比 to buy the 夜光套装. You cannot use 左纳乌能源, materials, or any other currency. Only 魄 count. If you do not have enough 魄, the purchase option will be grayed out. Farm more and return.
  • Individual piece purchases: Each armor piece must be bought separately with its own confirmation. There is no "buy all" option. Make sure you have 500 魄 total before committing to purchases — if you buy the Mask and Shirt first (150 + 200 = 350 魄) but only had 400 魄, you will be 50 short for the Tights and need to farm again.

⚠️ 防具 Set Limitations

  • Cannot be upgraded: The 夜光套装 has fixed stats and cannot be enhanced at 大妖精之泉s. The 3 defense per piece (9 total) is all you get. This makes it less viable for late-game hard content where enemy damage scales up significantly. Treat it as a specialized build piece, not your main tank armor.
  • Bone 攻击力提升 requires all 3 pieces: The set bonus ONLY activates when wearing the complete Mask, Shirt, and Tights simultaneously. Wearing one or two pieces gives you the 骷髅伪装 and glow but NOT the damage boost. Do not buy individual pieces expecting partial benefits — commit to the full set.
  • 骷髅伪装 breaks on attack: Stal enemies will ignore you while you walk past, but the moment you attack ANY enemy (Stal or otherwise), all nearby Stals will aggro. Use the disguise for positioning and ambush setup, not as a permanent immunity.
  • Bone weapons have low durability: Even with the damage boost, bone-fused weapons tend to break faster than metal weapons. Bring multiple backup weapons for extended combat. The good news is that bone materials are infinitely renewable — every night, new Stals rise from the ground.

6. Free 收益

Farming 500 魄 in the 地底 is not a single-purpose errand. The journey to acquire the 夜光套装 will inevitably reward you with far more than just the armor. Every minute spent in the 地底 yields permanent upgrades, new abilities, and valuable materials. Here is everything else you will walk away with:

破魔之根s also: illuminate massive areas (making future exploration trivial), nullify 瘴气 in their radius (creating safe zones for healing and restocking), and reveal the 地底 map in their light circle. Each one is a permanent upgrade to your account that persists across all future play sessions.

左纳乌能源 — Energy Cell Upgrades

⚙️

左纳乌能源 Ore and Crystals

The 地底 are the only source of 左纳乌能源 — the green crystalline ore used to upgrade your Energy Cell at crystal refining stations. An upgraded Energy Cell means more 左纳乌 device usage time, which means longer flight durations, more powerful vehicle builds, and extended combat with 左纳乌-powered weapons.

Also collect Large 左纳乌能源 deposits (distinguished by their larger size and brighter glow) — these refine into more crystal charges per unit.

The convenience of 自动组装 cannot be overstated — instead of spending 5 minutes attaching parts, you press one button and a functional vehicle materializes in seconds. 地底 exploration is the primary source of Schema Stones, and your farming route will take you past several guaranteed spawn points.
