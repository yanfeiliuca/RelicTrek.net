Lightscale Trident (光鳞之枪)

★★★★☆ Mid Game
Rare Weapon (Spear) 22 Base Attack Craftable

Mipha's legacy weapon. A ceremonial trident requiring a Zora Spear, Diamond, and 5 Flint — crafted by Dento at Zora's Domain. 22 base attack with exceptional reach and the ability to be re-crafted infinitely. The best spear in the game for players who value range, speed, and the emotional weight of wielding a Champion's weapon.

1 Why This Item Matters

The Lightscale Trident isn't just another spear — it's a direct connection to Mipha, the Zora Champion from Breath of the Wild, and one of the most emotionally resonant weapons in Tears of the Kingdom. When you hold this trident, you're wielding a piece of Hyrule's history.

What Makes It Special

At 22 base attack, the Lightscale Trident sits at the upper end of spear-class weapons. But raw numbers don't tell the full story. The trident's true value lies in its combination of exceptional reach, fast attack speed, and the ability to be re-crafted indefinitely as long as you have the materials. Unlike unique weapons that disappear forever when they break, the Lightscale Trident is a permanent addition to your arsenal — a renewable source of high-tier piercing damage.

Spears in TOTK are the fastest weapon class, and the Lightscale Trident pairs that speed with damage output that rivals many two-handed weapons. Its thrust attacks have longer range than standard swords, making it ideal for poking enemies from a safe distance, knocking swimmers out of water, and dealing with flying threats like Keese and Aerocudas. The weapon also features a unique water-themed aesthetic that glows faintly when equipped near bodies of water — a purely cosmetic but undeniably cool detail.

Why It's Worth the Effort

The journey to obtain this weapon takes you through some of the most beautiful and mechanically interesting areas of the game. Zora's Domain is no longer the serene waterfall city you remember from Breath of the Wild — it's been blanketed in mysterious Sludge, creating a quest chain that fundamentally changes the region. Completing this quest and earning the right to craft the Lightscale Trident means you've experienced one of TOTK's best regional storylines.

From a practical standpoint, the trident serves as an excellent Fuse base. Attach a high-damage material like a Silver Lynel Saber Horn (55 damage) and you're looking at a 77-damage spear with the fastest swing speed in the game. For players who prioritize DPS (damage per second) over raw per-hit numbers, this combination is arguably the best in the entire game. Even without Fuse enhancements, the 22 base attack is more than sufficient for the majority of mid-game content.

The FOMO Angle

If you're building a Champion weapon collection, the Lightscale Trident is non-negotiable. It pairs with the Scimitar of the Seven (Urbosa), Great Eagle Bow (Revali), and Boulder Breaker (Daruk) to form the complete set of four Champion armaments. Each tells a story. Each represents the legacy of a fallen hero who fought Calamity Ganon 100 years ago. Missing this one leaves a conspicuous hole in both your inventory and your narrative experience.

Additionally, the quest to unlock Dento's crafting services introduces you to Sidon's character arc — one of the most satisfying companion stories in the game. You'll miss significant character development and one of the most spectacular dungeon sequences if you skip this region entirely.

2 Fuse / Craft Recipe

The Lightscale Trident is not found in a chest or dropped by an enemy. It must be crafted by Dento, the elderly Zora blacksmith who operates a workshop in Zora's Domain. Bring the following materials to him and select the "Craft Lightscale Trident" dialogue option.

Material Qty Source / How to Obtain
Zora Spear 1 Dropped by Blue Lizalfos in Lanayru region (~15% drop rate). Also found in weapon racks inside Zora's Domain after clearing the Sludge questline. Check respawn points near Mogawak Shrine.
Diamond 1 Rare ore from Rare Ore Deposits (gold-veined black rocks). Best farmed in Hebra Mountains or from Rare Stone Talus enemies. ~10% drop rate per deposit.
Flint 5 Common drop from standard Ore Deposits (gray rocks with brown veins) found everywhere. Guaranteed 1-2 per deposit. Also drops from Stone Pebblits and regular Stone Talus.

Crafting Process

Once you have all materials in your inventory:

  1. Fast travel to Ihen-a Shrine (0474, 1003, 0012) inside Zora's Domain, or Mogawak Shrine (0254, 0945, 0005) nearby.
  2. Locate Dento's workshop — it's the small smithy on the middle tier of Zora's Domain, near the waterwheel. Look for the hammer icon on your map.
  3. Speak to Dento. If you've completed the Sludge questline, he'll offer the "Craft Lightscale Trident" option.
  4. Hand over the materials. A short cutscene plays showing Dento forging the trident.
  5. The Lightscale Trident appears in your inventory. Base attack: 22. Durability: 70.

Fuse Recommendations

While the base 22 attack is solid, the trident truly shines when Fused. Here are top recommendations:

  • Silver Lynel Saber Horn (55 damage): Best-in-slot. Total 77 attack on a spear frame = highest DPS in the game.
  • Gleeok Horn (30-40 damage): Excellent if you have extras from sky battles. Total 52-62 attack.
  • Blue-Maned Lynel Saber Horn (35 damage): Accessible mid-game option. Total 57 attack.
  • Diamond (+25 attack): Clean, no special effects, but reliable. Total 47 attack.
  • Ruby / Sapphire / Topaz: Elemental infusion for specific enemy weaknesses.

3 Materials & Locations

🔱 Zora Spear

Type: Base weapon (Spear class) — 7 base attack, used as the crafting core
Where to Find: The Zora Spear has several reliable spawn locations in and around the Lanayru region:

Primary Source — Blue Lizalfos Drops: Blue Lizalfos throughout the Lanayru Wetlands and along the Zora River have approximately a 15% chance to drop a Zora Spear on defeat. Focus your farming on the camps between Zodobon Highlands and Oren Bridge. There are typically 3-4 Lizalfos per camp, so expect to clear 3-5 camps for a drop.

Weapon Rack Spawns (Post-Sludge): After completing the "Restoring the Zora's Domain" regional questline, check the weapon racks inside Zora's Domain. A Zora Spear respawns on the rack near Mogawak Shrine (0254, 0945, 0005) every Blood Moon cycle. This is the most reliable source once unlocked.

Lanayru Promenade Ruins: Search the collapsed buildings and hollow logs in the promenade area (coordinates approximately 0570, 0800, 0010). Weapons occasionally spawn in these ruins.

Enemy Camp Near Ruto Lake: A small Lizalfos encampment on the eastern shore of Ruto Lake (0680, 1100, 0025) has a chance to contain a Zora Spear-wielding enemy. Clear the camp and check drops.

Pro Tip: The Zora Spear is a common weapon. If you're struggling to find one, save your game before attacking a Lizalfos camp. If no spear drops, reload and try again. The save-scum method works because weapon drops are randomized on enemy spawn.

Drop Rate: ~15% from Blue Lizalfos in Lanayru. Guaranteed 1 per Blood Moon from Zora's Domain weapon rack (after quest completion).

💎 Diamond

Type: Rare gemstone — used for crafting and Fuse material
Where to Find: Diamonds are among the rarest materials in the game. Here's every reliable method:

1. Rare Ore Deposits (Best Method): These are the black rock outcroppings with visible gold veins. They have a ~10% chance to contain a Diamond when broken. The best farming locations are:
• Hebra Mountains: Climb the peaks around Hebra Peak (-0020, 3570, 0250) and search the cave entrances. Rare Ore Deposits respawn every Blood Moon.
• Gerudo Highlands: The mountain ridges southwest of Gerudo Town have multiple Rare Ore Deposit spawns.
• Death Mountain Depths: If you've unlocked the Depths, the rare ore deposits near Death Mountain's chasm have excellent drop rates.

2. Rare Stone Talus (Guaranteed Drops): The rare golden variant of Stone Talus always drops gems, with a high probability of Diamonds. Locations include:
• Hebra Mountains, near Kopeeki Drifts (0150, 3210, 0310)
• Gerudo Highlands, near Lapar Canyon (-2100, 1850, 0120)
• Sokkala Sky Archipelago (sky island, requires building a flying device)

3. Ore Deposit Farming with Yunobo: Yunobo's charge ability breaks ore deposits instantly. Pair him with a high-density area and clear every deposit in minutes.

4. Purchase from NPCs: After completing certain side quests, the traveling merchant Ramella in Goron City will buy your gems in bulk and occasionally sell Diamonds for 1,000 Rupees each. The Gerudo Town jewelry shop also sells Diamonds for 2,000 Rupees once you have access.

Drop Rate: ~10% per Rare Ore Deposit. 2-3 guaranteed from Rare Stone Talus. Purchased Diamonds are 100% reliable but expensive.

Pro Tip: Always bring a Stone Smasher or Boulder Breaker when ore farming. These weapons break ore deposits in a single hit. Bomb Flowers work too but are slower and consume resources.

⚡ Flint × 5

Type: Common mineral — used for crafting and starting fires
Where to Find: Flint is the easiest material on this list. You'll likely pick up 5+ just by playing the game normally, but if you need to farm specifically:

1. Standard Ore Deposits: The gray-brown rocks found on cliff faces, in caves, and along riverbeds. Every single one has a high chance to drop 1-2 Flint. Look along the path from Lookout Landing to Zora's Domain — there are dozens of ore deposits on the cliff sides of Lanayru Road and Zora River.

2. Stone Pebblits: These small rock enemies drop Flint consistently. Found in rocky terrain across Hyrule. One-hit kills with any hammer-type weapon.

3. Stone Talus (Regular): Defeating a standard Stone Talus typically yields 3-5 Flint among other drops. Multiple Talus locations exist along your route to Zora's Domain.

4. Enemy Camps: Many Bokoblin and Moblin camps have Flint in their supply crates. Break wooden boxes with a sword swing to reveal contents.

Recommended Farming Route: Start at Jiosin Shrine (-0240, 0050, 0020) near Lookout Landing. Head east along the main road. Every 200 meters or so, climb the nearest cliff and break 2-3 ore deposits. By the time you reach Zora's Domain, you'll have 10-15 Flint minimum.

Drop Rate: ~80% per standard Ore Deposit (1-2 Flint each). Guaranteed from Stone Pebblits. 3-5 per Stone Talus kill.

Pro Tip: Flint has weight. If your inventory is full, drop some apples or mushrooms. You need 5 for the trident, but grab 10-15 while you're out — you'll need more for the Boulder Breaker later.

4 Optimal Route

This route assumes you're starting from Lookout Landing with basic traversal abilities (Ascend, Ultrahand, Recall). Total estimated time: 45-90 minutes depending on combat encounters and whether you complete the Sludge questline along the way.

LOOKOUT LANDING (starting point)
  ↘ Head EAST through Lanayru Road gate
  ↓ [Collect 3-5 Flint from cliff-side ore deposits]
    ↘ Cross Rebonae Bridge into Lanayru Wetlands
    ↓ [Fight Lizalfos camps for Zora Spear drop]
      ↘ Follow Zora River northward (stay on east bank)
      ↓ [Loot ore deposits along river cliffs for extra Flint]
        ↘ Pass Oren Bridge & Zodobon Highlands
        ↓ [More Lizalfos camps = more Zora Spear chances]
          ↘ Ascend to Zora's Domain entrance
          ↓ Clear the Sludge questline (if not done)
            ↘ Meet Yona, speak to Sidon
            ↓ Complete Water Temple dungeon
              ↘ Return to Zora's Domain
              ↓ Find Dento at his workshop
                ↘ Hand over: 1x Zora Spear + 1x Diamond + 5x Flint
                ↓ LIGHTSCALE TRIDENT CRAFTED ✅

Step-by-Step Breakdown

Step 1: Lookout Landing to Lanayru Wetlands (10-15 min)

Exit Lookout Landing through the east gate. Follow the main road until you reach Rebonae Bridge. Along the way, keep an eye on the cliff faces to your left and right. Break every ore deposit you see — you'll need the Flint, and you might get lucky with a Diamond from a Rare Ore Deposit. The Lanayru Wetlands begin immediately after the bridge. The terrain becomes marshy, so use Ascend to bypass shallow water sections.

Step 2: Lizalfos Camp Hunting in Wetlands (10-20 min)

Your priority is finding a Zora Spear. There are 4-5 Lizalfos camps scattered throughout the wetlands. The most reliable locations are: (1) the camp on the small island at coordinates (0350, 0650, 0005), (2) the camp under the overpass near Tukarok Shrine (0420, 0720, 0010), and (3) the camp on the eastern edge near Ruto Lake (0680, 0800, 0020). Clear all enemies in each camp. If no Zora Spear drops after 3 camps, consider saving before engaging the next camp and reloading if the drop fails.

Step 3: Zora River Path (10-15 min)

Follow the Zora River northward. The east bank has fewer enemies and more ore deposits. You'll pass beneath waterfalls — use Ascend to climb through them quickly. At Oren Bridge, cross to the west side briefly to check a potential Lizalfos camp, then continue north. The terrain becomes more mountainous as you approach Zora's Domain.

Step 4: Entering Zora's Domain (5 min)

The entrance to Zora's Domain is a massive stone staircase carved into the mountainside. If this is your first visit, you'll trigger a cutscene showing the Sludge covering the city. Proceed up the stairs and enter the domain. Fast-travel to Ihen-a Shrine if you've already unlocked it.

Step 5: Sludge Questline (30-60 min, first visit only)

If this is your first time in Zora's Domain post-upheaval, you MUST complete the regional questline to access Dento. Speak to Yona near the statue of Mipha, then find Sidon at Mipha Court. This triggers the Water Temple dungeon — a spectacular sky-based dungeon featuring Sidon as your companion. After completing the temple and defeating the boss, return to Zora's Domain. The Sludge will be cleared.

Step 6: Craft at Dento's Workshop (2 min)

With the questline complete, Dento is accessible at his workshop on the middle tier of Zora's Domain, near the large waterwheel. Speak to him, select "Craft Lightscale Trident," and hand over your materials. Enjoy the cutscene and your new weapon.

5 Gotcha Tips

⚠ WEAPON SPECIFICITY

Dento ONLY accepts the specific "Zora Spear." He will NOT accept the Silverscale Spear, Fishing Harpoon, or any other spear-type weapon. The Zora Spear has a distinct appearance with blue Zoran wrapping on the shaft. Double-check your inventory — if you have a Silverscale Spear instead, it won't work. The Silverscale Spear is silver/white and has a different model entirely.

⚠ SLUDGE QUEST PREREQUISITE

You must clear the Sludge Around Zora's Domain questline to access Dento. On your first visit, the entire city is covered in Sludge. Dento's workshop is inaccessible, and he won't offer crafting services until the Sludge is cleared. This requires completing the full regional quest culminating in the Water Temple. Plan for 30-60 minutes of questing before you can craft. The quest is excellent and worth doing, but don't expect to walk in and craft immediately.

⚠ DIAMOND RARITY

Diamonds are genuinely rare. Don't expect to find one on your first ore deposit. Bring a Stone Smasher or Boulder Breaker (or at minimum, a strong two-handed weapon) when farming Rare Ore Deposits. Bomb Flowers work in a pinch but are resource-inefficient. If you've been saving Diamonds from earlier in the game, use one now. If not, budget 15-20 minutes of dedicated Rare Ore Deposit farming in Hebra or the Depths before heading to Zora's Domain.

⚠ DURABILITY WARNING

The Lightscale Trident can break. Unlike the Master Sword, this is not an unbreakable weapon. It has 70 durability points, which is above average for spears but still finite. The good news: you can re-craft it as many times as you want by bringing the same materials back to Dento. Keep a stockpile of Zora Spears and Diamonds so you never have to scramble for materials after your trident breaks mid-dungeon.

⚠ LIZALFOS COMBAT TIPS

Lizalfos in the Lanayru region are often found near water, making them harder to hit with fire-based attacks. They also have a habit of swimming away and spitting projectiles from a distance. Use electric attacks to stun them (water conducts electricity), or snipe from range with arrows. If a Lizalfos dives underwater, wait on the shore — they'll resurface eventually.

⚠ FAST TRAVEL SHORTCUT

If you've already unlocked Ihen-a Shrine inside Zora's Domain, you can skip the entire journey and fast-travel directly. This is incredibly useful for re-crafting the trident after it breaks. Always activate every shrine you pass — the fast-travel network is your most valuable resource in TOTK.

6 Free Benefits

The journey to craft the Lightscale Trident is packed with side rewards. Here's everything you gain along the way that makes this route one of the most efficient in the game:

📍 Zora's Domain Fast Travel Point

Activating Ihen-a Shrine inside Zora's Domain gives you a permanent fast-travel hub for the entire eastern Lanayru region. This is invaluable for later game activities, including reaching the Akkala region to the northeast and accessing multiple sky island launch points.

🎯 Sidon's Quest Chain

The Sludge questline introduces Sidon as a dungeon companion — one of the best companion experiences in the game. Completing it unlocks Sidon's Sage ability (a powerful water bubble shield), which you can summon anywhere using the Vow of Sidon, Key Item. This ability blocks projectiles, creates a protective barrier, and is essential for certain late-game shrine puzzles.

🐟 Abundant Fish Collection

The Lanayru region and Zora's Domain are the best fishing spots in the game. You'll find Hearty Bass, Hyrule Bass, Chillfin Trout, and Sizzlefin Trout in abundance. The Water Temple area has exclusive fish spawns. Stock up on Hearty Bass for powerful cooking recipes that grant temporary extra hearts.

👖 Zora Armor Set Pieces

During the Sludge questline, you'll obtain pieces of the Zora Armor set — chestpiece, greaves, and helm. This armor grants Swim Speed Up and, crucially, the ability to swim up waterfalls. The full set bonus (Swim Dash Stamina Up) is essential for water traversal and makes collecting certain sky island treasures dramatically easier.

⛪ Multiple Shrines

The route to Zora's Domain passes within activation distance of at least 6 shrines: Jiosin Shrine (near Lookout Landing), Tukarok Shrine (Lanayru Wetlands), Ihen-a Shrine (Zora's Domain), Mogawak Shrine (Zora's Domain vicinity), Jochi-ihiga Shrine (near the mountain path), and several sky island shrines accessible via Zora's Domain waterfalls. That's 6+ Light of Blessings toward your next Heart Container or Stamina Vessel.

💎 Ore and Gem Stockpile

Breaking ore deposits along the route yields not just Flint but also Amber, Opal, Rock Salt, and potentially more Diamonds. The Lanayru cliffs have surprisingly dense ore deposit clusters. One full run typically yields 10-15 Amber, 3-5 Opals, and various other minerals worth 500+ Rupees if sold.

📚 Regional Knowledge & Korok Seeds

The wetlands and riverbanks are dense with Korok puzzles. Expect to find 8-12 Korok Seeds along the main path if you're observant. These feed directly into weapon/shield/bow inventory expansion — arguably the most important early-game upgrade category.

🎯 Recommended Next: Scimitar of the Seven

With the Lightscale Trident in hand, your next logical step is obtaining the Scimitar of the Seven — Urbosa's curved blade in Gerudo Desert. You'll already have Diamond-farming experience and a surplus of Flint. Head west from Lookout Landing across Hyrule Field toward the scorching sands of Gerudo. The Scimitar packs 32 base attack with a unique lightning-charged dash attack that combos beautifully with the trident's piercing speed.
