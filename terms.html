Our Mission

Every game has those legendary items — the ones that take hours to craft, require materials from every corner of the map, and transform how you play. But finding complete, accurate guides for them is harder than it should be.

RelicTrek exists to solve that. We build step-by-step crafting guides that cover the complete journey: why the item is worth it, every material needed, exact locations and drop rates, the gotchas that waste your time, and the optimal route to get everything.
