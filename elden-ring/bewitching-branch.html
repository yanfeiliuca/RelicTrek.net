Bewitching Branch

Turn Enemies into Allies for 20 Seconds — One of the Rarest and Most Unique Items in the Game
★★★★★ Very Hard Type: Legendary Tool Stack: 5

✨ Why Bewitching Branch Matters

The Bewitching Branch is one of the most unique and rare items in all of Elden Ring. Its effect is genuinely one-of-a-kind: when used on an enemy, that enemy becomes charmed and turns on its allies for 20 seconds. Imagine a room full of powerful foes, and suddenly the strongest one among them switches sides — the tactical value is immeasurable. There is simply nothing else in the game that replicates this effect. It is chaos incarnate, packaged as a consumable.

This item is not just a combat tool — it is a puzzle solution. Certain encounters in Elden Ring are designed with charm mechanics in mind as a intended simplification strategy. When facing two powerful enemies simultaneously, converting one to attack the other instantly turns a 1v2 disadvantage into a 2v1 advantage. The satisfaction of this reversal is unmatched by any other item in the game.

When to Use Bewitching Branch

  • Dual-Boss Encounters: When facing two powerful enemies, charm one and watch them fight each other while you pick off the survivor.
  • Invasion Chaos: When invading, charm a powerful world enemy to attack the host. Nothing creates panic like a suddenly-hostile knight turning on its "allies."
  • Hard Zone Enemy Reduction: In enemy-dense areas, charm the strongest foe first and let them thin the herd.
  • Tactical Experimentation: Charm different enemy types and watch them fight each other — this is entertainment in itself.
  • Questline Requirements: Certain NPC questlines may require or recommend using Bewitching Branches.

Understanding the Charm Mechanic

The 20-second charm duration is an eternity in Elden Ring combat. A charmed enemy will: actively attack its former allies; draw enemy aggro (functioning as a temporary Spirit Ash); and retain its full attack power and HP — meaning a powerful enemy you charm deals far more damage than any summon. A charmed knight can kill three or four lesser enemies before falling. A charmed troll can demolish an entire encampment. The value scales directly with the strength of the enemy you charm — always target the strongest available foe.

Comparison with Other Summon/Support Options

  • vs. Spirit Ashes: Ashes require FP and are limited to what you have collected. Bewitching Branch requires no FP and can use any enemy you encounter.
  • vs. NPC Summon Signs: Only available at specific boss doors. Bewitching Branch works anywhere there are enemies.
  • vs. Other Status Effects: No other item provides this exact effect. Bewitching Branch is truly unique.
  • vs. Direct Combat: Sometimes charming a powerful enemy is more efficient than fighting it yourself — especially when outnumbered.

The FOMO Factor

The Bewitching Branch recipe comes from Frenzied's Cookbook in Mohgwyn Palace — one of the game's most secret and highest-level areas. Many players never reach Mohgwyn Palace at all, let alone find the Cookbook. The item's uniqueness and rarity mean the vast majority of first-playthrough players have no idea it exists. When you see a video of someone making two bosses fight each other with a Bewitching Branch, the "why did I not know about this" feeling is intense. And because the effect is so unique and genuinely fun, missing it means missing one of Elden Ring's most creative mechanics.

🧪 Crafting Recipe

The Bewitching Branch recipe requires two of the rarest materials in the game: Miquella's Lily and Sacramental Bud. Both are among the most limited crafting materials in the entire Lands Between, which is why Bewitching Branches are so precious.

MaterialQuantityRarityFarmable
🌸 Miquella's Lily 1x Extremely Rare (Limited Spawns) ⚠️ Limited — Finite spawns
🌺 Sacramental Bud 1x Rare (Danger Zones) ✅ Yes — Limited respawns

Yield

Each crafting attempt produces 1x Bewitching Branch. The stack limit is only 5 — a deliberate balance restriction for such a powerful effect. Every Bewitching Branch should be used thoughtfully because material replenishment is extremely difficult.

⚠️ Recipe Key Notes

  • Charm duration is exactly 20 seconds — the charmed enemy fights its former allies with full commitment.
  • Does not work on bosses — only affects regular and elite enemies.
  • Stack limit of 5 is hard-capped by the game — cannot stockpile beyond this.
  • Material supply is genuinely limited — Miquella's Lily spawn points are extremely scarce.
  • Visual indicator: Charmed enemies have a pink particle effect above their heads.

📖 Required Cookbook: Frenzied's Cookbook

To unlock the Bewitching Branch recipe, you must acquire Frenzied's Cookbook. This Cookbook is located in Mohgwyn Palace — the blood dynasty's palace, one of the most secret and dangerous zones in the entire game.

📖 Frenzied's Cookbook

Location: Mohgwyn Palace, blood dynasty area

Coordinates: On a corpse in the palace interior, near the dynasty's central altar structures

Recipe Unlocked: Bewitching Branch

Also Unlocks: Other frenzy-themed utility recipes

How to Reach Mohgwyn Palace

Mohgwyn Palace is a hidden underground zone with two primary entrances. The most common method is through White Mask Varré's questline. Alternatively, find the teleporter gate in the western Consecrated Snowfield.

Method 1 — Varré's Questline (Recommended):

Step 1: Meet White Mask Varré at the First Step site of grace in Limgrave.

Step 2: Progress to the Rose Church in Liurnia (south of the Academy). Varré will be there.

Step 3: Complete three invasions using the Festering Bloody Finger he provides. You do not need to win — just complete three invasion attempts.

Step 4: Return to Varré. He gives you the Pureblood Knight's Medal, which teleports you directly to Mohgwyn Palace.

Method 2 — Snowfield Portal:

Step 1: Obtain both halves of the Haligtree Secret Medallion (one from Village of the Albinaurics, one from Castle Sol).

Step 2: Use the medallion at the Grand Lift of Rold to access the Consecrated Snowfield.

Step 3: Find the teleporter gate in the western snowfield. It transports you to Mohgwyn Palace.

Mohgwyn Palace Hazards

  • • Albinaurics: Massive numbers with rapid bleed buildup. They swarm aggressively.
  • • Blood Knights: Heavy knights with strong attacks and bleed infliction.
  • • Blood swamp: The ground itself builds bleed status. Equip immunity gear.
  • • Mohg, Lord of Blood (Boss): The demigod at the palace's depths — an extremely powerful and spectacular boss fight.
  • • High-level zone: Enemy scaling matches late-game areas. Come prepared.

Finding Frenzied's Cookbook in Mohgwyn Palace

Frenzied's Cookbook is in Mohgwyn Palace's interior — not at the entrance blood swamp, but inside the palace structures. From the Palace Approach Ledge-Road grace, push deeper into the palace. The Cookbook is on a corpse near the central altar structures. You must pass through Albinauric-dense areas to reach it — use stealth or ride Torrent quickly through. The corpse is on an elevated platform near the dynasty's ritual sites.

🌿 Materials & Farm Locations

Bewitching Branch's two materials are among the rarest crafting materials in the game. Miquella's Lily has limited spawn points, and Sacramental Bud, while farmable, is difficult. Your Bewitching Branch production is strictly limited — each one is a precious strategic resource.

🌸 Material 1: Miquella's Lily

🌸

Miquella's Lily ★★★★★ Extremely Rare — Finite Spawns

Miquella's Lily is one of the rarest crafting materials in the entire game. This pure white lily only spawns in specific locations related to Miquella, and the total number of spawns is finite — it cannot be infinitely farmed like ordinary materials. Every Miquella's Lily should be treasured and used carefully.

Known Miquella's Lily Spawn Locations

🥇 #1: Haligtree Area — Most Concentrated Source

Miquella's Haligtree is the most concentrated source — 5–8 lilies can be found in this massive late-game zone. But reaching the Haligtree requires endgame progression through the Hidden Path to Haligtree dungeon. This is the final major zone of the game.

🥈 #2: Consecrated Snowfield — Scattered Spawns

Consecrated Snowfield has 2–3 scattered lilies. This zone itself requires the Secret Medallion to access, making it late-game content as well.

🥉 #3: Various Locations Across the Lands Between

A small number of lilies can be found at other Miquella-related sites — certain churches and underground areas. The total global supply is extremely limited.

Miquella's Lily Critical Notes

  • ⚠️ Finite global supply: The total number of Miquella's Lily spawns across the entire game is limited. It cannot be infinitely farmed.
  • Plan every use: Each lily equals one Bewitching Branch. Think twice before crafting.
  • Do not waste on trivial encounters. Save Bewitching Branches for truly worthy moments.
  • New Game+ refreshes all material spawns — this is how you replenish your supply.

🌺 Material 2: Sacramental Bud

🌺

Sacramental Bud ★★★ Rare — Dangerous Zones

Sacramental Bud is a dark crimson bud that spawns in specific dangerous zones — primarily around Caelid's Divine Tower and Sellia, Town of Sorcery. While respawnable, spawn points are limited and the zones are dangerous.

Best Sacramental Bud Farm Locations

🥇 #1: Caelid Divine Tower — Exterior Grounds

Caelid Divine Tower exterior — 2–4 spawn points around the tower grounds. From the Divine Tower of Caelid: Basement grace, collect buds in the surrounding ruins.

🥈 #2: Sellia, Town of Sorcery

Sellia, Town of Sorcery — has Sacramental Bud spawns. Light three braziers to unseal the town. Beware invisible assassins.

Sacramental Bud Farming Tips

  • Respawns on grace rest — infinitely farmable, but low yield per run.
  • Caelid enemies are high-level — recommend level 40+.
  • Equip Discovery-boosting items for better farming efficiency.

⚠️ Gotcha Tips — What Can Go Wrong

Bewitching Branches are among the game's most precious items. A single mistake in usage can be devastating. Here is the complete warning list.

💎 Gotcha 1: Miquella's Lily Is FINITE — Use Wisely

This bears repeating: Miquella's Lily has a finite global supply. The number of Bewitching Branches you can craft per playthrough is capped. Each branch should be a deliberate strategic decision, not a casual toy. Save for: critical invasion moments, dual-elite encounters, and maximum entertainment value (making two trolls fight each other).

🚫 Gotcha 2: Does Not Work on Bosses

Bewitching Branch does not work on boss enemies. Do not waste this precious item attempting to charm a boss — you will lose the branch with zero effect. It only works on regular enemies and named mini-bosses (but not full boss encounters).

⏱️ Gotcha 3: 20 Seconds Feels Long But Is Not

Twenty seconds is long in combat — enough for a charmed enemy to kill multiple allies. But its ending is abrupt: your "ally" suddenly reverts to enemy, possibly right next to you. Track the timer and create distance before the charm expires to avoid a nasty surprise.

🎯 Gotcha 4: Choose Your Target Carefully

Charming the strongest enemy is not always optimal. Sometimes a fast-attacking enemy is better because it disrupts more frequently. Or charm a shield-bearing enemy to absorb damage. Adapt your target selection to the battlefield, not just enemy power level.

🗺️ Gotcha 5: Mohgwyn Palace Access Is Complicated

Mohgwyn Palace access requires either invasion functionality (online play) or both Secret Medallion halves (including defeating the Castle Sol boss). Neither is trivial. If you cannot invade, the Snowfield route is a lengthy process in itself.

📦 Gotcha 6: Stack Limit of 5 Is Strict

Even with abundant materials, you can only carry 5 Bewitching Branches. This is a hard cap. You must use some to make room for more — the game forces you to spend this resource rather than hoard it indefinitely.

Farming Caelid (Sacramental Bud)

Long-Term Strategic Value — Why Bewitching Branch Changes Everything

Mastering Bewitching Branch means you have completed one of Elden Ring's toughest challenges: reaching Mohgwyn Palace and the Haligtree. You are a master of the game. More importantly, the Bewitching Branch opens an entirely new tactical dimension — no longer just hacking and slashing, but manipulating the battlefield, turning enemy against enemy, and watching chaos unfold while you sit back and enjoy the show.

The Bewitching Branch represents the pinnacle of Elden Ring's design philosophy: give the player a unique, powerful, and genuinely fun tool, then balance it through rarity and acquisition difficulty. When you finally gather enough materials to craft your first branch, use it in a difficult encounter, and watch an enemy that was about to kill you turn to fight for you instead — the sense of triumph and pure joy is unmatched. This is Elden Ring's magic: explore, discover, prepare, and then unleash your creativity at the perfect moment.
