25 million Tarnished. The Lands Between hides 60+ Cookbooks across 6 regions — each unlocking recipes that can turn the tide of battle. Every cookbook location, every material farm, every hidden merchant.

Gold-Pickled Fowl Foot

★★★☆☆ Early Game

+30% rune gain for 3 minutes. Four-Toed Fowl Foot + Rowa Fruit + Gold Firefly. The essential farming consumable.

Exalted Flesh

★★★★☆ Mid Game

+20% physical damage for 60 seconds. Arteria Leaf + Rowa Fruit + Lump of Flesh. The melee DPS buff.

Uplifting Aromatic

★★★★★ Late Game

Bubble that negates one hit for entire party. Altus Bloom + Budding Cave Moss + Silver Tear Husk. Co-op essential.

Bloodboil Aromatic

★★★★☆ Mid Game

+30% attack, -25% defense. Bloodrose + Altus Bloom. The risk/reward DPS glass cannon buff.

Ironjar Aromatic

★★★★☆ Mid Game

Turn body to metal — super armor, reduced damage, deflect attacks. Altus Bloom + Living Jar Shard + Miranda Powder.

Preserving Boluses

★★★★☆ Mid Game

Cures and prevents scarlet rot. Crystal Cave Moss + Dewkissed Herba + Sacramental Bud. Essential for Caelid.

Drawstring Blood Grease

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

Quick-apply blood loss to weapon. Root Resin + Bloodrose + String. Fastest bleed application.

Spark Aromatic

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

AoE lightning damage around player. Altus Bloom + Fulgurbloom. Anti-swarm tool.

Warming Stone

★★★☆☆ Late Game

Healing zone that restores HP over time. Sanctuary Stone + Erdleaf Flower. Mountaintops of the Giants essential.

Bewitching Branch

★★★★★ Late Game

Turn enemies into allies for 20 seconds. Miquella's Lily + Sacramental Bud. Mohgwyn Palace farm required.
