艾尔登法环

2500万褪色者。交界地隐藏着60多本制作笔记，分布在6个区域——每本都解锁能扭转战局的配方。每本制作笔记位置、每个材料农场、每个隐藏商人。

黄金鸡爪

★★★☆☆ 前期

+30% rune gain持续3分钟. Four-Toed Fowl Foot + Rowa Fruit + Gold Firefly. 必备刷魂消耗品.

勇猛肉块

★★★★☆ 中期

+20% physical damage for 60 seconds. Arteria Leaf + Rowa Fruit + Lump of Flesh. 近战DPS增益.

振奋香药

★★★★★ 后期

Bubble that negates one hit for entire party. Altus Bloom + Budding Cave Moss + Silver Tear Husk. 联机必备.

狂热香药

★★★★☆ 中期

+30% attack, -25% defense. Bloodrose + Altus Bloom. 高风险高回报的玻璃大炮增益.

铁壶香药

★★★★☆ 中期

Turn body to metal — super armor, reduced damage, deflect attacks. Altus Bloom + Living Jar Shard + Miranda Powder.

腐败苔药

★★★★☆ 中期

Cures and prevents scarlet rot. Crystal Cave Moss + Dewkissed Herba + Sacramental Bud. 盖利德必备.

缚血油脂

★★★☆☆ 中期

Quick-apply blood loss to weapon. Root Resin + Bloodrose + String. 最快出血附魔.

火花香药

★★★☆☆ 中期

AoE lightning damage around player. Altus Bloom + Fulgurbloom. 反群怪工具.

暖石

★★★☆☆ 后期

Healing zone that restores HP over time. Sanctuary Stone + Erdleaf Flower. 巨人山顶必备.

魅惑树枝

★★★★★ 后期

将敌人转化为盟友 for 20 seconds. 米凯拉的百合+圣杯蓓蕾. 需要蒙格温宫殿farm.
