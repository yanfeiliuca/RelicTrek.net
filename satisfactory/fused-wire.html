Fused Wire & Quickwire

Difficulty: ★★★☆☆
Alternate Recipe Tier 5 Assembler Dual Output Caterium Required

1 Why This Recipe Matters

The Fused Wire alternate recipe is one of the most elegantly efficient production methods in Satisfactory. By combining Copper Ingots and Caterium Ingots in an Assembler, you produce Wire at triple the standard rate AND simultaneously create Quickwire as a bonus output. This is a true dual-production recipe — one machine, two critical resources, both at enhanced efficiency. Wire is the backbone of your electrical infrastructure: Cables, Circuit Boards, Stators, Motors, and countless other components all depend on it. Quickwire is the key to advanced electronics: AI Limiters, High-Speed Connectors, and eventually Supercomputers and Radio Control Units. Having both come from a single production line is a logistical dream come true.

Production Efficiency Gain

The default Wire recipe converts 1 Copper Ingot into 3 Wire in a Constructor. The Fused Wire recipe converts 2 Copper Ingots + 1 Caterium Ingot into 9 Wire + 3 Quickwire in an Assembler. Let's break this down per-ingot: the default recipe gives you 3 Wire per Copper Ingot. The Fused recipe gives you 4.5 Wire per Copper Ingot (9 Wire / 2 Copper) PLUS 3 Quickwire per Caterium Ingot. The Wire yield alone is 50% better per copper ingot, and the Quickwire output is pure bonus — you're getting a premium electronics resource essentially for free.

When to Unlock

Fused Wire becomes available at Tier 5, which requires you to have completed the Oil Processing milestone. However, the recipe also requires Caterium Ingots, which means you need to have found Caterium Ore and completed the Caterium research chain in the MAM. Caterium nodes are primarily found in the Dune Desert, Red Bamboo Fields, and some Rocky Desert areas — all biomes that require Tier 3-4 equipment to reach safely.

The practical earliest use of Fused Wire is typically in the mid-to-late Tier 5 timeframe, after you've established both oil production and Caterium mining. By this point, your Wire and Quickwire demands are scaling rapidly as you build more advanced electronics. The recipe arrives at the perfect moment to streamline your supply chain. Instead of building separate Wire and Quickwire factories, you consolidate into a single, more efficient production line.

Scaling to 100 Wire + 50 Quickwire Per Minute

To produce 100 Wire and 50 Quickwire per minute with default recipes, you need approximately 34 Wire Constructors (processing 34 Copper Ingots) and 17 Quickwire Constructors (processing 17 Caterium Ingots), for a total of 51 Constructors drawing 204 MW. With Fused Wire, you need only 17 Assemblers, consuming 34 Copper Ingots and 17 Caterium Ingots, drawing 255 MW. The power draw is slightly higher per machine, but the machine count drops from 51 to 17 — a 67% reduction. The real savings are in factory space, belt complexity, and maintenance overhead.

Comparison with Other Wire Recipes

The only other Wire alternate recipe is Iron Wire (Tier 1-2), which produces Wire from Iron Ingots instead of Copper. Iron Wire is useful as a temporary bridge when copper is scarce, but it is less efficient than even the default Copper Wire recipe. Fused Wire is the definitive late-game Wire solution — there is no better recipe for Wire production in the entire game. Once you have Fused Wire, you should never use the default Wire recipe again.

3 Hard Drive Acquisition

The Fused Wire alternate recipe is obtained by researching Hard Drives in the MAM. Hard Drives are physical items found at Crash Sites scattered across the map. The Fused Wire recipe appears in the Tier 5 alternate recipe pool, which is accessible from Crash Sites in mid-to-late game biomes. Because this recipe requires Caterium as an input, you'll likely be exploring Caterium-rich biomes anyway, making the Hard Drive hunt a natural extension of your resource exploration.

Recipe Pool Information

The Fused Wire recipe is part of the Tier 5 alternate recipe pool. This pool includes: Fused Wire, Pure Iron Ingot, Pure Copper Ingot, Polyester Fabric, Radio Control Unit, and several others. With approximately 20-25 recipes in this pool, your odds of seeing Fused Wire on any given Hard Drive are roughly 1 in 7 to 1 in 10. Save scumming is recommended if you're specifically targeting this recipe.

⚠ Recipe Pool Tip

Since Fused Wire requires Caterium, you'll be visiting Caterium-rich biomes anyway. Make the most of your expedition: bring a portable Miner and 10+ Portable Miners to tap every Caterium node you find. Also bring materials to build a small MAM research station (power, biomass burners) so you can start the Caterium research chain while you're in the field. The 10-minute MAM research time passes while you're exploring nearby Crash Sites.

Tier Requirements

Fused Wire is gated to Tier 5 for two reasons. First, the recipe appears in the Tier 5 Hard Drive pool, which is only accessible from mid-to-late game biomes. Second, Caterium processing requires MAM research that is typically completed around Tier 4-5. While you can find Caterium Ore earlier, the full processing chain (Ore → Ingot → Quickwire) requires MAM unlocks that most pioneers don't complete until they've established coal power and basic steel production.

5 Gotcha Tips

⚠ Caterium is a Limited Resource

Copper Alloy Ingots to stretch your copper further." data-zh="与地图上随处可见的铜不同，锣矿节点相对稀少。整个地图上只有约15-20个锣矿节点，而且很多在危险生态区。在投入熔合线生产之前，调查你可用的锣矿节点并计算长期的快速线需求。如果锣矿稀缺，可以考虑只将熔合线用于关键的快速线生产，同时用默认配方或铜合金锭补充线的生产。">Unlike Copper, which is abundant across the entire map, Caterium nodes are relatively rare. There are only about 15-20 Caterium nodes on the entire map, and many are in dangerous biomes. Before committing to Fused Wire production, survey your available Caterium nodes and calculate your long-term Quickwire needs. If Caterium is scarce in your area, consider using Fused Wire only for critical Quickwire production and supplementing Wire with the default recipe or Copper Alloy Ingots to stretch your copper further.

⚠ Use a Programmable Splitter for Dual Outputs

The Fused Wire Assembler produces both Wire and Quickwire from the same machine. If your downstream factories need different ratios of Wire to Quickwire, use a Programmable Splitter (Tier 5) to precisely control how much of each output goes to each destination. Without a Programmable Splitter, you'll need to manually balance splitters, which can be tricky when the output ratio is 3:1 (Wire:Quickwire). A common setup is: Wire output goes to Cable and Circuit Board factories, while Quickwire output goes directly to AI Limiter and High-Speed Connector production.

⚠ Combine with Stitched Iron Plate

Stitched Iron Plate recipe requires Wire instead of Screws for Reinforced Iron Plates. When you have Fused Wire running, your Wire supply becomes abundant and essentially free (since you're mainly after the Quickwire). This creates a beautiful synergy: Fused Wire feeds Wire into Stitched Iron Plate production, eliminating screws AND providing bonus Quickwire for advanced electronics. When you have Fused Wire running, your Wire supply becomes abundant and essentially free (since you're mainly after the Quickwire). This creates a beautiful synergy: Fused Wire feeds Wire into Stitched Iron Plate production, eliminating screws AND providing bonus Quickwire for advanced electronics. It's one of the most satisfying recipe pairings in the game.

⚠ Power Cost is Higher Per Machine

An Assembler running Fused Wire draws 15 MW, compared to 4 MW for a Constructor running default Wire. However, one Fused Assembler replaces three Wire Constructors (12 MW total) AND provides Quickwire bonus. When you factor in the eliminated Quickwire Constructors, the power math actually favors Fused Wire at scale. At 100 Wire + 50 Quickwire per minute, default recipes need 51 Constructors (204 MW) while Fused needs 17 Assemblers (255 MW). The 25% power increase is more than offset by the 67% machine reduction and infinitely simpler logistics.

6 Free Benefits

Other Recipes from the Same Hard Drive Pool

When you hunt for Fused Wire in Tier 5 biomes, you'll unlock numerous other valuable recipes:

🔗 Pure Ingots (All Three)

Pure Iron, Pure Copper, and Pure Caterium Ingots all use Water in the Refinery to double ore yield. Since Fused Wire requires both Copper and Caterium, Pure Copper and Pure Caterium directly support your Fused Wire production line. Unlock Tier: 5.

🔗 Diluted Fuel

Combines Heavy Oil Residue with Water in a Refinery for 2x Fuel production. Uses the same Refinery + Water infrastructure you'll build for Pure Ingots. Unlock Tier: 5.

⚡ Polyester Fabric

An alternate recipe for Fabric using Polymer Resin instead of Biomass. Useful for Filter production and the AWESOME Sink. Unlock Tier: 5.

⚡ Radio Control Unit (Alternate)

Simplifies Radio Control Unit production for Tier 7+ communications equipment. A natural extension of your Quickwire production chain. Unlock Tier: 5-6.
