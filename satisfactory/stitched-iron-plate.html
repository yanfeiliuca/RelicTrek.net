Stitched Iron Plate

Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

Production Efficiency Gain

The default Reinforced Iron Plate recipe requires 6 Iron Plates and 12 Screws to produce 1 Reinforced Iron Plate in an Assembler. Producing those 12 Screws requires an Iron Rod Constructor feeding a Screw Constructor, which in turn requires Iron Ingots from a Smelter. The full default chain is: Iron Ore → Smelter → Iron Ingot → Constructor (Plate) = 6 Plates. AND: Iron Ore → Smelter → Iron Ingot → Constructor (Rod) → Constructor (Screw) = 12 Screws. Then both feed into an Assembler. That's 4 machines just for the screw portion of ONE Reinforced Iron Plate.

The Stitched Iron Plate recipe changes this entirely. It requires 6 Iron Plates and 15 Wire to produce 2 Reinforced Iron Plates. Wire is made directly from Copper Ingots in a Constructor — no rod step, no screw step. The full chain becomes: Iron Ore → Smelter → Iron Ingot → Constructor (Plate) = 6 Plates. AND: Copper Ore → Smelter → Copper Ingot → Constructor (Wire) = 15 Wire. Both feed into an Assembler producing 2 Reinforced Iron Plates. You've eliminated the Iron Rod Constructor and the Screw Constructor entirely, and you're producing DOUBLE the output.

When to Unlock

Cast Screws, this recipe is available from the earliest Crash Sites in the Grass Fields starting area. You can potentially obtain it within your first few hours of gameplay. The recipe is usable immediately — you need nothing more than an Assembler (Tier 2), Iron Plates, and Wire, all of which are available from the very beginning of the game." data-zh="一旦达到T1并解锁MAM，你就应该优先解锁缝合铁板。像铸造螺丝一样，该配方可以从草原起始区域的最早坠机地点获得。你可能在游戏前几小时内就获得它。该配方可以立即使用——你只需要组装器（T2）、铁板和线，这些都是游戏一开始就有的。">You should prioritize unlocking Stitched Iron Plate as soon as you reach Tier 1 and gain access to the MAM. Like Cast Screws, this recipe is available from the earliest Crash Sites in the Grass Fields starting area. You can potentially obtain it within your first few hours of gameplay. The recipe is usable immediately — you need nothing more than an Assembler (Tier 2), Iron Plates, and Wire, all of which are available from the very beginning of the game.

The ideal timing is immediately after you unlock the Assembler at Tier 2. Before Tier 2, you can't craft Reinforced Iron Plates at all (they require an Assembler), so there's no rush. But the moment you place your first Assembler, every Reinforced Iron Plate you make should use the Stitched recipe. The savings compound rapidly: by Tier 3, you will have crafted hundreds of Reinforced Iron Plates, and the Stitched recipe saves you thousands of screws and dozens of machine-hours.

Scaling to 50 Reinforced Iron Plates Per Minute

To produce 50 Reinforced Iron Plates per minute with the default recipe, you need 10 Assemblers, 30 Iron Plate Constructors, 30 Iron Rod Constructors, and 30 Screw Constructors, consuming 600 Iron Ore per minute. With Stitched Iron Plate, you need only 10 Assemblers, 15 Iron Plate Constructors, and 15 Wire Constructors, consuming 300 Iron Ore and 150 Copper Ore per minute. The machine count drops from 100 to 40, and Iron Ore consumption is cut in half. The only 'cost' is Copper Ore, which is abundant in early-game biomes.

3 Hard Drive Acquisition

The Stitched Iron Plate alternate recipe is obtained by researching Hard Drives in the MAM. Hard Drives are physical items found at Crash Sites scattered across the map. The Stitched Iron Plate recipe appears in the Tier 1-2 alternate recipe pool, making it one of the earliest and most accessible alternate recipes in the game.

Recipe Pool Information

The Stitched Iron Plate recipe is part of the Tier 1-2 alternate recipe pool. This pool includes: Stitched Iron Plate, Cast Screws, Iron Wire, Biomass (Wood), Biomass (Leaves), Concrete (alternate), Copper Rotor, and several others. With approximately 15-20 recipes in the pool, your odds of seeing Stitched Iron Plate on your first Hard Drive are roughly 1 in 5 to 1 in 7. Given how accessible early Crash Sites are, most pioneers can obtain this recipe within 1-3 Hard Drive attempts.

⚠ Recipe Pool Tip

Stitched Iron Plate and Cast Screws are in the SAME recipe pool. If you get one early, your odds of getting the other improve because the pool shrinks. Prioritize visiting ALL accessible Tier 1-2 Crash Sites in the Grass Fields and Rocky Desert before moving to higher-tier biomes. The 15-20 early recipes can all be obtained from these easy-to-reach locations, and both Stitched Iron Plate and Cast Screws should be among your first unlocks.

5 Gotcha Tips

⚠ Save Scumming is Worth It Here

Because Stitched Iron Plate is in the early recipe pool and the pool is relatively small (15-20 recipes), save scumming is extremely effective. Save before inserting your first Hard Drive into the MAM. If you don't see Stitched Iron Plate among the three options, reload and try again. With patience, you can guarantee this recipe on your very first Hard Drive, setting your entire factory up for success from hour one.

⚠ Reinforced Iron Plate Demand Never Stops

Don't think of Stitched Iron Plate as just an early-game convenience. Reinforced Iron Plates are used in Conveyor Belt Mk.2, Conveyor Lifts, Jump Pads, the AWESOME Shop, and numerous decorative items. Even at Tier 8, you'll be building infrastructure that needs Reinforced Iron Plates. Having the Stitched recipe means every single one of those plates costs half the iron and zero screws. Over a 100+ hour playthrough, the cumulative savings are staggering.

6 Free Benefits

Other Recipes from the Same Hard Drive Pool

When you hunt for Stitched Iron Plate, you'll unlock several other valuable early-game recipes:

🔗 Cast Screws

The perfect pairing with Stitched Iron Plate. While Stitched eliminates screws for plates, Cast Screws makes all remaining screw applications 4x more efficient. Both are Tier 1-2 and in the same recipe pool. Unlock both ASAP.

⚡ Iron Wire

Produces Wire from Iron Ingots instead of Copper. Less efficient than copper wire but invaluable when copper nodes are far away. Useful as a bridge until you establish proper copper supply lines. Unlock Tier: 1-2.

⚡ Biomass (Wood) & Biomass (Leaves)

Doubles biomass yield from wood and leaves. Essential for early power generation before coal. More biomass per tree means fewer trees chopped and less refueling time. Unlock Tier: 1.

⚡ Copper Rotor

Replaces Steel Pipes with Copper Sheets in Rotor production. A useful mid-game alternative when steel is scarce but copper is abundant. Unlock Tier: 2-3.
