Silicon Circuit Board

Difficulty: ★★★★☆
Alternate Recipe Tier 7 Assembler No Plastic MAM Research

1 Why This Recipe Matters

The Silicon Circuit Board alternate recipe is one of the most transformative unlocks in the late game of Satisfactory. At Tier 7, when you're producing hundreds of Circuit Boards per minute for Computers, Supercomputers, Radio Control Units, and Adaptive Control Units, the standard recipe's dependence on Plastic becomes a critical bottleneck. Every Circuit Board made the default way consumes 4 Plastic, which means your entire oil processing line is under constant strain. The Silicon Circuit Board recipe completely eliminates this dependency, replacing Plastic with Silica — a resource made from Raw Quartz that is abundant and easy to automate.

Production Efficiency Gain

The default Circuit Board recipe requires 2 Copper Sheets and 4 Plastic to produce 1 Circuit Board in an Assembler. At scale, this means a Computer factory consuming 50 Circuit Boards per minute needs 100 Copper Sheets and 200 Plastic per minute. That 200 Plastic alone requires a significant portion of your oil production, competing with Rubber for tires, Fuel for power, and Petroleum Coke for aluminum processing.

The Silicon Circuit Board recipe replaces the 4 Plastic with 8 Silica and adds 8 Copper Sheets (up from 2), producing 4 Circuit Boards per cycle. Let's compare per-Circuit-Board costs: the default recipe uses 2 Copper Sheets + 4 Plastic per board, while Silicon Circuit Board uses 2 Copper Sheets + 2 Silica per board. The Plastic cost disappears entirely, replaced by Silica which is made from Raw Quartz — a completely separate resource chain that doesn't compete with your oil infrastructure.

When to Unlock

Silicon Circuit Board becomes available at Tier 7, which requires you to have completed the Bauxite Refinement milestone and unlocked the MAM's Quartz research chain. Specifically, you need to have researched Quartz in the MAM to unlock Silica production before this recipe becomes useful. The earliest you can realistically use Silicon Circuit Board is after establishing Raw Quartz mining and Silica processing, which typically happens in the mid-to-late Tier 6 timeframe.

By Tier 7, your factory is likely consuming massive amounts of Plastic for Circuit Boards, High-Speed Connectors, and various other products. This is the perfect time to make the switch. Silicon Circuit Boards free up your oil refineries to focus on Fuel, Rubber, and other petroleum products while your Quartz nodes handle the electronics supply chain. The logistical separation of your electronics and petrochemical lines makes your entire factory more modular and easier to expand.

Silica Supply Chain

Silica is produced from Raw Quartz in a Constructor. The default Silica recipe converts 3 Raw Quartz into 5 Silica with no byproducts. Raw Quartz nodes are found primarily in the Dune Desert, Red Bamboo Fields, and Rocky Desert biomes. A normal Raw Quartz node produces 60 ore per minute, which converts to 100 Silica per minute — enough to supply 12.5 Silicon Circuit Board Assemblers, producing 50 Circuit Boards per minute.

Scaling to 100 Circuit Boards Per Minute

To produce 100 Silicon Circuit Boards per minute, you need 25 Assemblers (each producing 4 per minute at 100% clock speed), consuming 200 Copper Sheets and 200 Silica per minute. The Silica demand requires 6.25 Constructors processing Raw Quartz, which in turn needs approximately 3.75 normal Raw Quartz nodes (or fewer pure nodes with overclocking). The Copper Sheet demand requires about 13.3 Assemblers making Copper Sheets from Copper Ingots, which needs roughly 267 Copper Ingots per minute — approximately 4.5 normal Copper nodes.

3 Hard Drive Acquisition

The Silicon Circuit Board alternate recipe is obtained by researching Hard Drives in the MAM. Hard Drives are physical items found at Crash Sites scattered across the map. Each Crash Site contains exactly one Hard Drive, and each Hard Drive unlocks one alternate recipe from a randomized pool. The Silicon Circuit Board recipe appears in the Tier 7+ recipe pool, which is only accessible from Crash Sites in late-game biomes.

Recipe Pool Information

The Silicon Circuit Board recipe is part of the Tier 7+ alternate recipe pool. When you research a Hard Drive from a Crash Site in these biomes, the MAM will present you with three random recipes from the pool. The Tier 7+ pool includes: Silicon Circuit Board, Supercomputer (alternate), Turbo Electric Motor, Fused Modular Frame, Heat Sink, Cooling System, and numerous other endgame recipes. Because the selection is random and the pool is large, targeted save scumming is highly recommended.

⚠ Recipe Pool Tip

The Tier 7+ recipe pool is one of the largest in the game, with 30+ recipes. Your chances of seeing Silicon Circuit Board on any given Hard Drive are approximately 1 in 10. Save before inserting each Hard Drive and reload if you don't see it. Alternatively, gather 5-10 Hard Drives from Tier 7+ biomes before researching any of them, then research them all in sequence — your odds improve as the pool shrinks with each unlock.

Tier Requirements

Silicon Circuit Board requires Tier 7 because it depends on both the Assembler building (Tier 2, already available) and Silica production from Raw Quartz (Tier 7 MAM research). While the building requirement is low, the resource chain is what gates this recipe to the late game. Raw Quartz nodes are exclusively found in biomes that require significant infrastructure to reach safely — Jetpacks, firearms, and often gas masks or elevated walkways.

5 Gotcha Tips

⚠ Raw Quartz Location Challenge

Raw Quartz is not found in the starting Grass Fields or Rocky Desert biomes. The nearest Raw Quartz nodes to the starting area are in the Dune Desert (southeast) or the Red Bamboo Fields (northwest). Both are dangerous biomes requiring Tier 5-6 equipment to explore safely. Plan a dedicated Raw Quartz expedition with full gear: Xeno-Basher, at least 100 health supplies, 200 Concrete for foundations, and a vehicle for transport.

⚠ Don't Dismantle Your Plastic Circuit Board Line Immediately

When you unlock Silicon Circuit Board, resist the urge to tear down your existing Plastic-based Circuit Board production immediately. You still need Plastic for a dozen other products: Computers (which also need Circuit Boards but through a different chain), High-Speed Connectors, Crystal Oscillators, and various alt recipes. Instead, run both lines in parallel: use Silicon Circuit Boards for new expansion while keeping the Plastic line running for products that specifically need Plastic.

⚠ MAM Research Priority

The Raw Quartz research chain in the MAM requires multiple research steps: first Quartz Crystals, then Silica, then Quartz-based alternate recipes. Each step takes real time to complete (10+ minutes per research). Start the Quartz research chain as soon as you find Raw Quartz, even before you have the Silicon Circuit Board recipe. This way, by the time you unlock the recipe, your Silica production is already running and you can immediately switch over.

6 Free Benefits

Other Recipes from the Same Hard Drive Pool

When you hunt for Silicon Circuit Board in Tier 7+ biomes, you'll unlock several other game-changing recipes. Here are the most valuable companions:

⚡ Supercomputer (Alternate Recipe)

Simplifies Supercomputer production by replacing some rare components with more common ones. Essential for Tier 8 Adaptive Control Units and the final Space Elevator delivery. Unlock Tier: 7+.

⚡ Turbo Electric Motor

An alternate recipe for Turbo Motors that reduces the need for Radio Control Units. Used in Nuclear Power Plants and the final Space Elevator phases. Unlock Tier: 7+.

⚡ Fused Modular Frame

Combines Modular Frames with Aluminum to create Heavy Modular Frames at a reduced resource cost. A key recipe for Tier 7-8 construction. Unlock Tier: 7+.

⚡ Cooling System (Alternate)

Simplifies Cooling System production for Nuclear Power and advanced electronics. Reduces Nitrogen Gas dependency. Unlock Tier: 8.
