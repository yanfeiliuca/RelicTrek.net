弥诺陶洛斯停靠站

★★★☆☆ 难度等级

在任何地点召唤机械地面载具。漫游者停靠站+镉+布线织机。

1. 概览

弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 是召唤 Minotaur 的门户——一种双足机械载具，将改变你在无人深空探索敌对星球的方式。

在 Origins 更新中引入，并在后续补丁中不断完善，Minotaur 填补了一个独特的空缺：它既是载具，也是移动式 hazard 盾牌。

Minotaur 还配备了强大的采矿激光和专用的地形操纵炮，安装在其手臂上。

然而，真正让 Minotaur 与众不同的是 Geobay 能够将机甲召唤到任何位置的能力。

弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 蓝图从太空异象上的 Construction Research Station 使用 回收数据 购买。

就进度而言，Minotaur 处于游戏中期阶段。

2. 配方与蓝图

弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 需要一组特定的组件，在基地建造之前需要先制作或收集。

Primary Recipe

弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 = 1x 漫游者停靠站 + 200x Cadmium + 5x 布线织机

Where to Learn the Blueprint

从太空异象上的 Construction Research Station 使用 10 个 回收数据 购买 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 蓝图。

如果你还没有解锁太空异象，推进 Artemis Path 剧情线直到你到达 A Leap in the Dark 任务。

Sub-Recipe: 漫游者停靠站

漫游者停靠站 是前置的地面载具停靠站，本身也需要制作。

漫游者停靠站 = 5x Metal Plating + 4x Ion Battery + 100x Paraffinium

  • Metal Plating: Crafted from 50 铁尘 each at the 外骨骼 inventory. Ferrite Dust is mined from small rocks on every planet type using your 多用途工具's 地形操纵器 or 采集光束. For 5 Metal Plating, you need 250 铁尘 total.
  • Ion Battery: Crafted from 50 铁尘 + 20 Cobalt each at the 外骨骼. Cobalt is found in stalactite formations in caves on virtually all planets. For 4 Ion Batteries, you need 200 铁尘 and 80 Cobalt.
  • Paraffinium: A raw resource found on Toxic (Toxic, Acidic, Noxious) planets. Use your 分析面罩 (default C on PC) to scan for Paraffinium deposits — they appear as yellow mineral pillars. Mine them with your 多用途工具. You need 100 units.

漫游者停靠站 蓝图也从 Construction Research Station 使用 5 个 回收数据 购买。

Sub-Recipe: Cadmium

Cadmium是一种在红色星系中独有的彩色金属。

  1. Direct Mining: Install the 镉驱动 on your starship (requires 艾梅里尔驱动 first, which requires 铟驱动, which requires 镉驱动 — the full chromatic drive chain). Warp to a red star system. Land on any planet and use your 分析面罩 to locate Cadmium deposits (they appear as red mineral formations). Mine with your 多用途工具. Planets with Cadmium often have extreme weather, so bring hazard protection modules.
  2. Refining from 有色金属: If you have a 中型精炼机 or 大型精炼机, place 1 Cadmium + 1 Chromatic Metal to produce 4 Cadmium. This creates a positive feedback loop if you have even a small seed amount. 有色金属 itself is made by refining Copper (1:1), Cadmium (1:2), Emeril (1:3), or Indium (1:4) in any refiner.
  3. Refining from Cadmium-rich Asteroids: Some asteroid fields in red star systems contain Cadmium-rich asteroids. Shoot them with your starship's Photon Cannon and collect the drops. This is slower than planetary mining but can supplement your supply.

布线织机

布线织机 是从空间站技术商人处购买的预制技术组件。

3. 材料与获取方式

完整材料清单

🪨 铁尘

Total needed: ~450 货币

来源: Every planet surface — small rocks

工具: 多用途工具 采集光束 or 地形操纵器

备注: The most common resource in the game. Used for Metal Plating and Ion Batteries.

🔷 Cobalt

Total needed: 80 货币

来源: Cave stalactites on all planet types

工具: 采集光束

备注: Look for blue crystalline formations on cave ceilings. Alternatively, refine Ionised Cobalt 1:2 in a refiner.

☣️ Paraffinium

Total needed: 100 货币

来源: Toxic planets (Toxic, Acidic, Noxious, Acrid biomes)

工具: 分析面罩 + 采集光束

备注: Scan with C to find deposits. Yellow mineral pillars. Also found as secondary element in some plants.

🔴 Cadmium

Total needed: 200 货币

来源: Red star systems (M-type)

工具: 分析面罩 + 采集光束

备注: Requires 镉驱动 or higher chromatic drive. Red mineral deposits. Refine 1:2 from 有色金属.

🔌 布线织机

Total needed: 5 货币

来源: Space Station technology merchants

成本: ~50,000–75,000 Units each

备注: Gear-icon NPC on station right side. Also from Manufacturing Facilities and damaged machinery.

📀 回收数据

Total needed: 15 units (5 for Roamer + 10 for Minotaur)

来源: Buried Technology Modules

工具: 分析面罩 + 地形操纵器

备注: Scan for wireless-tower icons. Dig up and interact. Each module gives 1 回收数据. Found on all planets near structures.

可能需要的次要资源

在收集主要材料的同时，你可能还会积累或需要这些次要资源。

  • 有色金属: If you choose the refiner route for Cadmium, you will need 有色金属 as feedstock. Refine Copper (found on most planets as green mineral deposits) at a 2:1 ratio to get started.
  • Sodium / Sodium Nitrate: Used to recharge your 危险防护 while on extreme worlds gathering Cadmium. Sodium is found in yellow flowering plants on all planets. Refine Sodium Nitrate 1:1 from Sodium in a refiner, or find it directly on extreme worlds.
  • Oxygen: Your 生命维持 will drain while exploring hostile planets. Oxygen is found in red flowering plants on most planets and can be harvested quickly with your 多用途工具.
  • Carbon / Condensed Carbon: Used to recharge your 多用途工具's 采集光束. Carbon comes from all vegetation. Condensed Carbon is refined from Carbon at a 2:1 ratio.

4. 最优采集路线

这条 farming 路线设计为单次游戏 session 约 60-90 分钟内完成。

Phase 1: Foundation (20 minutes)

  1. Warp to your Home Base system — Ensure you have a base with a 基地电脑 and at least one powered structure.
  2. Equip your 分析面罩 and 地形操纵器 — These are essential for the entire route.
  3. Scan for Buried Technology Modules — Fly low over the planet surface in your starship. Look for wireless-tower icons when you pulse-scan (default C). Mark 10–15 modules.
  4. Excavate 回收数据 — Land at each module, use the 地形操纵器 to dig down, and interact with the buried technology. Collect 15 回收数据.
  5. Summon the 太空异象 — From your Quick Menu (X), select Summon Anomaly. Fly aboard.
  6. Purchase Blueprints — Go to the Construction Research Station (lower level, near teleporter). Buy 漫游者停靠站 (5 回收数据) and 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 (10 回收数据).

Phase 2: Roamer Materials (15 minutes)

  1. Return to your base planet — Warp back if needed.
  2. Gather 铁尘 — Walk or fly to any rocky area. Mine small rocks until you have at least 450 铁尘. The large boulders give more per strike.
  3. Find a cave — Use your starship to locate a cave entrance (darker patches on terrain, or use the scanner to find cobalt deposits which indicate caves). Enter and mine 80 Cobalt from blue stalactites on the ceiling.
  4. Craft Metal Plating and Ion Batteries — In your 外骨骼 inventory, craft 5x Metal Plating (250 铁尘) and 4x Ion Battery (200 铁尘 + 80 Cobalt).
  5. Travel to a Toxic planet — If your current planet is not Toxic, warp to the nearest system with a Toxic, Acidic, Noxious, or Acrid planet. Use the 星系图 and look for systems with green-tinted planets.
  6. Gather Paraffinium — Land on the Toxic planet, activate your 分析面罩, and scan for Paraffinium (yellow mineral pillars). Mine 100 Paraffinium. Be aware of toxic storms — take shelter in your starship if needed.

Phase 3: Build the 漫游者停靠站 (5 minutes)

  1. Return to your base — Warp back to your home system.
  2. Open the Build Menu — Hold Z to open the build menu. Navigate to 地面载具 → 漫游者停靠站.
  3. Place the 漫游者停靠站 — Find a flat area near your base and place it. It requires a powered base but the Geobay itself does not need to be connected to power lines.
  4. Verify placement — The 漫游者停靠站 should show a green hologram before placement. Ensure it is on relatively flat terrain or it may clip into the ground.

Phase 4: Cadmium Run (25 minutes)

  1. Ensure you have the 镉驱动 — Check your starship's inventory. You need the 镉驱动 installed to warp to red star systems. If you do not have it yet, purchase the blueprint from the 飞船 Research Terminal on the 太空异象 (requires 2 Navigation Data or can be purchased with Nanites).
  2. Open the 星系图 — Press M to open the 星系图. Filter for Red Star Systems (M-type spectral class). The system name will show a red spectral class indicator.
  3. Warp to a Red System — Select a nearby red system and warp. The warp fuel cost is higher than yellow systems, so ensure your 超光速引擎 is at least 50% charged.
  4. Scan for Cadmium — Enter the system, scan the planets, and look for planets that list Cadmium in their resources. Not all red system planets have Cadmium — look for planets with 'Metallurgic' or 'Mineral Rich' in their description.
  5. Land and Mine — Land on a Cadmium-rich planet. Activate your 分析面罩 and search for red mineral deposits. Mine 200 Cadmium. The mining process takes 5–10 minutes depending on deposit density. Cadmium deposits in red systems are usually abundant.
  6. Alternative: Asteroid Mining — If planetary deposits are sparse, fly into the asteroid field and shoot Cadmium-rich asteroids (they have a reddish tint). This is slower but safer if the planet has hostile Sentinels.

Phase 5: 布线织机s (5 minutes)

  1. Warp to any high-wealth system — Use the 经济扫描仪 (or look for systems with 3-star wealth rating in the 星系图) to find a system with good prices. High-wealth systems have more stock and better prices.
  2. Dock at the Space Station — Fly to the station and land.
  3. Find the Technology Merchant — Walk to the right side of the station (facing the core). The technology merchant is behind a counter with a gear icon.
  4. Purchase 5 布线织机s — Interact and buy 5. Total cost: approximately 250,000–375,000 Units. If the merchant is out of stock, buy what they have, board your ship, exit to reset the shop, and buy the rest.

Phase 6: Build the 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 (5 minutes)

  1. Return to your base — Warp back to your home system where the 漫游者停靠站 is built.
  2. Open the Build Menu — Hold Z → 地面载具 → 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站.
  3. Place the 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 — The game will consume your 漫游者停靠站, 200 Cadmium, and 5 布线织机s. Place it near your base for easy access.
  4. Summon the Minotaur — Interact with the Geobay to claim your Minotaur. Then, use the Quick Menu (X) → 地面载具 → Summon Minotaur to call it to your location anywhere on the planet.

恭喜——你现在拥有一个完全运作的 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站，可以按需召唤你的机械 exocraft。

5. 注意事项与技巧

⚠️ The 漫游者停靠站 Is Consumed

建造 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 会消耗你的 漫游者停靠站。

⚠️ 镉驱动 Required for Red Stars

你的飞船上必须安装 镉驱动 才能到达红色星系。

⚠️ Minotaur Does Not Have Infinite Fuel

Minotaur 不使用传统燃料，但其 危险防护 护盾在极端世界会随时间衰减。

⚠️ Summoning Range Limitations

你只能在你建造了 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 的同一颗星球上召唤 Minotaur。

⚠️ Base Power Requirements

虽然 Geobay 本身不需要直接连接电源，但必须放置在通电的基地边界内。

💡 Pro Tip: Build Multiple Geobays Across Systems

一旦你拥有 Minotaur 并喜欢它，在你建立的每个主要基地都建造 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站。

💡 Pro Tip: Use the Minotaur for 风暴水晶 Farming

极端天气星球在暴风雨期间会生成 风暴水晶s——发光的柱子，每个价值 120,000+ Units。

💡 Pro Tip: Upgrade Modules at the Space Station

建造 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 后，访问任何空间站上的 地面载具 Technology Merchant。

6. 额外收益与解锁内容

建造 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 不仅仅是给你一个载具——它解锁了整个游戏收益生态系统。

即时收益

  • Complete Hazard Immunity: While piloting the Minotaur, all environmental hazards are negated. Toxic storms, scorching heat, freezing cold, radiation, and even the extreme storms that drain standard 危险防护 in seconds — none of them affect you. This is the single best tool for exploring extreme worlds.
  • Enhanced Mining: The Minotaur's built-in mining laser extracts resources faster than the Exosuit 采集光束 and does not require recharging with Carbon or Condensed Carbon. It can also break larger deposits that would take multiple 外骨骼 hits.
  • Terrain Manipulation: Built-in terrain cannon for digging tunnels, flattening building sites, creating roads between bases, or defensive earthworks during Sentinel combat. Uses no fuel or ammunition.
  • Fall Damage Immunity: Jump off the highest mountain or into the deepest canyon — the Minotaur takes zero fall damage. This makes it ideal for exploring mountainous terrain where the Roamer would crash and the Nomad would tumble.

解锁活动

  • 风暴水晶 Farming: As mentioned in the Pro Tips section, extreme weather planets spawn 风暴水晶s during storms. With the Minotaur, you can farm these in complete safety. A single 10-minute farming run on a high-activity storm planet can net 2–3 million Units. This alone makes the 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 worth building.
  • Activated Resource Mining: Extreme worlds also host Activated variants of metals — Activated Copper, Activated Cadmium, Activated Emeril, and Activated Indium. These sell for significantly more than their standard counterparts and are used in high-tier crafting recipes. The Minotaur lets you mine these without worrying about the extreme conditions.
  • 制造设施 Raiding: Need to blow open reinforced doors to access manufacturing facilities and secure facilities? The Minotaur can blast them open with its cannon, or you can simply park it nearby, use it as a safe room during Sentinel response, and pop out to hack the terminal.
  • Sentinel Combat: While not as agile as on-foot combat, the Minotaur provides a mobile bunker. You can step out, engage Sentinels with your 多用途工具, and step back in to recharge. The Minotaur's shield absorbs Sentinel laser fire, giving you a tactical advantage.
  • Deep Cave Exploration: The Minotaur's headlamp provides better illumination than the 外骨骼 flashlight, and its immunity to falling rocks and hazardous cave gases makes spelunking far safer. Many cave systems contain Vortex Cubes, Albumen Pearls, and Gravitino Balls.

后期解锁内容

  • 地面载具 Upgrade Chain: Once you have one Geobay, you will want more. The 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 is a stepping stone to building the 地面载具 Summoning Station at your freighter base, which lets you summon any exocraft (including the Minotaur) on any planet in any system — completely eliminating the need for planet-side Geobays.
  • 货船 地面载具 Room: After building the 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 and completing the appropriate research, you can construct an 地面载具 Materialiser aboard your freighter. This is the ultimate convenience — summon your Minotaur anywhere in the universe without any planetary infrastructure.
  • Achievement / Milestone Progress: Building the 弥诺陶洛斯停靠站 contributes to your Base Building milestones, which reward Nanites and unlock further base building parts.
