飞船AI阀门

★★★★★ 难度等级

每阀门5000万货币。从坠毁的护卫拦截机中回收。

1. 概览

飞船AI阀门 是无人深空中最有价值的贸易商品，每个阀门价值惊人的 5000 万 Units。

当然，问题在于 AI阀门 极其难以获得。

护卫拦截机 坠毁是在 Interceptor 更新（4.2）中引入的，代表了一种全新的游戏循环。

每个坠毁的 护卫拦截机 还包含一艘损坏的 Sentinel Ship，可以修复并作为你自己的飞船认领。

本指南涵盖完整的 AI Valve 获取流程：从触发你的第一个 护卫拦截机 太空生成，到导航 谐波营地 谜题，到定位坠毁地点，再到访问 AI阀门 货物所需的精确修复步骤。

2. 配方与蓝图

飞船AI阀门 没有传统的制作配方。相反，这里是从坠毁的 护卫拦截机 获取它们的完整逐步'配方'。

Phase 1: Acquire a 谐波大脑

Trigger: Reach 3+ star wanted level in a high-Sentinel-activity system and defeat a 护卫拦截机 in ship combat

Reward: 1x 谐波大脑

要触发 护卫拦截机 生成，传送到具有高 Sentinel 活动的系统（在 星系图 上标有红色 Sentinel 图标）。

Phase 2: Locate a 谐波营地

Method: Visit a planet with corrupted Sentinels and search near 护卫柱s

Output: Coordinates to crashed 护卫拦截机

谐波营地s 是在具有'Corrupted Sentinel'活动的星球上，护卫柱 附近 500 单位范围内发现的小型结构。

Phase 3: Locate and Repair the Crash Site

Input: Coordinates from 谐波营地

Materials needed at crash site:

  • 5 反转镜面
  • 3 辐射碎片
  • 1 Salvaged Frigate Module
  • 200 有色金属
  • 100 铁尘

飞到 谐波营地 提供的坐标。

  • Hull Fractures (×3): Repair each with 1 反转镜面 + 40 有色金属
  • Engine Core (×2): Repair each with 1 辐射碎片 + 30 有色金属
  • Navigation Array (×1): Repair with 1 Salvaged Frigate Module + 60 有色金属
  • Cargo Access Panel (×1): Repair with 100 铁尘

一旦所有组件修复完毕，Cargo Access Panel 会打开，露出船的货舱。

Phase 4 (Optional): Claim the Sentinel Ship

打捞货物后，你可以将坠毁的 Sentinel ship 认领为你自己的。

  • Launch Thrusters: 50 Di-hydrogen Jelly
  • Pulse Engine: 2 Hermetic Seal + 2 Metal Plating
  • Deflector Shields: 2 Sodium Diode
  • 超光速引擎: 2 有色金属 + 1 Microprocessor
  • Photon Cannon: 2 有色金属 + 1 布线织机

整艘船的修理总成本约为 1000-1500 万 Units 的材料。

3. 材料与获取方式

Complete Material Checklist (Per Crash Site)

🪞 反转镜面

Total needed: 5 per crash site

来源 1: 废品商人 on stations (3,200 污金属 each)

来源 2: 护卫拦截机 wreckage sites

来源 3: Derelict 货船 containers (rare)

备注: Best farmed via Derelict 货船s. Each freighter run yields 1,500–3,000 污金属 = ~1 mirror.

💎 辐射碎片

Total needed: 3 per crash site

来源 1: Crashed Sentinel ship sites (often found in nearby containers)

来源 2: 谐波营地 surrounding area

来源 3: Defeating 护卫拦截机 in space (uncommon drop)

备注: Usually found scattered around the crash crater. Walk the perimeter and scan for glowing red crystals.

🚀 Salvaged Frigate Module

Total needed: 1 per crash site

来源 1: Derelict 货船 end-room reward (guaranteed 1)

来源 2: Frigate expedition rewards (uncommon)

来源 3: Crashed freighter containers on planets (rare)

备注: Running Derelict 货船s is the most reliable method. You get 污金属 AND the module.

🔶 有色金属

Total needed: 200 per crash site

来源: Refine Copper 2:1 (need 400 Copper)

备注: Mine Copper with your Minotaur for speed. Or buy Copper from trade terminals and refine it.

🪨 铁尘

Total needed: 100 per crash site

来源: Every planet — small rocks

备注: Trivial. Mine in 30 seconds at any crash site.

🧠 谐波大脑

Total needed: 1 per crash site

来源: Defeat 护卫拦截机 in space combat

备注: Only drops from 护卫拦截机 (not regular Sentinels). Must reach 3-star wanted level in a high-Sentinel system.

💰 污金属 (for buying mirrors)

Total needed: ~16,000 (for 5 mirrors via 废品商人)

来源: Derelict 货船s

备注: Each Derelict 货船 run yields 1,500–3,000 污金属. Plan for 5–8 freighter runs per crash site.

4. 最优采集路线

这条路线将 AI阀门 获取的四个阶段串联成一个高效的 farming 循环。

Phase 1: 谐波大脑 Acquisition (10 minutes)

  1. Warp to a high-Sentinel system — Use the 星系图 to find a system with the red Sentinel activity icon. These systems have aggressive Sentinel presence on all planets.
  2. Fly into space and start shooting — Attack cargo pods, shoot at freighters, or destroy Sentinel drones. Keep going until your wanted level hits 3 stars.
  3. Wait for Interceptors — At 3 stars, 护卫拦截机 will warp in. They look like black crystalline ships with red engine glow.
  4. Defeat one — Use your starship's Photon Cannon and Positron Ejector. Interceptors are agile but lightly shielded. 5–10 seconds of concentrated fire will destroy one.
  5. Collect the 谐波大脑 — Fly through the drop to collect it. It goes into your inventory as a mission item.

Phase 2: 谐波营地 Location (10 minutes)

  1. Warp to a Corrupted Sentinel planet — In the same system (or a different high-Sentinel system), find a planet marked as having 'Corrupted Sentinels' or 'Aggressive Sentinels.'
  2. Scan for a 护卫柱 — Land and use your 地面载具 Radar or 信号增强器 to scan for Sentinel Structures. Alternatively, fly low and look for tall black towers.
  3. Search near the Pillar — Walk or fly in a 500-unit radius around the Pillar. Look for a small structure with three red pillars and a central terminal.
  4. Interface the 谐波大脑 — Interact with the terminal while holding the Brain. This consumes it and gives you crash site coordinates.

Phase 3: Crash Site Repair and Salvage (20 minutes)

  1. Fly to the coordinates — Use your starship's coordinate display or exocraft to reach the exact location.
  2. Survey the crash site — Walk the perimeter and collect any loose 辐射碎片s (red crystals) scattered around.
  3. Begin repairs — Interact with each damaged component. Repair Hull Fractures (反转镜面s), Engine Cores (辐射碎片s), and Navigation Array (Salvaged Frigate Module).
  4. Open the Cargo Panel — Repair with 100 铁尘. Collect 1–3 AI阀门.
  5. Store Valves safely — Put them in your starship inventory (not 外骨骼) where they are safe if you die.

Phase 4: Ship Claim (Optional, 15 minutes)

  1. Begin ship repairs — If you want the ship, repair Launch Thrusters, Pulse Engine, Deflector Shields, 超光速引擎, and Photon Cannon using the materials listed in the Recipe section.
  2. Claim the ship — Once flyable, interact with the cockpit to add it to your collection.
  3. Fly to a station and sell — If you do not want to keep it, fly the Sentinel ship to any space station and trade it in. An A-class or S-class Sentinel ship can sell for 30–50 million Units.

Phase 5: Derelict 货船 Prep (Between Runs)

  1. Buy Emergency Broadcast Receivers — From the 废品商人 on any station (150,000 Units each).
  2. Run Derelict 货船s — Each run yields 1,500–3,000 污金属 + 1 Salvaged Frigate Module.
  3. Trade 污金属 for 反转镜面s — 3,200 污金属 = 1 Mirror at the 废品商人.
  4. Stockpile for the next crash site — You need 5 Mirrors and 1 Frigate Module per site. 2–3 freighter runs provide enough.

5. 注意事项与技巧

⚠️ 谐波大脑s Are Single-Use

每个 谐波大脑 在 谐波营地 使用时会被消耗。

⚠️ Crash Sites Are Planet-Specific

谐波营地 的坐标总是指向同一星球上的坠毁地点。

⚠️ AI阀门 Cannot Be Crafted

尽管它们与其他高端组件相似，飞船AI阀门 没有制作配方。

⚠️ Valves Are Safe in 飞船 Inventory

放在飞船库存中的 飞船AI阀门 即使你死亡也是安全的。

⚠️ Multiplayer Competition

在多人游戏中，坠毁的 护卫拦截机 及其 AI阀门 在实例中的所有玩家之间共享。

💡 Pro Tip: Valves as Currency Storage

因为 AI阀门 每个库存槽可以堆叠 10 个，每个价值 5000 万 Units，它们是存储财富的最省空间方式。

💡 Pro Tip: The Derelict 货船 Loop

跑废弃货船不仅仅是为了 污金属——它是一个完整的材料解决方案。

💡 Pro Tip: Chain Multiple Crash Sites

一旦你储备了 谐波大脑s 和修理材料，你可以在单次长 session 中串联多个坠毁地点。

6. 额外收益与解锁内容

飞船AI阀门 不仅仅是现金注入——它们解锁了无人深空的整个 endgame 经济。

即时收益

  • 50 Million Units Per Valve: One Valve buys an A-class freighter, funds a full 外骨骼 upgrade, purchases 100+ 布线织机s, and still leaves millions in the bank.
  • 经济自由: Never grind for Units again. Buy anything from any merchant without checking prices. Economic constraints are permanently removed from your gameplay.
  • Space-Efficient Wealth Storage: 10 Valves in one inventory slot = 500 million Units. The most compact wealth storage in the game.

解锁购买

  • S-Class 飞船: The best S-class starships cost 50–120 million Units. One to three Valves and you are flying the best ship in the game.
  • S-Class 货船: Capital freighters cost 100–250 million Units. 2–5 Valves and you have a mobile base capable of holding 30 starships.
  • Infinite Upgrade Modules: S-class upgrade modules cost 480 Nanites each. Since you have unlimited Units, you can buy every module the merchant stocks every day.
  • Base Building Without Limits: No more budgeting for glass corridors, biodomes, or storage containers. Build the base of your dreams.

后期解锁内容

  • Living 飞船 Completion: Upgrading a 生物飞船 to S-class requires millions of Units in organic materials and technology. With AI阀门 wealth, this is trivial.
  • Frigate Fleet Expansion: Buy 30 S-class support frigates for your capital freighter. Costs ~150 million total. One good Valve farming session covers it.
  • Collection Completion: With unlimited funds, you can buy one of every 多用途工具, every 飞船 type, and every cosmetic option. Become the ultimate collector.
  • Multiplayer Generosity: AI阀门 can be transferred to other players in multiplayer. Fund your friends' early-game progression in seconds.
  • Galactic Center Rush: Buying all the 跃迁超核s you need to reach the Galactic Center costs millions. With Valve wealth, just buy them and go.
