1 此物品的重要性

聚变点火器是无人深空中两种最有价值的可制作交易品之一，与停滞装置并列，每单位售价高达1560万单位。

聚变点火器的配方链很复杂，但遵循逻辑结构：四个中间组件，每个由四种高级材料制成，而这些高级材料又由基础行星资源制成。

与其他赚钱方法相比，聚变点火器特别吸引人的是其每个库存格的价值密度。

对于追求舰队扩张的玩家，聚变点火器是购买S级主力货船（通常1-5亿单位）或收集整支S级飞船舰队的关键。

Complete Farming Summary Per Ignitor

To produce one 聚变点火器 from base resources, you need the following crops (assuming all components are crafted rather than purchased):

  • Solanium: 200 (for Heat Capacitor + related chain)
  • Frost Crystal: 200 (for Cryo-Pump + related chain)
  • Cactus Flesh: 200 (for Acid + related chain)
  • Star Bulb: 200 (for Poly Fibre + related chain)
  • Fungal Mould: 200 (for Lubricant + related chain)
  • Gamma Root: 200 (for Gamma Weed extract)
  • 活珍珠: 1 (for Portable Reactor)
  • Nitrogen: 250 (for Nitrogen Salt)
  • Carbon / Condensed Carbon: 500+ (for multiple refining steps)
  • 有色金属: 100 (for Superconductor)

The most efficient approach is to establish dedicated farming bases on each planet type with large biodomes growing the relevant crop. Each plant takes approximately 4 real-time hours to mature when outdoors, or 20 minutes when in a hydroponic tray. A full biodome (16 slots) of Solanium, for example, produces enough for 8 聚变点火器s per harvest cycle.

3 材料与位置

聚变点火器生产需要四种不同行星生物群落以及海洋世界和大气采集的资源。本节详细说明每种材料的确切位置和最佳采集方法。

● Solanium — Scorched World Crop

来源: Harvested from Solanium plants on Scorched planets, or grown in 生物圆顶s from Solanium seeds.

Best farming method: Build a base on a Scorched world with "Boiling" or "Scorched" climate classification. Plant Solanium outdoors — it grows natively on these worlds without environmental protection required for the plants. Use a Large 生物圆顶 (16 slots) if building off-planet. Solanium seeds are obtained by harvesting wild Solanium plants with your 多用途工具 — each harvest has a chance to drop a seed. Once you have one seed, you can plant unlimited copies. Solanium takes 4 real-time hours to mature outdoors, or 20 minutes in a 水培盘. For maximum efficiency, plant 16 Solanium per biodome across multiple biodomes. Each mature Solanium plant yields 50 Solanium, so one full biodome harvest provides 800 Solanium — enough for 4 聚变点火器s.

● Frost Crystal — Frozen World Crop

来源: Harvested from Frostwort plants on Frozen planets, or grown in 生物圆顶s.

Best farming method: Identical to Solanium but on a Frozen/Ice world. Frostwort grows natively on frozen planets and can be planted outdoors there for passive growth. In biodomes, Frost Crystal takes 20 minutes per harvest cycle. A 16-slot biodome yields 800 Frost Crystal per harvest. Frostwort is the fastest-growing of the climate-specific crops, making it the most forgiving if you are setting up your first farming base. The extreme cold on these planets requires Thermal Protection upgrades for the farmer, but the plants themselves thrive in it — no environmental controls needed.

● Cactus Flesh — Desert World Crop

来源: Harvested from Echinocactus plants on Desert planets, or grown in 生物圆顶s.

Best farming method: Desert worlds are classified with descriptors like "Barren," "Desert," or "Rocky." Echinocactus plants are tall, cylindrical cacti visible from a distance. Build your desert farming base near a cluster of wild Echinocactus for seed acquisition, then transplant 16 into a Large 生物圆顶. Each Echinocactus yields 50 Cactus Flesh at harvest. Cactus Flesh is used in crafting Acid, which feeds into Liquid Explosive for the Portable Reactor component. One full biodome provides enough Cactus Flesh for 4 Ignitors per cycle.

● Star Bulb — Lush World Crop

来源: Harvested from Star Bramble plants on Lush/Paradise planets, or grown in 生物圆顶s.

Best farming method: Star Bramble grows on all Lush, Paradise, Verdant, and Tropical worlds — the most common desirable planet type. If you already have a home base on a Paradise planet, you likely have Star Bramble nearby. Each plant yields 50 Star Bulb per harvest. Star Bulb is used in crafting Poly Fibre, which is a component of the Circuit Board for the Quantum Processor. A 16-slot biodome produces 800 Star Bulb per cycle. Because Paradise planets are the most popular base locations, this is often the easiest crop to establish.

● Fungal Mould — Toxic World Crop

来源: Harvested from Fungal Clusters on Toxic worlds, or grown in 生物圆顶s.

Best farming method: Toxic worlds have green-tinted atmospheres and hazardous flora. Fungal Clusters appear as bulbous mushrooms near cave entrances and in damp areas. Build a small base on a Toxic world with a Large 生物圆顶 for 16 Fungal Cluster plants. Each yields 50 Fungal Mould per harvest. Fungal Mould is refined into Lubricant, which combines with Glass to create Living Glass for the Cryogenic Chamber component. One biodome cycle yields enough for 4 Ignitors.

● Gamma Root — Radioactive World Crop

来源: Harvested from Gamma Weed on Radioactive planets, or grown in 生物圆顶s.

Best farming method: Radioactive worlds are identified by their yellow-green tint and high radiation levels. Gamma Weed appears as glowing plants on the surface. Radiation Protection upgrades are essential for the farmer, but the plants grow without issue. Gamma Root is refined into Enriched Carbon, a component of the Superconductor for the Quantum Processor. Build a 16-slot biodome for 800 Gamma Root per harvest cycle.

● 活珍珠 — Ocean World Harvest

来源: 巨蚌s in underwater caves on Ocean worlds.

Best farming method: Unlike the crop components, Living Pearls cannot be farmed in biodomes — they must be harvested from Giant Clams underwater. The most efficient method is to find an Ocean world with shallow, clear water and abundant underwater cave systems. Use the Nautilon exocraft with sonar to scan for underwater structures — 巨蚌s appear as "Harvestable Plant" signatures. Approach clams quickly — they snap shut after a few seconds. Alternatively, use the 地形操纵器 to blast them open from range. One 活珍珠 is needed per 聚变点火器 for the Portable Reactor component.

● Nitrogen — Atmospheric Harvesting

来源: 大气采集器s placed on Lush or Toxic worlds.

Best farming method: Build an Atmosphere Harvester (requires Metal Plating + Oxygen + 有色金属) on any Lush or Toxic world. When powered, it extracts Nitrogen from the atmosphere at a rate of 250 每单位货币 10 real-time minutes. You need 250 Nitrogen per Nitrogen Salt, which goes into the Fusion Accelerant. A single 大气采集器 operating for 10 minutes provides enough Nitrogen for one Ignitor. Build multiple harvesters for batch production — 10 harvesters provide enough Nitrogen for 10 Ignitors every 10 minutes.

● 有色金属 — Refined Resource

来源: Refined from Copper, Cadmium, Emeril, or Indium.

Best farming method: Chromatic Metal is needed for the Superconductor component. Use the 中型精炼机 multiplication trick: 2 Copper + 1 Chromatic Metal = 6 Chromatic Metal. With a seed stockpile of 50 Chromatic Metal and 100 Copper, you can produce unlimited Chromatic Metal. Alternatively, if you have an Indium mining operation (see our 铟驱动 guide), Indium refines to 有色金属 at 1:4 ratio, providing thousands of units effortlessly.

4 最优路线

此路线假设你已在每种所需生物群落的代表性行星上建立了农场基地。目标是在3小时游戏时间内生产10个聚变点火器。

PREPARATION PHASE (One-Time Setup)

  1. Establish 6 farming bases, one on each required planet type: Scorched, Frozen, Desert, Lush, Toxic, and Radioactive.
  2. At each base, build: 传送模块, 2 Large 生物圆顶s (32 crop slots total), and sufficient power (Solar Panels + Batteries or 电磁发电机).
  3. Plant the climate-specific crop at each base: Solanium (Scorched), Frost Crystal (Frozen), Cactus Flesh (Desert), Star Bulb (Lush), Fungal Mould (Toxic), Gamma Root (Radioactive).
  4. Build an Ocean base with Nautilon Moonpool for 活珍珠 harvesting.
  5. Build 10 大气采集器s on your Lush world base for Nitrogen production.
  6. Wait one 4-hour crop cycle for all plants to mature (or use 水培盘s for 20-minute cycles).

COLLECTION PHASE (30 minutes)

  1. Teleport to your Scorched base — harvest 2 Large 生物圆顶s of Solanium (1,600 units). Store in 货船.
  2. Teleport to your Frozen base — harvest 2 Large 生物圆顶s of Frost Crystal (1,600 units). Store in 货船.
  3. Teleport to your Desert base — harvest 2 Large 生物圆顶s of Cactus Flesh (1,600 units). Store in 货船.
  4. Teleport to your Lush base — harvest 2 Large 生物圆顶s of Star Bulb (1,600 units). Collect Nitrogen from 大气采集器s. Store all.
  5. Teleport to your Toxic base — harvest 2 Large 生物圆顶s of Fungal Mould (1,600 units). Store in 货船.
  6. Teleport to your Radioactive base — harvest 2 Large 生物圆顶s of Gamma Root (1,600 units). Store in 货船.
  7. Teleport to your Ocean base — use Nautilon to harvest 10 活珍珠s from 巨蚌s.

CRAFTING PHASE — Level 3 Materials (20 minutes)

  1. Craft Heat Capacitor from Solanium + Frost Crystal (need 10).
  2. Craft Poly Fibre from Cactus Flesh + Star Bulb (need 10).
  3. Craft Acid from Cactus Flesh + Mordite (need 10).
  4. Craft Lubricant from Fungal Mould + Coprite (need 10).
  5. Craft Enriched Carbon from Gamma Root + Carbon (need 10).
  6. Craft Glass from Frost Crystal via Refiner (need 10).
  7. Craft Nitrogen Salt from Nitrogen + Carbon Crystal (need 10).
  8. Craft Thermic Condensate from Solanium + Carbon (need 10).

CRAFTING PHASE — Level 2 Components (15 minutes)

  1. Craft Circuit Board from Poly Fibre + Heat Capacitor (need 10).
  2. Craft Superconductor from Enriched Carbon + Semiconductor (need 10).
  3. Craft Living Glass from Lubricant + Glass (need 10).
  4. Craft Cryo-Pump from Hot Ice + Thermic Condensate (need 10).
  5. Craft Organic Catalyst from Thermic Condensate + Nitrogen Salt (need 10).
  6. Craft Liquid Explosive from Acid + Unstable Gel (need 10).

CRAFTING PHASE — Level 1 Final Assembly (10 minutes)

  1. Craft Quantum Processor from Circuit Board + Superconductor (need 10).
  2. Craft Cryogenic Chamber from Living Glass + Cryo-Pump (need 10).
  3. Craft Fusion Accelerant from Organic Catalyst + Nitrogen Salt (need 10).
  4. Craft Portable Reactor from Liquid Explosive + 活珍珠 (need 10).
  5. Craft 10 聚变点火器s by combining all four Level 2 components. Total value: 156,000,000 货币.

SALES PHASE (5 minutes)

  1. Teleport to a high-wealth system (Wealthy, Prosperous, Opulent, or Affluent).
  2. Land at the space station and speak to the Galactic Trade Terminal.
  3. Sell all 10 聚变点火器s for 156,000,000 units.
  4. If the terminal's buy price is reduced after your sale (market saturation), switch to a different system's terminal.
  5. Store units in your account — there is no currency cap.

5 注意事项与技巧

市场饱和：

在单个空间站出售过多聚变点火器会导致当地市场价格崩溃。

蓝图需求：

你必须先从制造设施学习每个组件的蓝图，才能制作聚变点火器的任何组件。

作物计时器同步：

不同作物的生长计时器略有不同，户外农场的植物需要4小时成熟，而水培托盘中的植物只需20分钟。

货船库存限制：

单个聚变点火器售价1560万单位，但你一次只能携带有限数量。

停滞装置替代：

停滞装置售价同样是1560万单位，但需要不同的配方链，涉及不同的行星类型。

电力需求：

大型生物圆顶、大气采集器和传送模块都需要稳定的电力。

多人库存Bug：

在多人游戏中制作时，存放在货船库存中的材料可能无法被制作界面访问。
