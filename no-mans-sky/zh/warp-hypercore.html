跃迁超核

★★★☆☆

100%跃迁燃料。反物质外壳+反物质。星际旅行必备。

非凡 科技

1 此物品的重要性

跃迁超核是任何计划在恒星系统之间旅行的玩家最重要的制作消耗品。

资源节省是巨大的。五个跃迁电池需要5个反物质外壳和5个反物质单位——总共需要100铁尘、50氧气、50色金属和25赫里迪姆等价物。

跃迁超核每个库存格可堆叠至10个，而跃迁电池虽也可堆叠至10个但每个仅提供20%燃料。

跃迁超核的蓝图在阿耳忒弥斯之路主线任务中相对较早解锁，使中期玩家即可获得。

跃迁超核配方是一个两阶段制作，组合两个中间组件。了解两个组件及其子材料可以优化你的采集路线并找到最高效的材料来源。

中型精炼机 Optimization

Players with access to the 中型精炼机 can dramatically reduce their farming time using these refiner recipes:

  • 有色金属 Loop: Copper (x2) + Chromatic Metal (x1) = Chromatic Metal (x6) — This creates a net gain of 5 有色金属 per batch, allowing infinite production from a single initial Copper investment.
  • Oxygen + Condensed Carbon = 6 Condensed Carbon (carbon multiplication for related crafts).
  • Pure Ferrite + Carbon = Magnetised Ferrite (for advanced crafting and base building).

The Chromatic Metal loop is the single most important refiner recipe for Hypercore mass production. Once you have a small stockpile of Chromatic Metal, you never need to mine Copper again — simply keep recycling Copper into 有色金属 at an ever-increasing rate.

3 材料与位置

制作跃迁超核的三种基础材料在几乎每个行星上都常见，但了解每种材料最高效的采集方法可以将你的收集时间减少80%或更多。

● 铁尘 (80 per Hypercore)

来源: Surface rocks on every planet, asteroid mining, trader purchases.

Best farming method: Ferrite Dust is the most abundant resource in 无人深空. Every rocky surface contains harvestable deposits that yield 10-30 Ferrite Dust per rock with the Mining Laser. For mass production, the most efficient method is purchasing Ferrite Dust from space station galactic trade terminals — it typically costs 5-10 每单位货币 piece, meaning a full batch of 800 Ferrite Dust (for 10 Hypercores) costs only 4,000-8,000 units, which is trivial for any established player. If you prefer gathering it yourself, use the 地形操纵器 in "Mine" mode and aim for large boulders — they yield 40-60 Ferrite Dust each. Alternatively, visit a Dead or Exotic planet where 铁尘 forms the primary surface composition and every visible rock is harvestable.

● Oxygen (20 per Hypercore)

来源: Red bulbous flora on all planets, 氧气采集器s, 大气采集器s, trade terminals.

Best farming method: The single most efficient method for Oxygen acquisition is building 氧气采集器s at your base. Each harvester costs 50 Metal Plating to construct and produces 250 Oxygen every 10 real-time minutes when powered. A bank of 4 harvesters generates 1,000 Oxygen per 10 minutes — enough for 50 Hypercores — while you are offline or playing elsewhere. Place them near your base power source (电磁发电机 or Solar Panels + Battery) for continuous operation. For active farming, scan planets from space and target "Verdant," "Tropical," or "Paradise" worlds — these planet types have the densest red Oxygen-rich flora populations. A single Oxygen-rich planet can yield 200+ Oxygen in a 5-minute gathering run using the 多用途工具's mining laser on all red bulbous plants.

● 有色金属 (35 per Hypercore)

来源: Refined from Copper (Green systems), Cadmium (Red systems), Emeril (Blue systems), or Indium (Indium-rich planets).

Best farming method: The Chromatic Metal multiplication trick using the 中型精炼机 is the optimal approach. Input 2 Copper + 1 Chromatic Metal to receive 6 Chromatic Metal. This 6:3 ratio means every batch multiplies your Chromatic Metal by 2x (net gain of 3 units). Starting with just 100 Chromatic Metal, you can produce over 3,000 Chromatic Metal in under 30 minutes of active refining. For even greater efficiency, use the 大型精炼机 (3 slots) with the same recipe — 2 Copper + 1 Chromatic Metal = 6 Chromatic Metal, and the three-slot capacity allows larger batch processing. If you have access to a Cadmium, Emeril, or Indium mine (via the 铟驱动), these metals refine to Chromatic Metal at higher ratios: Cadmium 1:2, Emeril 1:3, and Indium 1:4. A single Activated Indium deposit can generate tens of thousands of 有色金属 per hour through refining.

● Copper (for 有色金属 refining)

来源: Green star system planets, surface deposits, subterranean minerals.

Best farming method: Copper is found on planets in Yellow (standard) star systems, but in much smaller quantities than on planets in Green star systems. If you have the 艾梅里尔驱动 installed, travel to a Green system and use your scanner to locate surface Copper deposits. Build a small mining base with an Autonomous Mining Unit over a large Copper deposit for truly passive resource generation. Each Autonomous Mining Unit produces 50 Copper every 10 minutes when powered. Alternatively, simply use the 地形操纵器 on visible Copper deposits — large deposits yield 100-200 Copper each. For massive stockpiles, establish an Electromagnetic-powered mining operation with multiple 矿物提取器s feeding 供应仓库s.

4 最优路线

此路线设计为在90分钟活跃游戏时间内生产50个跃迁超核，大部分生产通过基地设备被动进行。

PHASE 1: Base Preparation (20 minutes)

  1. Travel to a system with a Paradise or Verdant planet — these have the most red Oxygen flora if you need active gathering.
  2. Establish or visit your primary resource base. Ensure you have at least 4 氧气采集器s built and powered. If not, construct them now (200 Metal Plating total).
  3. Ensure your 中型精炼机 and 大型精炼机 are accessible. The 大型精炼机 at your base is preferred — it does not consume fuel and processes larger batches.
  4. Stockpile 100 Copper by mining surface deposits or activating your Autonomous Mining Units. This is your 有色金属 multiplication seed.
  5. Purchase 4,000 铁尘 from the space station trade terminal (costs approximately 20,000-40,000 units — negligible).

PHASE 2: 有色金属 Multiplication (15 minutes)

  1. Open your 大型精炼机. Place 2 Copper in the first slot and 1 有色金属 in the second slot.
  2. Process the batch. Output: 6 Chromatic Metal. Net gain: +3 有色金属.
  3. Repeat the process, always using 2 of your original Copper plus 1 有色金属 from the output. Each cycle multiplies your stockpile.
  4. Continue until you have 1,750 有色金属 in your inventory (enough for 50 Hypercores: 35 x 50 = 1,750).
  5. Pro tip: If you run out of Copper mid-process, quickly mine 2 more units from any surface deposit — Copper is universal on all planets. The multiplication chain can be restarted from any Copper source.

PHASE 3: Oxygen Collection (10 minutes active, 60 minutes passive)

  1. Collect the output from your 氧气采集器s if they have been running. 4 harvesters should yield 1,000+ Oxygen after 40 minutes.
  2. If you need additional Oxygen immediately, perform a surface gathering run: target every red bulbous plant in sight. A dense Paradise planet yields 200+ Oxygen in 10 minutes.
  3. Verify your Oxygen total: you need 1,000 Oxygen for 50 Hypercores.
  4. While you proceed to Phase 4, your 氧气采集器s continue producing — collect again before crafting if needed.

PHASE 4: Mass Crafting (20 minutes)

  1. Open your 外骨骼 inventory. You need at least 15 free slots — 5 for Antimatter Housings, 5 for Antimatter, and 5 for 跃迁超核s (crafting in batches of 5 avoids slot overflow).
  2. Craft 5 Antimatter Housings: requires 250 铁尘 + 100 Oxygen + 50 有色金属.
  3. Craft 5 Antimatter units: requires 125 有色金属 + Seal components.
  4. Craft 5 跃迁超核s by combining each Housing with one Antimatter. The craft is instant.
  5. Store completed Hypercores in your 货船 or 飞船. Repeat for remaining batches.
  6. Full 50-Hypercore production in 10 batches takes approximately 15-20 minutes of active crafting.

PHASE 5: Long-Term Passive Setup (One-time)

  1. Once you have experienced the efficiency of base-based resource production, consider establishing a dedicated Hypercore Factory base.
  2. Build 10 氧气采集器s on a Paradise planet near an Electromagnetic Power Hotspot.
  3. Build 5 Autonomous Mining Units over Copper deposits for continuous Copper generation.
  4. Set up 2 大型精炼机s as permanent fixtures.
  5. With this setup, you can craft 50 跃迁超核s every 2-3 days with only 5 minutes of active gameplay.

5 注意事项与技巧

需要蓝图：

你必须先学习蓝图才能制作跃迁超核。

格子管理：

跃迁超核是飞船消耗品，意味着必须在飞船库存中制作以直接为超光速引擎充能。

不要出售超核：

跃迁超核的出售价值约为每个1,000单位。这是一个陷阱。

铟驱动协同：

安装了铟驱动的玩家应优先在蓝色星系中开采铟来生产色金属。

采集器放置：

氧气采集器必须放置在陆地表面上——不能放在水下、超过45度的斜坡上或建筑物内部。

制作Bug解决方法：

有时制作界面不会识别到你有足够的跃迁超核材料。

6 沿途额外收益

建立跃迁超核生产基础设施会带来远超超光速燃料的实质性附带收益。

氧气采集器 Passive Income

The 氧气采集器s you build for Hypercore production generate far more Oxygen than you need for Hypercores alone. Excess Oxygen can be sold directly to trade terminals for approximately 30-40 每单位货币 piece, or it can be used as a critical component in the 停滞装置 and 聚变点火器 crafting chains — the two most valuable craftable items in the game at 15.6 million units each. Oxygen is also required for crafting 生命维持 Gel, which refills your hazard protection and life support systems. A well-maintained bank of 氧气采集器s effectively eliminates oxygen as a concern for the rest of your playthrough.

有色金属 Mastery

The Chromatic Metal multiplication technique you master for Hypercore production applies to virtually every advanced crafting recipe in 无人深空. Chromatic Metal is required for dozens of recipes including Circuit Boards, Superconductors, Fusion Accelerants, Quantum Processors, and the 停滞装置. By establishing a reliable Chromatic Metal production pipeline, you are simultaneously solving the material bottleneck for the entire endgame crafting economy. Many veteran players consider 有色金属 management the single most important logistical skill in the game.

Base Building Experience

Setting up an efficient resource base teaches you the fundamentals of base building: power management, hotspot location, supply depot routing, and extractor optimization. These skills are essential for building Activated Indium farms (the ultimate passive income source), Nano-harvesting operations, and elaborate showcase bases. The relatively simple Hypercore factory serves as an excellent training ground for the complex multi-extractor setups required by endgame farming operations.

Planetary Exploration Rewards

Finding the ideal planet for your 氧气采集器s and Copper deposits inevitably involves scanning multiple systems and planets. During this search, you will discover numerous valuable locations: Crashed Ships, Manufacturing Facilities, Drop Pods (for Exosuit upgrades), Ancient Ruins (for artifact excavation), and Trade Outposts. Mark these with 保存信标s or 基地电脑s as you find them — they represent significant progression opportunities that you can return to later. A single optimal-base search often yields 2-3 外骨骼 upgrade slots from Drop Pods alone.

Galactic Travel Proficiency

The frequent warping required to set up and maintain your Hypercore production makes you intimately familiar with the galactic map interface, economy scanning, system class filtering, and warp route optimization. These navigation skills are essential for community expeditions, coordinate-based item hunting, and reaching the galactic core. The 50 跃迁超核s you produce as your first batch will likely be spent on exploration that uncovers dozens of new systems, each with its own unique resources, fauna, and discovery rewards worth uploading for 纳米星团.

铁尘 Stockpile

The 4,000+ Ferrite Dust you purchase or mine for a 50-Hypercore batch represents one of the most versatile building materials in the game. Leftover Ferrite Dust can be refined into Pure Ferrite (1:1 ratio) and then Magnetised Ferrite (with Carbon), which are used in hundreds of base-building recipes, technology repairs, and exocraft upgrades. 铁尘 is also the primary material for constructing 保存点s, 信号增强器s, and 便携精炼机s — items you will place and replace hundreds of times throughout your journey. Having a massive stockpile means never interrupting your activities to gather basic building materials.
