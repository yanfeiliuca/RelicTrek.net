No Man's Sky

18 quintillion planets, billions of galaxies, and a crafting system deeper than most realize. 10 game-changing blueprints every explorer needs across the cosmos.

Void Egg (Living Ship)

★★★★★ Endgame

A biological starship that grows and upgrades itself. Requires completing the Starbirth mission chain across multiple star systems.

Warp Hypercore

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

100% warp cell for your starship. Craft Antimatter Housing + Antimatter. Essential for long-distance galactic travel.

Fusion Ignitor

★★★★★ Endgame

15.6 million units per sale. The most valuable craftable item. Requires materials from 4 different planet types across the galaxy.

Stasis Device

★★★★★ Endgame

15.6 million units — same value as Fusion Ignitor but different recipe. Build both for maximum profit from a single farming run.

Indium Drive

★★★★☆ Late Game

Travel to blue star systems (O-type, B-type). Unlock Chromatic Metal from Indium for massive refiner profits.

Minotaur Geobay

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

Summon a mechanical exocraft anywhere. Requires Roamer Geobay + Cadmium + Wiring Loom. The ultimate planetary explorer.

Underwater Protection Module

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

Extended underwater breathing for ocean planet exploration. Crafted at the Exosuit upgrade station with Chlorine and Oxygen.

Sentinel Exosuit Upgrades

★★★★☆ Late Game

Full combat exosuit from Sentinel pillars. Kill corrupted sentinels, collect shards, unlock the full blueprint tree.

S-Class Multi-Tool

★★★★☆ Late Game

24-slot experimental or royal multi-tool. Find at space stations, minor settlements, and sentinel pillars. Maximize damage and mining.

Starship AI Valves

★★★★★ Endgame

50 million units per valve. Salvage from crashed Sentinel interceptors or craft via the most complex recipe chain in the game.
