1 Why the Zenith Is the Ultimate Trophy

⚔

Zenith

★★★★★ — Game's Strongest Sword
Post-Game

The Zenith is the strongest sword in Terraria — a blade that fires a barrage of other swords at your cursor, melting bosses in seconds. With a base damage of 190 and the ability to hit through walls, it's not just powerful — it's the ultimate flex that proves you've conquered every stage of the game.

★ What This Means for You

The Zenith requires swords from Pre-Hardmode all the way to the Moon Lord. Crafting it is a journey through the entire game — and the reward is a weapon that makes every boss trivial.

4 Optimal Collection Route

Follow this order to minimize backtracking and maximize efficiency.

① Pre-Hardmode Collection

Get Copper Shortsword (starter) → Craft Blade of Grass (Jungle) → Craft Fiery Greatsword (Underworld) → Find Starfury (Sky Islands) → Find Enchanted Sword (Underground Shrines) → Craft Night's Edge (4 biome swords) → Defeat Queen Bee for Bee Keeper

② Hardmode Progression

Get Muramasa from Dungeon → Craft Excalibur from Hallowed Bars → Defeat all 3 Mechanical Bosses → Get Broken Hero Swords from Solar Eclipse → Craft True Excalibur and True Night's Edge → Craft Terra Blade

③ Endgame Grind

Defeat Moon Lord for Star Wrath AND Meowmere (11% each, expect 5-10 kills) → Trigger Martian Madness for Influx Waver (11%) → Find Terragrim/Arkhalis in Sword Shrines (very rare, grind many worlds)

④ Final Assembly

Combine ALL 10 swords at Mythril/Orichalcum Anvil → ZENITH obtained

5 ⚠ Gotcha Tips — Don't Waste Your Time

These mistakes will waste hours. Read this before you start.

⚠ Moon Lord drops are 11% each

Star Wrath and Meowmere each have 11% drop rate from Moon Lord. Expect 5-10 kills on average to get both. Use a strong farming setup.

⚠ Terragrim/Arkhalis is EXTREMELY rare

The Enchanted Sword Shrine that gives Terragrim is very rare. You may need to check 20+ worlds. Consider using a world seed known to have one.

⚠ Don't skip Broken Hero Swords

You need 3 Broken Hero Swords total (2 for True swords, context for progression). Solar Eclipse events are the only source.

⚠ Keep ALL swords you find

Many players accidentally sell or discard Copper Shortsword or early swords. Keep every sword — you need them all.

⚠ Martian Madness is a rare event

Influx Waver requires Martian Madness, triggered by a Martian Probe finding you in the outer space biome. Build a sky platform and wait.

6 🎁 Free Benefits — Loot You Pick Up Along the Way

While collecting materials, you'll pass through biomes full of valuable drops. Don't leave empty-handed.

🎁 Jungle exploration for Blade of Grass

While collecting Jungle Spores and Stingers: Get Rich Mahogany, M Jungle armor, Bezoar (for Ankh Shield later!), Vine (crafting), and jungle-exclusive accessories.

🎁 Underworld for Fiery Greatsword

Mining Hellstone: Collect Fireblossom, Obsidian, find Hellforge, explore Shadow Chests for unique weapons, and grab Lava Charm if you find one.

🎁 Dungeon for Muramasa

While getting Golden Keys: Farm Armor Polish and Nazar (both for Ankh Shield!), find Shadow Key, collect Ectoplasm post-Plantera.

🎁 Moon Lord farming

While grinding for Star Wrath/Meowmere: Moon Lord also drops Luminite (best endgame armor), Portal Gun, and other powerful weapons.
