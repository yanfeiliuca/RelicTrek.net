1 Why the PDA Is Half a Cell Phone

📟

PDA

★★★★☆ — 13 Info Displays
Hardmode

The PDA combines 13 different information accessories into one slot — showing depth, position, time, DPS, kill count, speed, weather, fishing info, and more. It's the main component of the Cell Phone, and crafting it is an achievement in itself.

★ What This Means for You

Information is the most underrated resource in Terraria. The PDA tells you everything: how deep you are, what time it is, what the weather will be, how fast you're moving, and how many enemies you've killed. Combined with a Magic Mirror, it becomes the legendary Cell Phone.

4 Optimal Collection Route

Follow this order to minimize backtracking and maximize efficiency.

① Buy from Traveling Merchant

Check every morning — buy Lifeform Analyzer (5g), Stopwatch (5g), and DPS Meter (5g) when available. These cannot be farmed any other way.

② Angler Quests (The Long Grind)

Do 1-2 Angler quests daily. You need Fisherman's Pocket Guide, Weather Radio, AND Sextant — each 3% reward. Expect 30-60 quests for all three.

③ Nymph for Metal Detector

Use Lifeform Analyzer to spot Nymphs disguised as Lost Girl Underground. 50% drop rate for Metal Detector — use Hunter Potion to reveal them.

④ Dungeon for Tally Counter

Farm Angry Bones / Dark Casters / Cursed Skulls in Dungeon (1% Tally Counter). Also grab Compass from Mimics if you haven't yet.

⑤ Underground for Compass + Depth Meter

Kill Mimics (Underground/Ice) for Compass (14%) → Kill Bats for Depth Meter (1%) or fish Wooden Crates (2%)

⑥ Craft and Assemble

Craft GPS (Compass+Depth+Watch) + REK 3000 (Radar+Tally+Analyzer) + Goblin Tech (Stopwatch+DPS+Detector) + Fish Finder (3 Angler items) → Combine ALL → PDA

5 ⚠ Gotcha Tips — Don't Waste Your Time

These mistakes will waste hours. Read this before you start.

⚠ Angler rewards are PURE RNG

The 3 fishing info items each have 3% quest reward rate. You might get duplicates. There is no bad luck protection. Keep doing quests daily — never skip.

⚠ Traveling Merchant items are time-gated

Lifeform Analyzer, Stopwatch, and DPS Meter appear randomly on the Traveling Merchant. Check EVERY morning. Can take 10-20 in-game days to see all three.

⚠ Gold Watch needs Platinum/Gold

You need 10 Gold or Platinum Bars + 1 Chain for the Watch. Don't sell your Gold/Platinum early game — you'll need it.

⚠ Keep ALL info accessories

Many players sell Compass, Depth Meter, or Radar. DO NOT SELL — you need every single one for the PDA. Store them in a chest if you must.

6 🎁 Free Benefits — Loot You Pick Up Along the Way

While collecting materials, you'll pass through biomes full of valuable drops. Don't leave empty-handed.

🎁 Angler quest fishing

While doing daily quests: Catch crates (valuable loot), find fishing-exclusive items, and build resources for potions (Ebonkoi, Prismite, etc.).

🎁 Nymph hunting

While hunting Nymphs: Explore deep caves for heart crystals, underground cabins (chests), and rare ores. Lifeform Analyzer also shows ALL rare enemies.

🎁 Dungeon for Tally Counter

While in Dungeon: Get Armor Polish and Nazar (Ankh Shield!), find Muramasa (Zenith/Night's Edge), and collect Bones.

🎁 Mimic hunting

Mimics drop Compass but also give excellent loot: Titan Glove, Philosopher's Stone, Cross Necklace, and gold. Use Mimic statues for AFK farms.
