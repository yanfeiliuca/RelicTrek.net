1 Why the Avenger Emblem Is Every Damage Build's Foundation

🔶

Avenger Emblem

★★★☆☆ — +12% All Damage
Hardmode Early

The Avenger Emblem gives +12% damage to ALL weapon types — melee, ranged, magic, and summon. It's the base ingredient for every specialized class emblem, making it the first crafting priority for any Hardmode damage dealer.

★ What This Means for You

Starting with +12% all-damage, you can then specialize: Warrior Emblem (+melee), Ranger Emblem (+ranged), Sorcerer Emblem (+magic), or Summoner Emblem (+minions). One emblem, five powerful paths.

4 Optimal Collection Route

Follow this order to minimize backtracking and maximize efficiency.

① Defeat Wall of Flesh (Multiple Times)

Wall of Flesh drops ONE random emblem per kill (~20% each). You need all 3 different emblems. Expect to fight WoF 3-5 times on average. Build a long Hellbridge for easier fights.

② Final Assembly

Combine all 3 emblems → AVENGER EMBLEM
Then upgrade to your class specialty: + Soul of Might → Warrior/Ranger/Sorcerer Emblem

5 ⚠ Gotcha Tips — Don't Waste Your Time

These mistakes will waste hours. Read this before you start.

⚠ Wall of Flesh drops are RANDOM

You might get the same emblem twice. There's no guarantee of getting all 3 quickly. Keep killing WoF until you have all three different emblems.

⚠ Guide Voodoo Doll is consumed

Each WoF fight requires sacrificing a Guide Voodoo Doll in lava. Stockpile dolls from Underground enemies, or wait for the Guide to respawn (next day).

6 🎁 Free Benefits — Loot You Pick Up Along the Way

While collecting materials, you'll pass through biomes full of valuable drops. Don't leave empty-handed.

🎁 Hellbridge construction

While building your Hellbridge for WoF fights: Mine Hellstone (for Fiery Greatsword / Obsidian Skull), find Shadow Chests, collect Fireblossom, and grab Lava Charms if lucky.

🎁 Wall of Flesh drops

Besides emblems, WoF drops Pwnhammer (smash Demon Altars), Breaker Blade (great early Hardmode sword), and starts Hardmode (new ores, new everything).
