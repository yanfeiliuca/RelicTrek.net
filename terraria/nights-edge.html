1 Why Night's Edge Is Your First Real Weapon

🌑

Night's Edge

★★☆☆☆ — First Great Sword
Pre-Hardmode

The Night's Edge is the strongest Pre-Hardmode sword — and a required ingredient for the legendary Terra Blade and ultimately the Zenith. It combines the power of four biome-specific swords into one blade with a sweeping dark energy attack.

★ What This Means for You

At 42 damage with a wide swing arc, the Night's Edge carries you through the Wall of Flesh fight and into early Hardmode. But more importantly, it's a key ingredient in the crafting chain that leads to the Terra Blade, True Night's Edge, and eventually the Zenith.

4 Optimal Collection Route

Follow this order to minimize backtracking and maximize efficiency.

① Jungle: Blade of Grass

Explore Underground Jungle → Collect 12 Jungle Spores (grow on grass) + 12 Stingers (Hornets) → Craft at Iron Anvil

② Dungeon: Muramasa

Defeat Skeletron → Enter Dungeon → Kill Dungeon Slimes for Golden Keys → Open Locked Gold Chests → Find Muramasa

③ Underworld: Fiery Greatsword

Mine Hellstone in Underworld (needs 60%+ pickaxe power) → Mix with Obsidian at Hellforge → Make 20 Hellstone Bars → Craft Fiery Greatsword

④ Evil Biome: Light's Bane / Blood Butcherer

Defeat Eater of Worlds / Brain of Cthulhu → Mine Demonite/Crimtane Ore → Smelt into Bars → Craft your world's evil sword

⑤ Final Assembly

All 4 swords + Demon/Crimson Altar → NIGHT'S EDGE

5 ⚠ Gotcha Tips — Don't Waste Your Time

These mistakes will waste hours. Read this before you start.

⚠ You need YOUR world's evil sword

Corruption worlds make Light's Bane. Crimson worlds make Blood Butcherer. You CANNOT craft the other. If you need the other, create a new world or trade with a friend.

⚠ Muramasa requires Skeletron

You MUST defeat Skeletron to enter the Dungeon. Talk to the Old Man at Dungeon entrance at night to summon him.

⚠ Hellstone requires good pickaxe

Hellstone requires 60% pickaxe power (Nightmare/Deathbringer Pickaxe or better). You get these from defeating Eater of Worlds/Brain of Cthulhu.

⚠ Don't craft at a regular crafting station

Night's Edge requires a Demon Altar or Crimson Altar — the purple/red altars found in Corruption/Crimson. You can't craft it at a normal Anvil.

6 🎁 Free Benefits — Loot You Pick Up Along the Way

While collecting materials, you'll pass through biomes full of valuable drops. Don't leave empty-handed.

🎁 Jungle exploration

While getting Blade of Grass: Find Bezoar (Ankh Shield!), Feral Claws, Boomstick, Life Crystals, and explore bee hives for Queen Bee.

🎁 Dungeon run

While getting Muramasa: Collect Golden Keys, find Shadow Key, get Band of Regeneration/Starpower, and stockpile Bones.

🎁 Underworld mining

While mining Hellstone: Find Shadow Chests (unique weapons), collect Fireblossom, grab Obsidian Rose (lava immunity), and find Hellforge.

🎁 Evil biome boss

Defeating Eater of Worlds/Brain: Drops Shadow Scale/Tissue Sample (armor), enables Meteorite to land (Space Gun ammo!), and unlocks Goblin Tinkerer (Rocket Boots!).
