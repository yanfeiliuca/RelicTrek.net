④ Optimal Route

①

Know Your Foe

Kirin is an Elder Dragon but with a small frame and extreme speed. Unlike most Elders, it zips across the arena constantly.

②

Learn Thunder Patterns

Study Kirin's thunder mode tells. It telegraphs lightning strikes and charged body attacks — learn to read these animations and roll through them.

③

Priority Breaks: Horn & Back

Focus on breaking the horn (Thunderhorn, 60%) and back (Mane, 40%) first. These give the most needed materials with the best drop rates.

④

The Ruby Grind — 1% Drop Rate

Kirin Ruby at 1% carve rate is one of the rarest gems in the game. Expect 50-80 Kirin hunts. Use investigations with gold/silver reward boxes to boost odds.

⑤

Forging

Once all materials are gathered, head to the Smithy. The full Kirin set provides Thunder Attack Lv.5 and Free Element — turning any elemental weapon into a thunder beast.

⑤ Gotcha Tips

💀

Rarest of the Rare

Kirin Ruby at 1% is among the game's most punishing grinds. Investigations with bonus reward boxes are mandatory — pure carves will break your spirit.

🎯

Small Target, Big Weapon

Kirin's tiny hitbox makes Great Sword, Hammer, and Hunting Horn extremely frustrating. Fast weapons like Dual Blades or Long Sword fare much better.

⚡

Unblockable Thunder

Most of Kirin's lightning attacks bypass Guard. Evade Extender and Evade Window skills are far more valuable than Guard Up for this fight.

🛡️

Thunder Resistance Required

Kirin's attacks deal massive thunder damage. Stack Thunder Res jewels or eat for Elemental Resist (L) — without 20+ thunder res, you'll be stunned constantly.

🔷

Azure Horn = High Rank Only

Kirin Azure Horn only drops from High Rank Kirin horn breaks (35%). Low Rank Kirin will never drop this — you must tackle HR quests.

⑥ Free Benefits

🐉

Elder Dragon Mastery

Farming Kirin builds essential Elder Dragon hunting experience — learning their patterns, AI behavior, and reward structures carries over to all other Elders.

⛏️

Eltalite Ore Stockpile

Kirin's habitat contains high-level mining nodes. While hunting, you'll collect Eltalite Ore (25% mine rate) used in numerous endgame armor pieces and weapons.

🎯

Precision Training

Kirin's small size and speed make it one of the best monsters for practicing targeting small, fast-moving weak points — a skill that pays off against Rajang and others.

🪲

Thunderbug Harvest

Kirin's locale is rich with Thunderbugs and other rare endemic life. Capture these for crafting Thunder Traps and selling for zenny.
