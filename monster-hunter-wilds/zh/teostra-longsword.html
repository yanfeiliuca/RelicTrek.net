爆破属性+高raw，Blast procs给全队加伤害，喷火古龙的刀刃从内部爆炸

④ 最优路线

①

掌握超新星

Learn 炎王龙's signature supernova attack. It has a clear wind-up — 炎王龙 roars, arches back, and flames gather around its body. Use this tell to sheathe and superman-dive or farcaster out.

②

优先破坏：头部和腿部

集中攻击头部（角，55%）和腿部（爪，30%）。破坏头部还能降低炎王龙火焰气场的威力, making the fight safer overall.

③

躲避粉尘爆炸

炎王龙在场地中散布红色爆炸粉尘。引爆时爆炸范围巨大。 Watch the ground — red particles mean danger zone. Move away before the boom.

④

The Gem Grind — 2% Drop Rate

炎王龙宝玉2%剥取率虽不如某些残酷，但仍需耐心。 Run investigations with gold and silver reward boxes to significantly improve your odds.

⑤

锻造

Forge at the Smithy once all materials are collected. The 炎王龙 太刀 delivers high raw damage plus Blast status — each proc deals bonus damage and helps the entire team.

⑤ 注意事项

☠️

超新星=几乎即死

炎王龙的超新星拥有游戏中最大的伤害倍率之一。 Without 200+ HP or fire res, it is a guaranteed cart. Always keep a Farcasters or be ready to superman-dive.

💥

粉尘爆炸范围具有欺骗性

炎王龙散布的红色粉尘爆炸范围远比视觉效果显示的大。 When you see it glowing bright, roll twice as far as you think you need to.

💎

Gem at 2% — Still a Grind

炎王龙宝玉2%剥取率意味着平均约50次狩猎出一个。 Investigations with 2+ silver/gold boxes can cut this to 15-20 runs. Don't burn out — pace yourself.

🔥

火抗必备

没有火属性抗性装备（20+），仅仅站在炎王龙附近其气场就会持续造成小额伤害。 Equip Fire Res jewels or eat for Fire Res (L) before every hunt.

🐉

多古龙任务极其残酷

炎王龙+另一只古龙（如钢龙）的任务难度呈指数增长。 炎王龙's explosions don't discriminate — they'll hit other monsters and cause chaos. Stick to solo 炎王龙 hunts for farming.

⑥ 额外收益

⛏️

高级采矿场

炎王龙's domain in the Elder's Recess contains the highest density of red outcrops in the game. While hunting, you'll gather Firecell Stones (20% rate) needed for all fire-element endgame gear.

💥

爆炸机制精通

Learning to read 炎王龙's tells teaches you universal explosion-avoidance skills that transfer directly to Bazelgeuse, Seething Bazelgeuse, and Brachydios fights.

🐉

古龙连击模式

炎王龙 has some of the most telegraphed but punishing combos among Elders. Mastering its pattern recognition gives you a huge advantage against 灭尽龙, 钢龙, and Velkhana.

🔥

狱炎石储备

The Firecell Stones you mine during 炎王龙 hunts are used in nearly every fire-element weapon and armor piece in the endgame. Your grind here pays dividends across your entire equipment roster.
