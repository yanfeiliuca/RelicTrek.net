04

最优路线

Follow this step-by-step route to acquire the 冥灯龙 长枪. This is a post-game weapon, so the route assumes you've completed the main story.

1

解锁最终Boss战

⏱ Prerequisite: Complete Main Story

冥灯龙 is the final boss of Monster Hunter Wilds. You must complete the entire main story campaign to unlock this hunt. After the credits roll, 冥灯龙 will be available as a repeatable assignment and eventually as an Investigation.

2

Learn 冥灯龙's Three-Phase Mechanics

⏱ 3-5 learning runs (20-30 min each)

冥灯龙 is a multi-phase, 20-30 minute battle that fundamentally changes attack patterns between phases. Don't rush — take time to learn each phase's tells and safe windows.

🟢 Phase 1: Ground Phase

冥灯龙 is grounded and relatively slow. Learn to counter-claw its lunges and break the front legs early. Focus on the head when it's down. This phase is about building damage and breaking parts.

🟡 Phase 2: Aerial Phase

冥灯龙 takes flight and gains new aerial beam attacks. The supercritical beam (ground sweep) has a massive wind-up — perfect for clutch claw mounting. Prioritize breaking wings to force it back down.

🔴 Phase 3: Critical State

冥灯龙 enters supercritical state — chest glows intensely, attack speed increases dramatically, and new explosive AoE patterns emerge. The chest takes massive damage when glowing — this is your DPS window. Play safe and exploit the chest glow.

3

破坏所有可破坏部位

⏱ Ongoing across all hunts

Maximize your material yield by breaking every possible part each hunt: Head (Horn), both Front Legs (Claws), Wings, and Tail. Each break adds reward rolls and increases your chances for the Gem.

Break Priority (Solo):
  1. Both Front Legs — Claw material + trip opportunities
  2. Head — Horn material (50% break rate)
  3. Tail — Extra carve opportunity
  4. Wings — Force out of aerial phase
4

宝玉刷取（3%）

⏱ 8-20 hours (highly RNG dependent)

The 冥灯龙 Gem (3%) is the ultimate bottleneck. At 20-30 minutes per hunt, even with optimal clears, this will take time. Squad up for faster clears and more fun. Sub-15 minute runs with a good team make this grind manageable.

05

注意事项

The 冥灯龙 长枪 journey is riddled with traps that have frustrated countless hunters. Know these gotchas before you commit.

⏱️

20-30 Minute Marathon Battles

冥灯龙 is not a quick hunt. Even experienced teams need 15-20 minutes, and solo hunters often face 25-30 minute slogs. Unlike 灭尽龙 (8-10 min), you cannot speed-run this efficiently. The Gem grind isn't just about drop rate — it's about the massive time investment per attempt.

Reality check: At 25 min/hunt × 23 hunts (50% gem chance) = ~9.5 hours of pure 冥灯龙 fighting. Plan your sessions accordingly.
💥

Phase 3 Critical State — Attack Pattern Overhaul

When 冥灯龙 enters supercritical state (Phase 3), its entire moveset changes. Beams become faster, explosion AoEs expand, and the attack cadence increases dramatically. Many cart (faint) here because they play Phase 3 like Phase 1. Adjust your rhythm — play safer, exploit the glowing chest, and don't get greedy.

Phase 3 changes:
  • Beam wind-up time reduced by ~40%
  • New double-explosion ground AoE pattern
  • Chest becomes a massive weak point (x3 damage)
  • Enrage lasts longer and hits harder
  • Some safe zones from Phase 1 are no longer safe
💎

3% Gem + Long Hunt = Brutal Farm Efficiency

Other 古龙 gems have similar or lower rates (灭尽龙: 2%, 炎王龙: 2%), but those hunts are 3× faster. 冥灯龙's gem is uniquely painful because you can't brute-force it quickly. Investigations help but are limited. This is a patience test above all else.

🔒

Story Completion Required

You cannot fight 冥灯龙 or craft this weapon until you complete the entire main story. For hunters who rush through story content, this means going back to complete anything you skipped. There's no shortcut — finish the campaign first.

💍

Two Decoration Slots = More Decoration Demand

Two Lv.3 slots are incredible if you have the decorations to fill them. If your decoration collection is sparse, those slots sit empty and the weapon underperforms. Before committing to this grind, check your decoration inventory — you need at least two solid Lv.3 decos to justify this weapon over simpler alternatives.

Recommended Lv.3 Decorations:
  • Weakness Exploit — +30% affinity on weak spots
  • Critical Boost — +critical hit damage
  • Handicraft — Extend white sharpness
  • Guard Up — Block unblockable attacks
  • Partbreaker — Faster part destruction
