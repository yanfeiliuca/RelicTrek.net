风漂龙 弓

难度 ★★★☆☆ 中期

① Overview

The 风漂龙 弓 stands as the premier Ice Element bow in Monster Hunter Wilds, offering hunters an exceptional entry point into mastering the bow weapon class. 凭借出色的临界距离性能和毁灭性的扩散射击能力， 这把武器对弱火怪物能持续造成高额伤害。

Its well-balanced stats and forgiving learning curve make it the perfect introductory bow for hunters transitioning from melee weapons or those looking to specialize in elemental ranged combat. The 风漂龙 弓's ice element proves devastating against 火龙, Anjanath, and other fire-based threats roaming the Coral Highlands.

③ Materials

风漂龙 Scale ×8

Obtained from body carves after defeating 风漂龙. The primary crafting component forms the bow's elegant ice-blue frame. At 35% carve rate, expect 3-4 hunts for enough scales.

Target carves — 3 per hunt

Break both wings — 40% rate each

风漂龙 Claw ×3

Gathered from broken legs. The sharp talons serve as the bow's grip stabilizers, ensuring steady aim during charged shots. Focus legs when 风漂龙 lands after aerial sweeps.

Break legs — 25% rate

The rarest component at a mere 3% carve rate. This pristine chest plating houses concentrated ice energy. Many hunters spend hours grinding for this elusive drop — patience and Persistence are key.

④ Optimal Route

1

Hunt 风漂龙 3-4 Times — Learn Its Patterns

在陆珊瑚台地刷取，专注于学习风漂龙的两个招牌攻击：横扫冰息（宽扇形、高伤害、侧向回避）和俯冲空袭（前滚穿过它）。 These hunts will naturally yield most Scales and Webbing.

2

Priority Target — Break Wings & Legs

During each hunt, concentrate fire on the wings first (Webbing at 40%) then the legs (Claw at 25%). Use the clutch claw to create weak points when needed. Breaking parts not only yields materials but also disables 风漂龙's aerial dominance.

3

Grind the Plate — The 3% Challenge

风漂龙的翼膜是最终的守门人。 With only a 3% carve chance, expect 10-15+ dedicated hunts. 如果有的话装备风漂龙的恩泽防具套装效果（幸运）, or eat for Lucky Cat to boost rewards. Investigations with silver/gold reward boxes help bypass pure RNG.

4

Mine Coral Crystals — Scenic Gathering Run

路线覆盖陆珊瑚台地全部5个采矿区域。 The crystal-rich areas near sector 8 and the highest ridge consistently spawn blue mining nodes. 带上地质学3级获得额外采集次数。

5

Forging — The Smith's Anvil

With all 29 materials gathered, visit the smithy. The 风漂龙 弓 branches from the Iron 弓 III tree. Total crafting cost: 24,000z. Enjoy your new ice element weapon!

⑤ Gotcha Tips

🎲 风漂龙 Plate — 3% Drop Rate Nightmare

The Plate is the single biggest time sink in this craft. At 3% carve rate with 3 carves per hunt, you have roughly a 9% chance per hunt. Use investigations with silver/gold reward boxes as a parallel farm route. Don't let frustration burn you out — mix in other hunts.

❄️ 冰冻袋仅限上位

Frost Sacs only drop in High Rank. Low Rank 风漂龙 hunts are a complete waste of time for this material. Make sure you're launching HR quests (6★ and above) before beginning this grind. Check your quest star rating before every hunt.

🏹 弓需要大量瓶

弓s consume coatings rapidly — Power Coating, Close-range Coating, and status coatings all require dedicated farming. Stockpile Empty Phials and Nitroshrooms between hunts. A bow without coatings loses 30-40% of its damage potential.

💡 闪光弹应对空战

风漂龙 spends roughly 60% of the fight airborne. Bring 3 Flash Pods and 10 Flashbugs to craft mid-hunt. A well-timed flash brings it crashing down, creating massive damage windows. Aim the flash slightly above 风漂龙 for best results.

🧊 冰属性相性很重要

Ice deals massive damage to 火龙, Rathian, Anjanath, and 炎王龙. However, it's completely ineffective against water monsters like Jyuratodus or mud-based foes. Always check monster weaknesses before equipping this bow — element matching is everything.

⑥ Free Benefits

🌺 陆珊瑚台地观光

The 风漂龙 grind takes you through one of the most visually stunning biomes in the game. The luminescent coral spires, floating jellyfish, and crystalline waterfalls make farming far more pleasant. Unique mining outcrops here also yield rare trade-in items.

🎯 掌握临界距离

Learning the bow means mastering Critical Distance — the sweet spot where arrows deal maximum damage. The 风漂龙 弓's forgiving spread pattern makes this learning curve smoother. This skill transfers directly to all other bow builds.

🫐 打消果实采集

The Highlands are rich with Nulberries, essential for curing Iceblight (风漂龙's status ailment). Stock up during your farm runs — you'll need them for this hunt and many future endgame encounters.

🐉 对飞行怪瞄准练习

风漂龙 is one of the best training dummies for ranged aiming. Its erratic flight patterns force you to lead shots, predict movement, and manage stamina. By the time you finish farming, you'll be a significantly better ranged hunter.
