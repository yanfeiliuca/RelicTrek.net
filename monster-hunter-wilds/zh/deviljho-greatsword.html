恐暴龙 大剑

难度 ★★★★☆ 后期

① Overview

The 恐暴龙 大剑 represents the absolute pinnacle of raw damage output in Monster Hunter Wilds. Forged from the parts of the fearsome World Eater himself, this colossal blade boasts the highest raw attack value of any greatsword in the game — 其数值连终盘配装都要刮目相看。

然而，这份力量有其代价：武器带有显著的负会心率(-30%)，意味着约三分之一的攻击会造成较低伤害。 熟练的猎人通过见切技能和真蓄力斩自带会心加成来弥补，将这一缺点转化为可控的特性。 Wielding the 恐暴龙 大剑 is as much a badge of honor as it is a weapon — proof that you have slain the apex predator of the New World.

③ Materials

恐暴龙厚鳞×10

Thick, dark scales harvested from the World Eater's hide. These form the foundation of the greatsword's massive blade. At 30% carve rate with 3 carves per kill, expect 4-5 hunts to accumulate enough scales.

Body carves — 30% rate, 3 per hunt

恐暴龙尖爪×4

Razor-sharp claws obtained by breaking 恐暴龙's powerful hind legs. These wicked talons form the crossguard of the greatsword. Focus your attacks on the legs when 恐暴龙 enters rage mode — they become more vulnerable.

Break legs — 25% rate

恐暴龙巨牙×2

从恐暴龙巨颚中破坏的超大型獠牙。50%破头掉率，最容易获取的素材。獠牙作为装饰性但令人畏惧的元素装饰在刀刃脊线上。

Break head — 50% rate (highest!)

④ Optimal Route

1

Locate 恐暴龙 — The Invading Apex

恐暴龙 is an invasion monster that appears randomly during High Rank expeditions and quests, or through special event assignments. Look for the "???" monster warning on your map, or follow the monster tracks (gigantic footprints, destroyed terrain) to track it. Some investigations guarantee 恐暴龙 spawns — prioritize these.

2

Lure or Track — Patience is Required

Carry Raw Meat as bait — 恐暴龙 will stop to eat when fatigued, creating massive attack windows. Alternatively, equip the Scholar skill to speed up investigation progress from tracks. Scoutflies turn green when tracking an invasion monster.

3

Priority Target — Break Head & Legs

Focus on the head first for Tallfangs (50% break rate) — this also reduces the bite attack hitbox. Then break the legs for Talons (25%) which will cause 恐暴龙 to stumble frequently. Avoid the front when enraged; its dragon breath has enormous range.

4

The Gem Grind — 2% Hell

With a 2% carve rate, the 恐暴龙 Gem is statistically the hardest material in this build. Run investigations with 2+ gold reward boxes to bypass carve RNG entirely. On average, expect 20-30 hunts without gold box luck. The Gem can also appear in the Elder Melder's pot — save your steel/gold wyverian prints.

5

Forging — The Apex Blade

Once all 29 materials are secured, visit the smithy. The 恐暴龙 大剑 branches from the Buster Blade tree. Total forging cost: 54,000z. Augment slots: 1. The resulting weapon's raw attack of 1,100 makes every hunt worth it.

⑤ Gotcha Tips

🌪️ 恐暴龙是不稳定的入侵怪物

Unlike standard monsters, 恐暴龙 cannot be reliably farmed through standard quest selection. It appears randomly during expeditions and certain quests. Save scumming investigations or joining SOS flares are often faster than waiting for natural spawns.

🎲 Gem 2% — The Ultimate RNG Gate

The 恐暴龙 Gem's 2% carve rate means you could theoretically go 50+ hunts without seeing one. Always check investigation reward boxes — silver and gold boxes have independent Gem drop chances that stack with carves. Use the Elder Melder as a last resort with wyverian prints.

➖ 负会心率需要技能投入

At -30% affinity, roughly 1 in 3 hits deals 25% less damage. You must slot in Critical Eye (ideally level 7 for +30%) or Weakness Exploit to bring effective affinity into positive territory. Without these skills, the "highest raw damage" becomes mediocre real damage.

⚔️ 极高攻击力=频繁弹刀

The greatsword's raw power is so high that even slightly hardened monster parts will cause your attacks to bounce. Always aim for wounded weak points or bring Mind's Eye (bypasses bouncing). Nothing is more frustrating than a charged True Charge Slash bouncing off a monster's leg.

🐲 龙息范围极大

Deviljjo's dragon element breath attack covers a deceptively wide cone and inflicts Dragonblight (nullifies your weapon's element). The tell is a brief head rear-back — dodge sideways, not backward. Bring Nulberries and consider Dragon Resistance decorations for comfort.

⑥ Free Benefits

🦴 高级骨头掉落

恐暴龙 consistently drops Monster Hardbone and Monster Keenbone in large quantities. These are used across dozens of other weapon and armor crafts. Your 恐暴龙 grind will likely stockpile enough bones to skip farming for multiple other gear pieces.

📋 额外调查点数

Invasion hunts award substantially more research points than standard quests. The ecological data from 恐暴龙 encounters also progresses your monster research level rapidly, unlocking more valuable investigations and wider scoutfly ranges.

🛡️ 掌握攻击性大型怪物

恐暴龙 is arguably the best training dummy for learning to fight large, highly aggressive monsters. Its telegraphed but punishing attacks teach dodge timing, positioning, and stamina management. Skills developed here transfer directly to endgame 古龙 fights.

💎 龙脉晶采矿

Tracking 恐暴龙 through the Elder's Recess and Rotten Vale leads you past numerous Dragonvein Crystal mining nodes. These crystals are needed for many late-game weapon augments and armor upgrades, making the search route a productive gathering run.
