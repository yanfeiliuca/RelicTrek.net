④ 最优路线

①

了解你的敌人

麒麟虽是古龙但体型小巧、速度极快。与其他古龙不同，它会在场地中快速穿梭。

②

掌握雷电模式

Study 麒麟's thunder mode tells. It telegraphs lightning strikes and charged body attacks — learn to read these animations and roll through them.

③

优先破坏：角和背部

优先破坏角（雷角，60%）和背部（鬃毛，40%）。 These give the most needed materials with the best drop rates.

④

The Ruby Grind — 1% Drop Rate

麒麟红宝石1%剥取率是游戏中最稀有的宝玉之一。 Expect 50-80 麒麟 hunts. Use investigations with gold/silver reward boxes to boost odds.

⑤

锻造

Once all materials are gathered, head to the Smithy. 全套麒麟防具提供雷属性攻击Lv.5和属性解放 — turning any elemental weapon into a thunder beast.

⑤ 注意事项

💀

稀有中的稀有

麒麟红宝石1%掉率是游戏中最折磨人的刷取之一。 Investigations with bonus reward boxes are mandatory — pure carves will break your spirit.

🎯

小目标，大武器

麒麟's tiny hitbox makes Great Sword, 锤子, and Hunting Horn extremely frustrating. Fast weapons like Dual Blades or Long Sword fare much better.

⚡

不可防御的雷电

麒麟大部分雷电攻击会穿透防御。回避距离和回避性能技能对这战远比防御强化更有价值。

🛡️

需要雷属性抗性

麒麟的攻击造成巨额雷属性伤害。堆砌耐雷珠或吃饭选择属性抗性（大） — without 20+ thunder res, you'll be stunned constantly.

🔷

苍角仅限上位

麒麟苍角只从上位麒麟破角掉落（35%）。下位麒麟永远不会掉落 — you must tackle HR quests.

⑥ 额外收益

🐉

古龙精通

Farming 麒麟 builds essential 古龙 hunting experience — learning their patterns, AI behavior, and reward structures carries over to all other Elders.

⛏️

灵鹤石储备

麒麟's habitat contains high-level mining nodes. While hunting, you'll collect Eltalite Ore (25% mine rate) used in numerous endgame armor pieces and weapons.

🎯

精准训练

麒麟's small size and speed make it one of the best monsters for practicing targeting small, fast-moving weak points — a skill that pays off against Rajang and others.

🪲

雷光虫采集

麒麟's locale is rich with Thunderbugs and other rare endemic life. Capture these for crafting Thunder Traps and selling for zenny.
