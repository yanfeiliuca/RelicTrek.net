Nergigante Hammer

★★★★★ Endgame
Elder Dragon Devastation

Overview

The Nergigante Hammer is one of the pinnacle raw damage weapons in Monster Hunter Wilds. Boasting a massive 1508 base attack, innate Elderseal capability, and some of the highest raw damage output of any hammer in the game, this weapon is the reward for mastering one of the most fearsome Elder Dragons.

Elderseal suppresses Elder Dragon auras and abilities — making this hammer not just a raw powerhouse but also a strategic tool against the toughest endgame foes. The dragon-element aesthetic with jagged spike detailing makes it one of the most visually intimidating hammers in the game.

Optimal Route

1

Master the Spike Mechanic

Nergigante grows white spikes that harden to black over time. White spikes break easily and drop Carapace. Black spikes cause weapon bounce (unless you have Mind’s Eye). Prioritize breaking white spikes before they mature.

Tip: When all body spikes turn black, Nergigante uses his dive-bomb super attack — a massive AoE. Superman-dive or sheathe and sprint to survive.

2

Systematic Part Destruction

Recommended order: Horns → Legs → Tail

  • Horns (2 breaks): Focus head when he’s tired or trapped. 65% Horn drop rate.
  • Legs (2 breaks): Front legs share break threshold. Safer than head during enrage.
  • Tail (sever): Use a cutting weapon or Palico with cutting damage.
3

Gem Grind (2% Drop Rate)

At 2%, the Gem is harder than Rathalos Plate. Expect 30–50 hunts on average. Maximize your odds:

  • Run Tempered Investigations with 4-5 purple reward boxes
  • Use Elder Melder to trade other Elder Dragon gems
  • Complete the Elder Dragon weekly bounty for a shot at trade-in tickets
  • Gold and Silver reward boxes can contain Gems
4

Forging

With Gem in hand, craft at the Smithy. The hammer has a direct upgrade path — be prepared to farm Nergigante again for the final upgrade stage.

Gotcha Tips

☠️ Gem 2% — Worse Than Rathalos Plate

At a brutal 2% carve rate, the Nergigante Gem is one of the rarest materials in the game. Expect a serious grind. The Elder Melder can trade other Elder Dragon gems for it — save your gold wyverian prints!

⚠️ Black Spikes = Bounce City

Without Mind’s Eye (_skill or decoration_), hitting blackened body parts will cause your weapon to bounce. This interrupts combos and leaves you vulnerable. Break white spikes ASAP or bring Mind’s Eye.

⚠️ Nergigante Gets Stronger as the Fight Goes On

His spikes regrow and harden faster the more damage he takes. Long fights become increasingly dangerous. Play aggressively and end hunts fast — don’t play defensively.

⚠️ Elder Dragon Blood Is Quest-Only

You cannot farm Elder Dragon Blood from carves or gathering. It only comes from quest reward boxes. This means Investigation quests with extra reward boxes are mandatory, not optional.

⚠️ Solo Farm Recommended

Nergigante scales heavily with multiplayer health. For efficient material farming (especially part breaks), solo hunts are significantly faster than SOS/random groups. Bring your Palico with a cutting weapon to help sever the tail.

Free Benefits

⛏️

Elder’s Recess Mining

Nergigante hunts are the perfect excuse to run full mining routes through Elder’s Recess. Stockpile Dragonite Ore and Carbalite Ore for future crafts.

⚔️

Endgame Combat Mastery

Nergigante is the ultimate skill check. Learning his patterns prepares you for Arch-Tempered Elder Dragons and endgame siege content. Every hunt makes you a better player.

🧬

Elder Dragon Material Stockpile

Farming Nergigante generates excess Carapace, Tails, and Talons. These are needed for other Nergigante weapons and armor — you’re building a material bank.

📋

Investigation Quest Economy

Nergigante Investigations with 4-5 reward boxes are among the most profitable activities in the game. Sell excess materials for zenny and build a fortune while grinding for your Gem.
