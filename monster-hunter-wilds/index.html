Monster Hunter Wilds Crafting Guides

10 essential armors, weapons, and upgrades that define your hunting career. Every monster part, every drop rate, every farming shortcut.

Rathalos Armor Set

★★★★☆ Mid Game

Attack Boost Lv.3 + Fire Resistance. The first armor set that transforms your hunter. Requires 4-6 Rathalos hunts and a 5% drop Plate.

Nergigante Hammer

★★★★★ Endgame

1508 raw attack with Elderseal. The spike-breaking endgame grind that teaches you everything about part targeting.

Legiana Bow

★★★☆☆ Mid Game

Best Ice element bow. Good critical distance and spread shot. Perfect for learning bow mechanics while farming a beautiful monster.

Mighty Charm

★★★★☆ Endgame

Attack Boost Lv.3 in a single charm slot. The most efficient use of your limited charm slot. Requires multiple Elder Dragon parts.

Deviljho Greatsword

★★★★☆ Late Game

Highest raw damage Greatsword in the game. Negative affinity but devastating True Charge Slashes. Requires hunting the world-eater itself.

Kirin Armor Set

★★★★★ Endgame

Elemental mastery set. Thunder Attack Lv.5 + Free Element. Turns elemental weapons into monsters. The unicorn grind is legendary.

Teostra Longsword

★★★★☆ Endgame

Blast element + high raw. Blast procs add team-wide damage. The fire-breathing Elder's blade that explodes monsters from within.

Vaal Hazak Armor Set

★★★★☆ Endgame

Super Recovery + Dragon Resistance. The zombie dragon's armor lets you regenerate health constantly. Tanky, sustainable, terrifying.

Attack Boost Decorations

★★★★★ Endgame

Attack Jewel 1, 2, and 4. The rarest decorations in the game. Melding pot, threat 3 investigations, and pure RNG. Worth every hour.

Xeno'Jiiqa Lance

★★★★★ Post-Game

Dragon element + white sharpness + two level 3 decoration slots. The final boss weapon that rewards mastery with endless customization.
