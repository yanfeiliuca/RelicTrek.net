Rathalos Armor Set

★★★★☆ Mid Game
Attack Boost + Fire Resist

Overview

The Rathalos Armor Set is your first truly game-changing full armor set in Monster Hunter Wilds. Featuring Attack Boost Lv.3, substantial Fire Resistance, and the iconic crimson winged appearance, this set marks your transition from early-game scrubs to a mid-game threat.

Not only does it dramatically boost your raw damage output, but the fire resistance makes subsequent Rathalos hunts and other fire-based monsters significantly more manageable. The signature red-wing look is one of the most recognizable silhouettes in the entire Monster Hunter franchise.

Optimal Route

1

Learn Rathalos Attack Patterns

Bring Flash Pods ×3 and Pitfall Trap + Shock Trap. Rathalos spends a lot of time airborne — flash him out of the sky for free damage windows. Learn his tell before the 180-degree fire sweep.

2

Targeted Part Breaks

Priority: Head → Back → Wings. Head breaks give extra Shell chances. Back break is guaranteed Shell. Both wing breaks drop Webbing. Use Partbreaker Lv.3 if available.

3

Plate Grind (5% Drop Rate)

Expect 15–25 hunts on average. Speed this up by:

  • Using Investigation Quests with gold/silver reward boxes
  • Wearing Legiana Blessing (Good Luck) for extra rewards
  • Breaking every possible part for more reward rolls
4

Mine on the Side

While running Rathalos hunts, hit every mining node on your route. Firestone and Machalite will come naturally — no need to dedicated farm.

5

Forging

Once you have the Plate and all other materials, head to the Smithy. Craft each piece individually: Head → Chest → Arms → Waist → Legs.

Gotcha Tips

⚠️ Plate is Pure RNG Torture

At 5%, the Plate is the single biggest bottleneck. Don’t burn out — mix in other hunts and come back. Investigation quests with 3+ gold boxes dramatically improve your odds.

⚠️ Marrow Requires High Rank

Rathalos Marrow does not drop from Low Rank Rathalos. You must progress the story to unlock High Rank quests. Don’t waste time grinding LR expecting Marrow.

⚠️ Never Sell Materials Early

That stack of 47 Rathalos Scales? You’ll need 12 just for this set, plus more for weapon upgrades. Keep everything until you’ve crafted everything you want.

⚠️ Blade/Gunner Are Different Sets

Blademaster and Gunner armor are separate crafts with different material requirements. Make sure you’re crafting the correct set for your weapon type.

⚠️ Fire Resist is Situational

Great against Rathalos, Teostra, and Lavasioth. Useless against ice or water monsters. This is a specialized set — don’t expect it to carry you everywhere.

Elder Dragon Skill Practice
