Overview

The Xeno'Jiiqa Lance is the ultimate Lance crafted from the final boss of Monster Hunter Wilds. This legendary weapon combines Dragon Element, White Sharpness, and an unprecedented two Level 3 decoration slots — a combination that makes it the most customizable and potentially highest-damage Lance in the entire game.

Optimal Route

Follow this step-by-step route to acquire the Xeno'Jiiqa Lance. This is a post-game weapon, so the route assumes you've completed the main story.

1

Unlock the Final Boss Battle

⏱ Prerequisite: Complete Main Story

Xeno'Jiva is the final boss of Monster Hunter Wilds. You must complete the entire main story campaign to unlock this hunt. After the credits roll, Xeno'Jiva will be available as a repeatable assignment and eventually as an Investigation.

2

Learn Xeno'Jiva's Three-Phase Mechanics

⏱ 3-5 learning runs (20-30 min each)

Xeno'Jiva is a multi-phase, 20-30 minute battle that fundamentally changes attack patterns between phases. Don't rush — take time to learn each phase's tells and safe windows.

🟢 Phase 1: Ground Phase

Xeno'Jiva is grounded and relatively slow. Learn to counter-claw its lunges and break the front legs early. Focus on the head when it's down. This phase is about building damage and breaking parts.

🟡 Phase 2: Aerial Phase

Xeno'Jiva takes flight and gains new aerial beam attacks. The supercritical beam (ground sweep) has a massive wind-up — perfect for clutch claw mounting. Prioritize breaking wings to force it back down.

🔴 Phase 3: Critical State

Xeno'Jiva enters supercritical state — chest glows intensely, attack speed increases dramatically, and new explosive AoE patterns emerge. The chest takes massive damage when glowing — this is your DPS window. Play safe and exploit the chest glow.

3

Break All Breakable Parts

⏱ Ongoing across all hunts

Maximize your material yield by breaking every possible part each hunt: Head (Horn), both Front Legs (Claws), Wings, and Tail. Each break adds reward rolls and increases your chances for the Gem.

Break Priority (Solo):
  1. Both Front Legs — Claw material + trip opportunities
  2. Head — Horn material (50% break rate)
  3. Tail — Extra carve opportunity
  4. Wings — Force out of aerial phase
4

Gem Grind (3%)

⏱ 8-20 hours (highly RNG dependent)

The Xeno'Jiva Gem (3%) is the ultimate bottleneck. At 20-30 minutes per hunt, even with optimal clears, this will take time. Squad up for faster clears and more fun. Sub-15 minute runs with a good team make this grind manageable.

Novacrystal Mining (If Needed)

⏱ 15-30 minutes

If you don't already have Novacrystals from general play, run a quick Wildspire Waste mining route. Equip Geologist skill and hit blue mining outcrops in Areas 6, 8, and 11. With Geologist Lv.3, you'll have your 3 Novacrystals in one expedition.

6

Forging at the Smithy

⏱ 2 minutes

With all 7 materials collected, visit the Smithy. Forge the base Xeno'Jiva Lance first, then upgrade it to the Xeno'Jiiqa Lance. Slot in your best decorations and prepare to dominate every hunt in the New World.

Gotcha Tips

The Xeno'Jiiqa Lance journey is riddled with traps that have frustrated countless hunters. Know these gotchas before you commit.

⏱️

20-30 Minute Marathon Battles

Xeno'Jiva is not a quick hunt. Even experienced teams need 15-20 minutes, and solo hunters often face 25-30 minute slogs. Unlike Nergigante (8-10 min), you cannot speed-run this efficiently. The Gem grind isn't just about drop rate — it's about the massive time investment per attempt.

Reality check: At 25 min/hunt × 23 hunts (50% gem chance) = ~9.5 hours of pure Xeno'Jiva fighting. Plan your sessions accordingly.
💥

Phase 3 Critical State — Attack Pattern Overhaul

When Xeno'Jiva enters supercritical state (Phase 3), its entire moveset changes. Beams become faster, explosion AoEs expand, and the attack cadence increases dramatically. Many cart (faint) here because they play Phase 3 like Phase 1. Adjust your rhythm — play safer, exploit the glowing chest, and don't get greedy.

Phase 3 changes:
  • Beam wind-up time reduced by ~40%
  • New double-explosion ground AoE pattern
  • Chest becomes a massive weak point (x3 damage)
  • Enrage lasts longer and hits harder
  • Some safe zones from Phase 1 are no longer safe
💎

3% Gem + Long Hunt = Brutal Farm Efficiency

Other Elder Dragon gems have similar or lower rates (Nergigante: 2%, Teostra: 2%), but those hunts are 3× faster. Xeno'Jiva's gem is uniquely painful because you can't brute-force it quickly. Investigations help but are limited. This is a patience test above all else.

🔒

Story Completion Required

You cannot fight Xeno'Jiva or craft this weapon until you complete the entire main story. For hunters who rush through story content, this means going back to complete anything you skipped. There's no shortcut — finish the campaign first.

💍

Two Decoration Slots = More Decoration Demand

Two Lv.3 slots are incredible if you have the decorations to fill them. If your decoration collection is sparse, those slots sit empty and the weapon underperforms. Before committing to this grind, check your decoration inventory — you need at least two solid Lv.3 decos to justify this weapon over simpler alternatives.

Recommended Lv.3 Decorations:
  • Weakness Exploit — +30% affinity on weak spots
  • Critical Boost — +critical hit damage
  • Handicraft — Extend white sharpness
  • Guard Up — Block unblockable attacks
  • Partbreaker — Faster part destruction

06

Free Benefits

Farming Xeno'Jiva for the Lance brings enormous side benefits that compound your overall account progress. This isn't just a weapon grind — it's a mastery course.

🏆

Complete Main Story Unlock

Just reaching Xeno'Jiva means you've completed the entire main campaign, unlocking all endgame content: Arch-Tempered hunts, Extreme Behemoth, sieges, event quests, and the full decoration/augmentation systems. The entire post-game world opens up.

Full Endgame Access
🦴

Massive Elder Dragonvein Material Stockpile

Xeno'Jiva drops enormous quantities of Elder Dragonvein Bones, Bloods, and Scales — materials needed for augmenting weapons and armor at the Elder Melder. By the time you get your Gem, you'll likely have enough to augment 3-4 pieces of gear.

~30+ Elder Dragonvein materials
🎓

Final Exam for All Learned Skills

Xeno'Jiva tests everything you've learned: positioning, dodge timing, part-breaking strategy, phase management, and resource conservation. Mastering this fight proves you're ready for any content in the game. Many hunters report that every other fight feels easier after learning Xeno'Jiva.

Combat Mastery
🌍

Unlock Investigations & New World Content

Clearing Xeno'Jiva multiple times unlocks Xeno'Jiva Investigations and contributes to your Research Level, opening more investigation types across all monsters. You'll also earn enough Research Points to fund months of Argosy and Botanical Box operations.

Progression Acceleration
👥

Squad Coordination & Team Building

Xeno'Jiva is one of the best squad bonding experiences in the game. The long fight, phase coordination, and shared triumph (or suffering) forge strong hunting teams. Many permanent squads are born in the Xeno'Jiva grind.

Social / Team Building
🎯

Complete Armor Set Bonus

Xeno'Jiva materials also craft the Xeno'Jiva α/β armor sets, featuring the powerful "Xeno'Jiva Divinity" set bonus that grants Razor Sharp/Spare Shot (halves sharpness consumption and ammo/coating use). This is one of the best universal armor sets in the game.

Bonus Armor Set

🎁 Bottom Line

By the time you forge the Xeno'Jiiqa Lance, you will have: completed the entire game, unlocked all endgame systems, stockpiled enough Elder Dragonvein materials to augment multiple gear pieces, mastered the hardest base-game fight, built a reliable hunting squad, and crafted a top-tier armor set as a bonus. The Lance is the crown jewel of a transformative journey.
