Permeation of Witching-Wards

★★★★★ Incantation — Legendary Unlock: Surface World Access

The Permeation of Witching-Wards is a legendary-tier Incantation in Hades 2 that permanently removes the magical wards blocking Melinoe's path to the Surface World. Without this ritual, every attempt to ascend beyond the Underworld results in immediate death from the witching-ward curse — a devastating HP drain that ticks away your health pool within seconds of crossing the threshold. This Incantation is not merely a convenience; it is a mandatory progression gate that transforms the entire Hades 2 experience from a confined Underworld crawl into a vast dual-realm adventure spanning both the depths below and the sunlit ruins above.

Once completed, this ritual fundamentally reshapes your runs. You gain the ability to freely travel between the Underworld and the Surface, unlocking entirely new Olympian gods (Zeus, Hera, Demeter in their Surface aspects), exclusive Surface-only materials like Golden Apples and Poppy, the city of Ephyra with its unique environmental hazards, and eventually the path to Mount Olympus itself. The FOMO here is real — every run spent without this Incantation is a run where you are locked out of roughly 40% of Hades 2's current content. If you have been struggling with repeated deaths upon surfacing, wondering why the game seems to punish exploration so brutally, this Incantation is the answer you have been seeking.

Total Cost: 1 Cinder + 1 Shadow + 3 Moly

Incantation Recipe

To perform the Permeation of Witching-Wards, approach the Cauldron of Incantations at The Crossroads (Melinoe's hub base) and select this ritual from the available options. The ritual requires the following exact materials, consumed upon casting:

Material Quantity Source Region
Cinder 1 Defeat Hecate (final boss of Erebus) Erebus
Shadow 1 Defeat Scylla and the Sirens (final boss of Oceanus) Oceanus
Moly 3 Gathered from glowing white herbs in Erebus Erebus

Note: All materials are consumed upon performing the ritual. Cinder and Shadow drop only one per boss defeat, making them precious resources in the early game. Moly, while more plentiful, requires dedicated gathering runs through Erebus and may take 2-3 full clears to accumulate the required 3 units if you are not prioritizing herb spawns.

Casting Steps

  1. Gather 3 Moly from Erebus

    Prioritize rooms with herb spawn indicators on the minimap.

  2. Defeat Hecate in Erebus

    Collect 1 Cinder from her defeat loot.

  3. Defeat Scylla and the Sirens in Oceanus

    Collect 1 Shadow from their defeat loot.

  4. Return to The Crossroads Cauldron

    Select Permeation of Witching-Wards and cast the ritual.

Materials & Gathering Spots

🌑 Cinder

x1 Required
Region: Erebus

Cinder is a rare boss-drop material obtained exclusively by defeating Hecate, the Headmistress of Erebus, at the end of the Erebus region. Hecate serves as the first major boss in Hades 2 and presents a significant challenge for new players due to her triple-phase fight pattern. Cinder cannot be gathered from the environment or purchased — it must be earned through combat. Only one Cinder drops per defeat, and the drop rate is 100% guaranteed. Early in the game, you will want to save your first Cinder for this Incantation rather than spending it on other recipes like the Consecration of Ashes, as the Surface World unlock provides far greater long-term value than incremental Arcana upgrades.

💨 Shadow

x1 Required
Region: Oceanus

Shadow is obtained by defeating Scylla and the Sirens, the three-singer boss ensemble at the terminus of the Oceanus region. This boss fight is mechanically complex, requiring you to manage multiple targets simultaneously while dodging devastating sonic attacks and arena-wide musical notes. Like Cinder, Shadow is a guaranteed 100% drop with one unit per defeat. The Shadow material is also used in other important Incantations like Shadow Extraction, so if you are torn between priorities, know that the Permeation of Witching-Wards should take precedence — no other Incantation opens up as much content as this one does.

🌿 Moly

x3 Required
Region: Erebus

Moly is a common gathering material found throughout the Erebus region. It appears as glowing white herbs that sprout from the dark ground in designated gathering rooms. On your minimap, rooms containing Moly will display a small herb icon, making them easy to identify. Moly does not require any special gathering tools — simply approach the herb and interact to collect it. Each herb node yields 1 unit of Moly. A typical Erebus run will spawn 1-2 Moly nodes, meaning you should expect to complete roughly 2-3 full Erebus clear attempts to gather the required 3 units. Moly is also used in several other early-game Incantations, so gathering extra while you are in the region is highly recommended.

Optimal Gathering Route

The most efficient approach to completing the Permeation of Witching-Wards follows a structured progression through the Underworld regions. Do not attempt to rush to the Surface before completing this ritual — the witching-ward curse will kill you every single time, wasting valuable resources and run progress.

Phase 1: Erebus Material Collection

Your first priority should be accumulating the 3 Moly needed for the ritual while simultaneously learning Hecate's attack patterns. Start runs in Erebus and use your minimap to identify rooms with herb spawn icons — these are your Moly targets. Clear each room fully to ensure you do not miss any herb nodes that may be tucked behind obstacles or in shadowed corners. After gathering Moly from the available rooms, proceed through the remaining Erebus chambers to reach Hecate. Do not reset your run after gathering herbs; instead, treat each run as both a material gathering attempt and a boss practice session. Hecate's fight has three distinct phases: her initial staff combos, her mirror clone phase where she splits into three identical copies, and her final enraged state with expanded AoE attacks. Learning to reliably dodge her crescent moon slash and shadow pool attacks is essential not just for the Cinder drop, but for all future Erebus clears.

Phase 2: Oceanus Challenge

Once you have defeated Hecate and secured your Cinder, the next objective is progressing through Oceanus to defeat Scylla and the Sirens. Oceanus represents a significant difficulty spike from Erebus — the environment shifts from dark caverns to sunken ruins filled with water hazards, ranged enemies, and environmental traps. The region introduces new enemy types including Oceanus Serpents (ranged poison spitters), Sunken Warriors (shield-bearing undead), and the dreaded Charybdis whirlpools that pull you into damage zones. Prioritize movement-oriented boons from Hermes or Athena's dash deflect for this region, as mobility is key to survival. When you reach the boss chamber, Scylla and the Sirens will test your ability to track multiple targets simultaneously. Focus on eliminating the supporting Sirens first to reduce overall arena damage, then concentrate on Scylla herself. The Shadow drop is guaranteed upon their defeat.

Phase 3: Ritual Completion

With Cinder, Shadow, and 3 Moly in your inventory, use the silver pool to return to The Crossroads. Approach the Cauldron of Incantations and select the Permeation of Witching-Wards from the list of available rituals. The casting animation plays, consuming your materials permanently. Once complete, a notification appears confirming that the witching-wards have been dispelled. You are now free to ascend to the Surface World without suffering the fatal HP drain curse. The immediate next step should be a Surface exploration run to familiarize yourself with the new enemies, materials, and the Ephyra city layout.

  • Start in Erebus — Priority: Moly Gathering

    Check minimap for herb icons. Collect all Moly nodes across 2-3 runs.

  • Defeat Hecate — Reward: 1 Cinder

    Practice her 3-phase pattern. Guaranteed Cinder drop on defeat.

  • Progress to Oceanus — Priority: Shadow Acquisition

    Clear Oceanus chambers. Defeat Scylla and the Sirens for 1 Shadow.

  • Return to The Crossroads Cauldron

    Cast Permeation of Witching-Wards. Surface World unlocked permanently.

  • First Surface Run — Explore Ephyra

    Test your new freedom. Gather Golden Apples, meet new gods.

Gotcha Tips

These are the most common mistakes and hidden requirements that trip up players attempting to unlock the Surface World. Read carefully to avoid wasting hours of progress.

⚠ Critical Warnings

  • DO NOT attempt the Surface before casting this ritual. The witching-ward curse drains your HP at an accelerating rate once you cross into the Surface threshold. No amount of healing, health upgrades, or defensive boons can outlast it. You will die. Every time. Guaranteed.
  • Cinder and Shadow are boss-exclusive drops. You cannot find these materials in gathering spots, treasure chests, or shop purchases. They must be earned by defeating Hecate and Scylla respectively. Plan your boss attempts accordingly.
  • Hecate's mirror phase can cause run-ending damage. During her second phase, Hecate creates two mirror clones of herself. The real Hecate deals full damage while the clones deal reduced damage but still apply status effects. Use AoE attacks to reveal the real one, or learn the subtle visual tell — the true Hecate's staff glows slightly brighter.
  • Oceanus requires different movement patterns than Erebus. Many players struggle in Oceanus because they try to use the same tactics that worked in Erebus. The underwater sections slow your movement, ranged enemies are more prevalent, and environmental hazards (whirlpools, collapsing pillars) demand constant repositioning. Adapt your boon priorities accordingly — movement speed and ranged damage become much more valuable here.
  • Moly only spawns in specific rooms. Not every Erebus room contains Moly herbs. Watch for the herb icon on your minimap when selecting your next chamber. If no herb icon is visible, choose rooms with other rewards (Centaur Hearts, boon offerings) to maximize run value while searching for Moly.
  • Save your first Cinder for this ritual, not Arcana upgrades. The Consecration of Ashes Incantation also requires Cinder and becomes available around the same time. While upgrading Arcana Cards is tempting, the Surface World unlock provides access to far more materials, boons, and permanent upgrades in the long run. Prioritize Permeation of Witching-Wards first.
  • You must reach the end of a region to fight its boss. Both Hecate (Erebus) and Scylla (Oceanus) are region-end bosses. You cannot challenge them from The Crossroads or through any shortcut. Full region clears are required each attempt.

Free Benefits Along the Route

While gathering materials for the Permeation of Witching-Wards, you will naturally accumulate numerous additional benefits that compound your account's overall progression. These are not incidental — they are substantial power increases that make future runs dramatically easier.

✓ Guaranteed Side Rewards

  • Multiple Erebus clears = practice + resources. Each Erebus clear attempt yields Darkness (for Arcana Card upgrades), healing items (Life Essence, Centaur Hearts), and potential boon offerings from gods. Even failed runs contribute permanent account progression through Darkness accumulation.
  • Hecate defeat unlocks new Crossroads features. Defeating Hecate for the first time triggers a story sequence that advances the main narrative and may unlock new NPC interactions at The Crossroads, including potential new shop inventory from the Wretched Broker.
  • Oceanus exploration reveals new boon combinations. Progressing through Oceanus exposes you to new gods and boon offerings that you may not have encountered in Erebus alone. Poseidon's ocean-themed boons, in particular, synergize extremely well with the water-heavy Oceanus environment.
  • Scylla defeat unlocks the remaining Underworld path to Tartarus. With both Erebus and Oceanus bosses defeated, you gain access to Tartarus — the third and most challenging Underworld region, home to Fate Fabric, Bronze, and the battle against Chronos himself.
  • Surface access unlocks entirely new material tiers. Golden Apples, Poppy, and other Surface-exclusive materials become available for gathering. These materials feed into high-tier Incantations like Insight Into Offerings and Shimmering Ambrosia.
  • New Olympian gods on the Surface. The Surface World features different god boon pools than the Underworld. You will encounter Surface-specific aspects of gods that offer unique boon combinations unavailable below, dramatically expanding your build potential.
  • Ephyra city access and new NPCs. The Surface city of Ephyra contains environmental storytelling, hidden lore entries, and potential future questlines that are entirely inaccessible without first completing this ritual.

Bottom line: Completing the Permeation of Witching-Wards as early as possible is the single most impactful decision you can make in Hades 2's progression system. The content and materials locked behind the Surface World dwarf everything available in the Underworld alone. Every run completed without this ritual is a run that misses out on approximately half of the game's current available content. Make this your top priority.
