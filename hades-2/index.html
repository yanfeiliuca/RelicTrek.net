Permeation of Witching-Wards

★★★★★ Story Essential

Removes surface wards. 1 Cinder + 1 Shadow + 3 Moly. Required to reach the Surface World and progress the story.

Unraveling a Fateful Bond

★★★★★ Story Essential

Removes HP-draining curse for surface survival. 2 Lotus + 2 Moss + 2 Nightshade + 2 Thalamus. Essential for Act 3.

Aspects of Night and Darkness

★★★★★ Endgame

Unlock weapon aspects. 5 Bronze + 1 Nightshade. Transforms your Nocturnal Arms with powerful new abilities.

Insight Into Offerings

★★★★☆ Endgame

Reveals all boons including legendary/duo. 2 Ambrosia + 2 Poppy + 1 Golden Apple. Gift nectar to all 10+ gods first.

Consecration of Ashes

Shimmering Ambrosia
