黑夜与黑暗之形态

★★★★★ 咒语 — 武器 效果：解锁武器形态

The Aspects of Night and Darkness Incantation is a weapon-transforming ritual that unlocks the hidden Aspect system for Melinoe's Nocturnal Arms. In Hades 2, each weapon possesses multiple "Aspects" — alternate versions that fundamentally change the weapon's moveset, special attacks, and passive bonuses. These are not minor stat tweaks; they are complete gameplay reimaginings that can turn a weapon you dislike into your new favorite build centerpiece. The Aspects of Night and Darkness ritual specifically unlocks the second Aspect tier for your weapons, granting access to powerful new abilities that dramatically expand your combat options and build-crafting potential.

This ritual is absolutely essential for players who want to experiment with different playstyles and discover the full combat depth of Hades 2. The base versions of Melinoe's weapons — the Witch's Staff, Sister Blades, Umbral Flames, Moonstone Axe, Argent Skull, and Black Coat — are solid but limited in their expression. The Aspects unlocked by this Incantation add entirely new mechanics: alternate special moves, changed attack animations, modified Omega abilities, and unique passive effects that synergize with specific god boons in ways the base weapons cannot. Without this ritual, you are playing with approximately 60% of your available combat toolkit. The FOMO is real and immediate — every run without weapon Aspects is a run where you are deliberately handicapping your damage output and build diversity.

总消耗： 5 青铜 + 1 颠茄

咒语配方

The Aspects of Night and Darkness recipe is deceptively simple, requiring only two distinct materials. However, obtaining these materials in the quantities needed demands access to mid-to-late game regions and a willingness to engage with Hades 2's gathering systems. Bronze in particular requires a substantial time investment due to the quantity needed (5 units) and its spawn mechanics.

Bronze is the bottleneck material here. Each Tartarus run typically yields only 1-2 Bronze units from mining outcrops, meaning you will need 3-5 full Tartarus clears to accumulate the required 5 units. Nightshade is easier — a single Oceanus run focused on herb gathering should provide the 1 unit needed, and you likely already have some in inventory from earlier gathering forays. Plan your farming schedule around Tartarus runs with Bronze as the primary goal and Nightshade as a quick pickup during Oceanus transit.

材料与采集地点

🟤 Bronze

x5 Required
区域： 塔尔塔罗斯

Bronze is a metallic ore gathered from Bronze outcrops scattered throughout the Tartarus region. These outcrops appear as dark bronze-colored mineral formations embedded in walls, floors, and occasionally ceiling structures. On your minimap, Bronze mining nodes display a pickaxe/ore icon that distinguishes them from other gathering spots. Each Bronze outcrop yields 1 unit when mined. The spawn rate for Bronze in Tartarus is moderate — expect 1-2 outcrops per full region clear, occasionally 3 if you get favorable room generation. Bronze nodes tend to appear in larger combat chambers and transition hallways rather than small enemy closets. Because Tartarus is the most difficult Underworld region with the highest enemy density and most punishing environmental hazards, each Bronze gathering run is a significant time investment. Bring strong defensive boons and prioritize survival over speed. Bronze is also used in the Consecration of Ashes Incantation and several weapon upgrade recipes, making it a material worth stockpiling beyond the 5 needed here.

🌑 Nightshade

x1 Required
区域： 俄刻阿诺斯

Nightshade is a dark-purple herb that grows in the shadowed recesses of Oceanus chambers. It is visually identified by its distinctive violet-black leaves and subtle bioluminescent pollen that drifts from the plant when approached. On the minimap, Nightshade rooms display a standard herb icon — the same icon used for Moly in Erebus, so use the region to distinguish which herb you are targeting. Each Nightshade plant yields 1 unit when gathered. Nightshade tends to spawn in rooms with heavier shadow coverage, often tucked behind destructible environmental objects like crumbling pillars or half-submerged statues. A single focused Oceanus gathering run should yield the 1 unit required, but having 2-3 extra on hand is recommended since Nightshade is also used in the Unraveling a Fateful Bond ritual (which requires 2 units). Unlike Bronze, Nightshade gathering is low-risk — Oceanus enemies are manageable and the herb rooms are easy to clear.

最优采集路线

The 5-unit Bronze requirement dominates your route planning for this Incantation. Unlike recipes where materials come from diverse regions, Aspects of Night and Darkness funnels you into Tartarus repeatedly. The following route optimizes for Bronze yield while opportunistically collecting Nightshade during transit.

Core Strategy: Tartarus Bronze Farming

Your primary goal is full Tartarus clears with Bronze outcrop collection along the way. Each run should prioritize rooms with ore icons on the minimap, but since Bronze spawns are somewhat random, the most reliable approach is simply clearing the entire region and checking every chamber thoroughly. Speed-clearing Erebus and Oceanus (without prioritizing gathering) keeps you healthy and well-booned for the Tartarus push.

  • Erebus Speed Clear — No Gathering

    Clear quickly. Collect boons but don't detour for herbs. Preserve health for Tartarus.

  • Oceanus Transit — Optional Nightshade

    If herb icon visible, grab Nightshade. Otherwise speed through to preserve resources.

  • Tartarus Full Clear — Bronze Priority

    Check every room for Bronze outcrops. Mine all ore nodes. Expect 1-2 per run.

  • Repeat 3-5 Times

    Accumulate 5 Bronze total. Each Tartarus clear also yields Darkness, gold, and practice.

  • Crossroads Cauldron — Cast Ritual

    With 5 Bronze + 1 Nightshade, unlock all weapon Aspects. Test new movesets immediately.

Single-Run Consolidation Strategy

If you are also working toward the Unraveling a Fateful Bond ritual (which requires 2 Moss and 2 Thalamus from Tartarus), consolidate your gathering by collecting Bronze, Moss, and Thalamus simultaneously during each Tartarus run. This triple-collection approach means each Tartarus clear contributes to both Incantations, reducing total required runs from 7+ to roughly 3 focused clears. The only additional material needed would be Lotus and Nightshade from Oceanus, which can be gathered in a single dedicated Oceanus run.

注意事项

The weapon Aspect system has important nuances that can trip up new players. Understanding these before casting the ritual ensures you get maximum value from your newly unlocked options.

⚠ Critical Warnings

  • Unlocking Aspects does not automatically upgrade them. Each Aspect has upgrade tiers that require additional resources (typically more Bronze and Darkness) to unlock their full potential. The initial unlock only gives you the base version of each Aspect. Budget extra resources for upgrades if you find an Aspect you love.
  • Not all Aspects suit all playstyles. Some Aspects fundamentally alter a weapon's identity. For example, certain Sister Blades Aspects shift the weapon from a fast melee tool into a ranged throwing weapon. Test each Aspect in the training area at The Crossroads before committing to a full run with it.
  • Aspect selection is done at the weapon pickup. When you choose a weapon at the beginning of a run (or when swapping via the purple weapon altar), you can cycle through unlocked Aspects before confirming your choice. You are NOT locked into the default Aspect — always check if an alternate version better suits your current boon build.
  • Bronze outcrops can be destroyed by enemy attacks. In Tartarus's hectic combat rooms, enemy AoE attacks and environmental explosions can occasionally destroy Bronze outcrops before you mine them. Clear enemies first, then gather materials to avoid losing nodes.
  • Weapon Aspects change which god boons are optimal. An Aspect that adds a ranged component to a melee weapon suddenly makes Artemis crit boons or Apollo blessing boons far more valuable. Reevaluate your boon priorities when trying a new Aspect for the first time.
  • The Nightshade requirement is small but easy to overlook. With all the focus on farming 5 Bronze, it is easy to forget the 1 Nightshade requirement from Oceanus. Before making your final Tartarus push, confirm you have Nightshade in inventory to avoid a frustrating extra run.
  • Aspect unlocks are account-wide and permanent. Once cast, all Aspects are available on every future run for all weapons. This is a one-time investment with infinite returns.

沿途附加收益

The repeated Tartarus clears required for Bronze gathering produce enormous secondary benefits. By the time you have mined your 5th Bronze unit, you will be a fundamentally stronger player with significantly more account progression than when you started.

✓ Guaranteed Side Rewards

  • Tartarus mastery through repetition. After 3-5 full Tartarus clears, you will have internalized every enemy attack pattern, trap timing sequence, and optimal pathing decision for the region. This knowledge directly translates to easier Chronos attempts and faster future clears.
  • Massive Darkness accumulation. Each Tartarus chamber yields substantial Darkness. Over 3-5 clears, you will accumulate enough Darkness to purchase multiple Arcana Card upgrades — further increasing your power for all future runs.
  • Gold economy boost. Tartarus enemies drop more gold than earlier regions, and the treasure rooms become more lucrative. Expect to have thousands of extra gold for Charon shop purchases and Well of Charon upgrades.
  • Boon diversity discovery. Extended Tartarus clears expose you to the full range of god boon offerings, including rarer Duo Boon opportunities that only appear when you have specific god combinations. You may discover powerful synergies you never knew existed.
  • Fate Fabric and additional Tartarus materials. While mining Bronze, you will also encounter Fate Fabric drops (from elite enemies) and incidental Moss/Thalamus spawns. These feed directly into the Consecration of Ashes and Unraveling a Fateful Bond rituals.
  • Narrative progression. Repeated Tartarus visits trigger unique dialogues with Chronos, Hecate, and other NPCs. Several story beats require Tartarus progression as a trigger condition.
  • Increased familiarity with weapon strengths and weaknesses. Using different weapons across multiple Tartarus clears naturally teaches you which weapons excel against which enemy types. This knowledge becomes invaluable when you unlock Aspects and need to choose the right tool for each situation.

总结： Unlocking weapon Aspects is one of the single most impactful power upgrades in Hades 2's progression system. The 5 Bronze requirement demands effort, but the payoff — permanent access to alternate weapon movesets for every run, forever — is unmatched in terms of gameplay variety and build potential. Combined with the natural skill development and resource accumulation from repeated Tartarus clears, this ritual should be prioritized immediately after you have unlocked the Surface World and removed the Fateful Bond curse. Your future runs will thank you.
