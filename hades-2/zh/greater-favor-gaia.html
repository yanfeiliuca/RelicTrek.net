盖亚的更大恩惠

★★★☆☆ 咒语 — 工具 效果：解锁采集工具升级

The Greater Favor of Gaia Incantation is one of the most practically valuable rituals in Hades 2, unlocking upgrades for your Gathering Tool that enable collection from resource nodes that were previously uninteractive. Before casting this ritual, you can only gather basic herbs and minerals from standard gathering spots. After casting it, your Gathering Tool gains the ability to harvest from advanced nodes — including golden-leaved trees (for Golden Apples), large mineral deposits (for additional ore yields), and special flower patches that contain premium materials. This effectively doubles your resource gathering efficiency and unlocks access to entirely new material types that are required for legendary-tier recipes.

This Incantation is a hard prerequisite for multiple advanced recipes. Without it, you cannot collect Golden Apples needed for the Shimmering Ambrosia recipe (the Ambrosia-brewing formula for romance progression), cannot mine large mineral deposits for bonus yields, and cannot access several gathering spots in the Surface World region. The FOMO here is immediate and severe — every run you complete without this upgrade is a run where you walked past harvestable resources that you simply could not interact with. Those lost resources add up quickly, especially when you realize how many Golden Apples, bonus Bronze, and premium herbs you missed during your pre-upgrade runs.

The ★★★☆☆ difficulty rating reflects the relatively accessible materials required. All three components are standard ore types that can be collected through regular region clearing without special prerequisites or timing restrictions. The challenge lies in the quantity of Bronze and Iron required (4 units each) and the need for Marble (2 units), which only appears in Tartarus. No Surface World access, no day/night restrictions, no special NPC interactions — just straightforward mining across the Underworld regions. This makes Greater Favor of Gaia an excellent early-to-mid game crafting goal that pays dividends for the rest of your playthrough.

总消耗： 2 大理石 + 4 青铜 + 4 铁

咒语配方

The Greater Favor of Gaia recipe requires three ore materials totaling 10 units. While the quantities are moderate, these ores only spawn in specific regions and require planned gathering routes to collect efficiently. All three materials are mined from mineral outcrops using your Gathering Tool — no combat drops, no reward screen selections, no shop purchases. Pure mining. This makes the recipe predictable but time-gated by ore spawn rates.

The ritual is cast at the Incantation Cauldron in The Crossroads. Upon casting, your Gathering Tool automatically upgrades and the new capabilities are immediately active in all regions. There is no need to equip or activate anything — the upgrade is passive and permanent. You will see a brief visual effect when approaching upgraded resource nodes for the first time, indicating they are now harvestable. The tool upgrade also adds a subtle visual indicator to your minimap for advanced nodes, making them easier to spot in subsequent runs.

施法步骤

  1. 从厄瑞玻斯/俄刻阿诺斯开采4个铁

    在墙壁和地面上寻找黑色金属矿床。每次区域清理生成1-2个铁。预计需要2-3次厄瑞玻斯/俄刻阿诺斯清理.

  2. 从塔尔塔罗斯开采4个青铜

    青铜矿脉呈现为青铜色矿物构造。每次塔尔塔罗斯清理生成1-2个。预计需要2-4次塔尔塔罗斯清理.

  3. 从塔尔塔罗斯开采2个大理石

    白色大理石矿脉出现在塔尔塔罗斯房间中。每次清理生成1-2个，通常在首领房间附近。可与青铜一同收集.

  4. Cast at The Crossroads Cauldron

    消耗全部10个矿石单位。采集工具永久升级。立即开始开采高级节点.

材料与采集地点

⚪ Marble

x2 Required
区域： 塔尔塔罗斯

Marble is a pristine white stone mined from marble outcrops in Tartarus. These outcrops appear as smooth, pale mineral formations that stand out sharply against the dark red and black environment of the region. On the minimap, Marble nodes display an ore icon with a distinctive white highlight. Each outcrop yields 1 unit when mined. Marble spawns 1-2 times per Tartarus clear, typically in larger chambers and frequently near boss room entrances. The spawn locations are somewhat random but tend to favor rooms with architectural elements — pillars, archways, and carved walls. Marble is also used in several other mid-to-late game Incantations and furniture upgrades at The Crossroads, making it worth stockpiling beyond the 2 units needed here. Because Tartarus is the game's most challenging region, bringing strong defensive boons and prioritizing survival over speed is essential while Marble farming.

🟤 Bronze

x4 Required
区域： 塔尔塔罗斯

Bronze is a metallic ore gathered from Bronze outcrops scattered throughout Tartarus. These outcrops appear as dark bronze-colored mineral formations embedded in walls, floors, and occasionally ceiling structures. On your minimap, Bronze mining nodes display a pickaxe/ore icon. Each Bronze outcrop yields 1 unit when mined. The spawn rate for Bronze in Tartarus is moderate — expect 1-2 outcrops per full region clear, occasionally 3 with favorable room generation. Bronze nodes tend to appear in larger combat chambers and transition hallways rather than small enemy closets. Because Tartarus is the most difficult Underworld region with the highest enemy density and most punishing environmental hazards, each Bronze gathering run is a significant time investment. Bronze is also used in the Aspects of Night and Darkness Incantation (requires 5 units) and several weapon upgrade recipes, making it a material worth stockpiling aggressively. Prioritize Bronze mining on every Tartarus run, not just when you need it.

⬛ Iron

x4 Required
区域： 厄瑞玻斯 / 俄刻阿诺斯

Iron is a dark metallic ore mined from iron deposits found in both Erebus and Oceanus. These deposits appear as rough, dark-gray mineral nodes embedded in walls and floor sections. On the minimap, Iron nodes display a standard ore icon. Each deposit yields 1 unit when mined. Iron spawns 1-2 times per Erebus clear and 1-2 times per Oceanus clear, meaning you can collect all 4 required units without ever entering Tartarus. This makes Iron the most accessible material in the recipe. Iron deposits in Erebus tend to spawn in rooms with more industrial or architectural elements — broken machinery, collapsed tunnels, and chambers with metal fixtures. In Oceanus, Iron appears near submerged structures and decaying mechanical remnants. Iron is also used in various weapon upgrade recipes and Crossroads furniture crafting, so maintaining a reserve of 6-8 units is recommended. The low difficulty of Erebus and Oceanus makes Iron farming a relaxing, low-stress activity between challenging Tartarus pushes.

最优采集路线

The Greater Favor of Gaia materials are spread across three regions, but Tartarus contains two of them (Marble and Bronze). Route optimization revolves around minimizing the number of Tartarus clears needed while efficiently collecting Iron from the easier regions. The following route completes all material gathering in 2-3 focused runs.

Core Strategy: Tartarus Ore Double-Collection

Since both Marble and Bronze spawn in Tartarus, each clear of that region can simultaneously collect both materials. This is the core insight that drives the route planning. Iron is collected from Erebus and Oceanus during transit, requiring no additional detours. The following consolidated route gathers all materials across two full Underworld clears.

  • Erebus Full Clear — Mine All Iron Deposits

    Clear Erebus thoroughly, checking every room for dark Iron deposits on walls and floors. Expect 1-2 Iron per clear. Also collect any Moly or herbs visible for other recipes.

  • Oceanus Full Clear — Mine Remaining Iron

    Clear Oceanus, mining all Iron deposits. Expect 1-2 Iron per clear. You should now have 3-4 Iron total. Opportunistically collect Nightshade and Lotus if visible.

  • Tartarus Full Clear — Mine Bronze + Marble

    Clear Tartarus, mining ALL ore outcrops. Bronze (bronze-colored) and Marble (white) both spawn here. Expect 1-2 Bronze and 1-2 Marble per clear. Also collect incidental Fate Fabric from elites.

  • Second Tartarus Clear (If Needed)

    If first Tartarus clear yielded only 1 Bronze or 1 Marble, a second clear is needed. This is common due to spawn randomness. Expect to need 2 Tartarus clears total.

  • The Crossroads — Cast Greater Favor of Gaia

    With 2 Marble + 4 Bronze + 4 Iron, cast the ritual. Gathering Tool upgrades permanently. Test on golden-leaved trees in next Erebus run.

Synergy with Aspects of Night and Darkness

Greater Favor of Gaia has enormous material synergy with the Aspects of Night and Darkness Incantation, which requires 5 Bronze. If you are farming for both (and you should be — both are mid-game priorities), you can collect all ore materials simultaneously. A single Tartarus run contributes Bronze toward both recipes while also yielding Marble for Gaia. Plan your crafting goals together: gather 5 Bronze + 2 Marble + 4 Iron across 2-3 Tartarus/Erebus clears, then cast both rituals back-to-back at The Crossroads. This combined approach is significantly more efficient than treating each Incantation as a separate project.

注意事项

Greater Favor of Gaia seems straightforward, but several key details can waste hours of your time if overlooked. Understanding these nuances before starting your material farm will save significant frustration.

⚠ Critical Warnings

  • This Incantation is a hard prerequisite for Shimmering Ambrosia. Without the upgraded Gathering Tool, you cannot harvest Golden Apples from golden-leaved trees in Erebus. Golden Apples are required for the Shimmering Ambrosia recipe (1 Golden Apple + 1 Myrtle + 1 Iris + 1 Star Dust = 3 Ambrosia). If romance progression is a goal, prioritize Greater Favor of Gaia early.
  • Upgraded nodes are NOT retroactive. Resources you walked past in previous runs do not respawn just because you now have the upgraded tool. However, all future runs will have additional harvestable nodes that were previously invisible/inactive. The upgrade applies to new room generation, not existing rooms.
  • Ore outcrops can be destroyed by enemy attacks. In Tartarus especially, enemy AoE attacks and environmental explosions can destroy Bronze and Marble outcrops before you mine them. Clear enemies first, then gather materials. This is particularly important in rooms with explosive barrels or chain-reaction environmental hazards.
  • Iron is the easiest material but also the most boring to farm. Because Iron spawns in Erebus and Oceanus (the easiest regions), collecting all 4 units feels like a chore compared to the exciting challenge of Tartarus mining. Spread your Iron collection across multiple normal runs rather than dedicating time to it specifically.
  • Marble and Bronze spawn independently. A room can contain Marble without Bronze, Bronze without Marble, both, or neither. Do not assume that finding one means the other is nearby. Check every room thoroughly regardless of what you have already found.
  • The tool upgrade is account-wide and permanent. Once cast, the upgraded Gathering Tool is available on all future runs forever. There is no durability, charge limit, or maintenance cost. This is a one-time investment with infinite returns across your entire playthrough.
  • Some advanced nodes require the upgraded tool PLUS a specific region. For example, Golden Apple trees only appear in Erebus — the upgrade does not make them spawn in other regions. Understanding which advanced nodes appear where helps you plan gathering routes post-upgrade.
  • Prioritize this BEFORE attempting Shimmering Ambrosia. The Golden Apple requirement in Shimmering Ambrosia cannot be fulfilled without the upgraded Gathering Tool from Greater Favor of Gaia. Attempting to brew Ambrosia before completing Gaia's ritual is impossible and will result in frustration when you discover the hard prerequisite after already collecting the other three materials.

沿途附加收益

The process of gathering ore materials for Greater Favor of Gaia generates substantial valuable byproducts and progression benefits. Every run dedicated to this Incantation simultaneously advances multiple goals, making it one of the most efficient crafting projects in the game.

✓ Guaranteed Side Rewards

  • Simultaneous progress toward Aspects of Night and Darkness. Both Incantations require Bronze from Tartarus. While mining for Gaia's 4 Bronze, you are also collecting toward the 5 Bronze needed for weapon Aspects. These two rituals should always be pursued together.
  • Tartarus combat mastery. The 2-3 Tartarus clears needed for Bronze and Marble provide excellent practice against the game's most challenging regular enemies. You will emerge a significantly better player.
  • Fate Fabric and other Tartarus materials. While mining in Tartarus, elite enemy encounters will yield Fate Fabric drops. You will also encounter incidental Moss and Thalamus spawns that contribute to the Unraveling a Fateful Bond ritual.
  • Darkness and gold accumulation. Full region clears yield substantial Darkness for Arcana upgrades and gold for Charon shop purchases. The 2-3 runs needed for Gaia's materials will fund multiple power upgrades.
  • Boon diversity and build experimentation. Multiple full clears expose you to the full range of god offerings, increasing your chances of finding powerful Duo Boons and discovering new build synergies.
  • Erebus and Oceanus low-stress clears. The Iron collection portion of this recipe takes you through the game's easiest regions, providing a relaxing contrast to challenging Tartarus pushes. These easy clears are perfect for testing new weapons or boon combinations.
  • Post-upgrade resource windfall. After casting Greater Favor of Gaia, your very next runs will yield significantly more resources per clear due to newly accessible advanced nodes. The upgrade effectively pays for itself within 2-3 post-upgrade runs.
  • Unlocks Golden Apple access for Shimmering Ambrosia. The upgraded Gathering Tool is the only way to harvest Golden Apples from golden-leaved trees in Erebus. These Golden Apples are a required component in the Shimmering Ambrosia recipe, which produces 3 Ambrosia per brew — the ultimate gift item for romance progression. Without Gaia's upgrade, you cannot craft Ambrosia in quantity.

总结： Greater Favor of Gaia is one of the best return-on-investment Incantations in Hades 2 — a one-time moderate material investment (2 Marble + 4 Bronze + 4 Iron) yields permanent doubled gathering efficiency and access to entirely new material types. The recipe materials are straightforward to collect from standard region clearing, and the farming process naturally progresses other important crafting goals like Aspects of Night and Darkness. Every player should prioritize this Incantation immediately after unlocking Tartarus access and before attempting any recipes that require Golden Apples or advanced mineral gathering. The upgraded Gathering Tool is not a luxury — it is a necessity for efficient late-game crafting.
