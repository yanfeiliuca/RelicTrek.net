Rite of Vapor-Cleansing

★★★★☆ Incantation — Utility Effect: Repair Hot Springs

The Rite of Vapor-Cleansing Incantation is one of the most unique rituals in Hades 2, repairing the broken Hot Springs at The Crossroads and transforming them into a functional healing and social hub. Before casting this ritual, the Hot Springs area in The Crossroads is a dilapidated, non-interactive ruin — atmospheric but mechanically useless. After casting Rite of Vapor-Cleansing, the springs are restored to their former glory, bubbling with therapeutic thermal waters that serve as the setting for some of the most memorable and sought-after content in the entire game: companion bath scenes that advance romantic relationships and unlock exclusive dialogue, artwork, and narrative moments.

The Hot Springs scenes are a central component of Hades 2's romance system. After repairing the springs, specific companions will appear after your runs and invite you to join them in the waters. Accepting these invitations triggers unique bath scene dialogues that cannot be accessed anywhere else in the game. These scenes advance relationship tracks with romanceable characters, provide deep character development and backstory exposition, and in some cases unlock powerful companion abilities or keepsake upgrades. The content is substantial — each companion has multiple bath scene variations, and the writing quality is among the best in the game. For players invested in Hades 2's narrative and character relationships, the Rite of Vapor-Cleansing is not optional content — it is essential.

The ★★★★☆ difficulty rating does not come from material rarity but from the Lotus requirement (2 units) — Lotus only spawns in specific aquatic environments in Oceanus — and the fact that many players simply do not know the Hot Springs can be repaired. The broken springs blend into The Crossroads environment as set dressing, and the repair Incantation is not prominently signaled in the crafting menu. This is a classic "hidden in plain sight" feature that many players discover hours or even days after they could have already been enjoying the content. The FOMO factor is significant: every run you complete without repaired springs is a potential missed bath scene invitation.

Total Cost: 2 Moly + 2 Lotus + 2 Nightshade

Incantation Recipe

The Rite of Vapor-Cleansing recipe requires three herb materials, 2 units each, totaling 6 units. All materials are pure gathering — no combat drops required. This makes the recipe accessible to any player regardless of combat skill, provided they can reach the regions where the herbs spawn. The even 2-2-2 split means no single material dominates the farming process, and all three herbs are used in other important recipes, making this an efficient use of gathering time.

The ritual is performed at the Incantation Cauldron in The Crossroads. Upon casting, the Hot Springs are immediately restored — no additional materials, no waiting time, no further action required. Walk over to the springs area and you will see the transformation: fresh water bubbling, steam rising, and the environment fully restored. The first time you return to The Crossroads after a run post-repair, you may receive an invitation from a companion to try out the newly restored springs, triggering your first bath scene.

Casting Steps

  1. Gather 2 Moly from Erebus

    Search for white flowering herb patches. Moly spawns 2-3 times per Erebus clear. Easy to collect in a single run.

  2. Gather 2 Lotus from Oceanus

    Look for pink lotus flowers floating in pools and water features. 1-2 spawn per Oceanus clear. May need 1-2 runs.

  3. Gather 2 Nightshade from Oceanus

    Search shadowed corners for dark-purple Nightshade herbs. 1-2 spawn per Oceanus clear. Often found alongside Lotus.

  4. Cast at The Crossroads Cauldron

    Consume all 6 herbs. Hot Springs immediately restored. Bath scenes unlocked with compatible companions.

Materials & Gathering Spots

🌼 Moly

x2 Required
Region: Erebus

Moly is a white flowering herb that grows abundantly throughout Erebus. It appears as a small plant with luminous white petals and a faint silver glow that makes it visible even in the dark environment. On the minimap, Moly patches display a standard herb-leaf icon. Each patch yields 1 unit when gathered. Moly is the most common herb in the game, typically spawning 2-3 times per full Erebus clear. It grows in small rooms, corridor dead-ends, and occasionally in larger combat chambers near treasure chests. Moly is also a component in the Permeation of Witching-Wards Incantation (requires 3 units) and several other Alchemy recipes, making it a perpetually useful material to stockpile. Gathering 2 Moly for the Rite of Vapor-Cleansing should take no more than a single Erebus clear and is by far the easiest portion of this recipe.

🪷 Lotus

x2 Required
Region: Oceanus

Lotus is a delicate pink flower that grows in and around the water features of Oceanus. It appears as floating blooms on pool surfaces or as rooted plants at the water's edge, with distinctive soft pink petals and a gentle fragrance effect when approached. On the minimap, Lotus patches display a flower icon distinct from standard herb icons. Each patch yields 1 unit when gathered. Lotus spawns 1-2 times per full Oceanus clear, always in rooms with significant water presence — large pools, cascading waterfalls, submerged chambers, and river crossings. Because Oceanus is an aquatic-themed region, water-feature rooms are common, but Lotus does not appear in every water room. Its spawn is somewhat selective, favoring the larger and more visually impressive water chambers. Lotus is also a critical component in the Unraveling a Fateful Bond Incantation (requires 2 units), making it one of the highest-priority materials for any player planning to reach the Surface World. Always gather Lotus when you see it.

🌑 Nightshade

x2 Required
Region: Oceanus

Nightshade is a dark-purple herb that grows in the shadowed recesses of Oceanus chambers. It is identified by its distinctive violet-black leaves and subtle bioluminescent pollen that drifts from the plant when approached. On the minimap, Nightshade rooms display a standard herb icon — the same icon used for Moly in Erebus, so use the region to distinguish which herb you are targeting. Each Nightshade plant yields 1 unit when gathered. Nightshade tends to spawn in rooms with heavier shadow coverage, often tucked behind destructible environmental objects like crumbling pillars or half-submerged statues. A single focused Oceanus gathering run should yield the 2 units required, but having 3-4 extra on hand is recommended since Nightshade is also used in the Unraveling a Fateful Bond ritual (requires 2 units) and several Alchemy recipes. Nightshade gathering is low-risk — Oceanus enemies are manageable and the herb rooms are easy to clear.

Optimal Gathering Route

The Rite of Vapor-Cleansing materials come from two regions — Erebus and Oceanus — making the route very straightforward. Lotus and Nightshade both spawn in Oceanus, so a single focused Oceanus run can collect both materials. Moly is gathered from a quick Erebus clear. The entire material set can be collected in 1-2 runs with minimal detouring.

Core Strategy: Single Oceanus Double-Gather

Since both Lotus and Nightshade spawn in Oceanus, a single full clear of the region can simultaneously collect both materials. This is the core optimization that drives the route. Moly from Erebus is collected during transit. The following route completes all material gathering in a single full Underworld run.

  • Erebus Clear — Gather 2 Moly

    Clear Erebus thoroughly, checking every room for white Moly herb patches. Collect 2 units (should find 2-3 per clear). Also gather any other herbs visible for stockpiling.

  • Oceanus Full Clear — Gather Lotus + Nightshade

    Clear all Oceanus rooms. Check water features for pink Lotus flowers (floating on pools) and shadowed corners for dark-purple Nightshade. Collect 2 of each. May find them in the same rooms.

  • Return to The Crossroads — Cast Ritual

    With 2 Moly + 2 Lotus + 2 Nightshade, cast Rite of Vapor-Cleansing at the Cauldron. Hot Springs immediately restored.

  • Complete a Run — Trigger First Bath Scene

    Complete any run (even a failed one). Upon returning to Crossroads, a compatible companion may invite you to the springs. Accept to trigger your first bath scene.

Synergy with Unraveling a Fateful Bond

The Rite of Vapor-Cleansing shares two materials with the Unraveling a Fateful Bond Incantation — both require 2 Lotus and 2 Nightshade. If you are gathering for one, you should absolutely be gathering for both simultaneously. A single Oceanus run can collect the Lotus and Nightshade needed for both rituals (4 Lotus + 4 Nightshade total). The only additional materials for Unraveling a Fateful Bond are 2 Moss and 2 Thalamus from Tartarus, which can be collected on a subsequent Tartarus clear. This combined approach means both rituals can be completed within 2-3 runs, unlocking both the Hot Springs bath scenes AND the Surface World curse removal — two of the most impactful mid-game milestones — in a single farming session.

Gotcha Tips

The Rite of Vapor-Cleansing has several important details and prerequisites to understand. Ignoring these can lead to missed bath scenes, delayed romance progression, and wasted farming effort that could have been avoided with proper planning.

⚠ Critical Warnings

  • Bath scenes require compatible relationship levels. Simply repairing the Hot Springs does not immediately trigger bath scenes with every companion. Each character has specific relationship prerequisites that must be met before they will invite you to the springs. Most require their friendship track to be advanced to at least mid-tier with Nectar gifts before bath invitations become available.
  • Not all companions have bath scenes. The Hot Springs interactions are reserved for romanceable characters with appropriate narrative context. Do not expect every NPC you have met to show up at the springs. The eligible roster expands as you progress through the story and deepen relationships.
  • Bath scenes trigger after runs, not immediately. After casting Rite of Vapor-Cleansing, you must complete at least one full run (successful or failed) before bath scene invitations begin appearing. The triggers check upon your return to The Crossroads after a run conclusion.
  • Lotus only spawns in water-feature rooms in Oceanus. Unlike Moly which appears in almost any Erebus room, Lotus is restricted to Oceanus chambers with significant water presence. If you speed-clear Oceanus without checking water rooms, you will miss Lotus spawns. Slow down in aquatic chambers and scan pool surfaces carefully.
  • The repaired springs also provide minor healing. Beyond the social/romance function, the restored Hot Springs provide a small amount of HP recovery when interacted with. This is not a major combat benefit but can be useful between runs when you are low on health and want to save healing items.
  • Bath scene dialogues are unique and unrepeatable. Each bath scene with each companion plays once and cannot be re-triggered. Pay close attention during these scenes — the writing contains important character development and story details that are not revealed anywhere else in the game. Missing or skipping through bath scene dialogue means permanently losing that narrative content.
  • Nightshade and Lotus can spawn in the same Oceanus room. When you find one, thoroughly search the entire room for the other. Their spawn patterns sometimes overlap, especially in large chambers with both water features and shadowed corners.

Free Benefits Along the Route

The process of gathering herbs for the Rite of Vapor-Cleansing naturally generates numerous valuable byproducts and progression benefits. Every run contributes meaningfully to your overall account advancement.

✓ Guaranteed Side Rewards

  • Simultaneous progress toward Unraveling a Fateful Bond. Both rituals require 2 Lotus and 2 Nightshade. Gathering for Vapor-Cleansing automatically completes half the material requirement for removing the Surface World curse.
  • Moly stockpiling for Permeation of Witching-Wards. While gathering Moly in Erebus, you will collect the 3 units needed for the Permeation of Witching-Wards Incantation (which unlocks Surface World access). These three crafting goals form a natural progression chain.
  • Oceanus combat practice. The Oceanus clear needed for Lotus and Nightshade provides excellent practice against aquatic-themed enemies with unique attack patterns. Mastering these encounters is essential for consistent Tartarus progression.
  • Boon collection from two regions. Clearing Erebus and Oceanus exposes you to 6+ god boon offerings per run, increasing your chances of finding powerful synergies and Duo Boon opportunities.
  • Darkness and gold accumulation. Full region clears yield substantial Darkness for Arcana Card upgrades and gold for Charon shop purchases. The runs needed for herb gathering fund your overall power growth.
  • Narrative progression. Clearing multiple regions triggers character dialogues and story beats. Several important narrative moments are gated behind total region clears rather than specific events.
  • Herb stockpiling for other recipes. While gathering the specific herbs for Vapor-Cleansing, you will inevitably collect extras of all three materials. These feed into Alchemy recipes, other Incantations, and Crossroads crafting projects.
  • Surface World preparation. The Lotus and Nightshade you gather for this ritual are also required for Unraveling a Fateful Bond (2 each), which removes the HP-draining surface curse. Completing both rituals in parallel sets you up for immediate Surface World exploration once Permeation of Witching-Wards is also cast.
  • Companion keepsake upgrades. Several companions grant upgraded keepsakes after reaching specific relationship milestones through bath scenes. These upgraded keepsakes provide significantly stronger passive bonuses than their base versions, directly improving your combat performance on all future runs. The difference between a base keepsake and its upgraded version can be substantial — sometimes doubling the passive bonus or adding entirely new effects that change how you approach combat encounters.

Bottom line: The Rite of Vapor-Cleansing is one of the most narratively rewarding Incantations in Hades 2 — a modest material investment (2 Moly + 2 Lotus + 2 Nightshade) unlocks exclusive character scenes and romance progression content that cannot be accessed any other way. The recipe materials are easy to collect from Erebus and Oceanus, and the gathering process naturally advances other critical crafting goals like Unraveling a Fateful Bond and Permeation of Witching-Wards. Combined with the substantial side benefits of full region clears, this ritual should be a top priority for any player who has reached Oceanus and cares about character relationships or narrative completion. The Hot Springs scenes are among the best-written content in the entire game — do not miss them.
