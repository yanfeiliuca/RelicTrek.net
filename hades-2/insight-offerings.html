Insight Into Offerings

★★★★☆ Incantation — Information Effect: Reveals All Boons

The Insight Into Offerings Incantation is an information-focused ritual that permanently reveals the full details of every boon you encounter during your runs. Without this ritual, higher-tier boons — particularly Legendary and Duo boons — appear as mysterious question marks or vague descriptions when offered by gods. You are essentially gambling your precious boon slots on unknown abilities, hoping they synergize with your current build. The Insight ritual eliminates this uncertainty entirely, showing exact boon names, descriptions, damage numbers, scaling percentages, and prerequisite conditions before you commit. For players who want to make informed, strategic decisions about their build rather than rolling the dice, this Incantation is transformative.

The practical impact of this ritual cannot be overstated. In Hades 2, build synergy determines your success far more than raw mechanical skill. A perfectly executed dodge means nothing if your boon combination lacks damage scaling or defensive coverage. Legendary and Duo boons represent the pinnacle of build power — they are the abilities that turn difficult encounters into manageable ones and make boss fights feel like victories rather than grinds. But these boons require specific prerequisite boons from particular gods, and without Insight Into Offerings, you cannot see what those prerequisites are or what the final boon actually does. This ritual transforms the entire boon selection experience from blind gambling into deliberate build engineering. The FOMO is significant: every Legendary or Duo boon you pass on because you cannot see what it does is potentially a game-winning combination lost forever.

Total Cost: 2 Ambrosia + 2 Poppy + 1 Golden Apple

Incantation Recipe

The Insight Into Offerings recipe requires three materials spanning both the Underworld and Surface World — meaning you must have already completed the Permeation of Witching-Wards ritual to even attempt gathering the necessary components. This prerequisite dependency makes Insight Into Offerings a mid-game goal rather than an early unlock, but the payoff justifies the wait. The materials are relatively modest in quantity (2 + 2 + 1) but the Ambrosia requirement in particular demands significant account progression to accumulate.

Material Quantity Source Region
Ambrosia 2 Rare drop from elite enemies; gift from gods Crossroads / All Regions
Poppy 2 Gathered from red flowers in the Surface World Surface World
Golden Apple 1 Gathered from golden apple trees in Ephyra Surface World

Ambrosia is the rarest material in this recipe. It drops sparingly from elite enemy encounters across all regions, occasionally appears as a reward from Pom of Power rooms, and can be gifted by gods at The Crossroads after reaching sufficient affinity levels. The 2-unit requirement may seem small, but for newer players who have not yet built up a reserve, accumulating 2 Ambrosia can take 5-10 full runs. Poppy and Golden Apple, while requiring Surface World access, are straightforward gathering materials that should be collected during normal Surface exploration.

Casting Steps

  1. Accumulate 2 Ambrosia

    From elite enemy drops, Pom of Power rewards, or god affinity gifts.

  2. Gather 2 Poppy and 1 Golden Apple from Surface World

    Requires Permeation of Witching-Wards. Explore Ephyra and beyond.

  3. Return to The Crossroads Cauldron

    Cast Insight Into Offerings. All future boons display full details permanently.

Materials & Gathering Spots

🏺 Ambrosia

x2 Required
Region: Crossroads / All Regions

Ambrosia is a rare premium material that serves as the primary currency for high-tier interactions in Hades 2. It appears as a glowing golden liquid in small crystalline vials. There are several acquisition methods, each with its own reliability and time investment. First, elite enemy encounters (marked by the skull icon on room entrances) have a chance to drop Ambrosia upon defeat — roughly 15-20% per elite pack. Second, Pom of Power rooms occasionally offer Ambrosia as an alternative reward choice to the standard pomegranate upgrade. Third, and most reliably, increasing your affinity level with gods at The Crossroads through gift-giving eventually triggers Ambrosia rewards as part of their relationship progression. Because Ambrosia is also needed for other important rituals (Shimmering Ambrosia, Greater Favor of Gaia), you should always pick it when offered and never spend it frivolously. Plan to accumulate Ambrosia passively over 5-10 runs rather than trying to farm it in a single session.

🌺 Poppy

x2 Required
Region: Surface World

Poppy is a Surface World exclusive gathering material found in the sunlit ruins of Ephyra and beyond. It appears as clusters of vibrant red-orange flowers growing from cracks in the ancient marble and limestone architecture. On your minimap, Poppy rooms display a flower icon similar to Lotus in Oceanus, but the Surface World context makes them easy to distinguish. Each Poppy cluster yields 1 unit when gathered. Poppy tends to spawn in outdoor courtyard areas and collapsed garden chambers where sunlight penetrates the ruined structures. Each Surface run typically contains 1-2 Poppy spawn locations, meaning you should gather the required 2 units within 1-2 dedicated Surface exploration runs. Poppy is also used in the Shimmering Ambrosia ritual, so collecting extra while on the Surface is highly efficient. No special gathering tools are required — simply approach and interact.

🍎 Golden Apple

x1 Required
Region: Surface World

The Golden Apple is a legendary-tier gathering material exclusive to the Surface World, specifically found in the Ephyra region. It appears as a single golden fruit hanging from a small, gnarled tree that sprouts improbably from the ruined architecture. On your minimap, Golden Apple rooms display a unique golden apple icon that is unmistakable once you have seen it. Only one Golden Apple can be gathered per tree, and each tree yields exactly 1 unit. Golden Apple trees are relatively rare — expect to find 1 tree every 1-2 Surface runs. The trees typically spawn in larger outdoor chambers with significant verticality, often near cliff edges or overlooking the destroyed cityscape. Because Golden Apples are used in multiple high-tier Incantations (Insight Into Offerings, Greater Favor of Gaia), each one you find is precious. Always prioritize Golden Apple rooms when they appear on your minimap, as missing a spawn may mean waiting several runs for the next opportunity.

Optimal Gathering Route

The Insight Into Offerings gathering route requires similar planning to the Unraveling a Fateful Bond ritual but adds a Surface World component. The key constraint is that you must have already completed the Permeation of Witching-Wards to even begin, which means this is inherently a mid-to-late early-game goal. By the time you unlock Surface access, you should already have some Ambrosia accumulated from prior runs, making the total gathering time shorter than it first appears.

Core Strategy: Passive Ambrosia + Focused Surface Gathering

Do not try to specifically farm Ambrosia — let it accumulate naturally during normal escape attempts. Instead, focus your dedicated gathering efforts on the Surface World materials (Poppy and Golden Apple) once you have unlocked the Permeation of Witching-Wards. A typical progression looks like this:

  • Passive Phase: Accumulate Ambrosia (5-10 runs)

    Continue normal Underworld/Surface runs. Take Ambrosia from elite drops and Pom rooms.

  • Surface Exploration Run — Poppy Focus

    Clear to Surface. Prioritize flower-icon rooms. Gather 2 Poppy across 1-2 runs.

  • Surface Exploration Run — Golden Apple

    Watch minimap for golden apple icon. Rare spawn — may take 1-2 runs to find one.

  • Verify Inventory — 2 Ambrosia, 2 Poppy, 1 Golden Apple

    If Ambrosia still needed, run more Underworld clears focusing on elite skull-icon rooms.

  • Crossroads Cauldron — Cast Ritual

    All future boon offerings show full details. Build planning becomes precise and strategic.

Elite Farming Route (If Ambrosia Is Needed)

If you find yourself short on Ambrosia, prioritize rooms with skull-marked entrances during your Underworld runs. These elite encounters have the highest non-guaranteed drop rate for Ambrosia. Focus on Erebus and Oceanus for faster clears — Tartarus elite encounters are more difficult and time-consuming for the same drop chance. Do not reset your run after an elite room; continue clearing for additional chances and Darkness accumulation. The Pom of Power rooms that offer Ambrosia as a reward choice should always be taken unless your current build is at a critical power threshold that demands an immediate upgrade.

Gotcha Tips

The Insight Into Offerings ritual has several nuances that are commonly misunderstood by new players. Understanding these ensures you get the maximum value from your investment and avoid common frustrations.

⚠ Critical Warnings

  • This ritual requires Surface World access as a prerequisite. You cannot gather Poppy or Golden Apple without first completing the Permeation of Witching-Wards. Do not attempt to start this ritual until you have removed the witching-ward curse and explored the Surface at least once.
  • Ambrosia is shared across multiple high-tier rituals. Shimmering Ambrosia, Greater Favor of Gaia, and Insight Into Offerings all require Ambrosia. Before spending your hard-earned Ambrosia, decide which ritual provides the most immediate value for your playstyle. For build-focused players, Insight is usually the correct first choice.
  • Golden Apple trees are rare and easy to miss. The golden apple minimap icon only appears when you are one room away from the spawn chamber. If you are speed-running the Surface without checking your map, you can easily run past a Golden Apple tree. Develop the habit of checking the minimap at every room transition.
  • This ritual is permanent and account-wide. Like all Incantations, Insight Into Offerings need only be cast once. The effect applies to every future run, on every weapon, in every region. There is no downside or maintenance cost.
  • Revealed boon information includes prerequisite chains. One of the most valuable features of this ritual is that it shows not just what a Legendary or Duo boon does, but exactly which prerequisite boons from which gods are required to make it appear. This enables deliberate "boon fishing" where you plan your entire run around assembling a specific powerful combination.
  • Poppy and Golden Apple gathering does not trigger combat. Like all herb and gathering interactions in Hades 2, collecting Surface World materials is silent and safe. Clear enemies first, then gather without worry.
  • God affinity Ambrosia gifts are the most reliable source. If you are struggling to find Ambrosia from enemy drops, focus on building relationships with gods at The Crossroads through the gift-giving system. Several gods award Ambrosia at their second or third affinity threshold, providing guaranteed acquisition.

Free Benefits Along the Route

The journey to complete Insight Into Offerings naturally produces a cascade of secondary benefits. Because this ritual requires Surface World exploration and Ambrosia accumulation, you will engage with multiple game systems simultaneously, each contributing to your overall account progression.

✓ Guaranteed Side Rewards

  • Surface World familiarity. The exploration runs needed to gather Poppy and Golden Apple force you to learn Ephyra's layout, enemy spawn patterns, and environmental hazards. This knowledge is essential for all future Surface content and makes the eventual Mount Olympus path significantly easier.
  • New god boon discovery. The Surface World features different god aspects and boon pools than the Underworld. Your gathering runs will expose you to new abilities and synergies that may become cornerstones of your favorite builds.
  • Elite encounter mastery. If you are farming Ambrosia from elite rooms, you will face dozens of elite enemy packs across multiple enemy types. This repetition builds combat instinct and teaches you which weapons and boons handle each elite variant most efficiently.
  • Relationship progression with gods. Pursuing Ambrosia through the affinity system means engaging in the gift-giving and dialogue systems, which unlock unique narrative content, side quests, and sometimes additional gameplay benefits beyond the Ambrosia itself.
  • Additional Surface materials. While gathering Poppy and Golden Apple, you will inevitably collect other Surface-exclusive materials (additional Poppy, random herb spawns) that feed into future Incantations like Shimmering Ambrosia.
  • Improved build-crafting skill. Even before casting the ritual, the discipline of learning boon prerequisites and planning synergies makes you a better player. The information economy of Hades 2 rewards knowledge — knowing what is possible is half the battle in assembling game-breaking builds.

Bottom line: For any player who takes build optimization seriously, Insight Into Offerings is non-negotiable. The ability to see exactly what every boon does — including the rare and powerful Legendary and Duo boons — transforms your runs from hopeful gambles into strategic masterplans. The material requirements (particularly Ambrosia) demand patience and progression, but the one-time investment pays dividends on every single future run. Complete the Permeation of Witching-Wards first, then make this ritual your next major goal.
