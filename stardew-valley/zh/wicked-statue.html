1 Why the 邪恶雕像 Protects Your 史莱姆 Investment

👹

邪恶雕像

★★★★☆ — Prevents Witch from Cursing 史莱姆屋
Secret Recipe — 科罗布斯 (20,000g)

The 邪恶雕像 is one of the most misunderstood and underrated items in 星露谷物语. This eerie decorative statue serves a single, critical purpose: when placed inside your 史莱姆屋, it prevents the Witch from cursing your slimes during her rare nighttime flyover events. Without a 邪恶雕像, the Witch can turn all your slimes black (cosmetic only, but unsettling) or worse — curse your hutch in ways that disrupt breeding and 史莱姆 Ball production. For players who have invested 10,000g+ in their 史莱姆屋 infrastructure, the 邪恶雕像 is cheap insurance.

The crafting recipe is a secret purchase from 科罗布斯 in the Sewers for 20,000g — making it one of the most expensive recipe purchases in the game. The materials themselves are trivial (5 煤炭 + 25 石头), but unlocking the recipe requires reaching the Sewers, befriending the shadow merchant, and saving up a significant chunk of gold. This guide covers how to access the Sewers, earn the 20,000g, understand the Witch's curse mechanics, and why this creepy statue is actually a smart investment for any slime farmer.

★ What This Means for You

The Witch has a 1% chance each night to fly over your farm and curse your 史莱姆屋 — turning all slimes black and halting breeding for several days. If you have 20 slimes producing 史莱姆 Balls worth 200-400 史莱姆 daily, a witch curse costs you 1,000-2,000g in lost production. The 邪恶雕像 costs 20,000g but pays for itself after just 10-20 witch events (which, statistically, will happen within 1-2 years). Plus, the statue looks delightfully creepy in your hutch.

🔓 Accessing the Sewers

地点： The Sewers are accessed via a manhole cover in the south of Pelican Town (near the river) or a sewer grate in 煤林森林.
Unlock Requirement: Donate 60 items to the Museum (minerals, artifacts, or a mix). After the 60th donation, 冈瑟 rewards you with the Rusty Key the next morning, which unlocks the Sewers.
Museum Donation Strategy:
• Minerals: Crack Geodes at the 铁匠铺 (25g each). Regular Geodes (层 1-39), Frozen Geodes (40-79), and Magma Geodes (80-120) each contain unique minerals. Omni Geodes contain the rarest ones.
• Artifacts: Found by tilling artifact spots (worm holes) with a Hoe, fishing treasure chests, and killing monsters. Check the Mountain, Town, and Beach for artifact spots after rain.
• Fastest 60 donations: Spend 2-3 days cracking 30-40 Geodes (buy from the Desert Trader or farm in Mines), plus artifact spots in 春季 after rain. Most players reach 60 donations by 夏季 第一年.
科罗布斯 Location: Inside the Sewers, 科罗布斯 stands near a small shop area. He's always present — the Sewers never close. He is a non-hostile Shadow Person and a potential roommate (can move into your farmhouse if you befriend him and have a free cabin).
技巧： Prioritize museum donations early. Every Geode you crack contributes, and many minerals are needed for crafting recipes, 社区中心 bundles, and 宝石复制机 duplication (see our 宝石复制机 guide). The Rusty Key also unlocks the Mutant Bug Lair in the Sewers, which contains unique fish.

💰 20,000 Gold

来源： Any income method in 星露谷物语
Fastest Methods to Earn 20,000g:

1. 古代水果 酒 in 小桶s (Best Late-Game)
Plant 古代水果 (from Ancient 种子 artifact, donated to Museum), process in 小桶s (see our 小桶 guide), and sell the wine for 2,310g each (base) or 4,620g (Iridium quality with Cask aging). 5 小桶s of Iridium 古代水果 wine = 23,100g — more than enough. Takes 14 days to brew + 56 days for Cask aging.

2. 杨桃 酒 (Best Mid-Game)
Buy 杨桃 seeds from the Oasis (400g each, 夏季 only), grow in 夏季, process in 小桶s. Base 杨桃 wine sells for 2,250g. With the 工匠 profession (耕种等级 10), it sells for 3,150g. 7 小桶s = 22,050g. Available every 夏季.

3. Pig 松露油 (Consistent)
Pigs find 松露s worth 1,491g each as 松露油 (with 工匠). 14 松露s = 20,874g. Available every 秋季 day (except rain). See our 雨水图腾 guide for pig farming details.

4. Fishing (Early Game)
High-value fish like Legend (5,000g), Crimsonfish (1,500g), Glacierfish (1,000g), and Angler (900g) plus daily ocean/river fishing. A full inventory of high-quality fish can yield 3,000-5,000g per day.

5. Gem Duplication (Passive)
Fill 宝石复制机s with 钻石s (750g every 5 days). 20 宝石复制机s = 15,000g every 5 days. See our 宝石复制机 guide for setup details.

6. 作物 Mass Farming (Reliable)
Plant high-value crops: Cranberries in 秋季 (each plant yields ~1,000g over the season), Blueberries in 夏季 (~800g per plant), or 古代水果 year-round in the 温室.
技巧： Don't rush the 20,000g if you're still early-game. The 邪恶雕像 is a quality-of-life convenience, not a progression gate. Buy it when you have spare gold after tool upgrades, barn construction, and seed purchases. By 秋季 第一年, most established farms have 20,000g in savings.

⚫ 煤炭 (5 units)

来源： Mines, Dust Sprites, 木炭窑, various enemies
季节： Any — available year-round
地点： All mine floors (dropped by rocks and monsters)
Best Methods:
• Dust Sprites (层 40-79) — Drop 1-2 煤炭 per kill. Kill 500 for the 盗贼戒指 (doubles drops).
• 木炭窑 (采集等级 2) — Convert 10 木头 into 1 煤炭. If you have excess 木头, this is reliable.
• Regular rock breaking — Every rock has a small chance to drop 煤炭.
• 铁匠铺 purchase — 克林特 sells 煤炭 for 150g each (750g for 5).
总计需要： 5 煤炭 — a trivial amount you'll likely have in inventory already.
技巧： 煤炭 is needed in enormous quantities for smelting (1 煤炭 per bar) and many crafting recipes. The 勘探者 profession at 采矿等级 10 doubles all 煤炭 drops. See our 史莱姆孵化器 guide for Dust Sprite farming strategies.

🪨 石头 (25 units)

来源： Break rocks literally anywhere
季节： Any — available year-round
地点： Your farm, Mines, Quarry, everywhere
最佳方法： Break 10-15 rocks on your farm or the first floor of the Mines. 25 石头 takes approximately 30 seconds to collect.
替代方案： Buy from 罗宾 at 20g each (500g total).
总计需要： 25 石头 — negligible amount
技巧： 石头 is the most abundant material in the game. You probably have 500+ 石头 in a chest already. 25 石头 is a rounding error. See our 楼梯 guide for bulk 石头 farming if you need more.

4 最优收集路线

Follow this order to unlock and craft the 邪恶雕像 efficiently.

① 春季 第一年 — Museum Donations for Rusty Key

From 日 1, save every Geode you find (regular, frozen, magma, and omni). Do NOT sell them. Also dig up every artifact spot (worm holes) with your Hoe — these appear in the Town, Mountain, Beach, and Railroad areas, especially after rain. Crack Geodes at the 铁匠铺 (25g each) and donate all unique minerals and artifacts to the Museum. Target 60 donations by mid-夏季. The Rusty Key is awarded the morning after your 60th donation.

② 夏季-秋季 第一年 — Build 史莱姆屋 + Income

Build your 史莱姆屋 from 罗宾 (10,000g + 500 石头 + 10 精炼石英 + 1 铱锭). This is the building the 邪恶雕像 protects — no point buying the statue without a hutch to protect! Simultaneously, build your farm income through crops, fishing, or artisan goods. Save 20,000g as a dedicated fund for the statue purchase.

③ Unlock Sewers + Buy Recipe from 科罗布斯

With 60 museum donations complete, you'll receive the Rusty Key. Enter the Sewers via the Pelican Town manhole cover (south of the river) or the 煤林森林 grate. Find 科罗布斯 inside and purchase the 邪恶雕像 recipe for 20,000g. This is a one-time purchase — once learned, you can craft unlimited statues. The recipe appears in your crafting menu permanently.

④ Gather Crafting Materials (Trivial)

Collect 5 煤炭 (you likely have 50+ in a chest) and 25 石头 (break 10 rocks on your farm). These materials are so common that this step takes under a minute. If somehow out of 煤炭, kill 3 Dust Sprites on Mines 层 40-79 or burn 50 木头 in a 木炭窑.

⑤ Craft + Place in 史莱姆屋

Open crafting menu → WICKED STATUE → Place inside your 史莱姆屋. The statue's effect is immediate and permanent — no activation required. The Witch will never curse this hutch again. You can craft additional statues for other farm buildings (decorative) or for friends in multiplayer.

5 ⚠ 避坑指南 — 别浪费时间

These mistakes will waste hours. Read this before you start.

⚠ The witch curse is rare but devastating

With only a 1% chance per night, the Witch might not visit for an entire in-game year — or she might visit twice in one month. The curse is random, which makes it easy to ignore. But when it hits, all your slimes turn black and breeding halts for 1-3 days. On a 20-slime hutch producing 200-400 史莱姆 daily, that's 600-1,200 史莱姆 lost per curse event. Over multiple years, the 邪恶雕像 pays for itself many times over.

⚠ 20,000g is a lot early-game

Twenty thousand gold represents: 40 小桶s worth of 杨桃 wine, 33 松露油s, 2 Deluxe Barn upgrades, or 4 铱制洒水器s. If you're still saving for tool upgrades or barn construction, prioritize those first. The 邪恶雕像 is a luxury purchase, not a necessity. Buy it when you have 50,000g+ in savings and nothing urgent to spend it on.

⚠ Museum donations take longer than you think

Reaching 60 museum donations requires significant effort. You'll need to crack 30-40 Geodes (found in Mines), dig up 15-20 artifacts (from worm spots), and possibly trade for Omni Geodes at the Desert Trader. Start donating from 日 1 — don't wait until you "need" the Rusty Key. Many of the minerals are also needed for 社区中心 bundles and crafting recipes.

⚠ The 邪恶雕像 only works INSIDE the 史莱姆屋

Placing a 邪恶雕像 outside the 史莱姆屋 (on your farm, in a shed, near the coop) does nothing. It must be placed on a tile inside the 史莱姆屋 building to block the witch's curse. The statue has no effect on the Witch's coop visit (which gives Void Eggs — usually desirable) or any other mechanic.

⚠ Multiple 邪恶雕像s are redundant

One 邪恶雕像 per 史莱姆屋 is all you need. Crafting multiple statues for the same hutch has no additional effect. However, if you build multiple 史莱姆屋es (via multiplayer or modding), each hutch needs its own statue. Extra statues can be placed as creepy decorations around your farm.

⚠ 科罗布斯 has other valuable items

While saving for the 邪恶雕像 recipe, check 科罗布斯's other offerings: the 星之果实 (20,000g, +permanent max energy) is arguably more valuable than the statue. Void Eggs (5,000g) let you hatch a Void Chicken. 铱锭s (3,000g, Fridays) save you a 骷髅洞穴 trip. Budget your Sewers shopping carefully — you can't buy everything at once.

⚠ The Witch's coop visit is usually GOOD

When the Witch visits your Coop, she turns one random egg into a Void Egg. Void Eggs hatch into Void Chickens that produce Void Eggs daily — a profitable and unique animal. Many players want the Witch to visit their coop. The 邪恶雕像 does NOT affect coop visits — it only protects the 史莱姆屋. Don't confuse the two events.

6 🎁 额外收益 — 顺路能拿到的好东西

While working toward the 邪恶雕像, you'll accumulate wealth, XP, and resources that benefit your entire farm. Don't leave these on the table.

🎁 Museum donations = 冈瑟 rewards

Every museum donation contributes to rewards from 冈瑟: 9 donations = Dwarvish Translation Guide (lets you understand the Dwarf in the Mines), 15 donations = unique furniture, 30 donations = rare seeds, 60 donations = Rusty Key (Sewers access). Many donated minerals are also needed for 社区中心 bundles and can be duplicated in 宝石复制机s for profit (see our 宝石复制机 guide).

🎁 Sewers access = unique fish and items

The Sewers contain the Mutant Bug Lair — a unique fishing spot with the Mutant Carp (one of the five legendary fish) and Radioactive Carp. The Sewers also have a Dark Shrine of Memory (resets ex-spouse memory for 30,000g), a Dark Shrine of Night Terrors (toggles monster spawns on farm), and 科罗布斯 as a shopkeeper with unique goods available every day.

🎁 史莱姆屋 = passive 史莱姆 income

Building the 史莱姆屋 (prerequisite for needing the 邪恶雕像) unlocks a passive 史莱姆 farm. With 20 slimes and incubators, you produce 200-400 史莱姆 daily — enough for endless 产油机s, 怪物香水, and 史莱姆 Egg Press operations. See our 史莱姆孵化器 guide for maximizing slime production. The hutch pays for itself through 史莱姆 sales alone.

🎁 Earning 20,000g builds your farm economy

The process of saving 20,000g forces you to develop profitable farm systems — whether that's 小桶 brewing, pig truffle farming, or crop optimization. By the time you can afford the 邪恶雕像, your farm will be generating consistent daily income. The statue purchase is proof that your economy has matured beyond subsistence farming.

🎁 科罗布斯 friendship + potential roommate

Visiting 科罗布斯 regularly to shop builds friendship. At high friendship, 科罗布斯 can become your roommate (alternative to marriage — he moves into a spare cabin on your farm). As a roommate, 科罗布斯 gives unique gifts, has special dialogue, and provides a different endgame experience. He doesn't do farm chores like a spouse, but he's a fascinating character with deep lore.

🎁 The 星之果实 from 科罗布斯

科罗布斯 sells a 星之果实 for 20,000g — the same price as the 邪恶雕像 recipe. A 星之果实 permanently increases your maximum energy by 34 points. There are only 7 星之果实s in the entire game, and this is one of the easiest to obtain. Many players buy the 星之果实 before the 邪恶雕像 recipe because +energy benefits everything you do. Budget for both — they're the two best 20,000g purchases in the Sewers.
