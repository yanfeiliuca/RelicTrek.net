1 Why the 雨水图腾 is a Farm-Changing Tool

🌧️

雨水图腾

★★★★☆ — 强制明天下雨
采集等级 9

The 雨水图腾 is one of the most powerful consumable tools in 星露谷物语. When used, it forces the next day's weather to be rain — regardless of season, current forecast, or RNG. This single-use item has massive implications for farm planning, fishing, foraging, and even social strategy. A 雨水图腾 on a 夏季 day means you can catch rare ocean fish like the Legend without waiting weeks for a lucky storm.

The crafting recipe unlocks at 采集等级 9 — one of the highest foraging skill gates in the game. The materials required are deceptively expensive: 1 硬木 (rare wood from secret sources), 1 松露油 (requires pigs and an 产油机 — a massive investment), and 1 松焦油 (from tapped pine trees, easy but slow). This guide covers every material source, the fastest path to 采集等级 9, and how to use 雨水图腾s strategically for maximum benefit.

★ What This Means for You

With a stockpile of 雨水图腾s, you control the weather. Want to catch the Legend (the rarest fish, only available in 春季 rain near the mountain lake)? Use a 雨水图腾. Need your 避雷针s to generate 电池组s? 雨水图腾 in 夏季. Want to skip watering crops? 雨水图腾 saves you an hour every morning. The strategic value is enormous — this is weather manipulation on demand.

3 材料与来源

Each material listed with exact source, season, location, and farming tips.

🪵 硬木

来源： Large Stumps, Large Logs, 秘密森林 stumps
季节： Any — available year-round
地点： 秘密森林 (northwest of 煤林森林, blocked by a Large Log — requires Steel Axe or better to chop). The 秘密森林 contains 6 Large Stumps that respawn every single day, each giving 2 硬木. This is the most reliable source in the game, yielding 12 硬木 per day.
其他来源：
• Farm Large Stumps — Forest Farm and Riverland Farm spawn Large Stumps that can be chopped for 2 硬木 each (Steel Axe required). Four Corners Farm has a small hardwood quadrant.
• Large Logs — Farm randomly spawns Large Logs that require Steel+ Axe. Each gives 8 硬木 but does not respawn.
• Mines — Breaking wooden barrels and crates in the mines sometimes drops 1 硬木.
• 木头 Skip — A fish caught in the 秘密森林 pond (20% chance) that gives 2 硬木 when placed in a 回收机.
• Tree Stumps — All farm maps have random stumps that give 2 硬木 each when chopped with a Copper+ Axe.
总计需要： 1 硬木 directly + 20 硬木 for 产油机 = 21 硬木 total for first 雨水图腾
技巧： Make visiting the 秘密森林 a daily habit. Enter through the northwest 煤林森林 entrance (Steel Axe required to break the blocking log), chop all 6 stumps, and leave. With 12 硬木 per day, you'll have enough for multiple 雨水图腾s within a week. Bring a chest near the entrance to store 硬木 so you don't waste inventory space.

🐷 松露油

来源： Place a 松露 into an 产油机
季节： 秋季 primarily — Pigs find 松露s when let outside on non-rain days. Pigs do NOT find 松露s in 冬季 (even with Heater, they stay indoors). 春季 and 夏季 also work if you have pigs.
地点： Your farm — Pigs must be let outside the barn to find 松露s. Each pig has a chance to find 1 松露 per day (multiple pigs = multiple 松露s). The truffle appears randomly on tillable ground near the pig.
Getting Pigs:
1. Build a Coop (not needed for pigs, but good for early farming)
2. Build a Barn (6,000g + 350 木头 + 150 石头 from 罗宾)
3. Upgrade to Big Barn (12,000g + 450 木头 + 200 石头) — unlocks goats
4. Upgrade to Deluxe Barn (25,000g + 550 木头 + 300 石头) — unlocks pigs and auto-feed
5. Buy a Pig from 玛妮 for 16,000g
Total Barn Investment: 43,000g + 1,350 木头 + 450 石头 + 16,000g pig = 59,000g minimum
产油机: Unlocked at 耕种等级 8. Recipe: 50 史莱姆 + 20 硬木 + 1 金锭. Produces 松露油 from 1 松露 in approximately 6 hours. 松露油 sells for 1,065g (base) — more than the raw 松露 (625g base).
Important: 松露s are NOT found in 冬季 or on rainy days. Plan your 雨水图腾 crafting for 秋季 when pig production is highest.
技巧： Build multiple 产油机s (3-5) so you can process 松露s in bulk. One 产油机 per pig is ideal. Keep 产油机s inside a 棚屋 or barn so they work year-round.

🌲 松焦油

来源： Place a 树液采集器 on a Pine Tree, wait 5-6 days
季节： Any — Pine Trees produce 松焦油 in all seasons
地点： Any Pine Tree on your farm or in 煤林森林. Pine Trees have a distinctive triangular needle shape and darker bark compared to Oak and Maple.
树液采集器 Recipe: Unlocked at 采集等级 3. Recipe: 40 木头 + 2 铜锭s. Place on tree, wait 5-6 in-game days, collect.
Tree Identification:
• Pine Tree — Dark green needles, rough dark bark → produces 松焦油
• Oak Tree — Round leaf canopy, smooth bark → produces 橡树树脂
• Maple Tree — Orange/yellow leaf canopy → produces Maple Syrup
Alternative Sources:
• Tiger Trout Fish Pond — At 6+ population, produces 5 松焦油 every 3-4 days
• Mines — 木头en barrels sometimes drop 松焦油
技巧： Plant a dedicated Pine Tree farm (20-30 Pine Trees in a grid) and place 树液采集器s on all of them. This gives you a steady supply of 松焦油 for 雨水图腾s, 传送图腾s, and other recipes. Pine Trees grow from Pine Cones (random drops when chopping trees or tilling soil).

🌿 Foraging XP — Reaching Level 9

经验来源：
• Picking up a forageable item (wild plant on ground): +3 XP each
• Chopping down a tree with an axe: +12 XP
• Removing a tree stump (after chopping tree): +1 XP
• Picking up a mushroom from the farm cave (if mushroom cave chosen): +3 XP each
• Collecting from tapped trees: +0 XP (does not give XP)
XP Requirements by Level:
• Level 1: 0 XP | Level 2: 100 | Level 3: 380 | Level 4: 770
• Level 5: 1,300 (choose profession) | Level 6: 2,150 | Level 7: 3,300
• Level 8: 4,800 | Level 9: 7,000 | Level 10: 10,000
Level 9 requires 7,000 XP total. That's approximately 580 forageable items, or 230 forageables + 150 trees chopped, or about 60-80 days of active foraging + tree chopping.
Fastest Method: In 春季 第一年, pick up every single forageable you see (Salmonberry bushes on 春季 15-18 give 4 berries per bush — massive XP boost). Chop every non-fruit tree on your farm. Visit the 秘密森林 daily. By 秋季 第一年, you should be Level 7-8. Grind the remaining XP in 冬季 by chopping trees in the Railroad area and 煤林森林.
Profession at Level 5: Choose "Gatherer" (20% chance of double harvest) — this helps with resource collection for the rest of the game. At Level 10, "Botanist" makes all foraged items highest quality.
技巧： The 春季 Onion in 煤林森林 (south of the lake) respawns daily and gives +3 XP each. There are 6-8 per day — an easy 20+ XP daily just from this spot alone.

⚡ 产油机 (Prerequisite Machine)

来源： Crafting menu (unlocked at 耕种等级 8)
Recipe: 50 史莱姆 + 20 硬木 + 1 金锭
季节： Any — craft as soon as you hit 耕种等级 8
地点： Craft anywhere from your inventory
史莱姆 Source: Kill 史莱姆s in the Mines (层 5-40). Each 史莱姆 drops 1-2 史莱姆. The 史莱姆屋 (purchased from 罗宾 for 10,000g + 500 石头 + 10 精炼石英 + 1 铱锭) lets you farm 史莱姆 passively. With 20 史莱姆s inside, they produce 史莱姆 Balls daily that yield 10-20 史莱姆 each.
硬木: 20 硬木 for the 产油机 — 2 days of 秘密森林 farming (12 per day).
金锭: Smelt 5 金矿石 + 1 煤炭 in a 熔炉. 金矿石 is abundant in Mines 80-120.
技巧： Build the 产油机 before you get pigs. You can also use it for Sunflower 种子s (makes cooking oil) and Corn (makes oil). Having it ready means you can process your first 松露 immediately.

4 最优收集路线

Follow this order to minimize backtracking and maximize efficiency. This route assumes a standard 第一年 playthrough.

① 春季 第一年 — Foraging Grind + Axe Upgrades

Pick up every forageable item you see. Chop every non-fruit tree on your farm (clears space + gives 木头 + Foraging XP). Upgrade your Axe to Copper (2,000g + 5 铜锭s) by 春季 10, then Steel (5,000g + 5 铁锭s) by 春季 20. The Steel Axe is required to break the Large Log blocking the 秘密森林 entrance. On 春季 15-18, shake every Salmonberry bush — each berry gives +3 Foraging XP and you'll collect 50-100 berries.

② 春季-夏季 第一年 — 秘密森林 Daily + Barn Construction

With your Steel Axe, break the Large Log blocking the 秘密森林 (northwest 煤林森林). Chop all 6 Large Stumps daily for 12 硬木. Start building your Barn (6,000g + 350 木头 + 150 石头). Upgrade to Big Barn (12,000g + 450 木头 + 200 石头), then Deluxe Barn (25,000g + 550 木头 + 300 石头). This takes 3 days per build from 罗宾. Place 树液采集器s on Pine Trees as soon as you reach 采集等级 3 (380 XP — very early). You need 1 松焦油 per 雨水图腾.

③ 秋季 第一年 — Pigs + 产油机

Buy your first Pig from 玛妮 for 16,000g (requires Deluxe Barn). Build the 产油机 (50 史莱姆 + 20 硬木 + 1 金锭 — requires 耕种等级 8). Let pigs outside every non-rain day. Collect 松露s and process them into 松露油. One 松露 in the 产油机 produces 1 松露油 in ~6 hours. By 秋季 14, you should have your first 松露油.

④ Grind Foraging to Level 9

If you haven't reached 采集等级 9 by 冬季, focus on chopping trees. The Railroad area (unlocked after Earthquake in 夏季 第一年 or via 社区中心) and 煤林森林 have dozens of trees that respawn. Each tree gives +12 XP. Chop 50 trees and you're at Level 9. Also pick up all 冬季 forageables (Crystal Fruit, Holly, Snow Yam, Crocus) for +3 XP each.

⑤ 最终组装

With all materials gathered and 采集等级 9 reached: Open crafting menu → RAIN TOTEM. The recipe produces one single-use totem. Use it strategically — it forces rain the next day, overriding the weather forecast completely.

5 ⚠ 避坑指南 — 别浪费时间

These mistakes will waste hours. Read this before you start.

⚠ Pigs don't find 松露s in 冬季 or Rain

Pigs will NOT produce 松露s in 冬季 (they stay indoors even with a Heater) or on rainy days (they stay inside the barn). This means you have approximately 56 usable pig days in 秋季 (秋季 has 28 days, minus ~8 rainy days = ~20 good days). With one pig, expect 15-20 松露s in 秋季. Plan accordingly — you cannot craft 雨水图腾s in 冬季 unless you stockpiled 松露油 in 秋季.

⚠ The Deluxe Barn is a massive gold sink

Getting pigs requires 59,000g total investment (Barn 6,000g + Big Barn 12,000g + Deluxe Barn 25,000g + Pig 16,000g). This is one of the most expensive animal progression paths in the game. Prioritize this only after you have stable crop income (小桶s with 古代水果 or 杨桃). Don't rush pigs if you're still struggling with daily farm expenses.

⚠ 采集等级 9 takes consistent effort

Many players reach 采集等级 7-8 and stagnate because they stop picking up forageables. The gap from Level 8 (4,800 XP) to Level 9 (7,000 XP) requires 2,200 additional XP — equivalent to picking up ~730 forageables OR chopping ~180 trees. Make foraging part of your daily route: check the Bus Stop, Railroad, 秘密森林, and Beach every morning.

⚠ 雨水图腾 doesn't work on Festival days

Using a 雨水图腾 the day before a Festival (e.g., Egg Festival on 春季 13, Luau on 夏季 11) will NOT produce rain on the festival day. The game overrides the weather for festivals. Check the calendar before using your precious totem.

⚠ You need a Steel Axe for 秘密森林

The Large Log blocking the 秘密森林 entrance requires a Steel Axe or better. A Copper Axe won't cut it (literally). Upgrade at 克林特's 铁匠铺 (5,000g + 5 铁锭s, takes 2 days). Without the 秘密森林, your 硬木 supply is extremely limited — you'll be stuck waiting for random farm stump spawns.

⚠ 松露油 is worth more than raw 松露s

Don't sell raw 松露s! A base-quality 松露 sells for 625g, but 松露油 sells for 1,065g. With the 工匠 profession (耕种等级 10), 松露油 sells for 1,491g. Always process 松露s into Oil before selling. Only use 松露油 for crafting 雨水图腾s if you have a surplus — selling it is extremely profitable.

⚠ 硬木 is needed for many other recipes

硬木 is a bottleneck material. You need it for: 压酪机 (10), 产油机 (20), 硬木 Fence (1 each), Stump Brazier (10), 传送图腾：农场 (1), and 软木浮标 (10). With only 12 硬木 per day from 秘密森林, plan your crafting priorities carefully. Check out our 传送图腾：农场 guide for another 硬木-hungry recipe.

6 🎁 额外收益 — 顺路能拿到的好东西

While gathering 雨水图腾 materials, you'll accumulate wealth, XP, and resources that benefit your entire farm. Don't leave these on the table.

🎁 秘密森林 daily farming

Visiting the 秘密森林 every day gives you 12 硬木, plus forageables (Morels in 春季, Fiddlehead Ferns in 夏季, Chanterelles in 秋季, Holly in 冬季). These are used in cooking recipes (Strange Bun, Fried Mushroom, Fiddlehead Risotto) and 社区中心 bundles. The 秘密森林 pond also has unique fish (木头skip, Green Algae).

🎁 Pig farming = massive passive income

Pigs are the highest-value animal in 星露谷物语. A single pig produces 松露s worth 1,491g each (as 松露油 with 工匠). With 12 pigs, that's 15,000-20,000g per day in 秋季. The 雨水图腾 is a side benefit of an already-profitable pig operation. You're building an animal empire, not just crafting a totem.

🎁 采集等级 9 + Level 10 professions

At 采集等级 10, the "Botanist" profession (if you chose Gatherer at Level 5) makes all foraged items highest quality (Iridium). This doubles the value of everything you pick up off the ground. The "Tracker" profession (if you chose Forester) shows the location of all forageables on screen — incredibly useful for completing the foraging collection.

🎁 树液采集器s produce multiple valuable resources

While waiting for 松焦油, your Oak and Maple 树液采集器s are producing 橡树树脂 (needed for 小桶s — see our 小桶 guide) and Maple Syrup (needed for 蜂房s and cooking). A full tapper farm of 30+ trees generates hundreds of gold per day passively. 树液采集器s are a "set and forget" income source.

🎁 Barn upgrades unlock all animals

Building a Deluxe Barn doesn't just unlock pigs — it unlocks sheep and goats too. Goats produce Goat Milk (worth more than Cow Milk), and sheep produce Wool (can be processed into Cloth in a 织布机). The auto-feed system in the Deluxe Barn also saves you time every morning.

🎁 产油机 for cooking and profit

Once built, the 产油机 can process Sunflower 种子s into cooking oil (for recipes like Fried Calamari and Hashbrowns) and Corn into oil. It's also essential for the 传送图腾：农场 (see our 传送图腾：农场 guide) which requires 蜂蜜 instead of 松露油. Multiple 产油机s = multiple crafting paths.
