1 Why the 楼梯 is Essential for 骷髅洞穴 Success

⬇️

楼梯

★★★☆☆ — Skip a 层 in 骷髅洞穴
采矿等级 2

The 楼梯 is the single most important consumable item for 骷髅洞穴 expeditions. When placed on any mine floor, it instantly creates a ladder to the next floor — bypassing the need to break rocks, kill monsters, or find the exit. In the 骷髅洞穴 where every floor is randomly generated and some layouts are brutal (infested floors, monster-heavy rooms, sparse rock distributions), having a stack of 50-100 楼梯s means the difference between reaching 层 100 and passing out on 层 20.

What makes the 楼梯 truly special is its accessibility. The recipe unlocks at 采矿等级 2 — achievable within the first few days of 春季 by breaking rocks on your farm. The only material needed is 99 石头 — one of the most abundant resources in the game. Unlike other items requiring 铱锭s or 松露油, 楼梯s can be mass-produced from day one by any player willing to collect rocks. This guide covers the most efficient stone farming methods, strategic 楼梯 usage, and how to build a stockpile that carries you to 骷髅洞穴 层 100 and beyond.

★ What This Means for You

A stack of 99 楼梯s lets you skip 99 entire floors of the 骷髅洞穴. At an average of 2-3 minutes per floor (including combat, rock-breaking, and searching for exits), that's 3-5 hours of saved in-game time per run — enough to reach 层 100+ where Iridium nodes are abundant, 五彩碎片s drop, and the legendary Mr. Qi event triggers. 楼梯s are the difference between a profitable 骷髅洞穴 run and a waste of a day.

3 材料与来源

Each material listed with exact source, season, location, and farming tips.

🪨 石头 (99 units per 楼梯)

来源： Break rocks literally anywhere in the game
季节： Any — available year-round in all locations
地点： Everywhere — your farm, all mine floors, the Quarry, the Volcano Dungeon, 姜岛.
Best Farming Methods (Ranked by Efficiency):

1. Buy from 罗宾 (Fastest, Costs Gold)
罗宾 at the Carpenter's Shop sells 石头 for 20g each. That's 1,980g for 99 石头 (one 楼梯). 罗宾 stocks unlimited 石头. If you have gold to spare (from crop sales, fishing, or artisan goods), this is the fastest method — walk in, buy 99 石头, craft a 楼梯 instantly. Open 9am-5pm, closed Tuesdays.

2. Mines 层 1-10 (Best Free Method Early Game)
The first 10 floors of the regular Mines have the highest density of small breakable rocks. Each brown rock gives 1-2 石头. Enter 层 1, break every rock (30-50 rocks per floor), take the ladder to 层 2, repeat. Use the elevator to return to 层 1 when you reach 层 5. This loop yields 200-300 石头 per hour — enough for 2-3 楼梯s. Rocks respawn when you re-enter a floor.

3. Quarry (Best Free Method Mid-Game) The Quarry (unlocked by repairing the Bridge at the 社区中心 or via Joja) spawns large rocks, boulders, and ore nodes daily. Large rocks give 10+ 石头 each. Mining cart rocks give 5-8 石头. The Quarry respawns rocks every few days, making it a renewable source. A full Quarry clear yields 100-200 石头.

4. Farm Rock Clearing (One-Time Early Boost)
Your farm spawns with 50-150 rocks at the start of the game. Breaking all of them in the first 5 days gives 100-300 石头 — enough for 1-3 楼梯s right away. This is a one-time source but great for initial stockpiling. More rocks spawn randomly over time but at a slow rate.

5. Volcano Dungeon (Best Late-Game Method)
The Volcano Dungeon on 姜岛 (unlocked after repairing Willy's Boat) has rock-rich floors with stone clusters that yield 5-10 石头 per hit. Using bombs here is extremely effective — one bomb can destroy 10+ rocks for 30-50 石头. A full Volcano run yields 300-500 石头.

6. 炸弹 Farming (Fastest Bulk Method)
Craft 炸弹s (4 铁矿石 + 1 煤炭) or buy 樱桃炸弹s (50g from the Dwarf). Enter any mine floor with dense rock clusters, place bombs, and watch 10-20 rocks explode simultaneously. This method yields 500-800 石头 per hour but costs resources/gold for bombs. Best used when you need 50+ 楼梯s quickly.

Alternative Sources:
• Fishing Treasure Chests — 5-10% chance for 5-10 石头
• 回收机 — Broken Glasses give 1-2 石头
• Museum rewards — Some mineral donations reward 石头
• Train Station — Rare train drops include 石头
• Geodes — Regular Geodes sometimes contain 石头 when cracked
总计需要： 99 石头 per 楼梯. For a serious 骷髅洞穴 run, bring 50-100 楼梯s = 4,950-9,900 石头.
技巧： Buy 石头 from 罗宾 if you have gold. At 20g per 石头, 99 石头 costs 1,980g — less than the profit from a single 金锭 (250g) or a good fish. Your time is worth more than the gold. Only farm 石头 manually if you're broke or enjoying the mining. For serious 骷髅洞穴 pushes, buy 5,000-10,000 石头 from 罗宾 in one trip and craft 50-100 楼梯s.

⛏️ Mining XP — Reaching Level 2

经验来源：
• Breaking a regular rock: +1 XP
• Mining a 铜矿石 node: +3 XP
• Mining an 铁矿石 node: +4 XP
• Mining a 金矿石 node: +5 XP
• Killing a mine monster: +3-10 XP
XP Requirements:
• Level 1: 0 XP | Level 2: 100 XP
Level 2 requires only 100 XP total. Breaking 100 rocks gets you there. Most players reach this on 日 1 or 2 while clearing their farm.
Fastest Method: Spend your first morning breaking every rock on your farm. With 50-150 rocks, you'll easily hit Level 2 and have 50-150 石头 as a bonus.
Profession at Level 5: Choose "Miner" (+1 ore per node) for more resources, or "Geologist" (chance for gems in pairs) for gem duplication. At Level 10, "勘探者" doubles coal drops (essential), and "Excavator" doubles geode drops.
技巧： 采矿等级 2 is so early that you'll unlock it without even trying. The real bottleneck for 楼梯s is 石头 collection, not the skill requirement. Focus on building a 石头 stockpile from day one.

4 最优收集路线

Follow this order to build a 楼梯 stockpile efficiently. This route assumes a standard playthrough.

① 日 1-5 — Farm Clearing + 采矿等级 2

Break every rock on your farm with your starting Pickaxe. Each rock gives 1-2 石头 and +1 Mining XP. With 50-150 farm rocks, you'll reach 采矿等级 2 automatically and collect 100-200 石头 — enough for 1-2 楼梯s. Store all 石头 in a chest. Do NOT sell 石头 — you'll need thousands of it.

② 春季 第一年 — Early 石头 Stockpiling

Every time you visit the Mines for ore, spend an extra 10 minutes on 层 1-10 breaking every rock. These floors are small, have no dangerous monsters, and respawn instantly when you re-enter via the elevator. A 20-minute session yields 100-150 石头. Do this 2-3 times per week and you'll have 1,000+ 石头 by 夏季 — enough for 10 楼梯s.

③ 夏季-秋季 第一年 — Quarry + Bulk Collection

Unlock the Quarry via 社区中心 Craft Room bundle (Bridge Repair) or Joja. The Quarry spawns rocks, ore nodes, and geodes every few days. A full clear gives 100-200 石头. Visit weekly. If you have spare gold, start buying 石头 from 罗宾 (20g each). Buy 2,000-3,000g worth of 石头 (100-150 楼梯s worth) before your first 骷髅洞穴 trip.

④ Late Game — 罗宾 Bulk Purchase + Volcano

Once you have stable income (古代水果 wine in 小桶s, see our 小桶 guide), buy 石头 from 罗宾 in bulk. 10,000g gets you 500 楼梯s worth of 石头 — enough for 5+ deep 骷髅洞穴 runs. On 姜岛, the Volcano Dungeon provides massive 石头 yields from bomb farming. One Volcano run with 20 bombs can yield 500+ 石头.

⑤ 骷髅洞穴 Strategy — When to Use 楼梯s

Bring 50-100 楼梯s per deep run. Use them on: Prehistoric floors (dinosaurs are slow and dangerous), Monster-infested floors (must kill all monsters to spawn ladder — skip entirely), and sparse rock floors (no efficient way to find the exit). Below 层 50, use 楼梯s every 5-10 floors to maintain descent speed. Your goal: reach 层 100 before 2:00 AM. With 50+ 楼梯s, 层 100 is achievable by 10:00 PM.

5 ⚠ 避坑指南 — 别浪费时间

These mistakes will waste hours. Read this before you start.

⚠ 楼梯s CANNOT be placed on the regular Mines elevator floors

楼梯s only work on mine floors (regular Mines and 骷髅洞穴). They cannot be placed on elevator landing floors, in town, on your farm, or anywhere else. Attempting to place a 楼梯 outside a mine will consume the item with no effect. Always check you're on an actual mine floor before placing.

⚠ 99 石头 per 楼梯 adds up FAST

A serious 骷髅洞穴 push to 层 100 requires 50-100 楼梯s — that's 4,950 to 9,900 石头. If you try to farm this manually by breaking rocks, you'll spend 10-15 hours of real time. Buy 石头 from 罗宾. At 20g each, 9,900 石头 costs 198,000g — expensive but achievable with a few 小桶s of 杨桃 wine (see our 小桶 guide). Your time is worth more than the gold.

⚠ 石头 is needed for farm buildings too

Before converting all your 石头 to 楼梯s, remember you need 石头 for: Silo (100 石头), Well (75 石头), Coop (100 石头), Barn (150 石头), 棚屋 (100 石头), and many other buildings. Keep at least 500-1,000 石头 in reserve for building projects. A dedicated "石头" chest prevents accidentally using building materials.

⚠ The 骷髅洞穴 has no elevator

Unlike the regular Mines (which have elevator checkpoints every 5 floors), the 骷髅洞穴 has NO elevator. If you pass out or leave, you start from 层 1 next time. This is why 楼梯s are essential — every minute spent on a bad floor is a minute you can't recover. Use 楼梯s liberally; the Iridium you collect on deep floors pays for them many times over.

⚠ Prehistoric floors are 楼梯-worthy

Prehistoric floors (identifiable by the jungle-themed tileset and dinosaur enemies) are extremely dangerous and time-consuming. Pepper Rex dinosaurs have 300 HP and deal 15 damage. Iridium Bats and Iridium Crabs also spawn here. Always use a 楼梯 on Prehistoric floors unless you're specifically hunting Dinosaur Eggs or 五彩碎片s.

⚠ You can craft 楼梯s during a 骷髅洞穴 run

If you brought raw 石头 instead of pre-crafted 楼梯s, you can craft them inside the 骷髅洞穴. Open your crafting menu and craft 楼梯s on-demand. However, this wastes precious in-game time (each craft takes a moment). It's better to pre-craft 50+ 楼梯s before entering and keep them in your inventory or a chest at the Desert entrance.

⚠ The 完美雕像 eliminates 楼梯 need for Iridium

Once you obtain the 完美雕像 from 爷爷的神龛 (4 candles lit), it produces 2-8 铱矿石 daily automatically. With this, you no longer need deep 骷髅洞穴 runs primarily for Iridium. 楼梯s become mainly for 五彩碎片 hunting and Mr. Qi quests rather than daily Iridium farming.

6 🎁 额外收益 — 顺路能拿到的好东西

While gathering 石头 for 楼梯s, you'll accumulate wealth, XP, and resources that benefit your entire farm. Don't leave these on the table.

🎁 Mining XP for higher-level recipes

While farming 石头, you'll naturally gain Mining XP. Higher Mining levels unlock: 采矿等级 5 (profession choice — Miner or Geologist), 采矿等级 6 (炸弹 and 樱桃炸弹 recipes), 采矿等级 9 (宝石复制机 — see our 宝石复制机 guide), and 采矿等级 10 (勘探者 doubles coal drops, Excavator doubles geodes). 石头 farming accelerates your entire mining progression.

🎁 Ore collection while 石头 farming

Mining rocks for 石头 also yields 铜矿石, 铁矿石, 煤炭, and geodes. A typical 石头 farming session in the Mines produces 20-50 铜矿石, 10-20 铁矿石, and 5-10 煤炭 as byproducts. These are essential for tool upgrades, crafting, and smelting. You get ore "for free" while collecting 石头.

🎁 Geodes for museum and resources

While breaking rocks, you'll collect regular Geodes (层 1-39), Frozen Geodes (层 40-79), and Magma Geodes (层 80-120). Crack these at the 铁匠铺 (25g each) for minerals, gems, and artifacts. Many minerals are needed for museum donations (rewards from 冈瑟), and gems can be duplicated in 宝石复制机s (see our 宝石复制机 guide).

🎁 Deep 骷髅洞穴 = 五彩碎片s

The ultimate goal of 楼梯-fueled deep runs is finding 五彩碎片s — the rarest and most valuable item in the game. Used for: 银河之剑 (hold at the Three Pillars in the Desert), enchanting tools at the Forge (姜岛), museum donation, and gifting (every villager loves it). 五彩碎片 drop rate increases on deeper floors — 层 100+ has approximately 4% chance from Iridium Nodes.

🎁 Mr. Qi rewards at 层 100

Reaching 骷髅洞穴 层 100 on your first visit triggers a unique cutscene with Mr. Qi. He rewards you with "Mr. Qi's Challenge" — a special order that leads to further quests, the Qi's Walnut Room on 姜岛, and access to the most powerful late-game items and challenges. This is gated behind deep 骷髅洞穴 runs, which are only possible with 楼梯s.

🎁 楼梯 crafting unlocks at 采矿等级 2

Because 楼梯s unlock so early (Level 2), you can start stockpiling them immediately. A new player who clears their farm on 日 1 and starts collecting 石头 can have 5-10 楼梯s ready by the time they unlock the 骷髅洞穴. Unlike most items in this guide, there's no waiting for a high skill level — just collect rocks and craft. It's the most accessible "advanced" item in the game.
