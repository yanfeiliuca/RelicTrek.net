1 Why the 铱环 Is the Best Ring in 星露谷物语

💍

铱环

★★★★★ — Glow, 磁铁ism, +10% Damage
战斗等级 9

The 铱环 is the most powerful ring in 星露谷物语, combining three separate ring effects into a single slot. It provides the glow effect (like the 发光戒指 — emits light in a radius around you), the magnetism effect (like the 磁力戒指 — dramatically increases item pickup range), and +10% damage to all weapons. Wearing two 铱环s simultaneously gives you double the glow radius, double the magnetism, and +20% damage — a combination that makes both combat and farming dramatically more efficient.

But this power comes at a steep cost. You need 战斗等级 9 (requiring hundreds of monster kills), 50 太阳精华 (from specific monsters in the 骷髅洞穴 and late mines), 50 虚空精华 (from Shadow monsters deep underground), and 5 铱锭s (the rarest metal in the game). This guide breaks down exactly where to find each material, which monsters to farm, and the optimal route to crafting your first 铱环 before the end of 第一年.

★ What This Means for You

With one 铱环 equipped, you never need a torch in the mines again (glow), you'll automatically pick up drops from 4 tiles away (magnetism), and every sword swing hits 10% harder (+10% damage). Two bands means +20% damage — the difference between killing a Serpent in 3 hits instead of 4, which saves your life in the 骷髅洞穴.

3 材料与来源

Each material listed with exact monster sources, locations, drop rates, and farming optimization.

🟣 铱锭

来源： Smelt 5 铱矿石 + 1 煤炭 in 熔炉
季节： Any — available year-round
地点： 骷髅洞穴 exclusively (铱矿石)
Best Farming: 骷髅洞穴 层 50+ — Iridium nodes spawn frequently. Use bombs to clear rock clusters quickly.
Alternative Sources: 完美雕像 (2-8 铱矿石/day), Meteorite events (mine with Gold+ Pickaxe), Fishing Treasure Chests (rare), 科罗布斯 sells 1 铱锭 on Fridays for 3,000g
总计需要： 5 bars = 25 铱矿石 + 5 煤炭
技巧： The 完美雕像 (from 爷爷的神龛, 4 candles) produces enough 铱矿石 daily that you may not need 骷髅洞穴 farming for the 5 bars.

☀️ 太阳精华

来源： Dropped by specific monsters
季节： Any — available year-round
掉率： 65-75% per qualifying monster kill
Best Monsters:
• Ghost — Mines 层 51-79 (75% drop rate) — BEST TARGET
• Squid Kid — Mines 层 80-120 (65% drop rate)
• Metal Head — Mines 层 80-120 (65% drop rate)
• Lava Bat — Mines 层 80-120 (65% drop rate)
• Hot Head / Magma Sprite / Magma Sparker — Volcano Dungeon (65-75% drop rate)
最快获取： Descend to Mines 层 55-65. Ghosts spawn frequently on these floors and have relatively low HP (96). Exit and re-enter the elevator to reset monster spawns. With good luck, expect ~15-20 太阳精华 per hour.
替代方案： 科罗布斯 sells 太阳精华 for 80g each on Tuesdays (limited stock)
总计需要： 50 太阳精华 ≈ 70-80 qualifying monster kills

🌑 虚空精华

来源： Dropped by Shadow-family monsters and Serpents
季节： Any — available year-round
掉率： 65-99% per qualifying monster kill
Best Monsters:
• Shadow Brute — Mines 层 80-120 (75% drop rate) — BEST TARGET
• Shadow Shaman — Mines 层 80-120 (65% drop rate)
• Shadow Sniper — Advanced Mines, late floors (75% drop rate)
• Serpent — 骷髅洞穴 (99% drop rate!) — BEST IF IN SKULL CAVERN
• Purple 史莱姆 — 骷髅洞穴 (65% drop rate)
最快获取： Two equally good options:
1. Mines 层 85-95 — Shadow Brutes and Shadow Shamans spawn in groups. Take the elevator to 层 85, clear the floor, exit to lobby, repeat.
2. 骷髅洞穴 Serpents — With a good weapon (银河之剑 +), Serpents die in 1-2 hits and have a 99% 虚空精华 drop rate. Farm 层 1-30 for rapid Serpent spawns.
替代方案： 科罗布斯 sells 虚空精华 for 100g each on Thursdays (limited stock)
总计需要： 50 虚空精华 ≈ 55-70 qualifying monster kills

⚔️ Combat XP — Reaching Level 9

经验来源： Bug (+3), Bat (+3), 史莱姆 (+3-8), Skeleton (+10), Ghost (+10), Shadow Brute (+15), Serpent (+15), Pepper Rex (+10)
等级9需求： 15,000 XP (Level 8 = 12,000 XP, Level 9 = 15,000 XP)
Fastest Method: 骷髅洞穴 monster grinding. Serpents give 15 XP each and spawn infinitely. A typical 3-hour 骷髅洞穴 run nets 60-100 Serpent kills = 900-1,500 XP.
替代方案： Monster Eradication Goals — Gil at the 冒险家公会 rewards you for killing specific monster counts. Completing all goals gives significant Combat XP and unique rewards including the Infinity Gavel recipe.
技巧： Eat food with Attack buffs (香辣鳝鱼 +1 Attack, Crab Cakes +1 Defense) before combat sessions. The 尤巴之戒 (Crafting: 5 铁锭 + 5 金锭 + 1 钻石) gives a chance to spawn a shield when hit — invaluable for deep-mine survival.

4 最优收集路线

Follow this order to minimize backtracking and maximize Combat XP efficiency. This route assumes a standard 第一年 playthrough.

① 春季 第一年 — Mine Descent + Early Combat XP

As you descend the regular Mines for ore, kill every monster you see. Combat XP accumulates passively — don't skip monsters even on early floors. Bats, Bugs, and 史莱姆s on 层 1-40 give small but meaningful XP. Target 层 80 by end of 春季 to unlock 金矿石 and mid-tier monsters. Buy the Steel Small Sword from the 冒险家公会 once available.

② 夏季 第一年 — Reach 层 120 + 骷髅洞穴 Prep

Push to Mines 层 120 to unlock the 骷髅洞穴. On 层 80-120, you'll encounter Ghosts (太阳精华, 75% drop) and Shadow Brutes/Shamans (虚空精华, 65-75% drop). Farm these floors extensively — this is your primary Essence collection phase. Complete the 金库包 or Joja Bus repair (42,500g) to unlock 卡利科沙漠.

③ 秋季 第一年 — 骷髅洞穴 Essence + Iridium Farming

Enter the 骷髅洞穴 with a 熔岩武士刀 (冒险家公会, 25,000g) or 银河之剑 (obtained by bringing a 五彩碎片 to the Three Pillars in the 卡利科沙漠). Serpents in the 骷髅洞穴 have a 99% 虚空精华 drop rate — farm 层 1-30 for rapid Serpent kills. Mine all Iridium nodes you see — you need 25 铱矿石 total. Use bombs liberally; they destroy rock clusters and damage monsters simultaneously.

④ 太阳精华 Focus — Ghost Farming

After your 骷髅洞穴 runs, use the Mines elevator to teleport to 层 55. Ghosts spawn frequently on 层 55-65. Each Ghost has 75 HP and a 75% 太阳精华 drop rate. Exit and re-enter via elevator to reset spawns. This is the fastest 太阳精华 farm in the game — expect 15-20 per hour with a strong sword.

⑤ 战斗等级 9 — The Final Push

If you haven't reached 战斗等级 9 by late 秋季, dedicate a full day to 骷髅洞穴 Serpent farming. Each Serpent gives 15 XP. With 香辣鳝鱼 (+1 Speed, +1 Luck) and good gear, you can kill 80-120 Serpents in one run. Repeat 2-3 times to close the gap. Check your Combat XP at the Skills tab to track progress.

⑥ 最终组装

Smelt 25 铱矿石 into 5 铱锭s (25 ore + 5 coal). Combine with 50 太阳精华 + 50 虚空精华. Open crafting menu → IRIDIUM BAND. Equip immediately. Feel the power.

5 ⚠ 避坑指南 — 别浪费时间

These mistakes will waste hours and get you killed. Read this before you start.

⚠ 战斗等级 9 is the MAX — it takes forever

战斗等级 9 requires 15,000 total XP — more than any other skill except Farming. The gap from Level 8 to 9 alone is 3,000 XP, equivalent to killing 200 Serpents or 300 Shadow Brutes. Start killing monsters from 日 1 and never stop. Skipping monsters during your mine descent is XP you can never recover.

⚠ Don't enter 骷髅洞穴 unprepared

Serpents deal 25-35 damage per hit and attack in swarms. Without at least 100 HP, healing food, and a weapon dealing 30+ damage, you will pass out and lose 1,000g. Minimum prep: 熔岩武士刀 (25,000g), 10+ Cheese/Salad, 5 楼梯s, and a 尤巴之戒.

⚠ 太阳精华 and 虚空精华 are NOT interchangeable

Many players assume both essences drop from the same monsters. They don't. 太阳精华 comes from fire/ghost monsters (Ghost, Squid Kid, Hot Head). 虚空精华 comes from shadow monsters (Shadow Brute, Shadow Shaman, Serpent). Track both counts separately — it's easy to end up with 50 Solar and 20 Void.

⚠ 银河之剑 is essential for 骷髅洞穴

The 银河之剑 (60-80 damage) makes 骷髅洞穴 farming 3x faster than the 熔岩武士刀. Obtain it by bringing a 五彩碎片 to the Three Pillars in the northeast of the 卡利科沙漠. 五彩碎片s have a 4% drop rate from Iridium Nodes in 骷髅洞穴 — ironic, but once you get one, combat becomes trivial.

⚠ Essences stack to 999 — collect early, store often

Both Solar and 虚空精华 stack to 999 in a single inventory slot. Start collecting them as soon as you reach the relevant mine floors (50+ for Solar, 80+ for Void). Every Essence you pick up before actively farming saves time later.

⚠ The 铱环 is NOT sold anywhere

Unlike many rings, the 铱环 cannot be purchased from the 冒险家公会 or any NPC. You must craft it yourself at 战斗等级 9. Don't waste time checking shop inventories — focus on the skill grind and material collection.

6 🎁 额外收益 — 顺路能拿到的好东西

While grinding for the 铱环, you'll accumulate resources, XP, and unique items that supercharge your entire farm. Don't leave these on the table.

🎁 Monster loot sells for serious gold

While farming essences, you'll collect monster drops that sell well: 蝙蝠翅膀s (15g), 史莱姆 (5g each, stacks to 999), Bone Fragments (12g), and rare drops like Dwarf Scrolls (museum donations) and Rare Discs. A full day of monster farming can yield 3,000-5,000g in incidental loot.

🎁 冒险家公会 rewards

Gil at the 冒险家公会 gives unique rewards for Monster Eradication Goals. Killing 200 Bats gives the 盗贼戒指 (monsters drop double loot). Killing 150 Skeletons gives the Skeleton Mask. Killing 500 Dust Sprites gives the 木炭窑 recipe. These are among the best rewards in the game.

🎁 五彩碎片s for 银河之剑

While mining Iridium in 骷髅洞穴, you have a 4% chance per Iridium Node to find a 五彩碎片. One 五彩碎片 at the Three Pillars gives the 银河之剑 — the second-best sword in the entire game. Many players find their first 五彩碎片 while Iridium farming for the 宝石复制机 or 铱环.

🎁 战斗等级 10 profession choice

Reaching 战斗等级 10 unlocks two profession paths: Fighter → Brute (+15% damage, +15 HP) or Scout → Acrobat/Desperado (critical strike chance). The Brute path is recommended for general combat; Desperado is for critical strike builds with specific weapons. Both are powerful endgame choices.

🎁 Mine exploration unlocks crafting recipes

Descending deep mines unlocks numerous crafting recipes at the 冒险家公会 and through level-ups: 楼梯 (Mining 2), Explosive Ammo (Combat 8), 倒刺钩 (Fishing), and more. Deep mine exploration also reveals rare mushrooms and forage items.

🎁 Excess essences have multiple uses

Both Solar and 虚空精华 are used in other recipes beyond the 铱环. 太阳精华 is needed for Mega 炸弹 (4 Solar + 4 Void + 1 金锭) and 发光石戒指 (5 Solar + 8 Manganese). 虚空精华 is needed for Mega 炸弹 and can be gifted to Sebastian (he loves it) and the Wizard (liked gift). Collect extra — you'll use them.
