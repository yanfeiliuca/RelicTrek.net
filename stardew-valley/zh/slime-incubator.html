The 史莱姆孵化器 turns a dangerous monster into a renewable crafting resource factory.

3 材料与来源

Each material listed with exact source, season, location, and farming tips.

🟣 铱锭

来源： Smelt 5 铱矿石 + 1 煤炭 in a 熔炉
季节： Any — available year-round
地点： 骷髅洞穴 exclusively (before Railroad unlock). The 卡利科沙漠 is accessed by repairing the Bus via the Vault bundle at the 社区中心 (42,500g total) or Joja Community Development Form.
最佳方法： Reach 骷髅洞穴 层 50+ — Iridium nodes appear frequently. Each purple Iridium Node drops 1-3 铱矿石 when broken with a Gold Pickaxe or better. On 层 100+, massive Iridium clusters appear that can yield 10-20 ore per cluster with bombs.
Alternative Sources:
• 完美雕像 (爷爷的神龛, 4 candles) — Produces 2-8 铱矿石 daily automatically. This is the most reliable long-term source.
• Meteorite events — Random night event leaves a meteorite on your farm. Mine it with a Gold+ Pickaxe for 6+ 铱矿石.
• Omni Geodes — 5-8% chance to contain 1-3 铱矿石 when cracked at the 铁匠铺 or Geode Crusher.
• 科罗布斯 — Sells 1 铱锭 every Friday for 3,000g (late-game convenience purchase).
总计需要： 2 铱锭s = 10 铱矿石 + 2 煤炭
技巧： A single 骷髅洞穴 run to 层 50+ with bombs typically yields 15-30 铱矿石 — enough for both bars in one trip. Bring 樱桃炸弹s or regular 炸弹s to blast rock clusters efficiently. See our 宝石复制机 guide for more Iridium farming strategies.

💚 史莱姆 (100 units)

来源： Defeat 史莱姆 enemies in the Mines and 史莱姆屋
季节： Any — available year-round
地点： All mine floors, with different colors on different levels:
• Green 史莱姆s — Mines 层 1-29. Drop 1 史莱姆 (50% chance). Weakest — 24 HP, 5 damage.
• Blue 史莱姆s — Mines 层 30-79. Drop 1-2 史莱姆 (75% chance). Moderate — 106 HP, 7 damage.
• Red 史莱姆s — Mines 层 80-120. Drop 1-3 史莱姆 (100% chance). Strong — 205 HP, 16 damage.
• Purple 史莱姆s — 骷髅洞穴 only. Drop 2-4 史莱姆. Dangerous — 410 HP, 28 damage.
• Tiger 史莱姆s — 姜岛 only. Drop 3-5 史莱姆. Very dangerous — 900 HP, 35 damage.
Best Farming Method — Mines 层 1-15:
Green 史莱姆s respawn every time you re-enter a mine floor. Enter 层 1, kill all Green 史莱姆s (typically 3-5 per floor), take the ladder down to 层 2, repeat. When you reach 层 5, take the elevator back to 层 1 and start over. This loop gives 10-15 史莱姆 per 5-minute cycle. At this rate, 100 史莱姆 takes roughly 30-40 minutes of focused farming.
Alternative — 史莱姆屋 (passive farming):
Purchase a 史莱姆屋 from 罗宾 (10,000g + 500 石头 + 10 精炼石英 + 1 铱锭). Place slimes inside (up to 20). Male and female slimes breed when a full water trough is present. With 5+ male and 5+ female slimes, they reproduce rapidly. 史莱姆s inside the hutch produce 史莱姆 Balls on the floor — breaking these yields 10-20 史莱姆 each.
Dangerous Mines (Mr. Qi quest):
After completing the "Danger in the Deep" quest from Mr. Qi, the mines become significantly harder but all slime types spawn on every floor, dramatically increasing 史莱姆 drops.
技巧： Wear a 盗贼戒指 (drops from Dust Sprites in the 冒险家公会 after killing 500) — it doubles monster loot drops, giving you 2-6 史莱姆 per kill instead of 1-3. This halves your farming time. Eat a 怪物香水 (30 史莱姆 + 30 蝙蝠翅膀 — 战斗等级 8) to double monster spawn rates while farming.

⚔️ Combat XP — Reaching Level 8

经验来源：
• Defeating a Green 史莱姆: +3 XP
• Defeating a Blue/Red 史莱姆: +6 XP
• Defeating a Bat/Duggy/Rock Crab: +4-6 XP
• Defeating a Shadow Brute/Shaman: +10 XP
• Defeating a Skeleton/Serpent: +15 XP
• Defeating a Purple 史莱姆: +10 XP
XP Requirements by Level:
• Level 1: 0 XP | Level 2: 100 | Level 3: 300 | Level 4: 540
• Level 5: 860 (choose profession) | Level 6: 1,320 | Level 7: 2,060
• Level 8: 3,000 | Level 9: 4,200 | Level 10: 6,000
Level 8 requires 3,000 XP total. That's approximately 500 Green 史莱姆s, 250 Blue/Red 史莱姆s, or 200 Shadow Brutes. In practice, natural mine progression gets you to Level 5-6 by 层 80.
Fastest Method: Descend the mines while killing every monster you see. On 层 40-79, focus on killing Dust Sprites (+4 XP each) — you need to kill 500 for the 盗贼戒指 anyway. On 层 80-120, kill every Shadow Brute, Shaman, and Skeleton for +10-15 XP each. A single dedicated combat run on 层 80-120 can yield 200-300 XP.
Profession at Level 5: Choose "Fighter" (+10% damage, +15 HP) for easier combat, or Scout" (+50% crit chance) for burst damage. At Level 10, "Brute" (Fighter path) adds +15% more damage, while "Defender" adds +25 HP.
技巧： Complete the 冒险家公会 Monster Eradication Goals — kill 125 Cave Insects for a Bug Steak recipe, 125 Duggies for a Crystal 层 recipe, and 500 Dust Sprites for the 盗贼戒指 (doubles loot drops). These goals give you extra motivation to farm monsters for Combat XP.

🏠 史莱姆屋 (Recommended Building)

来源： Purchase from 罗宾 at Carpenter's Shop
Cost: 10,000g + 500 石头 + 10 精炼石英 + 1 铱锭
Size: 11x6 (66 tiles) interior
Capacity: Up to 20 slimes
地点： Carpenter's Shop (9am-5pm, closed Tuesdays)
How It Works:
1. Place 史莱姆 Eggs in the 史莱姆孵化器 inside the hutch
2. Hatched slimes need a full water trough to breed (fill with your watering can)
3. Male and female slimes breed, producing more slimes (up to 20 max)
4. 史莱姆s produce 史莱姆 Balls on the hutch floor daily
5. Break 史莱姆 Balls for 10-20 史莱姆 each

Danger: 史莱姆s inside the hutch are hostile and will attack you! They deal 7-28 damage per hit. Build fences inside the hutch to separate yourself from the slimes while collecting 史莱姆 Balls. Wear a 史莱姆魅惑戒指 (冒险家公会 reward for killing 1,000 史莱姆s) to become immune to slime damage.
技巧： Build the 史莱姆屋 BEFORE crafting the 史莱姆孵化器. You can place the incubator inside the hutch, where it benefits from the hutch's slime breeding mechanics. Without the hutch, the incubator can only be placed outdoors or in sheds where it still works but lacks the breeding bonus.

4 最优收集路线

Follow this order to minimize backtracking and maximize efficiency. This route assumes a standard 第一年 playthrough.

① 春季 第一年 — Mine Descent + Combat XP Grind

Descend the regular Mines while killing every monster. Don't skip floors — each monster gives valuable Combat XP. On 层 1-29, kill all Green 史莱姆s and collect their 史莱姆 drops (1 per kill, 50% drop rate). By 层 40, you should have 15-25 史莱姆. Continue to 层 80 by end of 春季, fighting Blue and Red slimes for higher drops. Your Combat skill should reach Level 4-5.

② 夏季 第一年 — Farm 100 史莱姆 + Weapon Upgrade

Return to Mines 层 1-15 and farm Green 史莱姆s in a loop (enter floor, kill all slimes, go down one floor, repeat, elevator back to 1). Bring a good weapon — the 熔岩武士刀 from the 冒险家公会 (25,000g, available after reaching 层 120) is ideal. If you can't afford it, the Steel Smallsword (crafted at 战斗等级 4) works fine against Green 史莱姆s. Farm until you have 100 史莱姆. This takes 30-45 minutes of focused grinding.

③ 秋季 第一年 — 骷髅洞穴 + 铱矿石

Unlock the 骷髅洞穴 via 社区中心 Vault bundle (42,500g) or Joja Bus repair. Bring bombs, food, and 楼梯s. Target 层 50+ where Iridium nodes spawn regularly. Mine 10 铱矿石 (for 2 铱锭s). Use bombs on purple Iridium clusters — one well-placed bomb can destroy 3-4 nodes simultaneously, yielding 10-20 ore. Smelt the ore into bars at your furnace.

④ Grind Combat to Level 8

If you haven't reached 战斗等级 8, return to Mines 层 80-120. These floors have Shadow Brutes, Shamans, Skeletons, and Red 史莱姆s — all giving 10-15 XP each. A single run from 层 80 to 120 can yield 300-500 XP. Equip your best weapon, bring healing food (Cheese, Salad, or Life Elixir), and fight every monster. The "Danger in the Deep" quest (Mr. Qi, after completing the Special Orders board) makes the mines significantly harder but gives double XP.

⑤ 最终组装

With all materials gathered and 战斗等级 8 reached: Open crafting menu → SLIME INCUBATOR. Place it inside your 史莱姆屋 for best results. Insert any 史莱姆 Egg to begin the 3-day hatching process.

5 ⚠ 避坑指南 — 别浪费时间

These mistakes will waste hours. Read this before you start.

⚠ 史莱姆s in the hutch will attack you!

史莱姆s inside your 史莱姆屋 are hostile and deal 7-28 damage per hit. They cannot be tamed or made friendly. Always wear a 史莱姆魅惑戒指 (冒险家公会 reward for killing 1,000 史莱姆s) before entering the hutch — it makes you completely immune to slime damage. Without this ring, you'll spend more on healing than you earn from 史莱姆.

⚠ The 史莱姆屋 is expensive to build

The 史莱姆屋 costs 10,000g + 500 石头 + 10 精炼石英 + 1 铱锭. The 铱锭 alone requires a 骷髅洞穴 trip. Many players build the hutch thinking it's required for the 史莱姆孵化器 — it's NOT. The 史莱姆孵化器 can be crafted independently and placed anywhere (outdoors, in sheds, etc.). However, the hutch provides breeding bonuses and 史莱姆 Ball production that make the whole operation profitable.

⚠ 史莱姆 eggs are rare drops

史莱姆 Eggs are NOT guaranteed drops from slimes. The drop rates are approximately: Green 1%, Blue 1.5%, Red 2%, Purple 3%, Tiger 3.5%. This means you need to kill 50+ Green 史莱姆s on average to get one egg. The 史莱姆 Egg Press (战斗等级 6 recipe: 25 煤炭 + 1 火水晶 + 1 电池组) converts 100 史莱姆 into a random 史莱姆 Egg — this is a more reliable source than farming drops.

⚠ You must fill the water trough daily

史莱姆s in the hutch will NOT breed if the water trough is empty. There are 4 trough sections inside the hutch. Fill all 4 with your watering can every single morning. Forgetting even one day halts reproduction. After 姜岛, you can trade Golden Walnuts at the Island Trader for an auto-water system that eliminates this chore entirely.

⚠ 战斗等级 8 takes focused grinding

Many players reach 战斗等级 6-7 naturally through mine progression but get stuck before Level 8. The gap from Level 7 (2,060 XP) to Level 8 (3,000 XP) requires 940 additional XP — equivalent to killing ~160 Green 史莱姆s or ~65 Shadow Brutes. Plan a dedicated combat farming session on Mines 层 80-120 rather than hoping for passive gains.

⚠ 盗贼戒指 is essential for 史莱姆 farming

The 盗贼戒指 (冒险家公会 reward for killing 500 Dust Sprites) doubles all monster loot drops. With this ring, each Blue 史莱姆 drops 2-4 史莱姆 instead of 1-2. This literally halves your farming time for the 100 史莱姆 requirement. Farm Dust Sprites on Mines 层 40-79 while working toward your 史莱姆 collection.

⚠ 史莱姆 Eggs sell for a LOT of gold

Before hatching every 史莱姆 Egg, consider selling the rare ones. Tiger 史莱姆 Eggs sell for 8,000g each, Purple for 5,000g, and Red for 2,500g. If you're short on cash, selling a Purple or Tiger egg is often better than hatching it — especially early-game when every gold piece counts. Only hatch eggs if you need more slimes for your hutch.

6 🎁 额外收益 — 顺路能拿到的好东西

While gathering 史莱姆孵化器 materials, you'll accumulate wealth, XP, and resources that benefit your entire farm. Don't leave these on the table.

🎁 盗贼戒指 — the best combat accessory

Killing 500 Dust Sprites (层 40-79) unlocks the 盗贼戒指 — it doubles ALL monster loot drops permanently. This means double 煤炭 from Dust Sprites, double 史莱姆 from slimes, double 蝙蝠翅膀s from bats, and double geodes from Duggies. It is the single most impactful combat reward in the game and should be your first priority after reaching the 冒险家公会.

🎁 史莱姆魅惑戒指 — immunity to slimes

Killing 1,000 史莱姆s total unlocks the 史莱姆魅惑戒指 from the 冒险家公会. This ring makes you completely immune to all slime damage — essential for safely entering your 史莱姆屋. Without it, high-level slimes (Purple, Tiger) can deal 20+ damage per hit and kill you in seconds.

🎁 战斗等级 8 unlocks 怪物香水 recipe

At 战斗等级 8, you also unlock the 怪物香水 recipe (30 史莱姆 + 30 蝙蝠翅膀). 怪物香水 doubles monster spawn rates for 10 minutes — perfect for future 史莱姆 farming, Dust Sprite grinding, or 骷髅洞穴 monster hunting. It's a self-reinforcing crafting loop: farm slimes → make 怪物香水 → farm more slimes faster.

🎁 冒险家公会 weapon shop

Reaching deeper mine floors unlocks better weapons at the 冒险家公会 shop: Steel Smallsword (战斗等级 2, 250g), Obsidian Edge (层 90, 9,000g), 熔岩武士刀 (层 120, 25,000g). The 熔岩武士刀 (55-64 damage, +3 defense, +25 crit power) is the best pre-骷髅洞穴 weapon and essential for deep 骷髅洞穴 runs.

🎁 铱锭s for other recipes

The 2 铱锭s you craft are also needed for: 宝石复制机 (see our 宝石复制机 guide), 铱制洒水器 (the best sprinkler in the game), and 铱环 (best combat ring). Iridium is never wasted — collect extra ore whenever you visit the 骷髅洞穴.

🎁 史莱姆 farming = infinite crafting materials

Once your 史莱姆屋 is operational with incubators, you'll generate more 史莱姆 than you can spend. Use excess 史莱姆 for: 史莱姆 Egg Press (100 史莱姆 → 史莱姆 Egg worth 1,000-8,000g), 尤巴之戒 (defensive ring), 坚固戒指 (+1 defense), and 洒水器 crafting for farm expansion. A fully operational 史莱姆屋 is a gold-printing machine disguised as a monster farm.
