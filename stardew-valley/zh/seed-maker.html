1 Why the 种子生产器 Unlocks Infinite Farming Wealth

🌱

种子生产器

★★★★☆ — Turn 作物s Into 种子s
耕种等级 9

The 种子生产器 is the gateway to infinite crop replication in 星露谷物语. Place any crop into the 种子生产器 and it produces 1-3 seeds of that crop after 20 minutes of processing. With an average yield of 2 seeds per crop, you effectively double your seed stock every cycle — turning a single 古代水果 into an entire greenhouse full of 古代水果 plants. The 种子生产器 is the engine behind every endgame farming empire, and its most famous use — replicating Ancient 种子s — transforms your farm's profitability forever.

古代水果 is the holy grail of 种子生产器 usage. Ancient 种子s grow into 古代水果 plants that regrow every 7 days year-round in the 温室, and each fruit processes into 古代水果 酒 worth 2,310g with the 工匠 profession. But Ancient 种子s are incredibly rare — they come from Ancient 种子 artifacts (donate to museum first) or rare drops. Once you have your first 古代水果, the 种子生产器 lets you turn each fruit into 1-3 more seeds, exponentially expanding your 古代水果 farm until every tile of your 温室 is producing 2,310g wine every week.

Beyond 古代水果, the 种子生产器 has powerful applications for rare or out-of-season crops. Turn a single Sweet Gem Berry (rare fall crop, 3,000g base value) into 1-3 seeds. Replicate expensive flower seeds like Fairy Rose or Poppy when they're out of season. And most importantly, the 种子生产器 is the only way to get large quantities of seeds for crops that Pierre doesn't stock or that are prohibitively expensive.

★ What This Means for You

One 种子生产器 running 古代水果 can theoretically produce infinite seeds. With 2 seeds per fruit on average, 1 古代水果 → 2 seeds → 2 fruits → 4 seeds → 4 fruits → 8 seeds. Within a single season, a single 种子生产器 can fill your entire 温室 with 古代水果 plants. Each plant produces 2,310g 工匠 酒 per week — a full 温室 of 116 plants generates 268,000g per week with zero seed costs after the first fruit.

3 材料与来源

Each material listed with exact source, season, location, and farming tips.

⚫ 煤炭

来源： Break rocks in mines, kill Dust Sprites, craft in 木炭窑 (10 木头 → 1 煤炭)
季节： Any — available year-round
地点： Every mine floor; Dust Sprites on 层 40-79
Best Farm: Dust Sprites on Mines 层 55 — they spawn in groups of 3-5 and each has a 50% coal drop chance. Use a burglar ring if available.
替代方案： 木炭窑 (10 木头 → 1 煤炭) — slow but reliable. Place 5-10 Kilns and feed them wood while you do other tasks.
技巧： 煤炭 is needed for every smelting operation in the game. By the time you need 种子生产器 materials, you'll likely have 50+ coal from regular mining. If not, a single Dust Sprite run on 层 55 nets 10-20 coal in 10 minutes.

🟡 金锭

来源： Smelt 5 金矿石 + 1 煤炭 in 熔炉
季节： Any — available year-round
地点： Mines 层 80-120 (abundant), 骷髅洞穴 (every floor)
Best 层: 100-120 have the densest gold node spawns
替代方案： Buy from 铁匠铺 克林特 (400g each 第二年+), Fish Treasure Chests, panning spots, 克林特's shop random stock
技巧： If you're already at 耕种等级 9, you've almost certainly descended to 层 100+ in the mines. 5 金矿石 is trivial — you'll collect it passively while farming other materials. Just smelt one bar and you're done.

🪵 木头

来源： Chop any tree with Axe
季节： Any — available year-round
地点： Your farm (trees regrow naturally), 煤林森林, Railroad area
Yield: 4-6 木头 per tree (more with Forester profession at 采集等级 5)
替代方案： 罗宾 sells 木头 for 10g each (250g for 25)
技巧： 25 木头 is negligible — you'll collect this chopping a single tree cluster on your farm. The real challenge is 耕种等级 9, not the materials.

🌾 Farming XP — Reaching Level 9

经验来源： Harvesting crops (+1-8 XP based on crop value), animal care (+5 XP per pet/milk/shear), collecting animal products (+5 XP)
等级9需求： 10,000 XP total (Level 8 = 7,500 XP, Level 9 = 10,000 XP — a 2,500 XP gap)
Fastest Method: Run a full 小桶 operation (see our 小桶 guide) — process crops in 小桶s while harvesting new ones. Cranberries in 秋季 are exceptional: each harvest gives 3-4 berries + XP, and they regrow every 5 days.
替代方案： Animals — build a Barn with 12 Cows. Pet (+5 XP) and milk (+5 XP) all 12 daily = 120 XP/day. Over 21 days = 2,520 XP, enough for the Level 8→9 gap.
Professions: Tiller (Level 5) → 工匠 (Level 10). The 工匠 path is universally recommended and makes all 小桶/Jar products 40% more valuable.

4 最优收集路线

The 种子生产器's real challenge is 耕种等级 9. The materials are trivial — this route focuses on reaching Level 9 as fast as possible while preparing for 古代水果 replication.

① 春季 第一年 — Foundation Farming

Plant maximized crop fields starting 日 1. Parsnips (4-day growth, cheap, good early XP) → Potatoes (high sell value, chance for extra yield) → Kale/Rhubarb (if you have the Bus to Desert by 日 5-6 via 金库包, Rhubarb seeds from Sandy at 100g are exceptional). Target 40+ crops planted by 日 7. Each harvest contributes to Farming XP. Descend the mines for ore but prioritize farming — the 种子生产器 is a Farming tool, not a Mining tool.

② 夏季 第一年 — 小桶s + Hops XP Burst

Build 小桶s as soon as you hit 耕种等级 8 (see our 小桶 guide). Plant Hops on trellises — they harvest daily after 11 days and give solid XP. Plant Melons in bulk (8 XP per harvest, high value). Process everything in 小桶s — the 工匠 goods sell for more, and the cycle of harvest→process→sell→buy more seeds accelerates your farm growth. With 40+ crops and 5-10 小桶s running, you should reach 耕种等级 8 by mid-夏季 and Level 9 by late 夏季 or early 秋季.

③ 秋季 第一年 — Cranberry XP + Level 9 Push

秋季 日 1: Plant Cranberries — they are the best Farming XP crop in the game. Each plant produces 2 berries on first harvest (after 7 days), then regrows 1-2 berries every 5 days. With 40 Cranberry plants, you'll earn 80-120 berries per harvest cycle, each giving Farming XP. Continue processing in 小桶s for Cranberry 酒 (225g base, 315g 工匠).

If you're close to Level 9 by mid-秋季, supplement with Bok Choy (fast 4-day growth, replant frequently) or expand your animal operations. Pet every animal, collect every product. Every point of XP counts.

Get your Ancient 种子: While farming, keep an eye out for the Ancient 种子 artifact (found by digging artifact spots with Hoe, killing bugs in mines, or fishing treasure chests). When you find one, donate it to 冈瑟 at the Museum — he rewards you with plantable Ancient 种子s AND the recipe.

④ Craft 种子生产器 + Start 古代水果 Replication

Upon reaching 耕种等级 9: Craft your first 种子生产器 (1 煤炭 + 1 金锭 + 25 木头 — all trivial by this point). Place it in your house or a shed.

Your first 古代水果 from the 温室 goes directly into the 种子生产器. Wait 20 minutes → collect 1-3 Ancient 种子s. Plant immediately. Repeat with every new 古代水果. With average luck (2 seeds per fruit), your 古代水果 count doubles every growth cycle (7 days).

Pro tip: Build 3-5 种子生产器s and run them simultaneously. Queue up multiple 古代水果s at once — one 种子生产器 processes one fruit every 20 minutes, so 5 种子生产器s process 5 fruits in parallel.

⑤ 冬季 第一年 — 温室 Empire

With the 温室 unlocked (complete the 食材包 at the 社区中心, or buy from Joja for 35,000g), fill every available tile with 古代水果. The 温室 has 116 plantable tiles (after accounting for sprinkler placement with 铱制洒水器s).

Each 古代水果 plant produces one fruit every 7 days. Harvest → 种子生产器 → plant new seeds → repeat until full. Once full, all 古代水果s go to 小桶s for 酒 production. With 工匠 profession, each bottle sells for 2,310g. A full 温室 generates 268,000g per week in 酒 alone.

5 ⚠ 避坑指南 — 别浪费时间

These mistakes will delay your 古代水果 empire by entire seasons. Read this before you start.

⚠ Donate your FIRST Ancient 种子 artifact to the Museum

When you find an Ancient 种子 artifact (the brown artifact, NOT the plantable seed), you MUST donate it to 冈瑟 at the Museum. He will give you the plantable Ancient 种子s AND the crafting recipe to turn future Ancient 种子 artifacts into plantable seeds. If you try to craft the artifact into seeds without donating first, you waste your only seed. This is the #1 mistake new players make.

⚠ 种子生产器 has a 1.25% chance to produce Mixed 种子s instead

Approximately 1 in 80 种子生产器 uses produces "Mixed 种子s" instead of the target crop's seeds. For 古代水果, this means losing a precious fruit with nothing to show for it. Always process more fruits than you strictly need, and never put your last 古代水果 into a 种子生产器 without a backup.

⚠ 古代水果 takes a full season to mature

古代水果 plants take 28 days to mature and then produce fruit every 7 days. In the 温室 (year-round growing), this isn't a problem. But never plant 古代水果 outdoors — it won't survive season changes. The 温室 is mandatory for 古代水果 farming.

⚠ 耕种等级 9 is a significant grind

The gap from Level 8 to Level 9 is 2,500 XP. At ~5 XP per crop harvest, that's 500 crops. With regrowing crops (Cranberries, Hops), this accelerates significantly. But don't expect to hit Level 9 without planting 40+ crops per season and maintaining them consistently. Animal care (petting + collecting) provides a steady XP supplement.

⚠ 种子生产器 works on MOST crops, not all

The 种子生产器 cannot process: Blackberries, Salmonberries, Coconut, Cactus Fruit, Cave Carrot, Fiddlehead Fern, Fruit Tree fruits, Salmonberry, Spice Berry, or Wild Plum. It also cannot process Foraged items. Stick to farmed crops — 古代水果, 杨桃, Melons, Cranberries, and flowers work perfectly.

⚠ Quality of input crop doesn't affect seed output

A Gold Quality 古代水果 and a Base Quality 古代水果 produce the exact same seed output. Sell your high-quality crops directly (they're worth more as-is) and use base-quality crops for the 种子生产器. The seed output probabilities are identical regardless of input quality.

6 🎁 额外收益 — 顺路能拿到的好东西

While pushing for 耕种等级 9 and building your 种子生产器 operation, you'll accumulate massive benefits across your entire farm. Don't leave these on the table.

🎁 小桶 infrastructure is already built

By the time you reach 耕种等级 9, you've already built 小桶s (Level 8), processed crops, and established your artisan goods pipeline. The 种子生产器 slots directly into this workflow: Harvest → 种子生产器 (replicate) + 小桶 (profit). Your 小桶 棚屋 is ready — you just need the seeds to feed it.

🎁 耕种等级 10 + 工匠 profession

After crafting the 种子生产器, 耕种等级 10 is just 5,000 XP away. The 工匠 profession (+40% to all artisan goods) transforms your economy. 古代水果 酒 goes from 1,650g to 2,310g. 杨桃 酒 goes from 2,250g to 3,150g. 工匠 is the single most impactful profession choice in the game.

🎁 温室 unlocks year-round farming

Completing the 食材包 (or Joja 温室 purchase) gives you a 10x12 tile growing space that is immune to seasons. 古代水果, Coffee Beans, and fruit trees all thrive year-round. The 温室 is the single most valuable farm upgrade and is essential for 种子生产器 strategies.

🎁 Museum rewards from artifact hunting

While searching for Ancient 种子 artifacts (digging worm spots, fishing treasure, killing mine bugs), you'll find dozens of other artifacts and minerals. Donating them to 冈瑟 unlocks unique rewards: the Rusty Key (access to Sewers), 星之果实 (permanent energy increase), and rare crafting recipes. Complete the museum for a special reward from 冈瑟.

🎁 种子生产器 has other powerful uses

Beyond 古代水果, the 种子生产器 excels at: Sweet Gem Berry replication (rare 秋季 crop, 3,000g base value), Flower seed production (Fairy Rose, Poppy for 蜂蜜), and out-of-season crop replication (plant 夏季 crops in the 温室 during 冬季 by making seeds from stored crops).

🎁 种子生产器 materials are needed for other recipes

The 煤炭 and 金锭 you collect are also used for: Quality 洒水器s (1 Iron + 1 Gold + 1 精炼石英), 铱制洒水器s (1 Battery + 1 Iridium + 1 Gold), and dozens of other crafting recipes. 金锭s are one of the most versatile crafting materials in the game — stockpile extra.
