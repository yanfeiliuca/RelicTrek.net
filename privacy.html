Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RelicTrek (relictrek.net) collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website. By using RelicTrek, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We are committed to being transparent about our data practices. Please read this policy carefully to understand how we handle information related to your visit.

Information We Collect

RelicTrek does not directly collect any personally identifiable information from our visitors. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number to use our website.

However, we use third-party services that may automatically collect certain data when you visit our site:

Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This service collects anonymous usage data, including pages visited, time spent on each page, browser type, device information, and referring websites. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally. For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

Google AdSense: We display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to our website and other websites across the internet. This helps deliver ads that are more relevant to your interests. For more details, see the Advertising section below.

Language Preference: We store your preferred language setting (English or Chinese) using your browser's localStorage. This allows us to remember your choice when you return to our site. This data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

RelicTrek uses the following types of cookies:

Necessary Cookies: These cookies are essential for the basic functionality of our website. They enable core features such as language preference storage and cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies: These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous information. This data helps us improve our content and user experience. These cookies are set by Google Analytics.

Advertising Cookies: These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are relevant to you and your interests. They are also used to limit the number of times you see an ad and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. These cookies are set by Google as a third-party vendor.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our website. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

Managing Cookies: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to accept or decline cookies, or to be notified when a cookie is being sent. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website and other websites you visit. To learn more about managing cookies, visit AboutCookies.org. You can also opt out of personalized advertising from Google by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Advertising

RelicTrek uses Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. These ads help us keep our content free for all users.

Google uses the DART cookie to serve ads to our visitors based on their visit to RelicTrek and other websites on the internet. The DART cookie allows Google to display ads that are tailored to your interests, but it does NOT track personal information such as your name, email address, or physical address.

You can choose to disable the DART cookie and opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page. Please note that even if you opt out of personalized advertising, you will still see ads on our website, but they will not be tailored to your interests.

For more information about how Google uses data for advertising purposes, please visit Google's Advertising Policies.

Google AdSense — We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and web beacons to collect information about your visits to our website and other websites to provide advertisements about goods and services that may be of interest to you.
